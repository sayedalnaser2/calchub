BTU to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert BTU to foot-pounds instantly. 1 BTU = 778.16926 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert BTU to foot-pounds, multiply the number of BTU by 778.16926. One BTU equals 778.16926 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 0.0012850675 BTU. The table shows common values.

BTU to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = BTU × 778.16926

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 778.16926 = 9,727.1158 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 0.0012850675 (or divide by 778.16926).
