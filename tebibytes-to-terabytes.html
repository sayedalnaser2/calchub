Convert tebibytes to terabytes instantly. 1 TiB = 1.0995116 TB.

How it works

To convert tebibytes to terabytes, multiply the number of tebibytes by 1.0995116. One tebibyte equals 1.0995116 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 0.9094947 tebibytes. The table shows common values.

Tebibytes to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = tebibytes × 1.0995116

Worked example: convert 12.5 tebibytes to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.0995116 = 13.743895 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 0.9094947 (or divide by 1.0995116).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
