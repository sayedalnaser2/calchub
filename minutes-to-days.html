Minutes to Days Converter

Convert minutes to days instantly. 1 min = 0.00069444444 days.

How it works

To convert minutes to days, multiply the number of minutes by 0.00069444444. One minute equals 0.00069444444 days, and one day equals 1,440 minutes. The table shows common values.

Minutes to Days formula

Days = minutes × 0.00069444444

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to days. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00069444444 = 0.0086805556 days. To go the other way, multiply days by 1,440 (or divide by 0.00069444444).
