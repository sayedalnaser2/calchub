How it works

To convert minutes to days, multiply the number of minutes by 0.00069444444. To convert days to minutes, multiply by 1,440 (or divide by 0.00069444444). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Minutes to Days formula

Days = minutes × 0.00069444444

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to days. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00069444444 = 0.0086805556 days. To go the other way, multiply days by 1,440 (or divide by 0.00069444444).
