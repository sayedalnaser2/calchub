Momentum Calculator

Calculate momentum and kinetic energy from mass and velocity.

How it works

Momentum p = m x v. It is conserved in collisions. Kinetic energy KE = 1/2 m v^2 is not always conserved because some becomes heat and deformation.

Frequently asked questions

What is impulse?

Impulse is force x time and equals the change in momentum.

Is momentum a vector?

Yes, it has direction.
