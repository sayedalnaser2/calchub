Watt Hours to Joules Converter

Convert watt hours to joules instantly. 1 Wh = 3,600 J.

How it works

To convert watt hours to joules, multiply the number of watt hours by 3,600. One watt hour equals 3,600 joules, and one joule equals 0.00027777778 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Joules formula

Joules = watt hours × 3,600

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600 = 45,000 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 0.00027777778 (or divide by 3,600).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
