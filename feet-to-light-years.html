How it works

To convert feet to light years, multiply the number of feet by 3.22174e-17. One foot equals 3.22174e-17 light years, and one light year equals 3.10391e+16 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Light Years formula

Light years = feet × 3.22174e-17

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
