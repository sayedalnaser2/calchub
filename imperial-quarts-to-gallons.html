How it works

To convert imperial quarts to gallons, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 0.30023748. One imperial quart equals 0.30023748 gallons, and one gallon equals 3.3306967 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Gallons formula

Gallons = imperial quarts × 0.30023748

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.30023748 = 3.7529685 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 3.3306967 (or divide by 0.30023748).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
