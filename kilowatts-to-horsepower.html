Kilowatts to Horsepower Converter

Convert kilowatts to horsepower instantly. 1 kW = 1.3410221 hp.

How it works

To convert kilowatts to horsepower, multiply the number of kilowatts by 1.3410221. One kilowatt equals 1.3410221 horsepower, and one horsepower equals 0.74569987 kilowatts. The table shows common values.

Kilowatts to Horsepower formula

Horsepower = kilowatts × 1.3410221

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatts to horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3410221 = 16.762776 hp. To go the other way, multiply horsepower by 0.74569987 (or divide by 1.3410221).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
