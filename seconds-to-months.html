How it works

To convert seconds to months, multiply the number of seconds by 3.80265e-07. To convert months to seconds, multiply by 2,629,746 (or divide by 3.80265e-07). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Seconds to Months formula

Months = seconds × 3.80265e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to months. Multiply 12.5 × 3.80265e-07 = 4.75331e-06 months. To go the other way, multiply months by 2,629,746 (or divide by 3.80265e-07).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
