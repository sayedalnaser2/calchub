How it works

Margin is profit as a share of the selling price. Markup is profit as a share of cost. A 50% markup equals a 33.3% margin.

Formula

Margin = profit / price and markup = profit / cost. Enter any two of cost, price, margin and markup (for example margin alone with cost) to solve the rest.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin divides profit by price; markup divides profit by cost. Markup is always the larger number.
