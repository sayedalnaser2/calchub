Liters per Minute to Cubic Feet per Minute Converter

How it works

To convert liters per minute to cubic feet per minute, multiply the number of liters per minute by 0.035314667. One liter per minute equals 0.035314667 cubic feet per minute, and one cubic foot per minute equals 28.316847 liters per minute. The table shows common values.

Liters per Minute to Cubic Feet per Minute formula

Cubic feet per minute = liters per minute × 0.035314667

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per minute to cubic feet per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 0.035314667 = 0.44143333 cfm. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet per minute by 28.316847 (or divide by 0.035314667).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
