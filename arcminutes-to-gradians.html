Arcminutes to Gradians Converter

Convert arcminutes to gradians instantly. 1 arcmin = 0.018518519 grad.

How it works

To convert arcminutes to gradians, multiply the number of arcminutes by 0.018518519. One arcminute equals 0.018518519 gradians, and one gradian equals 54 arcminutes. The table shows common values.

Arcminutes to Gradians formula

Gradians = arcminutes × 0.018518519

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcminutes to gradians. Multiply 12.5 × 0.018518519 = 0.23148148 grad. To go the other way, multiply gradians by 54 (or divide by 0.018518519).
