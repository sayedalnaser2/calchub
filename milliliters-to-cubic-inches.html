Milliliters to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert milliliters to cubic inches instantly. 1 ml = 0.061023744 in³.

How it works

To convert milliliters to cubic inches, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.061023744. One milliliter equals 0.061023744 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 16.387064 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = milliliters × 0.061023744

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.061023744 = 0.7627968 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 16.387064 (or divide by 0.061023744).
