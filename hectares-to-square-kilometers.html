How it works

To convert hectares to square kilometers, multiply the number of hectares by 0.01. To convert square kilometers to hectares, multiply by 100 (or divide by 0.01). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Hectares to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = hectares × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 100 (or divide by 0.01).

About area units

Area measures a flat surface. The square meter is the SI unit; square feet and acres are widely used for property, flooring and land.
