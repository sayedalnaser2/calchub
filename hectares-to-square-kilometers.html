Hectares to Square Kilometers Converter

Convert hectares to square kilometers instantly. 1 ha = 0.01 km².

How it works

To convert hectares to square kilometers, multiply the number of hectares by 0.01. One hectare equals 0.01 square kilometers, and one square kilometer equals 100 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = hectares × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 100 (or divide by 0.01).
