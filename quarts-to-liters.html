How it works

To convert quarts to liters, multiply the number of quarts by 0.94635295. To convert liters to quarts, multiply by 1.0566882 (or divide by 0.94635295). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Quarts to Liters formula

Liters = quarts × 0.94635295

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.94635295 = 11.829412 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 1.0566882 (or divide by 0.94635295).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
