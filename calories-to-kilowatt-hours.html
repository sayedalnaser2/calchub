Calories to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert calories to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 cal = 1.16222e-06 kWh.

How it works

To convert calories to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of calories by 1.16222e-06. One calorie equals 1.16222e-06 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 860,420.65 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = calories × 1.16222e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 1.16222e-06 = 1.45278e-05 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 860,420.65 (or divide by 1.16222e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
