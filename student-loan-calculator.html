Estimate student loan payments and how extra payments shorten repayment.

How it works

Payments are calculated on a standard fixed schedule. Entering an extra monthly amount shows how much sooner you finish and how much interest you avoid.

Frequently asked questions

Standard repayment term?

Federal student loans default to a 10-year standard plan. Income-driven plans extend the term and change the payment.

Does this include income-driven plans?

No. This uses a fixed-rate amortization schedule.
