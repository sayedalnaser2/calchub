Cups of Granulated Sugar to Grams

Convert cups of granulated sugar to grams. 1 cup of granulated sugar weighs about 198 g.

How it works

Granulated Sugar is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 198 g per US cup, convert cups to grams by multiplying by 198.

1 US cup of granulated sugar weighs about 198 g (7.0 oz), 1 tablespoon about 12.4 g and 1 teaspoon about 4.1 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
