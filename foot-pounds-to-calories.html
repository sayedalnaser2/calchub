Foot-Pounds to Calories Converter

Convert foot-pounds to calories instantly. 1 ft-lb = 0.32404827 cal.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to calories, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 0.32404827. One foot-pound equals 0.32404827 calories, and one calorie equals 3.08596 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Calories formula

Calories = foot-pounds × 0.32404827

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.32404827 = 4.0506033 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 3.08596 (or divide by 0.32404827).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
