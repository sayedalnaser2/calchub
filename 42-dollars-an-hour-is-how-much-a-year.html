$42 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

$42 an hour is $87,360 a year at 40 hours a week. Adjust hours and weeks for weekly, monthly and biweekly pay.

How it works

Annual pay is the hourly rate times hours per week times weeks per year. At $42 an hour and 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, that is $87,360. Monthly pay divides the annual figure by 12.

Take-home pay illustration

Gross pay of $87,360 a year is before taxes and deductions. If roughly 20% were withheld, take-home pay would be about $69,888 a year or $5,824 a month. Your real withholding depends on filing status, state and benefits.
