Quarts to Cubic Centimeters Converter

Convert quarts to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 qt = 946.35295 cm³.

How it works

To convert quarts to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of quarts by 946.35295. One quart equals 946.35295 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.0010566882 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = quarts × 946.35295

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 946.35295 = 11,829.412 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.0010566882 (or divide by 946.35295).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
