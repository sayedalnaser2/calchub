Mortgage Points Calculator

Decide whether paying discount points on a mortgage is worth it.

How it works

One point costs 1% of the loan and typically lowers the rate by about 0.25%. Divide the cost by monthly savings to see how long until it pays off.

Frequently asked questions

When do points make sense?

When you will keep the loan well past the break-even point.
