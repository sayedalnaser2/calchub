Turkey Cooking Time Calculator

Estimate roasting time for a turkey by weight at 325 °F.

How it works

Times follow the USDA guide for a fresh or fully thawed turkey roasted at 325 °F. Always confirm doneness with a food thermometer reaching 165 °F in the thickest part of the breast, thigh and stuffing.

Frequently asked questions

How long to thaw a turkey?

Allow about 24 hours in the refrigerator for every 4-5 lb.

Should I cover the turkey?

Tent it with foil if the skin browns too quickly.
