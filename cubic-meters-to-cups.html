How it works

To convert cubic meters to cups, multiply the number of cubic meters by 4,226.7528. One cubic meter equals 4,226.7528 cups, and one cup equals 0.00023658824 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Cups formula

Cups = cubic meters × 4,226.7528

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 4,226.7528 = 52,834.41 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.00023658824 (or divide by 4,226.7528).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
