Teaspoons to Tablespoons Converter

Convert teaspoons to tablespoons instantly. 1 tsp = 0.33333333 tbsp.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to tablespoons, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.33333333. One teaspoon equals 0.33333333 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 3 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = teaspoons × 0.33333333

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.33333333 = 4.1666667 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 3 (or divide by 0.33333333).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
