Months to Years Converter

Convert months to years instantly. 1 months = 0.083333333 years.

How it works

To convert months to years, multiply the number of months by 0.083333333. One month equals 0.083333333 years, and one year equals 12 months. The table shows common values.

Months to Years formula

Years = months × 0.083333333

Worked example: convert 12.5 months to years. Multiply 12.5 × 0.083333333 = 1.0416667 years. To go the other way, multiply years by 12 (or divide by 0.083333333).
