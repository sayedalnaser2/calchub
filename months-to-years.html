How it works

To convert months to years, multiply the number of months by 0.083333333. To convert years to months, multiply by 12 (or divide by 0.083333333). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Months to Years formula

Years = months × 0.083333333

Worked example: convert 12.5 months to years. Multiply 12.5 × 0.083333333 = 1.0416667 years. To go the other way, multiply years by 12 (or divide by 0.083333333).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
