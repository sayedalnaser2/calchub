Kibibytes to Kilobits Converter

Convert kibibytes to kilobits instantly. 1 KiB = 8.192 Kb.

How it works

To convert kibibytes to kilobits, multiply the number of kibibytes by 8.192. One kibibyte equals 8.192 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 0.12207031 kibibytes. The table shows common values.

Kibibytes to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = kibibytes × 8.192

Worked example: convert 12.5 kibibytes to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 8.192 = 102.4 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 0.12207031 (or divide by 8.192).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
