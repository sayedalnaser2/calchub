Ounce-Inches to Pound-Feet Converter

Convert ounce-inches to pound-feet instantly. 1 oz-in = 0.0052083333 lb-ft.

How it works

To convert ounce-inches to pound-feet, multiply the number of ounce-inches by 0.0052083333. One ounce-inch equals 0.0052083333 pound-feet, and one pound-foot equals 192 ounce-inches. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Inches to Pound-Feet formula

Pound-feet = ounce-inches × 0.0052083333

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounce-inches to pound-feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0052083333 = 0.065104166 lb-ft. To go the other way, multiply pound-feet by 192 (or divide by 0.0052083333).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
