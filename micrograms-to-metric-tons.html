Convert micrograms to metric tons instantly. 1 µg = 1e-12 t.

How it works

To convert micrograms to metric tons, multiply the number of micrograms by 1e-12. One microgram equals 1e-12 metric tons, and one metric ton equals 1,000,000,000,000 micrograms. The table shows common values.

Micrograms to Metric Tons formula

Metric tons = micrograms × 1e-12

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrograms to metric tons. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-12 = 1.25e-11 t. To go the other way, multiply metric tons by 1,000,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-12).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
