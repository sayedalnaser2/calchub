Fuel Economy Converter (MPG to L/100km)

Convert miles per gallon to litres per 100 km and kilometres per litre.

How it works

L/100km = 235.2146 / MPG (US). The metric figure shows fuel used per distance, so lower is better.

Frequently asked questions

Why do the units run opposite ways?

MPG measures distance per fuel (higher is better) while L/100 km measures fuel per distance (lower is better).
