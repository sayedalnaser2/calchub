Kilobytes to Gibibytes Converter

Convert kilobytes to gibibytes instantly. 1 KB = 9.31323e-07 GiB.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to gibibytes, multiply the number of kilobytes by 9.31323e-07. One kilobyte equals 9.31323e-07 gibibytes, and one gibibyte equals 1,073,741.8 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Gibibytes formula

Gibibytes = kilobytes × 9.31323e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to gibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 9.31323e-07 = 1.16415e-05 GiB. To go the other way, multiply gibibytes by 1,073,741.8 (or divide by 9.31323e-07).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
