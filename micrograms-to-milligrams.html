Micrograms to Milligrams Converter

Convert micrograms to milligrams instantly. 1 µg = 0.001 mg.

How it works

To convert micrograms to milligrams, multiply the number of micrograms by 0.001. One microgram equals 0.001 milligrams, and one milligram equals 1,000 micrograms. The table shows common values.

Micrograms to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = micrograms × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrograms to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
