Kilohertz to Hertz Converter

Convert kilohertz to hertz instantly. 1 kHz = 1,000 Hz.

How it works

To convert kilohertz to hertz, multiply the number of kilohertz by 1,000. One kilohertz equals 1,000 hertz, and one hertz equals 0.001 kilohertz. The table shows common values.

Kilohertz to Hertz formula

Hertz = kilohertz × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilohertz to hertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 Hz. To go the other way, multiply hertz by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
