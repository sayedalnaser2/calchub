Hours to Minutes Converter

Convert hours to minutes instantly. 1 h = 60 min.

How it works

To convert hours to minutes, multiply the number of hours by 60. One hour equals 60 minutes, and one minute equals 0.016666667 hours. The table shows common values.

Hours to Minutes formula

Minutes = hours × 60

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 60 = 750 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 0.016666667 (or divide by 60).
