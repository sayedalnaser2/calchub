Dew Point Calculator

Calculate the dew point from air temperature and relative humidity.

How it works

The dew point is the temperature at which air becomes saturated and dew forms. It is calculated with the Magnus formula using constants a = 17.62 and b = 243.12 for temperatures in °C.

Frequently asked questions

What dew point feels uncomfortable?

Above about 65 °F most people find the air muggy.

Why can dew point be better than humidity?

It measures actual moisture in the air, while relative humidity changes with temperature.
