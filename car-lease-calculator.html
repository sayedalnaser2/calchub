Car Lease Calculator

Estimate a monthly lease payment from price, residual value and money factor.

How it works

Lease payment = depreciation ((cap cost - residual) / term) + finance charge ((cap cost + residual) x money factor), plus tax. Money factor = APR / 2400.

Frequently asked questions

What is the money factor?

A lease interest figure. Multiply it by 2400 to get the approximate APR.
