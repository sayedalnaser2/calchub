How it works

To convert imperial pints to milliliters, multiply the number of imperial pints by 568.26125. To convert milliliters to imperial pints, multiply by 0.001759754 (or divide by 568.26125). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Imperial Pints to Milliliters formula

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
