Imperial Pints to Milliliters Converter

Convert imperial pints to milliliters instantly. 1 imp pt = 568.26125 ml.

How it works

To convert imperial pints to milliliters, multiply the number of imperial pints by 568.26125. One imperial pint equals 568.26125 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.001759754 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = imperial pints × 568.26125

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 568.26125 = 7,103.2656 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.001759754 (or divide by 568.26125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
