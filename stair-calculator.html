Stair Calculator

Calculate the number of steps, riser height and stringer length.

How it works

Divide the total rise by a target riser height, round up, then recompute an even riser. Total run = tread depth x (risers - 1).

Frequently asked questions

What are code limits?

US residential code typically allows risers up to 7.75 in with treads at least 10 in.
