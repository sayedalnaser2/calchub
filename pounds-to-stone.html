Pounds to Stone Converter

Convert pounds to stone instantly. 1 lb = 0.071428571 st.

How it works

To convert pounds to stone, multiply the number of pounds by 0.071428571. One pound equals 0.071428571 stone, and one stone equals 14 pounds. The table shows common values.

Pounds to Stone formula

Stone = pounds × 0.071428571

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 0.071428571 = 0.89285714 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 14 (or divide by 0.071428571).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
