Weeks to Seconds Converter

Convert weeks to seconds instantly. 1 week = 604,800 s.

How it works

To convert weeks to seconds, multiply the number of weeks by 604,800. One week equals 604,800 seconds, and one second equals 1.65344e-06 weeks. The table shows common values.

Weeks to Seconds formula

Seconds = weeks × 604,800

Worked example: convert 12.5 weeks to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 604,800 = 7,560,000 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 1.65344e-06 (or divide by 604,800).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
