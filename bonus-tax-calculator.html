Bonus Tax Calculator

Estimate what you keep from a bonus after withholding.

How it works

Bonuses are often withheld at a flat 22% federal supplemental rate plus FICA and state tax. Your actual tax is settled on your return.

Frequently asked questions

Is a bonus taxed more?

Not really. Withholding can be higher, but you get any excess back at tax time.
