Seconds to Milliseconds Converter

Convert seconds to milliseconds instantly. 1 s = 1,000 ms.

How it works

To convert seconds to milliseconds, multiply the number of seconds by 1,000. One second equals 1,000 milliseconds, and one millisecond equals 0.001 seconds. The table shows common values.

Seconds to Milliseconds formula

Milliseconds = seconds × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to milliseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 ms. To go the other way, multiply milliseconds by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
