Seconds to Milliseconds Converter (Both Ways)

Convert seconds to milliseconds and milliseconds to seconds instantly. 1 s = 1,000 ms.

Type in any 1 field and the rest are calculated.

How it works

To convert seconds to milliseconds, multiply the number of seconds by 1,000. To convert milliseconds to seconds, multiply by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Seconds to Milliseconds formula

Milliseconds = seconds × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to milliseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 ms. To go the other way, multiply milliseconds by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
