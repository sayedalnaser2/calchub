Micrometers to Miles Converter

Convert micrometers to miles instantly. 1 µm = 6.21371e-10 mi.

How it works

To convert micrometers to miles, multiply the number of micrometers by 6.21371e-10. One micrometer equals 6.21371e-10 miles, and one mile equals 1,609,344,000 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Miles formula

Miles = micrometers × 6.21371e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 6.21371e-10 = 7.76714e-09 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1,609,344,000 (or divide by 6.21371e-10).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
