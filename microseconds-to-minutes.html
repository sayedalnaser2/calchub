Microseconds to Minutes Converter

Convert microseconds to minutes instantly. 1 µs = 1.66667e-08 min.

How it works

To convert microseconds to minutes, multiply the number of microseconds by 1.66667e-08. One microsecond equals 1.66667e-08 minutes, and one minute equals 60,000,000 microseconds. The table shows common values.

Microseconds to Minutes formula

Minutes = microseconds × 1.66667e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 microseconds to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.66667e-08 = 2.08333e-07 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 60,000,000 (or divide by 1.66667e-08).
