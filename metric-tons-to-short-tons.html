How it works

To convert metric tons to short tons, multiply the number of metric tons by 1.1023113. One metric ton equals 1.1023113 short tons, and one short ton equals 0.90718474 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Short Tons formula

Short tons = metric tons × 1.1023113

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to short tons. Multiply 12.5 × 1.1023113 = 13.778891 short tons. To go the other way, multiply short tons by 0.90718474 (or divide by 1.1023113).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
