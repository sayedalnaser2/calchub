How it works

To convert days to centuries, multiply the number of days by 2.73791e-05. To convert centuries to days, multiply by 36,524.25 (or divide by 2.73791e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Days to Centuries formula

Centuries = days × 2.73791e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to centuries. Multiply 12.5 × 2.73791e-05 = 0.00034223838 centuries. To go the other way, multiply centuries by 36,524.25 (or divide by 2.73791e-05).
