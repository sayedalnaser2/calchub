How it works

To convert horsepower to megawatts, multiply the number of horsepower by 0.00074569987. One horsepower equals 0.00074569987 megawatts, and one megawatt equals 1,341.0221 horsepower. The table shows common values.

Horsepower to Megawatts formula

Megawatts = horsepower × 0.00074569987

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
