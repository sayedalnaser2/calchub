Yards to Fathoms Converter

Convert yards to fathoms instantly. 1 yd = 0.5 fathoms.

How it works

To convert yards to fathoms, multiply the number of yards by 0.5. One yard equals 0.5 fathoms, and one fathom equals 2 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = yards × 0.5

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.5 = 6.25 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 2 (or divide by 0.5).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
