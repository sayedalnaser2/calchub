Years to Months Converter

Convert years to months instantly. 1 years = 12 months.

How it works

To convert years to months, multiply the number of years by 12. One year equals 12 months, and one month equals 0.083333333 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Months formula

Months = years × 12

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to months. Multiply 12.5 × 12 = 150 months. To go the other way, multiply months by 0.083333333 (or divide by 12).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
