Cubic Yards to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert cubic yards to fluid ounces instantly. 1 yd³ = 25,852.675 fl oz.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to fluid ounces, multiply the number of cubic yards by 25,852.675. One cubic yard equals 25,852.675 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 3.86807e-05 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = cubic yards × 25,852.675

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 25,852.675 = 323,158.44 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 3.86807e-05 (or divide by 25,852.675).

About volume units
