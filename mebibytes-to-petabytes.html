Mebibytes to Petabytes Converter

Convert mebibytes to petabytes instantly. 1 MiB = 1.04858e-09 PB.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to petabytes, multiply the number of mebibytes by 1.04858e-09. One mebibyte equals 1.04858e-09 petabytes, and one petabyte equals 953,674,320 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Petabytes formula

Petabytes = mebibytes × 1.04858e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to petabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.04858e-09 = 1.31072e-08 PB. To go the other way, multiply petabytes by 953,674,320 (or divide by 1.04858e-09).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
