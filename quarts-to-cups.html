Quarts to Cups Converter

Convert quarts to cups instantly. 1 qt = 4 cup.

How it works

To convert quarts to cups, multiply the number of quarts by 4. One quart equals 4 cups, and one cup equals 0.25 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Cups formula

Cups = quarts × 4

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 4 = 50 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.25 (or divide by 4).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
