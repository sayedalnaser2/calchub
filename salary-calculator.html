Salary Calculator (Hourly to Annual and Back)

Convert hourly pay to annual salary, or annual salary to an hourly rate.

Type in any 3 fields and the rest are calculated.

How it works

Yearly pay is hourly rate times hours per week times weeks worked. Figures are before taxes and deductions.

Formula

Annual pay = hourly rate x hours per week x weeks per year. Enter any three values, for example an annual salary, hours and weeks to find the hourly wage.

Frequently asked questions

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax. Your take-home pay will be lower depending on income tax, Social Security and other deductions.
