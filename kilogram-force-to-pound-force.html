Convert kilogram-force to pound-force instantly. 1 kgf = 2.2046226 lbf.

How it works

To convert kilogram-force to pound-force, multiply the number of kilogram-force by 2.2046226. One kilogram-force equals 2.2046226 pound-force, and one pound-force equals 0.45359237 kilogram-force. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force to Pound-Force formula

Pound-force = kilogram-force × 2.2046226

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force to pound-force. Multiply 12.5 × 2.2046226 = 27.557783 lbf. To go the other way, multiply pound-force by 0.45359237 (or divide by 2.2046226).
