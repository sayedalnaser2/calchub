How it works

To convert gallons to imperial pints, multiply the number of gallons by 6.6613935. One gallon equals 6.6613935 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.15011874 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = gallons × 6.6613935

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 6.6613935 = 83.267418 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.15011874 (or divide by 6.6613935).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
