Calories Burned by Activity (MET) Calculator

Estimate calories burned for walking, cycling, swimming, yoga and more using MET values.

How it works

Calories = MET x body weight (kg) x hours. A MET is the ratio of the activity's energy use to resting energy use (1 MET at rest). Values are from the Compendium of Physical Activities and are averages.

Frequently asked questions

What is a MET?

A metabolic equivalent; sitting quietly is 1 MET.

Why do results differ from my watch?

Wearables use heart rate and personal data, so they may differ.
