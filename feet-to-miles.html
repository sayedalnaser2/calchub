How it works

To convert feet to miles, multiply the number of feet by 0.00018939394. One foot equals 0.00018939394 miles, and one mile equals 5,280 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Miles formula

Miles = feet × 0.00018939394

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00018939394 = 0.0023674242 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 5,280 (or divide by 0.00018939394).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
