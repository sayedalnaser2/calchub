Grams of Cornstarch to Cups

Convert grams of cornstarch to cups. 1 cup of cornstarch weighs about 112 g.

How it works

Cornstarch is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 112 g per US cup, convert grams to cups by multiplying by 0.008929.

1 US cup of cornstarch weighs about 112 g (4.0 oz), 1 tablespoon about 7.0 g and 1 teaspoon about 2.3 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
