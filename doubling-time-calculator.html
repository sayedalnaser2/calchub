Doubling Time Calculator

Find how many years it takes money (or anything growing) to double.

How it works

Exact doubling time is ln(2) / ln(1 + r). The rule of 72 approximates it as 72 divided by the percentage rate.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the rule of 72?

Very close for rates between about 4% and 12%.

Does it work for inflation?

Yes. At 3% inflation prices double in about 23 years.
