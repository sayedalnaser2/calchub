How it works

To convert knots to meters per second, multiply the number of knots by 0.51444444. To convert meters per second to knots, multiply by 1.9438445 (or divide by 0.51444444). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Knots to Meters per Second formula

Meters per second = knots × 0.51444444

Worked example: convert 12.5 knots to meters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.51444444 = 6.4305556 m/s. To go the other way, multiply meters per second by 1.9438445 (or divide by 0.51444444).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
