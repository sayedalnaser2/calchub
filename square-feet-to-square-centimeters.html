Square Feet to Square Centimeters Converter

Convert square feet to square centimeters instantly. 1 ft² = 929.0304 cm².

How it works

To convert square feet to square centimeters, multiply the number of square feet by 929.0304. One square foot equals 929.0304 square centimeters, and one square centimeter equals 0.001076391 square feet. The table shows common values.

Square Feet to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square feet × 929.0304

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 929.0304 = 11,612.88 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 0.001076391 (or divide by 929.0304).
