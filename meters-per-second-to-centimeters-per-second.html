How it works

To convert meters per second to centimeters per second, multiply the number of meters per second by 100. To convert centimeters per second to meters per second, multiply by 0.01 (or divide by 100). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Meters per Second to Centimeters per Second formula

Centimeters per second = meters per second × 100

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters per second to centimeters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 100 = 1,250 cm/s. To go the other way, multiply centimeters per second by 0.01 (or divide by 100).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.

Frequently asked questions

How many centimeters per second are in one meter per second?

There are 0.01 meters per second in one centimeter per second.
