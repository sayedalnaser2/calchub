Meters per Second to Centimeters per Second Converter

Convert meters per second to centimeters per second instantly. 1 m/s = 100 cm/s.

How it works

To convert meters per second to centimeters per second, multiply the number of meters per second by 100. One meter per second equals 100 centimeters per second, and one centimeter per second equals 0.01 meters per second. The table shows common values.

Meters per Second to Centimeters per Second formula

Centimeters per second = meters per second × 100

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters per second to centimeters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 100 = 1,250 cm/s. To go the other way, multiply centimeters per second by 0.01 (or divide by 100).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
