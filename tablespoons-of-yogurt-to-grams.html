Tablespoons of Yogurt to Grams

Convert tablespoons of yogurt to grams. 1 cup of yogurt weighs about 245 g.

How it works

Yogurt is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 245 g per US cup, convert tablespoons to grams by multiplying by 15.31.
