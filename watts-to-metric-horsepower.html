How it works

To convert watts to metric horsepower, multiply the number of watts by 0.0013596216. To convert metric horsepower to watts, multiply by 735.49875 (or divide by 0.0013596216). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Watts to Metric Horsepower formula

Metric horsepower = watts × 0.0013596216

Worked example: convert 12.5 watts to metric horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013596216 = 0.01699527 PS. To go the other way, multiply metric horsepower by 735.49875 (or divide by 0.0013596216).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
