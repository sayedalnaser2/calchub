How it works

To convert liters per minute to cubic meters per hour, multiply the number of liters per minute by 0.06. To convert cubic meters per hour to liters per minute, multiply by 16.666667 (or divide by 0.06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Liters per Minute to Cubic Meters per Hour formula

Cubic meters per hour = liters per minute × 0.06

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per minute to cubic meters per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 0.06 = 0.75 m³/h. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters per hour by 16.666667 (or divide by 0.06).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
