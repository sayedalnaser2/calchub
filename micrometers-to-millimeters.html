Micrometers to Millimeters Converter

Convert micrometers to millimeters instantly. 1 µm = 0.001 mm.

How it works

To convert micrometers to millimeters, multiply the number of micrometers by 0.001. One micrometer equals 0.001 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 1,000 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = micrometers × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
