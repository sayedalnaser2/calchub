How it works

To convert BTU to watt hours, multiply the number of BTU by 0.29307107. To convert watt hours to BTU, multiply by 3.4121416 (or divide by 0.29307107). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

BTU to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = BTU × 0.29307107

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.29307107 = 3.6633884 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 3.4121416 (or divide by 0.29307107).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
