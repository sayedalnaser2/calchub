How it works

To convert cups to imperial quarts, multiply the number of cups by 0.20816855. One cup equals 0.20816855 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 4.8037997 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = cups × 0.20816855

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.20816855 = 2.6021068 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 4.8037997 (or divide by 0.20816855).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
