How it works

To convert oil barrels to imperial pints, multiply the number of oil barrels by 279.77853. One oil barrel equals 279.77853 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.0035742557 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = oil barrels × 279.77853
