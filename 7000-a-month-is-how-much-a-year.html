$7,000 a Month Is How Much a Year?

$7,000 a month is $84,000 a year, or $40.38 an hour at 40 hours a week.

How it works

Annual pay is monthly pay times 12. $7,000 x 12 = $84,000. Dividing by 2,080 working hours gives the hourly equivalent.
