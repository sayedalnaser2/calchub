Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to cubic inches instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.061023744 in³.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to cubic inches, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.061023744. One cubic centimeter equals 0.061023744 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 16.387064 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = cubic centimeters × 0.061023744

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.061023744 = 0.7627968 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 16.387064 (or divide by 0.061023744).
