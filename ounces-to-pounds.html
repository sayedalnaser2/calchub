Ounces to Pounds Converter

Convert ounces to pounds instantly. 1 oz = 0.0625 lb.

How it works

To convert ounces to pounds, multiply the number of ounces by 0.0625. One ounce equals 0.0625 pounds, and one pound equals 16 ounces. The table shows common values.

Ounces to Pounds formula

Pounds = ounces × 0.0625

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0625 = 0.78125 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 16 (or divide by 0.0625).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
