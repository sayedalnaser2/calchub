How it works

To convert kilocalories to gigajoules, multiply the number of kilocalories by 4.184e-06. To convert gigajoules to kilocalories, multiply by 239,005.74 (or divide by 4.184e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilocalories to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = kilocalories × 4.184e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 4.184e-06 = 5.23e-05 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 239,005.74 (or divide by 4.184e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
