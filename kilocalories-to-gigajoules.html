Kilocalories to Gigajoules Converter

Convert kilocalories to gigajoules instantly. 1 kcal = 4.184e-06 GJ.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to gigajoules, multiply the number of kilocalories by 4.184e-06. One kilocalorie equals 4.184e-06 gigajoules, and one gigajoule equals 239,005.74 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = kilocalories × 4.184e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 4.184e-06 = 5.23e-05 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 239,005.74 (or divide by 4.184e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
