Milliliters to Imperial Pints Converter

Convert milliliters to imperial pints instantly. 1 ml = 0.001759754 imp pt.

How it works

To convert milliliters to imperial pints, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.001759754. One milliliter equals 0.001759754 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 568.26125 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = milliliters × 0.001759754

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001759754 = 0.021996925 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 568.26125 (or divide by 0.001759754).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
