Liters to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert liters to fluid ounces instantly. 1 L = 33.814023 fl oz.

How it works

To convert liters to fluid ounces, multiply the number of liters by 33.814023. One liter equals 33.814023 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.02957353 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = liters × 33.814023

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 33.814023 = 422.67528 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.02957353 (or divide by 33.814023).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
