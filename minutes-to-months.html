How it works

To convert minutes to months, multiply the number of minutes by 2.28159e-05. To convert months to minutes, multiply by 43,829.1 (or divide by 2.28159e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Minutes to Months formula

Months = minutes × 2.28159e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to months. Multiply 12.5 × 2.28159e-05 = 0.00028519865 months. To go the other way, multiply months by 43,829.1 (or divide by 2.28159e-05).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
