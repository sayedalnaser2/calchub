Square Feet to Acres Converter

Convert square feet to acres instantly. 1 ft² = 2.29568e-05 acres.

How it works

To convert square feet to acres, multiply the number of square feet by 2.29568e-05. One square foot equals 2.29568e-05 acres, and one acre equals 43,560 square feet. The table shows common values.

Square Feet to Acres formula

Acres = square feet × 2.29568e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 2.29568e-05 = 0.00028696051 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 43,560 (or divide by 2.29568e-05).
