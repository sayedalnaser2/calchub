How it works

To convert inches of mercury to pascals, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 3,386.389. To convert pascals to inches of mercury, multiply by 0.0002952998 (or divide by 3,386.389). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Inches of Mercury to Pascals formula

Pascals = inches of mercury × 3,386.389

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
