How it works

To convert inches of mercury to pascals, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 3,386.389. One inch of mercury equals 3,386.389 pascals, and one pascal equals 0.0002952998 inches of mercury. The table shows common values.

Inches Of Mercury to Pascals formula

Pascals = inches of mercury × 3,386.389

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 3,386.389 = 42,329.863 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 0.0002952998 (or divide by 3,386.389).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
