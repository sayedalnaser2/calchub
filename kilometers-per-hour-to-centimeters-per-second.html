Kilometers per Hour to Centimeters per Second Converter

Convert kilometers per hour to centimeters per second instantly. 1 km/h = 27.777778 cm/s.

How it works

To convert kilometers per hour to centimeters per second, multiply the number of kilometers per hour by 27.777778. One kilometer per hour equals 27.777778 centimeters per second, and one centimeter per second equals 0.036 kilometers per hour. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Hour to Centimeters per Second formula

Centimeters per second = kilometers per hour × 27.777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per hour to centimeters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 27.777778 = 347.22222 cm/s. To go the other way, multiply centimeters per second by 0.036 (or divide by 27.777778).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
