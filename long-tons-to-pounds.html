How it works

To convert long tons to pounds, multiply the number of long tons by 2,240. One long ton equals 2,240 pounds, and one pound equals 0.00044642857 long tons. The table shows common values.

Long Tons to Pounds formula

Pounds = long tons × 2,240

Worked example: convert 12.5 long tons to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 2,240 = 28,000 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 0.00044642857 (or divide by 2,240).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
