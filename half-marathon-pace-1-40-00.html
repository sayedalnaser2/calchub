Half Marathon Pace for a 1:40:00 Finish Time

To run a Half Marathon in 1:40:00 you need 7:38 per mile (4:44 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Half Marathon is 21.0975 km (13.11 miles), so 1:40:00 works out to 7:38 per mile or 4:44 per kilometer.
