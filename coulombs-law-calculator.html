Solve electric force, a charge, or distance from any three values.

How it works

F = k|q1 q2|/r² with k = 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C². Like charges repel and opposite charges attract.

Formula

Coulomb's law: F = k x q1 x q2 / r squared, with k = 8.9876 x 10^9 N·m²/C². Enter any three of the four values.

Frequently asked questions

What is a microcoulomb?
