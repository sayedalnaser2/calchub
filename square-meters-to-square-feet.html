Square Meters to Square Feet Converter

Convert square meters to square feet instantly. 1 m² = 10.76391 ft².

How it works

To convert square meters to square feet, multiply the number of square meters by 10.76391. One square meter equals 10.76391 square feet, and one square foot equals 0.09290304 square meters. The table shows common values.

Square Meters to Square Feet formula

Square feet = square meters × 10.76391

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to square feet. Multiply 12.5 × 10.76391 = 134.54888 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 0.09290304 (or divide by 10.76391).
