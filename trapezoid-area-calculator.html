Trapezoid Area Calculator

Calculate the area and median of a trapezoid from its parallel sides and height.

How it works

Area = (a + b) / 2 x h, the average of the two parallel sides times the perpendicular height.

Frequently asked questions

Which sides are a and b?

The two parallel sides (the bases).

What if I know the legs?

You need the perpendicular height, which can be found with the Pythagorean theorem.
