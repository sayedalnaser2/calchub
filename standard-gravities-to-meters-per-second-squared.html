Standard Gravities to Meters per Second Squared Converter

Convert standard gravities to meters per second squared instantly. 1 g = 9.80665 m/s².

How it works

To convert standard gravities to meters per second squared, multiply the number of standard gravities by 9.80665. One standard gravity equals 9.80665 meters per second squared, and one meter per second squared equals 0.10197162 standard gravities. The table shows common values.

Standard Gravities to Meters per Second Squared formula

Meters per second squared = standard gravities × 9.80665

Worked example: convert 12.5 standard gravities to meters per second squared. Multiply 12.5 × 9.80665 = 122.58312 m/s². To go the other way, multiply meters per second squared by 0.10197162 (or divide by 9.80665).
