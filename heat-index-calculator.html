Heat Index Calculator

Find how hot it feels when humidity is combined with temperature.

How it works

The heat index uses the Rothfusz regression from the US National Weather Service, combining air temperature and relative humidity. It is meant for 80 °F and above, in shade.

Frequently asked questions

Why does humidity matter?

High humidity slows sweat evaporation, so your body cools less effectively.

Is full sun hotter than the heat index?

Yes. Direct sunlight can add up to about 15 °F to the heat index.
