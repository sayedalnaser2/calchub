How it works

To convert tablespoons to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of tablespoons by 14.786765. To convert cubic centimeters to tablespoons, multiply by 0.067628045 (or divide by 14.786765). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Tablespoons to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = tablespoons × 14.786765

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 14.786765 = 184.83456 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.067628045 (or divide by 14.786765).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
