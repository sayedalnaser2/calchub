Convert hectares to square miles instantly. 1 ha = 0.0038610216 mi².

How it works

To convert hectares to square miles, multiply the number of hectares by 0.0038610216. One hectare equals 0.0038610216 square miles, and one square mile equals 258.99881 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Square Miles formula

Square miles = hectares × 0.0038610216

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0038610216 = 0.04826277 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 258.99881 (or divide by 0.0038610216).
