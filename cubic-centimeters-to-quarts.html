How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to quarts, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.0010566882. One cubic centimeter equals 0.0010566882 quarts, and one quart equals 946.35295 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Quarts formula

Quarts = cubic centimeters × 0.0010566882

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0010566882 = 0.013208603 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 946.35295 (or divide by 0.0010566882).
