Days to Months Converter

Convert days to months instantly. 1 days = 0.032854884 months.

How it works

To convert days to months, multiply the number of days by 0.032854884. One day equals 0.032854884 months, and one month equals 30.436875 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Months formula

Months = days × 0.032854884

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to months. Multiply 12.5 × 0.032854884 = 0.41068605 months. To go the other way, multiply months by 30.436875 (or divide by 0.032854884).
