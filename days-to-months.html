How it works

To convert days to months, multiply the number of days by 0.032854884. To convert months to days, multiply by 30.436875 (or divide by 0.032854884). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Days to Months formula

Months = days × 0.032854884

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to months. Multiply 12.5 × 0.032854884 = 0.41068605 months. To go the other way, multiply months by 30.436875 (or divide by 0.032854884).
