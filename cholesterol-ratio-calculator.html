Cholesterol Ratio and LDL Calculator

Calculate total/HDL ratio and estimate LDL from a lipid panel.

How it works

Friedewald: LDL = total - HDL - triglycerides/5, valid when triglycerides are below 400 mg/dL. This is an educational estimate, not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

What ratio is good?

Lower is better; many guidelines target under 5 and ideally under 3.5.
