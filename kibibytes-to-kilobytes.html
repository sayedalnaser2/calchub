Kibibytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert kibibytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 KiB = 1.024 KB.

How it works

To convert kibibytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of kibibytes by 1.024. One kibibyte equals 1.024 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 0.9765625 kibibytes. The table shows common values.

Kibibytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = kibibytes × 1.024

Worked example: convert 12.5 kibibytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.024 = 12.8 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 0.9765625 (or divide by 1.024).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
