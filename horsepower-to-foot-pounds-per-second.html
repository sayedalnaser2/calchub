How it works

To convert horsepower to foot-pounds per second, multiply the number of horsepower by 550. To convert foot-pounds per second to horsepower, multiply by 0.0018181818 (or divide by 550). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Horsepower to Foot-Pounds per Second formula

Foot-pounds per second = horsepower × 550

Worked example: convert 12.5 horsepower to foot-pounds per second. Multiply 12.5 × 550 = 6,875 ft-lb/s. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds per second by 0.0018181818 (or divide by 550).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
