Oil Barrels to Gallons Converter

Convert oil barrels to gallons instantly. 1 bbl = 42 gal.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to gallons, multiply the number of oil barrels by 42. One oil barrel equals 42 gallons, and one gallon equals 0.023809524 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Gallons formula

Gallons = oil barrels × 42

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 42 = 525 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 0.023809524 (or divide by 42).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
