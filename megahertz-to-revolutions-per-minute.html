Megahertz to Revolutions per Minute Converter

Convert megahertz to revolutions per minute instantly. 1 MHz = 60,000,000 rpm.

How it works

To convert megahertz to revolutions per minute, multiply the number of megahertz by 60,000,000. One megahertz equals 60,000,000 revolutions per minute, and one revolution per minute equals 1.66667e-08 megahertz. The table shows common values.

Megahertz to Revolutions per Minute formula

Revolutions per minute = megahertz × 60,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megahertz to revolutions per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 60,000,000 = 750,000,000 rpm. To go the other way, multiply revolutions per minute by 1.66667e-08 (or divide by 60,000,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
