How it works

To convert megahertz to revolutions per minute, multiply the number of megahertz by 60,000,000. To convert revolutions per minute to megahertz, multiply by 1.66667e-08 (or divide by 60,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Megahertz to Revolutions per Minute formula

Revolutions per minute = megahertz × 60,000,000

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
