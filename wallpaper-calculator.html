Wallpaper Calculator

Estimate the number of wallpaper rolls for a room.

How it works

Rolls = wall area divided by usable coverage per roll, plus waste for pattern matching. Single rolls often give 25-28 usable square feet.

Frequently asked questions

Does pattern repeat matter?

Yes. Large repeats waste more paper, so buy extra.
