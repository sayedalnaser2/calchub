How it works

To convert millimeters to kilometers, multiply the number of millimeters by 1e-06. To convert kilometers to millimeters, multiply by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Millimeters to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = millimeters × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
