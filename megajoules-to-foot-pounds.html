Megajoules to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert megajoules to foot-pounds instantly. 1 MJ = 737,562.15 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert megajoules to foot-pounds, multiply the number of megajoules by 737,562.15. One megajoule equals 737,562.15 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 1.35582e-06 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = megajoules × 737,562.15

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 737,562.15 = 9,219,526.9 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 1.35582e-06 (or divide by 737,562.15).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
