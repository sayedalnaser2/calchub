Short Tons to Ounces Converter

Convert short tons to ounces instantly. 1 short tons = 32,000 oz.

How it works

To convert short tons to ounces, multiply the number of short tons by 32,000. One short ton equals 32,000 ounces, and one ounce equals 3.125e-05 short tons. The table shows common values.

Short Tons to Ounces formula

Ounces = short tons × 32,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 short tons to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 32,000 = 400,000 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 3.125e-05 (or divide by 32,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
