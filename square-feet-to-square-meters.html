How it works

To convert square feet to square meters, multiply the number of square feet by 0.09290304. To convert square meters to square feet, multiply by 10.76391 (or divide by 0.09290304). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Feet to Square Meters formula

Square meters = square feet × 0.09290304

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.09290304 = 1.161288 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 10.76391 (or divide by 0.09290304).
