Square Feet to Square Meters Converter

Convert square feet to square meters instantly. 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m².

How it works

To convert square feet to square meters, multiply the number of square feet by 0.09290304. One square foot equals 0.09290304 square meters, and one square meter equals 10.76391 square feet. The table shows common values.

Square Feet to Square Meters formula

Square meters = square feet × 0.09290304

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.09290304 = 1.161288 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 10.76391 (or divide by 0.09290304).
