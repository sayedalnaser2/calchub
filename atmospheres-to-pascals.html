Atmospheres to Pascals Converter

Convert atmospheres to pascals instantly. 1 atm = 101,325 Pa.

How it works

To convert atmospheres to pascals, multiply the number of atmospheres by 101,325. One atmosphere equals 101,325 pascals, and one pascal equals 9.86923e-06 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Atmospheres to Pascals formula

Pascals = atmospheres × 101,325

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 101,325 = 1,266,562.5 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 9.86923e-06 (or divide by 101,325).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
