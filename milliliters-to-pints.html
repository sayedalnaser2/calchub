Milliliters to Pints Converter

Convert milliliters to pints instantly. 1 ml = 0.0021133764 pt.

How it works

To convert milliliters to pints, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.0021133764. One milliliter equals 0.0021133764 pints, and one pint equals 473.17647 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Pints formula

Pints = milliliters × 0.0021133764

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0021133764 = 0.026417205 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 473.17647 (or divide by 0.0021133764).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
