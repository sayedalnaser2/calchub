How it works

To convert kilowatts to megawatts, multiply the number of kilowatts by 0.001. To convert megawatts to kilowatts, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilowatts to Megawatts formula

Megawatts = kilowatts × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatts to megawatts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 MW. To go the other way, multiply megawatts by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
