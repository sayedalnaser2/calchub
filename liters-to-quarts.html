Liters to Quarts Converter

Convert liters to quarts instantly. 1 L = 1.0566882 qt.

How it works

To convert liters to quarts, multiply the number of liters by 1.0566882. One liter equals 1.0566882 quarts, and one quart equals 0.94635295 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Quarts formula

Quarts = liters × 1.0566882

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 1.0566882 = 13.208603 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 0.94635295 (or divide by 1.0566882).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
