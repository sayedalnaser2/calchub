Radians to Degrees Converter

Convert radians to degrees instantly. 1 rad = 57.29578 °.

How it works

To convert radians to degrees, multiply the number of radians by 57.29578. One radian equals 57.29578 degrees, and one degree equals 0.017453293 radians. The table shows common values.

Radians to Degrees formula

Degrees = radians × 57.29578

Worked example: convert 12.5 radians to degrees. Multiply 12.5 × 57.29578 = 716.19724 °. To go the other way, multiply degrees by 0.017453293 (or divide by 57.29578).
