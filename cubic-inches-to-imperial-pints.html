How it works

To convert cubic inches to imperial pints, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.028837201. To convert imperial pints to cubic inches, multiply by 34.677429 (or divide by 0.028837201). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Inches to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = cubic inches × 0.028837201

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
