Cubic Inches to Imperial Pints Converter

Convert cubic inches to imperial pints instantly. 1 in³ = 0.028837201 imp pt.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to imperial pints, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.028837201. One cubic inch equals 0.028837201 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 34.677429 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = cubic inches × 0.028837201

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.028837201 = 0.36046501 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 34.677429 (or divide by 0.028837201).
