Mean, Median and Mode Calculator

Enter a list of numbers to get the mean, median, mode and range.

How it works

The mean is the sum divided by the count. The median is the middle value of the sorted list (or the average of the two middle values). The mode is the most frequent value, and the range is the largest value minus the smallest.

Frequently asked questions

Can there be more than one mode?

Yes. If several values tie for most frequent, all of them are modes.

When is the median better than the mean?

When outliers skew the data, such as incomes or house prices.
