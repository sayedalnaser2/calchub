Gigajoules to Kilojoules Converter

Convert gigajoules to kilojoules instantly. 1 GJ = 1,000,000 kJ.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to kilojoules, multiply the number of gigajoules by 1,000,000. One gigajoule equals 1,000,000 kilojoules, and one kilojoule equals 1e-06 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = gigajoules × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
