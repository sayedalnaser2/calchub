Foot-Pounds per Second to Kilowatts Converter

Convert foot-pounds per second to kilowatts instantly. 1 ft-lb/s = 0.0013558179 kW.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds per second to kilowatts, multiply the number of foot-pounds per second by 0.0013558179. One foot-pound per second equals 0.0013558179 kilowatts, and one kilowatt equals 737.56215 foot-pounds per second. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds per Second to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = foot-pounds per second × 0.0013558179

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds per second to kilowatts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013558179 = 0.016947724 kW. To go the other way, multiply kilowatts by 737.56215 (or divide by 0.0013558179).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
