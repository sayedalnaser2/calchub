BTU to Joules Converter

Convert BTU to joules instantly. 1 BTU = 1,055.0559 J.

How it works

To convert BTU to joules, multiply the number of BTU by 1,055.0559. One BTU equals 1,055.0559 joules, and one joule equals 0.00094781712 BTU. The table shows common values.

BTU to Joules formula

Joules = BTU × 1,055.0559

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 1,055.0559 = 13,188.198 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 0.00094781712 (or divide by 1,055.0559).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
