Newtons to Kilogram-Force Converter

Convert newtons to kilogram-force instantly. 1 N = 0.10197162 kgf.

How it works

To convert newtons to kilogram-force, multiply the number of newtons by 0.10197162. One newton equals 0.10197162 kilogram-force, and one kilogram-force equals 9.80665 newtons. The table shows common values.

Newtons to Kilogram-Force formula

Kilogram-force = newtons × 0.10197162

Worked example: convert 12.5 newtons to kilogram-force. Multiply 12.5 × 0.10197162 = 1.2746453 kgf. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force by 9.80665 (or divide by 0.10197162).
