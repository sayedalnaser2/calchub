How it works

To convert newtons to kilogram-force, multiply the number of newtons by 0.10197162. To convert kilogram-force to newtons, multiply by 9.80665 (or divide by 0.10197162). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Newtons to Kilogram-Force formula

Kilogram-force = newtons × 0.10197162

Worked example: convert 12.5 newtons to kilogram-force. Multiply 12.5 × 0.10197162 = 1.2746453 kgf. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force by 9.80665 (or divide by 0.10197162).
