How it works

To convert BTU per hour to kilowatts, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 0.00029307107. To convert kilowatts to BTU per hour, multiply by 3,412.1416 (or divide by 0.00029307107). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

BTU per Hour to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = BTU per hour × 0.00029307107

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
