BTU per Hour to Kilowatts Converter

Convert BTU per hour to kilowatts instantly. 1 BTU/h = 0.00029307107 kW.

How it works

To convert BTU per hour to kilowatts, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 0.00029307107. One BTU per hour equals 0.00029307107 kilowatts, and one kilowatt equals 3412.1416 BTU per hour. The table shows common values.

BTU per Hour to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = BTU per hour × 0.00029307107

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU per hour to kilowatts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00029307107 = 0.0036633884 kW. To go the other way, multiply kilowatts by 3412.1416 (or divide by 0.00029307107).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
