How it works

To convert quarts to imperial pints, multiply the number of quarts by 1.6653484. One quart equals 1.6653484 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.60047496 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = quarts × 1.6653484

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 1.6653484 = 20.816855 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.60047496 (or divide by 1.6653484).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
