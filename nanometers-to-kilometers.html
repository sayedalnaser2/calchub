Nanometers to Kilometers Converter

Convert nanometers to kilometers instantly. 1 nm = 1e-12 km.

How it works

To convert nanometers to kilometers, multiply the number of nanometers by 1e-12. One nanometer equals 1e-12 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 1,000,000,000,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = nanometers × 1e-12

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-12 = 1.25e-11 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 1,000,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-12).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
