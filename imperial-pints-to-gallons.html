Convert imperial pints to gallons instantly. 1 imp pt = 0.15011874 gal.

How it works

To convert imperial pints to gallons, multiply the number of imperial pints by 0.15011874. One imperial pint equals 0.15011874 gallons, and one gallon equals 6.6613935 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Gallons formula

Gallons = imperial pints × 0.15011874

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.15011874 = 1.8764843 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 6.6613935 (or divide by 0.15011874).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
