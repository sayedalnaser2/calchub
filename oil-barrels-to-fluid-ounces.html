How it works

To convert oil barrels to fluid ounces, multiply the number of oil barrels by 5,376. One oil barrel equals 5,376 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.0001860119 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = oil barrels × 5,376

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 5,376 = 67,200 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.0001860119 (or divide by 5,376).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
