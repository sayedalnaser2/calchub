How it works

To convert horsepower to BTU per hour, multiply the number of horsepower by 2,544.4336. To convert BTU per hour to horsepower, multiply by 0.00039301478 (or divide by 2,544.4336). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
