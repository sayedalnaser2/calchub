How it works

To convert horsepower to BTU per hour, multiply the number of horsepower by 2,544.4336. One horsepower equals 2,544.4336 BTU per hour, and one BTU per hour equals 0.00039301478 horsepower. The table shows common values.

Horsepower to BTU per Hour formula

Btu per hour = horsepower × 2,544.4336

Worked example: convert 12.5 horsepower to BTU per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 2,544.4336 = 31,805.42 BTU/h. To go the other way, multiply BTU per hour by 0.00039301478 (or divide by 2,544.4336).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
