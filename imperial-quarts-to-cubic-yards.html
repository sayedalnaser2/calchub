Imperial Quarts to Cubic Yards Converter

Convert imperial quarts to cubic yards instantly. 1 imp qt = 0.0014865153 yd³.

How it works

To convert imperial quarts to cubic yards, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 0.0014865153. One imperial quart equals 0.0014865153 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 672.71423 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = imperial quarts × 0.0014865153

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0014865153 = 0.018581441 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 672.71423 (or divide by 0.0014865153).
