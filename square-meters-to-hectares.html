Square Meters to Hectares Converter

Convert square meters to hectares instantly. 1 m² = 0.0001 ha.

How it works

To convert square meters to hectares, multiply the number of square meters by 0.0001. One square meter equals 0.0001 hectares, and one hectare equals 10,000 square meters. The table shows common values.

Square Meters to Hectares formula

Hectares = square meters × 0.0001

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0001 = 0.00125 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 10,000 (or divide by 0.0001).
