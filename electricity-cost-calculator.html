Electricity Cost Calculator

Solve cost, power, hours or electricity price from any three values.

Type in any 3 fields and the rest are calculated.

How it works

Energy in kilowatt-hours equals watts / 1000 x hours. Multiply by your electricity rate for the cost.

Formula

Cost = kW x hours x price per kWh. Enter any three of the four values.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find an appliance's wattage?

It is on the label or in the manual. You can also measure it with a plug-in power meter.
