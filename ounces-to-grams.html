Convert ounces to grams instantly. 1 oz = 28.349523 g.

How it works

To convert ounces to grams, multiply the number of ounces by 28.349523. One ounce equals 28.349523 grams, and one gram equals 0.035273962 ounces. The table shows common values.

Ounces to Grams formula

Grams = ounces × 28.349523

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to grams. Multiply 12.5 × 28.349523 = 354.36904 g. To go the other way, multiply grams by 0.035273962 (or divide by 28.349523).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
