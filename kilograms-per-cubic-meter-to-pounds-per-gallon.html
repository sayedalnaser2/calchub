Kilograms per Cubic Meter to Pounds per Gallon Converter

Convert kilograms per cubic meter to pounds per gallon instantly. 1 kg/m³ = 0.0083454045 lb/gal.

How it works

To convert kilograms per cubic meter to pounds per gallon, multiply the number of kilograms per cubic meter by 0.0083454045. One kilogram per cubic meter equals 0.0083454045 pounds per gallon, and one pound per gallon equals 119.82643 kilograms per cubic meter. The table shows common values.

Kilograms per Cubic Meter to Pounds per Gallon formula

Pounds per gallon = kilograms per cubic meter × 0.0083454045
