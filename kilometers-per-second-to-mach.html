Kilometers per Second to Mach Converter

Convert kilometers per second to mach instantly. 1 km/s = 2.93867 Mach.

How it works

To convert kilometers per second to mach, multiply the number of kilometers per second by 2.93867. One kilometer per second equals 2.93867 mach, and one Mach equals 0.34029 kilometers per second. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Second to Mach formula

Mach = kilometers per second × 2.93867

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per second to mach. Multiply 12.5 × 2.93867 = 36.733374 Mach. To go the other way, multiply mach by 0.34029 (or divide by 2.93867).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
