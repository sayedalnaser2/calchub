Foot-Pounds to Kilojoules Converter

Convert foot-pounds to kilojoules instantly. 1 ft-lb = 0.0013558179 kJ.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to kilojoules, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 0.0013558179. One foot-pound equals 0.0013558179 kilojoules, and one kilojoule equals 737.56215 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = foot-pounds × 0.0013558179

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013558179 = 0.016947724 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 737.56215 (or divide by 0.0013558179).
