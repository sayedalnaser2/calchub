Gallons to Tablespoons Converter

Convert gallons to tablespoons instantly. 1 gal = 256 tbsp.

How it works

To convert gallons to tablespoons, multiply the number of gallons by 256. One gallon equals 256 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.00390625 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = gallons × 256

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 256 = 3,200 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.00390625 (or divide by 256).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
