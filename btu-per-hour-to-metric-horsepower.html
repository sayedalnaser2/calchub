How it works

To convert BTU per hour to metric horsepower, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 0.00039846576. One BTU per hour equals 0.00039846576 metric horsepower, and one metric horsepower equals 2,509.6259 BTU per hour. The table shows common values.

BTU per Hour to Metric Horsepower formula

Metric horsepower = BTU per hour × 0.00039846576

About power units
