Calculate your swimming pace per 100 meters and projected race times.

How it works

Pace per 100 m = total time / distance x 100. Swim pace is quoted per 100 m (pools in meters) or 100 yards.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good 100 m pace?

Beginners often swim 2:30-3:00 per 100 m; competitive swimmers go under 1:30.

Does turning count?

Yes, pool swims include turns and push-offs, making them faster than open water.
