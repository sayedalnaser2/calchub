How it works

To convert milligrams to grams, multiply the number of milligrams by 0.001. To convert grams to milligrams, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Milligrams to Grams formula

Grams = milligrams × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to grams. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 g. To go the other way, multiply grams by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
