Days to Nanoseconds Converter

Convert days to nanoseconds instantly. 1 days = 86,400,000,000,000 ns.

How it works

To convert days to nanoseconds, multiply the number of days by 86,400,000,000,000. One day equals 86,400,000,000,000 nanoseconds, and one nanosecond equals 1.15741e-14 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = days × 86,400,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 86,400,000,000,000 = 1.08e+15 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 1.15741e-14 (or divide by 86,400,000,000,000).
