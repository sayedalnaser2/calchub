Gigajoules to Kilocalories Converter

Convert gigajoules to kilocalories instantly. 1 GJ = 239,005.74 kcal.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to kilocalories, multiply the number of gigajoules by 239,005.74. One gigajoule equals 239,005.74 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 4.184e-06 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = gigajoules × 239,005.74

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 239,005.74 = 2,987,571.7 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 4.184e-06 (or divide by 239,005.74).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
