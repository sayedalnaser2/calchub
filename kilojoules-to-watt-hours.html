Kilojoules to Watt Hours Converter

Convert kilojoules to watt hours instantly. 1 kJ = 0.27777778 Wh.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to watt hours, multiply the number of kilojoules by 0.27777778. One kilojoule equals 0.27777778 watt hours, and one watt hour equals 3.6 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = kilojoules × 0.27777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.27777778 = 3.4722222 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 3.6 (or divide by 0.27777778).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
