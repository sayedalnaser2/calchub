Imperial Gallons to Cubic Yards Converter

Convert imperial gallons to cubic yards instantly. 1 imp gal = 0.0059460612 yd³.

How it works

To convert imperial gallons to cubic yards, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 0.0059460612. One imperial gallon equals 0.0059460612 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 168.17856 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = imperial gallons × 0.0059460612

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0059460612 = 0.074325765 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 168.17856 (or divide by 0.0059460612).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
