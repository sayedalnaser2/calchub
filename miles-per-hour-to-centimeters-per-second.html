How it works

To convert miles per hour to centimeters per second, multiply the number of miles per hour by 44.704. To convert centimeters per second to miles per hour, multiply by 0.022369363 (or divide by 44.704). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles per Hour to Centimeters per Second formula

Centimeters per second = miles per hour × 44.704

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles per hour to centimeters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 44.704 = 558.8 cm/s. To go the other way, multiply centimeters per second by 0.022369363 (or divide by 44.704).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
