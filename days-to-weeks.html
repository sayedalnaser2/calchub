Days to Weeks Converter

Convert days to weeks instantly. 1 day = 0.14285714 weeks.

How it works

To convert days to weeks, multiply the number of days by 0.14285714. One day equals 0.14285714 weeks, and one week equals 7 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Weeks formula

Weeks = days × 0.14285714

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to weeks. Multiply 12.5 × 0.14285714 = 1.7857143 weeks. To go the other way, multiply weeks by 7 (or divide by 0.14285714).
