How it works

To convert days to weeks, multiply the number of days by 0.14285714. To convert weeks to days, multiply by 7 (or divide by 0.14285714). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Days to Weeks formula

Weeks = days × 0.14285714

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to weeks. Multiply 12.5 × 0.14285714 = 1.7857143 weeks. To go the other way, multiply weeks by 7 (or divide by 0.14285714).
