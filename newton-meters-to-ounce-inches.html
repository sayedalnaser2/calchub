Newton Meters to Ounce-Inches Converter

Convert newton meters to ounce-inches instantly. 1 N·m = 141.61193 oz-in.

How it works

To convert newton meters to ounce-inches, multiply the number of newton meters by 141.61193. One newton meter equals 141.61193 ounce-inches, and one ounce-inch equals 0.0070615518 newton meters. The table shows common values.

Newton Meters to Ounce-Inches formula

Ounce-inches = newton meters × 141.61193

Worked example: convert 12.5 newton meters to ounce-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 141.61193 = 1,770.1492 oz-in. To go the other way, multiply ounce-inches by 0.0070615518 (or divide by 141.61193).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
