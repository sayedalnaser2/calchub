How it works

To convert inches to light years, multiply the number of inches by 2.68478e-18. One inch equals 2.68478e-18 light years, and one light year equals 3.7247e+17 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Light Years formula

Light years = inches × 2.68478e-18

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
