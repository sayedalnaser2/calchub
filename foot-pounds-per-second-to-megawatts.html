How it works

To convert foot-pounds per second to megawatts, multiply the number of foot-pounds per second by 1.35582e-06. To convert megawatts to foot-pounds per second, multiply by 737,562.15 (or divide by 1.35582e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
