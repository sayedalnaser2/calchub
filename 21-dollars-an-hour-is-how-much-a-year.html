$21 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

$21 an hour is $43,680 a year at 40 hours a week. Adjust hours and weeks for weekly, monthly and biweekly pay.

How it works

Annual pay is the hourly rate times hours per week times weeks per year. At $21 an hour and 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, that is $43,680. Monthly pay divides the annual figure by 12.

Take-home pay illustration

Gross pay of $43,680 a year is before taxes and deductions. If roughly 20% were withheld, take-home pay would be about $34,944 a year or $2,912 a month. Your real withholding depends on filing status, state and benefits.
