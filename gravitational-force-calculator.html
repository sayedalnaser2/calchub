How it works

Newton's law of gravitation: F = G m1 m2 / r², with G = 6.674×10⁻¹¹. Defaults are the Earth and Moon.

Formula

Newton's law of gravitation: F = G x m1 x m2 / r squared, with G = 6.6743 x 10^-11. Enter any three values.

Frequently asked questions

Why is gravity so weak between small objects?

G is tiny, so masses must be planet-sized for a noticeable force.
