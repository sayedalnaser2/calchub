Years to Weeks Converter

Convert years to weeks instantly. 1 year = 52.1775 weeks.

How it works

To convert years to weeks, multiply the number of years by 52.1775. One year equals 52.1775 weeks, and one week equals 0.019165349 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Weeks formula

Weeks = years × 52.1775

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to weeks. Multiply 12.5 × 52.1775 = 652.21875 weeks. To go the other way, multiply weeks by 0.019165349 (or divide by 52.1775).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
