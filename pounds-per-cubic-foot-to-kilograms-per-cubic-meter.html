Pounds per Cubic Foot to Kilograms per Cubic Meter Converter

Convert pounds per cubic foot to kilograms per cubic meter instantly. 1 lb/ft³ = 16.018463 kg/m³.

How it works

To convert pounds per cubic foot to kilograms per cubic meter, multiply the number of pounds per cubic foot by 16.018463. One pound per cubic foot equals 16.018463 kilograms per cubic meter, and one kilogram per cubic meter equals 0.062427961 pounds per cubic foot. The table shows common values.

Pounds per Cubic Foot to Kilograms per Cubic Meter formula

Kilograms per cubic meter = pounds per cubic foot × 16.018463

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds per cubic foot to kilograms per cubic meter. Multiply 12.5 × 16.018463 = 200.23079 kg/m³. To go the other way, multiply kilograms per cubic meter by 0.062427961 (or divide by 16.018463).
