Boyle's Law Calculator

Calculate the new volume or pressure of a gas at constant temperature.

How it works

Boyle's law: P1 x V1 = P2 x V2 for a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature. Pressure and volume are inversely proportional.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need absolute pressure?

Yes. Use absolute pressure, not gauge pressure.

Which units work?

Any, as long as pressure units match and volume units match.
