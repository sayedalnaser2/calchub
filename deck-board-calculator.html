Deck Board Calculator

Estimate deck boards for a given deck size.

How it works

Rows across the deck width are board width plus gap. Multiply by boards per row and add 10% for waste.

Frequently asked questions

What gap should I leave?

About 1/8-1/4 inch for wood, depending on moisture and manufacturer.
