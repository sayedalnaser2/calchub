How it works

To convert newtons to pound-force, multiply the number of newtons by 0.22480894. One newton equals 0.22480894 pound-force, and one pound-force equals 4.4482216 newtons. The table shows common values.

Newtons to Pound-Force formula

Pound-force = newtons × 0.22480894

Worked example: convert 12.5 newtons to pound-force. Multiply 12.5 × 0.22480894 = 2.8101118 lbf. To go the other way, multiply pound-force by 4.4482216 (or divide by 0.22480894).
