How it works

To convert grains to milligrams, multiply the number of grains by 64.79891. One grain equals 64.79891 milligrams, and one milligram equals 0.015432358 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = grains × 64.79891

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 64.79891 = 809.98637 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 0.015432358 (or divide by 64.79891).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
