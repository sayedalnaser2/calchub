Convert mils to nautical miles instantly. 1 mil = 1.37149e-08 nmi.

How it works

To convert mils to nautical miles, multiply the number of mils by 1.37149e-08. One mil equals 1.37149e-08 nautical miles, and one nautical mile equals 72,913,386 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Nautical Miles formula

Nautical miles = mils × 1.37149e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to nautical miles. Multiply 12.5 × 1.37149e-08 = 1.71436e-07 nmi. To go the other way, multiply nautical miles by 72,913,386 (or divide by 1.37149e-08).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
