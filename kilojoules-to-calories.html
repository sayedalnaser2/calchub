Kilojoules to Calories Converter

Convert kilojoules to calories instantly. 1 kJ = 239.00574 cal.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to calories, multiply the number of kilojoules by 239.00574. One kilojoule equals 239.00574 calories, and one calorie equals 0.004184 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Calories formula

Calories = kilojoules × 239.00574

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 239.00574 = 2,987.5717 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 0.004184 (or divide by 239.00574).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
