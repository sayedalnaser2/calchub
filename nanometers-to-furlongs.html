Nanometers to Furlongs Converter

Convert nanometers to furlongs instantly. 1 nm = 4.97097e-12 furlongs.

How it works

To convert nanometers to furlongs, multiply the number of nanometers by 4.97097e-12. One nanometer equals 4.97097e-12 furlongs, and one furlong equals 201,168,000,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = nanometers × 4.97097e-12

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 4.97097e-12 = 6.21371e-11 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 201,168,000,000 (or divide by 4.97097e-12).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
