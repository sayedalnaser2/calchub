Hertz to Megahertz Converter

Convert hertz to megahertz instantly. 1 Hz = 1e-06 MHz.

How it works

To convert hertz to megahertz, multiply the number of hertz by 1e-06. One hertz equals 1e-06 megahertz, and one megahertz equals 1,000,000 hertz. The table shows common values.

Hertz to Megahertz formula

Megahertz = hertz × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 hertz to megahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 MHz. To go the other way, multiply megahertz by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
