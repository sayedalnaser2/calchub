Minutes to Weeks Converter

Convert minutes to weeks instantly. 1 min = 9.92063e-05 weeks.

How it works

To convert minutes to weeks, multiply the number of minutes by 9.92063e-05. One minute equals 9.92063e-05 weeks, and one week equals 10,080 minutes. The table shows common values.

Minutes to Weeks formula

Weeks = minutes × 9.92063e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to weeks. Multiply 12.5 × 9.92063e-05 = 0.0012400794 weeks. To go the other way, multiply weeks by 10,080 (or divide by 9.92063e-05).
