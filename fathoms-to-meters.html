Fathoms to Meters Converter

Convert fathoms to meters instantly. 1 fathom = 1.8288 m.

How it works

To convert fathoms to meters, multiply the number of fathoms by 1.8288. One fathom equals 1.8288 meters, and one meter equals 0.54680665 fathoms. The table shows common values.

Fathoms to Meters formula

Meters = fathoms × 1.8288

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1.8288 = 22.86 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 0.54680665 (or divide by 1.8288).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
