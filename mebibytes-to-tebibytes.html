Mebibytes to Tebibytes Converter

Convert mebibytes to tebibytes instantly. 1 MiB = 9.53674e-07 TiB.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to tebibytes, multiply the number of mebibytes by 9.53674e-07. One mebibyte equals 9.53674e-07 tebibytes, and one tebibyte equals 1,048,576 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Tebibytes formula

Tebibytes = mebibytes × 9.53674e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to tebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 9.53674e-07 = 1.19209e-05 TiB. To go the other way, multiply tebibytes by 1,048,576 (or divide by 9.53674e-07).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
