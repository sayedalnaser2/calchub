Convert cups to milliliters instantly. 1 cup = 236.58824 ml.

How it works

To convert cups to milliliters, multiply the number of cups by 236.58824. One cup equals 236.58824 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.0042267528 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = cups × 236.58824

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 236.58824 = 2957.353 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.0042267528 (or divide by 236.58824).

About volume units
