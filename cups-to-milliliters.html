How it works

To convert cups to milliliters, multiply the number of cups by 236.58824. To convert milliliters to cups, multiply by 0.0042267528 (or divide by 236.58824). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cups to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = cups × 236.58824

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 236.58824 = 2,957.353 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.0042267528 (or divide by 236.58824).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
