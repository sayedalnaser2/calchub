mmHg to Kilopascals Converter

Convert mmHg to kilopascals instantly. 1 mmHg = 0.13332239 kPa.

How it works

To convert mmHg to kilopascals, multiply the number of mmHg by 0.13332239. One mmHg equals 0.13332239 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 7.5006158 mmHg. The table shows common values.

mmHg to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = mmHg × 0.13332239

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.13332239 = 1.6665298 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 7.5006158 (or divide by 0.13332239).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
