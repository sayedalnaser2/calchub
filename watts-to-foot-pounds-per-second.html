Watts to Foot-Pounds per Second Converter

Convert watts to foot-pounds per second instantly. 1 W = 0.73756215 ft-lb/s.

How it works

To convert watts to foot-pounds per second, multiply the number of watts by 0.73756215. One watt equals 0.73756215 foot-pounds per second, and one foot-pound per second equals 1.3558179 watts. The table shows common values.

Watts to Foot-Pounds per Second formula

Foot-pounds per second = watts × 0.73756215

Worked example: convert 12.5 watts to foot-pounds per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.73756215 = 9.2195269 ft-lb/s. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds per second by 1.3558179 (or divide by 0.73756215).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
