Cubic Feet to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert cubic feet to cubic inches instantly. 1 ft³ = 1,728 in³.

How it works

To convert cubic feet to cubic inches, multiply the number of cubic feet by 1,728. One cubic foot equals 1,728 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.0005787037 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = cubic feet × 1,728

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 1,728 = 21,600 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.0005787037 (or divide by 1,728).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
