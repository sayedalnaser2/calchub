Convert teaspoons to cubic meters instantly. 1 tsp = 4.92892e-06 m³.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to cubic meters, multiply the number of teaspoons by 4.92892e-06. One teaspoon equals 4.92892e-06 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 202,884.14 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = teaspoons × 4.92892e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 4.92892e-06 = 6.16115e-05 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 202,884.14 (or divide by 4.92892e-06).
