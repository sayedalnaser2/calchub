How it works

To convert hertz to gigahertz, multiply the number of hertz by 1e-09. To convert gigahertz to hertz, multiply by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Hertz to Gigahertz formula

Gigahertz = hertz × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 hertz to gigahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 GHz. To go the other way, multiply gigahertz by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
