Fluid Ounces to Teaspoons Converter

Convert fluid ounces to teaspoons instantly. 1 fl oz = 6 tsp.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to teaspoons, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 6. One fluid ounce equals 6 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.16666667 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = fluid ounces × 6

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 6 = 75 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.16666667 (or divide by 6).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
