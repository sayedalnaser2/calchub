How it works

To convert pints to imperial gallons, multiply the number of pints by 0.10408427. One pint equals 0.10408427 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 9.6075994 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = pints × 0.10408427

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.10408427 = 1.3010534 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 9.6075994 (or divide by 0.10408427).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
