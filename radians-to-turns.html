Radians to Turns Converter

Convert radians to turns instantly. 1 rad = 0.15915494 turns.

How it works

To convert radians to turns, multiply the number of radians by 0.15915494. One radian equals 0.15915494 turns, and one turn equals 6.2831853 radians. The table shows common values.

Radians to Turns formula

Turns = radians × 0.15915494

Worked example: convert 12.5 radians to turns. Multiply 12.5 × 0.15915494 = 1.9894368 turns. To go the other way, multiply turns by 6.2831853 (or divide by 0.15915494).
