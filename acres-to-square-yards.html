How it works

To convert acres to square yards, multiply the number of acres by 4840. One acre equals 4840 square yards, and one square yard equals 0.00020661157 acres. The table shows common values.

Acres to Square Yards formula

Square yards = acres × 4840

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square yards. Multiply 12.5 × 4840 = 60500 yd². To go the other way, multiply square yards by 0.00020661157 (or divide by 4840).
