How it works

To convert oil barrels to imperial gallons, multiply the number of oil barrels by 34.972316. One oil barrel equals 34.972316 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 0.028594046 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = oil barrels × 34.972316

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
