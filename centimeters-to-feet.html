How it works

To convert centimeters to feet, multiply the number of centimeters by 0.032808399. One centimeter equals 0.032808399 feet, and one foot equals 30.48 centimeters. The table shows common values.

Centimeters to Feet formula

Feet = centimeters × 0.032808399

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.032808399 = 0.41010499 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 30.48 (or divide by 0.032808399).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
