Megabytes to Kilobits Converter

Convert megabytes to kilobits instantly. 1 MB = 8,000 Kb.

How it works

To convert megabytes to kilobits, multiply the number of megabytes by 8,000. One megabyte equals 8,000 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 0.000125 megabytes. The table shows common values.

Megabytes to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = megabytes × 8,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabytes to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,000 = 100,000 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 0.000125 (or divide by 8,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
