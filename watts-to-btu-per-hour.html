Watts to BTU per Hour Converter

Convert watts to BTU per hour instantly. 1 W = 3.4121416 BTU/h.

How it works

To convert watts to BTU per hour, multiply the number of watts by 3.4121416. One watt equals 3.4121416 BTU per hour, and one BTU per hour equals 0.29307107 watts. The table shows common values.

Watts to BTU per Hour formula

Btu per hour = watts × 3.4121416

Worked example: convert 12.5 watts to BTU per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 3.4121416 = 42.65177 BTU/h. To go the other way, multiply BTU per hour by 0.29307107 (or divide by 3.4121416).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
