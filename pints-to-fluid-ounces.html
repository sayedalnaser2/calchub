Pints to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert pints to fluid ounces instantly. 1 pt = 16 fl oz.

How it works

To convert pints to fluid ounces, multiply the number of pints by 16. One pint equals 16 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.0625 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = pints × 16

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 16 = 200 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.0625 (or divide by 16).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
