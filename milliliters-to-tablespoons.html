Milliliters to Tablespoons Converter

Convert milliliters to tablespoons instantly. 1 ml = 0.067628045 tbsp.

How it works

To convert milliliters to tablespoons, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.067628045. One milliliter equals 0.067628045 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 14.786765 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = milliliters × 0.067628045

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.067628045 = 0.84535057 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 14.786765 (or divide by 0.067628045).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
