Rhombus Area Calculator

Calculate area, side length and perimeter of a rhombus from its diagonals.

How it works

Area = d1 x d2 / 2. The diagonals bisect each other at right angles, so each side is sqrt((d1/2)^2 + (d2/2)^2).

Frequently asked questions

Is a square a rhombus?

Yes, with equal diagonals.

How else can I find area?

Base x height or side^2 x sin(angle).
