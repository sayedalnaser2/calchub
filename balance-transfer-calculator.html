Balance Transfer Calculator

Compare paying a card at its current APR versus a transfer offer.

How it works

With a transfer you pay a fee up front but interest is charged at the promo rate for the promo months, then reverts to your normal APR on any remaining balance.

Frequently asked questions

Are transfers worth it?

Usually if you can pay off most of the balance during the promotion and the fee is lower than the interest saved.
