Tebibytes to Gibibytes Converter

Convert tebibytes to gibibytes instantly. 1 TiB = 1,024 GiB.

How it works

To convert tebibytes to gibibytes, multiply the number of tebibytes by 1,024. One tebibyte equals 1,024 gibibytes, and one gibibyte equals 0.0009765625 tebibytes. The table shows common values.

Tebibytes to Gibibytes formula

Gibibytes = tebibytes × 1,024

Worked example: convert 12.5 tebibytes to gibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,024 = 12,800 GiB. To go the other way, multiply gibibytes by 0.0009765625 (or divide by 1,024).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
