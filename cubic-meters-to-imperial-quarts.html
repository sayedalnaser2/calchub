Convert cubic meters to imperial quarts instantly. 1 m³ = 879.87699 imp qt.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to imperial quarts, multiply the number of cubic meters by 879.87699. One cubic meter equals 879.87699 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 0.0011365225 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = cubic meters × 879.87699

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 879.87699 = 10,998.462 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 0.0011365225 (or divide by 879.87699).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
