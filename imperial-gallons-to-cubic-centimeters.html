How it works

To convert imperial gallons to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 4,546.09. To convert cubic centimeters to imperial gallons, multiply by 0.00021996925 (or divide by 4,546.09). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Imperial Gallons to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = imperial gallons × 4,546.09

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 4,546.09 = 56,826.125 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.00021996925 (or divide by 4,546.09).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
