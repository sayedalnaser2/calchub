Kilopascals to PSI Converter

Convert kilopascals to psi instantly. 1 kPa = 0.14503774 psi.

How it works

To convert kilopascals to psi, multiply the number of kilopascals by 0.14503774. One kilopascal equals 0.14503774 psi, and one psi equals 6.8947573 kilopascals. The table shows common values.

Kilopascals to PSI formula

Psi = kilopascals × 0.14503774

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to psi. Multiply 12.5 × 0.14503774 = 1.8129717 psi. To go the other way, multiply psi by 6.8947573 (or divide by 0.14503774).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
