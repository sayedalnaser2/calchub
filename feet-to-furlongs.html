How it works

To convert feet to furlongs, multiply the number of feet by 0.0015151515. One foot equals 0.0015151515 furlongs, and one furlong equals 660 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = feet × 0.0015151515

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0015151515 = 0.018939394 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 660 (or divide by 0.0015151515).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
