Convert kilometers to inches instantly. 1 km = 39370.079 in.

How it works

To convert kilometers to inches, multiply the number of kilometers by 39370.079. One kilometer equals 39370.079 inches, and one inch equals 2.54e-05 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Inches formula

Inches = kilometers × 39370.079

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 39370.079 = 492125.98 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 2.54e-05 (or divide by 39370.079).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
