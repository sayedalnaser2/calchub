Miles per Hour to Feet per Second Converter

Convert miles per hour to feet per second instantly. 1 mph = 1.4666667 ft/s.

How it works

To convert miles per hour to feet per second, multiply the number of miles per hour by 1.4666667. One mile per hour equals 1.4666667 feet per second, and one foot per second equals 0.68181818 miles per hour. The table shows common values.

Miles per Hour to Feet per Second formula

Feet per second = miles per hour × 1.4666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles per hour to feet per second. Multiply 12.5 × 1.4666667 = 18.333333 ft/s. To go the other way, multiply feet per second by 0.68181818 (or divide by 1.4666667).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
