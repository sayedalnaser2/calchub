Feet per Second Squared to Meters per Second Squared Converter

Convert feet per second squared to meters per second squared instantly. 1 ft/s² = 0.3048 m/s².

How it works

To convert feet per second squared to meters per second squared, multiply the number of feet per second squared by 0.3048. One foot per second squared equals 0.3048 meters per second squared, and one meter per second squared equals 3.2808399 feet per second squared. The table shows common values.

Feet per Second Squared to Meters per Second Squared formula

Meters per second squared = feet per second squared × 0.3048

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet per second squared to meters per second squared. Multiply 12.5 × 0.3048 = 3.81 m/s². To go the other way, multiply meters per second squared by 3.2808399 (or divide by 0.3048).
