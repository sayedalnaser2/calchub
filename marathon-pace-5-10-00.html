Marathon Pace for a 5:10:00 Finish Time

To run a Marathon in 5:10:00 you need 11:49 per mile (7:21 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Marathon is 42.195 km (26.22 miles), so 5:10:00 works out to 11:49 per mile or 7:21 per kilometer.
