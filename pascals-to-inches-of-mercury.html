Pascals to Inches of Mercury Converter

Convert pascals to inches of mercury instantly. 1 Pa = 0.0002952998 inHg.

How it works

To convert pascals to inches of mercury, multiply the number of pascals by 0.0002952998. One pascal equals 0.0002952998 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 3,386.389 pascals. The table shows common values.

Pascals to Inches of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = pascals × 0.0002952998

Worked example: convert 12.5 pascals to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0002952998 = 0.0036912475 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 3,386.389 (or divide by 0.0002952998).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
