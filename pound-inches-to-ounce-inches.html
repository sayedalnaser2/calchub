How it works

To convert pound-inches to ounce-inches, multiply the number of pound-inches by 16. To convert ounce-inches to pound-inches, multiply by 0.0625 (or divide by 16). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pound-Inches to Ounce-Inches formula

Ounce-inches = pound-inches × 16

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-inches to ounce-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 16 = 200 oz-in. To go the other way, multiply ounce-inches by 0.0625 (or divide by 16).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
