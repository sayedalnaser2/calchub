Kilobits to Kibibytes Converter

Convert kilobits to kibibytes instantly. 1 Kb = 0.12207031 KiB.

How it works

To convert kilobits to kibibytes, multiply the number of kilobits by 0.12207031. One kilobit equals 0.12207031 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 8.192 kilobits. The table shows common values.

Kilobits to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = kilobits × 0.12207031

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobits to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.12207031 = 1.5258789 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 8.192 (or divide by 0.12207031).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
