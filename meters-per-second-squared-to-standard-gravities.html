Meters per Second Squared to Standard Gravities Converter

Convert meters per second squared to standard gravities instantly. 1 m/s² = 0.10197162 g.

How it works

To convert meters per second squared to standard gravities, multiply the number of meters per second squared by 0.10197162. One meter per second squared equals 0.10197162 standard gravities, and one standard gravity equals 9.80665 meters per second squared. The table shows common values.

Meters per Second Squared to Standard Gravities formula

Standard gravities = meters per second squared × 0.10197162

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters per second squared to standard gravities. Multiply 12.5 × 0.10197162 = 1.2746453 g. To go the other way, multiply standard gravities by 9.80665 (or divide by 0.10197162).
