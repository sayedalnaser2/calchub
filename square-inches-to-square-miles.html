Square Inches to Square Miles Converter

Convert square inches to square miles instantly. 1 in² = 2.49098e-10 mi².

How it works

To convert square inches to square miles, multiply the number of square inches by 2.49098e-10. One square inch equals 2.49098e-10 square miles, and one square mile equals 4,014,489,600 square inches. The table shows common values.

Square Inches to Square Miles formula

Square miles = square inches × 2.49098e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 2.49098e-10 = 3.11372e-09 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 4,014,489,600 (or divide by 2.49098e-10).
