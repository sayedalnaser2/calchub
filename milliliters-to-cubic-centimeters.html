How it works

To convert milliliters to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of milliliters by 1. To convert cubic centimeters to milliliters, multiply by 1 (or divide by 1). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Milliliters to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = milliliters × 1

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1 = 12.5 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 1 (or divide by 1).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
