Factorial Calculator

Calculate n! for any whole number up to 170.

How it works

n! is the product of all positive integers up to n, with 0! = 1. It counts the ways to arrange n distinct items.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 0! equal to 1?

There is exactly one way to arrange zero items: do nothing.
