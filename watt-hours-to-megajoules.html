Watt Hours to Megajoules Converter

Convert watt hours to megajoules instantly. 1 Wh = 0.0036 MJ.

How it works

To convert watt hours to megajoules, multiply the number of watt hours by 0.0036. One watt hour equals 0.0036 megajoules, and one megajoule equals 277.77778 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = watt hours × 0.0036

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0036 = 0.045 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 277.77778 (or divide by 0.0036).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
