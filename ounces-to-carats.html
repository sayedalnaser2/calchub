How it works

To convert ounces to carats, multiply the number of ounces by 141.74762. To convert carats to ounces, multiply by 0.0070547924 (or divide by 141.74762). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Ounces to Carats formula

Carats = ounces × 141.74762

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 141.74762 = 1,771.8452 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 0.0070547924 (or divide by 141.74762).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
