Gradians to Arcminutes Converter

Convert gradians to arcminutes instantly. 1 grad = 54 arcmin.

How it works

To convert gradians to arcminutes, multiply the number of gradians by 54. One gradian equals 54 arcminutes, and one arcminute equals 0.018518519 gradians. The table shows common values.

Gradians to Arcminutes formula

Arcminutes = gradians × 54

Worked example: convert 12.5 gradians to arcminutes. Multiply 12.5 × 54 = 675 arcmin. To go the other way, multiply arcminutes by 0.018518519 (or divide by 54).
