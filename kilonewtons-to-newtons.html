Kilonewtons to Newtons Converter

Convert kilonewtons to newtons instantly. 1 kN = 1000 N.

How it works

To convert kilonewtons to newtons, multiply the number of kilonewtons by 1000. One kilonewton equals 1000 newtons, and one newton equals 0.001 kilonewtons. The table shows common values.

Kilonewtons to Newtons formula

Newtons = kilonewtons × 1000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilonewtons to newtons. Multiply 12.5 × 1000 = 12500 N. To go the other way, multiply newtons by 0.001 (or divide by 1000).
