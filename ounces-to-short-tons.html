How it works

To convert ounces to short tons, multiply the number of ounces by 3.125e-05. To convert short tons to ounces, multiply by 32,000 (or divide by 3.125e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Ounces to Short Tons formula

Short tons = ounces × 3.125e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to short tons. Multiply 12.5 × 3.125e-05 = 0.000390625 short tons. To go the other way, multiply short tons by 32,000 (or divide by 3.125e-05).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
