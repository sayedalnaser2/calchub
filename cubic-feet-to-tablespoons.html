How it works

To convert cubic feet to tablespoons, multiply the number of cubic feet by 1,915.013. One cubic foot equals 1,915.013 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.00052218967 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = cubic feet × 1,915.013

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 1,915.013 = 23,937.662 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.00052218967 (or divide by 1,915.013).
