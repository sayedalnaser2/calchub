Kilometers to Mils Converter

Convert kilometers to mils instantly. 1 km = 39,370,079 mil.

How it works

To convert kilometers to mils, multiply the number of kilometers by 39,370,079. One kilometer equals 39,370,079 mils, and one mil equals 2.54e-08 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Mils formula

Mils = kilometers × 39,370,079

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 39,370,079 = 492,125,980 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 2.54e-08 (or divide by 39,370,079).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
