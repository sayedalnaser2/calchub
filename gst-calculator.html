GST Calculator

Add or remove GST, or find the rate, from any two values.

Type in any 2 fields and the rest are calculated.

How it works

GST is a percentage of the pre-tax price. For intra-state supplies in India the tax splits equally into CGST and SGST.

Formula

Gross = net x (1 + GST rate). Enter any two of net, rate and gross.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove GST from a total?

Divide the GST-inclusive price by 1 + rate (for example 1.18 for 18%).
