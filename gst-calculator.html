GST Calculator

Calculate GST inclusive and exclusive prices with CGST and SGST split.

How it works

GST is a percentage of the pre-tax price. For intra-state supplies in India the tax splits equally into CGST and SGST.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove GST from a total?

Divide the GST-inclusive price by 1 + rate (for example 1.18 for 18%).
