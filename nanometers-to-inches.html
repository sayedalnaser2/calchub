Nanometers to Inches Converter

Convert nanometers to inches instantly. 1 nm = 3.93701e-08 in.

How it works

To convert nanometers to inches, multiply the number of nanometers by 3.93701e-08. One nanometer equals 3.93701e-08 inches, and one inch equals 25,400,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Inches formula

Inches = nanometers × 3.93701e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 3.93701e-08 = 4.92126e-07 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 25,400,000 (or divide by 3.93701e-08).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
