Cubic Yards to Quarts Converter

Convert cubic yards to quarts instantly. 1 yd³ = 807.8961 qt.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to quarts, multiply the number of cubic yards by 807.8961. One cubic yard equals 807.8961 quarts, and one quart equals 0.0012377829 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Quarts formula

Quarts = cubic yards × 807.8961

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 807.8961 = 10,098.701 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 0.0012377829 (or divide by 807.8961).

About volume units
