Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Yards Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to cubic yards instantly. 1 cm³ = 1.30795e-06 yd³.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to cubic yards, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 1.30795e-06. One cubic centimeter equals 1.30795e-06 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 764,554.86 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = cubic centimeters × 1.30795e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1.30795e-06 = 1.63494e-05 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 764,554.86 (or divide by 1.30795e-06).
