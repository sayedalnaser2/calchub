Nanometers to Miles Converter

Convert nanometers to miles instantly. 1 nm = 6.21371e-13 mi.

How it works

To convert nanometers to miles, multiply the number of nanometers by 6.21371e-13. One nanometer equals 6.21371e-13 miles, and one mile equals 1,609,344,000,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Miles formula

Miles = nanometers × 6.21371e-13

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 6.21371e-13 = 7.76714e-12 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1,609,344,000,000 (or divide by 6.21371e-13).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
