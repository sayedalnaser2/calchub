Millimeters to Yards Converter

Convert millimeters to yards instantly. 1 mm = 0.0010936133 yd.

How it works

To convert millimeters to yards, multiply the number of millimeters by 0.0010936133. One millimeter equals 0.0010936133 yards, and one yard equals 914.4 millimeters. The table shows common values.

Millimeters to Yards formula

Yards = millimeters × 0.0010936133

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0010936133 = 0.013670166 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 914.4 (or divide by 0.0010936133).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
