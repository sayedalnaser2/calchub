Long Tons to Stone Converter

Convert long tons to stone instantly. 1 long ton = 160 st.

How it works

To convert long tons to stone, multiply the number of long tons by 160. One long ton equals 160 stone, and one stone equals 0.00625 long tons. The table shows common values.

Long Tons to Stone formula

Stone = long tons × 160

Worked example: convert 12.5 long tons to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 160 = 2,000 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 0.00625 (or divide by 160).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
