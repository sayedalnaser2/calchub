Tablespoons of Mayonnaise to Grams

Convert tablespoons of mayonnaise to grams. 1 cup of mayonnaise weighs about 220 g.

How it works

Mayonnaise is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 220 g per US cup, convert tablespoons to grams by multiplying by 13.75.
