Centimeters to Miles Converter

Convert centimeters to miles instantly. 1 cm = 6.21371e-06 mi.

How it works

To convert centimeters to miles, multiply the number of centimeters by 6.21371e-06. One centimeter equals 6.21371e-06 miles, and one mile equals 160,934.4 centimeters. The table shows common values.

Centimeters to Miles formula

Miles = centimeters × 6.21371e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 6.21371e-06 = 7.76714e-05 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 160,934.4 (or divide by 6.21371e-06).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
