Ounce-Force to Pound-Force Converter

Convert ounce-force to pound-force instantly. 1 ozf = 0.0625 lbf.

How it works

To convert ounce-force to pound-force, multiply the number of ounce-force by 0.0625. One ounce-force equals 0.0625 pound-force, and one pound-force equals 16 ounce-force. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Force to Pound-Force formula

Pound-force = ounce-force × 0.0625

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounce-force to pound-force. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0625 = 0.78125 lbf. To go the other way, multiply pound-force by 16 (or divide by 0.0625).
