How it works

To convert calories to gigajoules, multiply the number of calories by 4.184e-09. To convert gigajoules to calories, multiply by 239,005,740 (or divide by 4.184e-09). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Calories to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = calories × 4.184e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 4.184e-09 = 5.23e-08 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 239,005,740 (or divide by 4.184e-09).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
