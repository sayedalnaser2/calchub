How it works

To convert stone to carats, multiply the number of stone by 31,751.466. One stone equals 31,751.466 carats, and one carat equals 3.14946e-05 stone. The table shows common values.

Stone to Carats formula

Carats = stone × 31,751.466

Worked example: convert 12.5 stone to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 31,751.466 = 396,893.32 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 3.14946e-05 (or divide by 31,751.466).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
