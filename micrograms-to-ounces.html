Micrograms to Ounces Converter

Convert micrograms to ounces instantly. 1 µg = 3.5274e-08 oz.

How it works

To convert micrograms to ounces, multiply the number of micrograms by 3.5274e-08. One microgram equals 3.5274e-08 ounces, and one ounce equals 28,349,523 micrograms. The table shows common values.

Micrograms to Ounces formula

Ounces = micrograms × 3.5274e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrograms to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 3.5274e-08 = 4.40925e-07 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 28,349,523 (or divide by 3.5274e-08).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
