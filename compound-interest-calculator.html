How it works

Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. Each month the balance grows by the effective monthly rate and your deposit is added. The longer the money stays invested, the faster it grows.

Frequently asked questions

What return rate should I use?

Savings accounts have recently paid roughly 3-5%. Long-run stock market averages are often quoted near 7% after inflation, but returns are never guaranteed.

Does compounding frequency matter?

Slightly. Daily compounding earns a little more than yearly, but rate and time matter far more.
