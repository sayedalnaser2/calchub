What is compound interest?

Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. The longer your money stays invested and the more often it compounds, the faster it grows. Starting early matters more than the size of the deposits.

Frequently asked questions

What return rate should I use?

High-yield savings accounts have paid roughly 3–5% recently; the long-run stock market average is often quoted near 7% after inflation adjustments vary. Returns are never guaranteed.

Does compounding frequency matter?

Slightly. Daily compounding earns a little more than yearly, but the rate and time invested matter far more.
