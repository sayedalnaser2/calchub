Turns to Arcseconds Converter

Convert turns to arcseconds instantly. 1 turn = 1,296,000 arcsec.

How it works

To convert turns to arcseconds, multiply the number of turns by 1,296,000. One turn equals 1,296,000 arcseconds, and one arcsecond equals 7.71605e-07 turns. The table shows common values.

Turns to Arcseconds formula

Arcseconds = turns × 1,296,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 turns to arcseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,296,000 = 16,200,000 arcsec. To go the other way, multiply arcseconds by 7.71605e-07 (or divide by 1,296,000).
