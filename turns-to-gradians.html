Turns to Gradians Converter

Convert turns to gradians instantly. 1 turn = 400 grad.

How it works

To convert turns to gradians, multiply the number of turns by 400. One turn equals 400 gradians, and one gradian equals 0.0025 turns. The table shows common values.

Turns to Gradians formula

Gradians = turns × 400

Worked example: convert 12.5 turns to gradians. Multiply 12.5 × 400 = 5,000 grad. To go the other way, multiply gradians by 0.0025 (or divide by 400).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
