How it works

To convert BTU to megajoules, multiply the number of BTU by 0.0010550559. To convert megajoules to BTU, multiply by 947.81712 (or divide by 0.0010550559). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

BTU to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = BTU × 0.0010550559

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0010550559 = 0.013188198 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 947.81712 (or divide by 0.0010550559).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
