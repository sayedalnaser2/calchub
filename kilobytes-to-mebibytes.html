Convert kilobytes to mebibytes instantly. 1 KB = 0.00095367432 MiB.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to mebibytes, multiply the number of kilobytes by 0.00095367432. One kilobyte equals 0.00095367432 mebibytes, and one mebibyte equals 1,048.576 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Mebibytes formula

Mebibytes = kilobytes × 0.00095367432

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to mebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00095367432 = 0.011920929 MiB. To go the other way, multiply mebibytes by 1,048.576 (or divide by 0.00095367432).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
