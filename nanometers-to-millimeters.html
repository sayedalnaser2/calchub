Nanometers to Millimeters Converter

Convert nanometers to millimeters instantly. 1 nm = 1e-06 mm.

How it works

To convert nanometers to millimeters, multiply the number of nanometers by 1e-06. One nanometer equals 1e-06 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 1,000,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = nanometers × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
