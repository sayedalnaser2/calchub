Cubic Yards to Imperial Gallons Converter

Convert cubic yards to imperial gallons instantly. 1 yd³ = 168.17856 imp gal.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to imperial gallons, multiply the number of cubic yards by 168.17856. One cubic yard equals 168.17856 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 0.0059460612 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = cubic yards × 168.17856

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 168.17856 = 2,102.232 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 0.0059460612 (or divide by 168.17856).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
