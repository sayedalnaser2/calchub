Pound-Inches to Pound-Feet Converter

Convert pound-inches to pound-feet instantly. 1 lb-in = 0.083333333 lb-ft.

How it works

To convert pound-inches to pound-feet, multiply the number of pound-inches by 0.083333333. One pound-inch equals 0.083333333 pound-feet, and one pound-foot equals 12 pound-inches. The table shows common values.

Pound-Inches to Pound-Feet formula

Pound-feet = pound-inches × 0.083333333

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-inches to pound-feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.083333333 = 1.0416667 lb-ft. To go the other way, multiply pound-feet by 12 (or divide by 0.083333333).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
