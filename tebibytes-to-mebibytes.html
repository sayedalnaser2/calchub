Tebibytes to Mebibytes Converter

Convert tebibytes to mebibytes instantly. 1 TiB = 1,048,576 MiB.

How it works

To convert tebibytes to mebibytes, multiply the number of tebibytes by 1,048,576. One tebibyte equals 1,048,576 mebibytes, and one mebibyte equals 9.53674e-07 tebibytes. The table shows common values.

Tebibytes to Mebibytes formula

Mebibytes = tebibytes × 1,048,576

Worked example: convert 12.5 tebibytes to mebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,048,576 = 13,107,200 MiB. To go the other way, multiply mebibytes by 9.53674e-07 (or divide by 1,048,576).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
