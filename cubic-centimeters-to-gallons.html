Cubic Centimeters to Gallons Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to gallons instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.00026417205 gal.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to gallons, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.00026417205. One cubic centimeter equals 0.00026417205 gallons, and one gallon equals 3,785.4118 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Gallons formula

Gallons = cubic centimeters × 0.00026417205

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00026417205 = 0.0033021507 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 3,785.4118 (or divide by 0.00026417205).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
