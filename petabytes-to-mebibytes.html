Petabytes to Mebibytes Converter

Convert petabytes to mebibytes instantly. 1 PB = 953,674,320 MiB.

How it works

To convert petabytes to mebibytes, multiply the number of petabytes by 953,674,320. One petabyte equals 953,674,320 mebibytes, and one mebibyte equals 1.04858e-09 petabytes. The table shows common values.

Petabytes to Mebibytes formula

Mebibytes = petabytes × 953,674,320

Worked example: convert 12.5 petabytes to mebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 953,674,320 = 11,920,929,000 MiB. To go the other way, multiply mebibytes by 1.04858e-09 (or divide by 953,674,320).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
