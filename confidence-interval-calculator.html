Confidence Interval Calculator

Calculate a confidence interval for a mean.

How it works

The interval is the mean ± z x σ/√n, using the normal approximation (suitable for larger samples).

Frequently asked questions

What does 95% confidence mean?

If you repeated the study many times, about 95% of such intervals would contain the true mean.
