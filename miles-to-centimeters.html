How it works

To convert miles to centimeters, multiply the number of miles by 160,934.4. To convert centimeters to miles, multiply by 6.21371e-06 (or divide by 160,934.4). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = miles × 160,934.4

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 160,934.4 = 2,011,680 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 6.21371e-06 (or divide by 160,934.4).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
