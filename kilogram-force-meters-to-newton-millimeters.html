Kilogram-Force Meters to Newton Millimeters Converter

Convert kilogram-force meters to newton millimeters instantly. 1 kgf·m = 9,806.65 N·mm.

How it works

To convert kilogram-force meters to newton millimeters, multiply the number of kilogram-force meters by 9,806.65. One kilogram-force meter equals 9,806.65 newton millimeters, and one newton millimeter equals 0.00010197162 kilogram-force meters. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force Meters to Newton Millimeters formula

Newton millimeters = kilogram-force meters × 9,806.65

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force meters to newton millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 9,806.65 = 122,583.12 N·mm. To go the other way, multiply newton millimeters by 0.00010197162 (or divide by 9,806.65).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
