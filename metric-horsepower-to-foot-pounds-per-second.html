Metric Horsepower to Foot-Pounds per Second Converter

Convert metric horsepower to foot-pounds per second instantly. 1 PS = 542.47604 ft-lb/s.

How it works

To convert metric horsepower to foot-pounds per second, multiply the number of metric horsepower by 542.47604. One metric horsepower equals 542.47604 foot-pounds per second, and one foot-pound per second equals 0.0018433994 metric horsepower. The table shows common values.

Metric Horsepower to Foot-Pounds per Second formula

Foot-pounds per second = metric horsepower × 542.47604

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric horsepower to foot-pounds per second. Multiply 12.5 × 542.47604 = 6,780.9505 ft-lb/s. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds per second by 0.0018433994 (or divide by 542.47604).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
