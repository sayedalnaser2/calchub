Kilometers to Meters Converter

Convert kilometers to meters instantly. 1 km = 1,000 m.

How it works

To convert kilometers to meters, multiply the number of kilometers by 1,000. One kilometer equals 1,000 meters, and one meter equals 0.001 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Meters formula

Meters = kilometers × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
