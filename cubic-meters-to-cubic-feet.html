Convert cubic meters to cubic feet instantly. 1 m³ = 35.314667 ft³.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to cubic feet, multiply the number of cubic meters by 35.314667. One cubic meter equals 35.314667 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 0.028316847 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = cubic meters × 35.314667

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 35.314667 = 441.43333 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 0.028316847 (or divide by 35.314667).
