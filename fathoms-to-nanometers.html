Fathoms to Nanometers Converter

Convert fathoms to nanometers instantly. 1 fathoms = 1,828,800,000 nm.

How it works

To convert fathoms to nanometers, multiply the number of fathoms by 1,828,800,000. One fathom equals 1,828,800,000 nanometers, and one nanometer equals 5.46807e-10 fathoms. The table shows common values.

Fathoms to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = fathoms × 1,828,800,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1,828,800,000 = 22,860,000,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 5.46807e-10 (or divide by 1,828,800,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
