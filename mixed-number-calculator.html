Mixed Number to Improper Fraction Calculator

Convert a mixed number to an improper fraction and a decimal.

How it works

Improper numerator = whole x denominator + numerator, kept over the same denominator. 3 2/5 = (3x5 + 2)/5 = 17/5.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert back to a mixed number?

Divide the numerator by the denominator; the quotient is the whole part and the remainder is the new numerator.

What is an improper fraction?

One whose numerator is at least its denominator.
