How it works

To convert cubic feet to oil barrels, multiply the number of cubic feet by 0.17810761. One cubic foot equals 0.17810761 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 5.6145833 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = cubic feet × 0.17810761

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to oil barrels. Multiply 12.5 × 0.17810761 = 2.2263451 bbl. To go the other way, multiply oil barrels by 5.6145833 (or divide by 0.17810761).
