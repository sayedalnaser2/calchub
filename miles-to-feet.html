Miles to Feet Converter

Convert miles to feet instantly. 1 mi = 5,280 ft.

How it works

To convert miles to feet, multiply the number of miles by 5,280. One mile equals 5,280 feet, and one foot equals 0.00018939394 miles. The table shows common values.

Miles to Feet formula

Feet = miles × 5,280

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 5,280 = 66,000 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 0.00018939394 (or divide by 5,280).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
