Gallons to Cubic Centimeters Converter

Convert gallons to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 gal = 3,785.4118 cm³.

How it works

To convert gallons to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of gallons by 3,785.4118. One gallon equals 3,785.4118 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.00026417205 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = gallons × 3,785.4118

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 3,785.4118 = 47,317.647 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.00026417205 (or divide by 3,785.4118).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
