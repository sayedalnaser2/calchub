Discount Calculator

Enter any two of original price, discount % and sale price and get the third.

Type in any 2 fields and the rest are calculated.

How it works

Each discount is applied one after another to the remaining price. Two discounts of 25% and 10% equal 32.5% off, not 35%.

Formula

Sale price = original price x (1 - discount%). Enter any two of original price, discount and sale price.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't a 25% plus 10% discount equal to 35%?

The second discount applies to the already reduced price, so the total saving is slightly less than the sum.
