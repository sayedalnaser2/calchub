Hectares to Square Yards Converter

Convert hectares to square yards instantly. 1 ha = 11959.9 yd².

How it works

To convert hectares to square yards, multiply the number of hectares by 11959.9. One hectare equals 11959.9 square yards, and one square yard equals 8.36127e-05 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Square Yards formula

Square yards = hectares × 11959.9

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square yards. Multiply 12.5 × 11959.9 = 149498.76 yd². To go the other way, multiply square yards by 8.36127e-05 (or divide by 11959.9).
