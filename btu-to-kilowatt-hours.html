BTU to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert BTU to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 BTU = 0.00029307107 kWh.

How it works

To convert BTU to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of BTU by 0.00029307107. One BTU equals 0.00029307107 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 3,412.1416 BTU. The table shows common values.

BTU to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = BTU × 0.00029307107

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00029307107 = 0.0036633884 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 3,412.1416 (or divide by 0.00029307107).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
