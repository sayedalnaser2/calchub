How it works

To convert kilometers to feet, multiply the number of kilometers by 3280.8399. One kilometer equals 3280.8399 feet, and one foot equals 0.0003048 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Feet formula

Feet = kilometers × 3280.8399

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3280.8399 = 41010.499 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 0.0003048 (or divide by 3280.8399).

About length units
