Joules to Kilojoules Converter

Convert joules to kilojoules instantly. 1 J = 0.001 kJ.

How it works

To convert joules to kilojoules, multiply the number of joules by 0.001. One joule equals 0.001 kilojoules, and one kilojoule equals 1,000 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = joules × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
