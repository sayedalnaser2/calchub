Convert imperial gallons to fluid ounces instantly. 1 imp gal = 153.72159 fl oz.

How it works

To convert imperial gallons to fluid ounces, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 153.72159. One imperial gallon equals 153.72159 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.0065052671 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = imperial gallons × 153.72159

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 153.72159 = 1,921.5199 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.0065052671 (or divide by 153.72159).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
