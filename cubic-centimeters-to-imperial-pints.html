Cubic Centimeters to Imperial Pints Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to imperial pints instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.001759754 imp pt.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to imperial pints, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.001759754. One cubic centimeter equals 0.001759754 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 568.26125 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = cubic centimeters × 0.001759754

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001759754 = 0.021996925 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 568.26125 (or divide by 0.001759754).

About volume units
