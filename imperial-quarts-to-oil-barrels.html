How it works

To convert imperial quarts to oil barrels, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 0.0071485115. One imperial quart equals 0.0071485115 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 139.88926 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = imperial quarts × 0.0071485115
