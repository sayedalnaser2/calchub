Kilometers to Yards Converter

Convert kilometers to yards instantly. 1 km = 1,093.6133 yd.

How it works

To convert kilometers to yards, multiply the number of kilometers by 1,093.6133. One kilometer equals 1,093.6133 yards, and one yard equals 0.0009144 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Yards formula

Yards = kilometers × 1,093.6133

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1,093.6133 = 13,670.166 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 0.0009144 (or divide by 1,093.6133).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
