Gallons to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert gallons to fluid ounces instantly. 1 gal = 128 fl oz.

How it works

To convert gallons to fluid ounces, multiply the number of gallons by 128. One gallon equals 128 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.0078125 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = gallons × 128

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 128 = 1600 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.0078125 (or divide by 128).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
