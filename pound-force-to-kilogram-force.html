Pound-Force to Kilogram-Force Converter

Convert pound-force to kilogram-force instantly. 1 lbf = 0.45359237 kgf.

How it works

To convert pound-force to kilogram-force, multiply the number of pound-force by 0.45359237. One pound-force equals 0.45359237 kilogram-force, and one kilogram-force equals 2.2046226 pound-force. The table shows common values.

Pound-Force to Kilogram-Force formula

Kilogram-force = pound-force × 0.45359237

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-force to kilogram-force. Multiply 12.5 × 0.45359237 = 5.6699046 kgf. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force by 2.2046226 (or divide by 0.45359237).
