How it works

To convert kilograms to carats, multiply the number of kilograms by 5,000. To convert carats to kilograms, multiply by 0.0002 (or divide by 5,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilograms to Carats formula

Carats = kilograms × 5,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 5,000 = 62,500 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 0.0002 (or divide by 5,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
