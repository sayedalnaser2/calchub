How it works

To convert megahertz to gigahertz, multiply the number of megahertz by 0.001. One megahertz equals 0.001 gigahertz, and one gigahertz equals 1,000 megahertz. The table shows common values.

Megahertz to Gigahertz formula

Gigahertz = megahertz × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 megahertz to gigahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 GHz. To go the other way, multiply gigahertz by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
