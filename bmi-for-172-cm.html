BMI for 172 cm (5 ft 7.7 in)

Check your BMI at 172 cm and see the healthy weight range for this height.

How it works

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) squared. At 172 cm, a healthy BMI of 18.5-24.9 corresponds to 55-74 kg.

BMI is a screening tool. It does not measure body fat directly and can misclassify very muscular people, older adults and some ethnic groups. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy weight at 172 cm?

Roughly 55 to 74 kg.

Which BMI is considered overweight?

25 to 29.9 is classed as overweight; 30 and above as obese.
