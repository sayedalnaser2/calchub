Centimeters to Kilometers Converter

Convert centimeters to kilometers instantly. 1 cm = 1e-05 km.

How it works

To convert centimeters to kilometers, multiply the number of centimeters by 1e-05. One centimeter equals 1e-05 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 100000 centimeters. The table shows common values.

Centimeters to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = centimeters × 1e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-05 = 0.000125 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 100000 (or divide by 1e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
