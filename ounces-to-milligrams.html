Ounces to Milligrams Converter

Convert ounces to milligrams instantly. 1 oz = 28,349.523 mg.

How it works

To convert ounces to milligrams, multiply the number of ounces by 28,349.523. One ounce equals 28,349.523 milligrams, and one milligram equals 3.5274e-05 ounces. The table shows common values.

Ounces to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = ounces × 28,349.523

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 28,349.523 = 354,369.04 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 3.5274e-05 (or divide by 28,349.523).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
