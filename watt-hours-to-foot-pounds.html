Watt Hours to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert watt hours to foot-pounds instantly. 1 Wh = 2,655.2237 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert watt hours to foot-pounds, multiply the number of watt hours by 2,655.2237. One watt hour equals 2,655.2237 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 0.0003766161 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = watt hours × 2,655.2237

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 2,655.2237 = 33,190.297 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 0.0003766161 (or divide by 2,655.2237).

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
