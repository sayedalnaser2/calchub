How it works

To convert gigahertz to megahertz, multiply the number of gigahertz by 1,000. One gigahertz equals 1,000 megahertz, and one megahertz equals 0.001 gigahertz. The table shows common values.

Gigahertz to Megahertz formula

Megahertz = gigahertz × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigahertz to megahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 MHz. To go the other way, multiply megahertz by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
