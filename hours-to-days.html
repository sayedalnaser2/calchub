Hours to Days Converter

Convert hours to days instantly. 1 h = 0.041666667 days.

How it works

To convert hours to days, multiply the number of hours by 0.041666667. One hour equals 0.041666667 days, and one day equals 24 hours. The table shows common values.

Hours to Days formula

Days = hours × 0.041666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to days. Multiply 12.5 × 0.041666667 = 0.52083333 days. To go the other way, multiply days by 24 (or divide by 0.041666667).
