Megajoules to Kilojoules Converter

Convert megajoules to kilojoules instantly. 1 MJ = 1,000 kJ.

How it works

To convert megajoules to kilojoules, multiply the number of megajoules by 1,000. One megajoule equals 1,000 kilojoules, and one kilojoule equals 0.001 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = megajoules × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
