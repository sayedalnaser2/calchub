How Many Days Until Cinco de Mayo?

Live countdown showing the days, weeks and hours left until Cinco de Mayo.

How it works

The countdown compares today's date with the next occurrence of Cinco de Mayo. If the holiday has passed this year it counts to next year's date. Moving holidays such as Easter or Thanksgiving are calculated from their calendar rules.
