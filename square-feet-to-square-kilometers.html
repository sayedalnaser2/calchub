How it works

To convert square feet to square kilometers, multiply the number of square feet by 9.2903e-08. To convert square kilometers to square feet, multiply by 10,763,910 (or divide by 9.2903e-08). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Feet to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = square feet × 9.2903e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 9.2903e-08 = 1.16129e-06 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 10,763,910 (or divide by 9.2903e-08).
