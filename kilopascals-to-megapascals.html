How it works

To convert kilopascals to megapascals, multiply the number of kilopascals by 0.001. To convert megapascals to kilopascals, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilopascals to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = kilopascals × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
