How it works

To convert miles per hour to knots, multiply the number of miles per hour by 0.86897624. To convert knots to miles per hour, multiply by 1.1507794 (or divide by 0.86897624). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles per Hour to Knots formula

Knots = miles per hour × 0.86897624

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles per hour to knots. Multiply 12.5 × 0.86897624 = 10.862203 kn. To go the other way, multiply knots by 1.1507794 (or divide by 0.86897624).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
