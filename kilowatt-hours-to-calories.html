Kilowatt Hours to Calories Converter

Convert kilowatt hours to calories instantly. 1 kWh = 860,420.65 cal.

How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to calories, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 860,420.65. One kilowatt hour equals 860,420.65 calories, and one calorie equals 1.16222e-06 kilowatt hours. The table shows common values.

Kilowatt Hours to Calories formula

Calories = kilowatt hours × 860,420.65

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 860,420.65 = 10,755,258 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 1.16222e-06 (or divide by 860,420.65).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
