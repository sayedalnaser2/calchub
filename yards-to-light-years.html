Yards to Light Years Converter

Convert yards to light years instantly. 1 yd = 9.66522e-17 ly.

How it works

To convert yards to light years, multiply the number of yards by 9.66522e-17. One yard equals 9.66522e-17 light years, and one light year equals 1.03464e+16 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Light Years formula

Light years = yards × 9.66522e-17

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 9.66522e-17 = 1.20815e-15 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 1.03464e+16 (or divide by 9.66522e-17).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
