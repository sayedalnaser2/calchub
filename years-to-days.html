Years to Days Converter

Convert years to days instantly. 1 years = 365.2425 days.

How it works

To convert years to days, multiply the number of years by 365.2425. One year equals 365.2425 days, and one day equals 0.002737907 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Days formula

Days = years × 365.2425

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to days. Multiply 12.5 × 365.2425 = 4565.5312 days. To go the other way, multiply days by 0.002737907 (or divide by 365.2425).
