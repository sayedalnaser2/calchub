Years to Nanoseconds Converter

Convert years to nanoseconds instantly. 1 years = 3.1557e+16 ns.

How it works

To convert years to nanoseconds, multiply the number of years by 3.1557e+16. One year equals 3.1557e+16 nanoseconds, and one nanosecond equals 3.16887e-17 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = years × 3.1557e+16

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3.1557e+16 = 3.94462e+17 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 3.16887e-17 (or divide by 3.1557e+16).
