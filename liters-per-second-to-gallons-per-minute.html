Liters per Second to Gallons per Minute Converter

Convert liters per second to gallons per minute instantly. 1 L/s = 15.850323 gal/min.

How it works

To convert liters per second to gallons per minute, multiply the number of liters per second by 15.850323. One liter per second equals 15.850323 gallons per minute, and one gallon per minute equals 0.063090196 liters per second. The table shows common values.

Liters per Second to Gallons per Minute formula

Gallons per minute = liters per second × 15.850323

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per second to gallons per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 15.850323 = 198.12904 gal/min. To go the other way, multiply gallons per minute by 0.063090196 (or divide by 15.850323).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
