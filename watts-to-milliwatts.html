Watts to Milliwatts Converter

Convert watts to milliwatts instantly. 1 W = 1,000 mW.

How it works

To convert watts to milliwatts, multiply the number of watts by 1,000. One watt equals 1,000 milliwatts, and one milliwatt equals 0.001 watts. The table shows common values.

Watts to Milliwatts formula

Milliwatts = watts × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 watts to milliwatts. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 mW. To go the other way, multiply milliwatts by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
