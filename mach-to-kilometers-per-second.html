How it works

To convert mach to kilometers per second, multiply the number of mach by 0.34029. To convert kilometers per second to mach, multiply by 2.93867 (or divide by 0.34029). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Mach to Kilometers per Second formula

Kilometers per second = mach × 0.34029

Worked example: convert 12.5 mach to kilometers per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.34029 = 4.253625 km/s. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per second by 2.93867 (or divide by 0.34029).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
