Kilocalories to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert kilocalories to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 kcal = 0.0011622222 kWh.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of kilocalories by 0.0011622222. One kilocalorie equals 0.0011622222 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 860.42065 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = kilocalories × 0.0011622222

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0011622222 = 0.014527778 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 860.42065 (or divide by 0.0011622222).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
