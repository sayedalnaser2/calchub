How it works

To convert kilocalories to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of kilocalories by 0.0011622222. To convert kilowatt hours to kilocalories, multiply by 860.42065 (or divide by 0.0011622222). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilocalories to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = kilocalories × 0.0011622222

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
