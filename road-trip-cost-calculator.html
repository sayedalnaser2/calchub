Road Trip Cost Calculator

Estimate fuel and total cost of a road trip and the cost per traveler.

How it works

Gallons = miles / mpg. Fuel cost = gallons x price. Add tolls and other expenses, then divide by travelers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to include wear and tear?

For a fuller cost, add about 10-20 cents per mile for maintenance and depreciation.

Does highway driving change mpg?

Yes. Speeds above 65 mph reduce efficiency.
