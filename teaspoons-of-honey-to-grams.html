Teaspoons of Honey to Grams

Convert teaspoons of honey to grams. 1 cup of honey weighs about 336 g.

How it works

Honey is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 336 g per US cup, convert teaspoons to grams by multiplying by 7.

1 US cup of honey weighs about 336 g (11.9 oz), 1 tablespoon about 21.0 g and 1 teaspoon about 7.0 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
