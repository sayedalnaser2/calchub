How it works

To convert pound-inches to newton meters, multiply the number of pound-inches by 0.11298483. To convert newton meters to pound-inches, multiply by 8.8507458 (or divide by 0.11298483). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pound-Inches to Newton Meters formula

Newton meters = pound-inches × 0.11298483

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
