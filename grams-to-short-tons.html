How it works

To convert grams to short tons, multiply the number of grams by 1.10231e-06. One gram equals 1.10231e-06 short tons, and one short ton equals 907,184.74 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Short Tons formula

Short tons = grams × 1.10231e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to short tons. Multiply 12.5 × 1.10231e-06 = 1.37789e-05 short tons. To go the other way, multiply short tons by 907,184.74 (or divide by 1.10231e-06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
