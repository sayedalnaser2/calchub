Property Tax Calculator

Estimate annual and monthly property tax from value and rate.

How it works

Property tax is the assessed value multiplied by the local rate. The US average effective rate is roughly 1%.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my rate?

Your county assessor or tax collector publishes local rates.
