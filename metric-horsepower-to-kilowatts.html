Metric Horsepower to Kilowatts Converter

Convert metric horsepower to kilowatts instantly. 1 PS = 0.73549875 kW.

How it works

To convert metric horsepower to kilowatts, multiply the number of metric horsepower by 0.73549875. One metric horsepower equals 0.73549875 kilowatts, and one kilowatt equals 1.3596216 metric horsepower. The table shows common values.

Metric Horsepower to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = metric horsepower × 0.73549875

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric horsepower to kilowatts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.73549875 = 9.1937344 kW. To go the other way, multiply kilowatts by 1.3596216 (or divide by 0.73549875).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
