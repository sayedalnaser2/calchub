Gigajoules to Therms Converter

Convert gigajoules to therms instantly. 1 GJ = 9.4804343 therms.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to therms, multiply the number of gigajoules by 9.4804343. One gigajoule equals 9.4804343 therms, and one therm equals 0.1054804 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Therms formula

Therms = gigajoules × 9.4804343

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 9.4804343 = 118.50543 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 0.1054804 (or divide by 9.4804343).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
