Geometric Mean Calculator

Calculate the geometric and harmonic mean of positive numbers.

How it works

The geometric mean is the nth root of the product, suited to growth rates and ratios. The harmonic mean suits rates like speed.

Frequently asked questions

When use geometric mean?

For multiplicative changes, such as average investment returns over several years.
