Mils to Inches Converter

Convert mils to inches instantly. 1 mil = 0.001 in.

How it works

To convert mils to inches, multiply the number of mils by 0.001. One mil equals 0.001 inches, and one inch equals 1,000 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Inches formula

Inches = mils × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
