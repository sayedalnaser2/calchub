How it works

To convert kilograms to metric tons, multiply the number of kilograms by 0.001. To convert metric tons to kilograms, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilograms to Metric Tons formula

Metric tons = kilograms × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to metric tons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 t. To go the other way, multiply metric tons by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
