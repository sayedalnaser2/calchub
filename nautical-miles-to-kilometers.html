How it works

To convert nautical miles to kilometers, multiply the number of nautical miles by 1.852. To convert kilometers to nautical miles, multiply by 0.5399568 (or divide by 1.852). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Nautical Miles to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = nautical miles × 1.852

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1.852 = 23.15 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 0.5399568 (or divide by 1.852).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
