Convert ounces to metric tons instantly. 1 oz = 2.83495e-05 t.

How it works

To convert ounces to metric tons, multiply the number of ounces by 2.83495e-05. One ounce equals 2.83495e-05 metric tons, and one metric ton equals 35,273.962 ounces. The table shows common values.

Ounces to Metric Tons formula

Metric tons = ounces × 2.83495e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to metric tons. Multiply 12.5 × 2.83495e-05 = 0.00035436904 t. To go the other way, multiply metric tons by 35,273.962 (or divide by 2.83495e-05).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
