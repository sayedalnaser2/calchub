How it works

To convert tablespoons to imperial gallons, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.0032526335. To convert imperial gallons to tablespoons, multiply by 307.44318 (or divide by 0.0032526335). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
