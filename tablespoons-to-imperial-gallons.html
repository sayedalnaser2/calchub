How it works

To convert tablespoons to imperial gallons, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.0032526335. One tablespoon equals 0.0032526335 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 307.44318 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = tablespoons × 0.0032526335

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0032526335 = 0.040657919 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 307.44318 (or divide by 0.0032526335).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
