Dynes to Newtons Converter

Convert dynes to newtons instantly. 1 dyn = 1e-05 N.

How it works

To convert dynes to newtons, multiply the number of dynes by 1e-05. One dyne equals 1e-05 newtons, and one newton equals 100,000 dynes. The table shows common values.

Dynes to Newtons formula

Newtons = dynes × 1e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 dynes to newtons. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-05 = 0.000125 N. To go the other way, multiply newtons by 100,000 (or divide by 1e-05).

About force units

Force is measured in newtons in the SI system. Pound-force and kilogram-force are common in engineering and everyday weight contexts.
