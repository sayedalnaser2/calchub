Customer Lifetime Value Calculator

Estimate how much profit an average customer brings over their lifetime.

How it works

Lifetime value = average order value x purchases per year x lifespan x profit margin. Comparing it with the cost of acquiring a customer shows whether growth is profitable; many businesses target an LTV:CAC of at least 3:1.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy LTV:CAC ratio?

3:1 or better is a common benchmark.

Should I use revenue or profit?

Profit (margin-adjusted) gives a truer picture.
