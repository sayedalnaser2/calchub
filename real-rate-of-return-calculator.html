Real Rate of Return Calculator

Adjust an investment return for inflation to see your real return.

How it works

The real return is (1 + nominal) / (1 + inflation) - 1, slightly lower than simply subtracting inflation. Compounding it over the years shows what the money buys in today's dollars.

Frequently asked questions

Is real return just nominal minus inflation?

That is a close approximation; the exact formula divides rather than subtracts.

Why does it matter?

Cash earning less than inflation loses purchasing power every year.
