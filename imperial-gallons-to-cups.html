How it works

To convert imperial gallons to cups, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 19.215199. One imperial gallon equals 19.215199 cups, and one cup equals 0.052042137 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Cups formula

Cups = imperial gallons × 19.215199

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 19.215199 = 240.18999 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.052042137 (or divide by 19.215199).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
