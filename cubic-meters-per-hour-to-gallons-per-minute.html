Cubic Meters per Hour to Gallons per Minute Converter

Convert cubic meters per hour to gallons per minute instantly. 1 m³/h = 4.4028675 gal/min.

How it works

To convert cubic meters per hour to gallons per minute, multiply the number of cubic meters per hour by 4.4028675. One cubic meter per hour equals 4.4028675 gallons per minute, and one gallon per minute equals 0.22712471 cubic meters per hour. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters per Hour to Gallons per Minute formula

Gallons per minute = cubic meters per hour × 4.4028675

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters per hour to gallons per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 4.4028675 = 55.035844 gal/min. To go the other way, multiply gallons per minute by 0.22712471 (or divide by 4.4028675).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
