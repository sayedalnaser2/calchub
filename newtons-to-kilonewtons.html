How it works

To convert newtons to kilonewtons, multiply the number of newtons by 0.001. To convert kilonewtons to newtons, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Newtons to Kilonewtons formula

Kilonewtons = newtons × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 newtons to kilonewtons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kN. To go the other way, multiply kilonewtons by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

Force is measured in newtons in the SI system. Pound-force and kilogram-force are common in engineering and everyday weight contexts.
