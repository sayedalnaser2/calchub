How it works

Monthly principal and interest comes from the standard amortization formula P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), with r the monthly rate and n the number of payments. A $1,700,000 loan at 6.5% for 30 years costs $10,745.16 a month before taxes and insurance.
