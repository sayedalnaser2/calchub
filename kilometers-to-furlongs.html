Convert kilometers to furlongs instantly. 1 km = 4.9709695 furlongs.

How it works

To convert kilometers to furlongs, multiply the number of kilometers by 4.9709695. One kilometer equals 4.9709695 furlongs, and one furlong equals 0.201168 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = kilometers × 4.9709695

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 4.9709695 = 62.137119 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 0.201168 (or divide by 4.9709695).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
