Megabits to Gigabytes Converter

Convert megabits to gigabytes instantly. 1 Mb = 0.000125 GB.

How it works

To convert megabits to gigabytes, multiply the number of megabits by 0.000125. One megabit equals 0.000125 gigabytes, and one gigabyte equals 8,000 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = megabits × 0.000125

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.000125 = 0.0015625 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 8,000 (or divide by 0.000125).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
