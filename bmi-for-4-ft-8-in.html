BMI for 4 ft 8 in (142 cm)

Check your BMI at 4 ft 8 in and see the healthy weight range for this height.

How it works

BMI = 703 x weight (lb) / height (in) squared. At 4 ft 8 in (56 inches), a healthy BMI of 18.5-24.9 corresponds to 83-111 lb.

BMI is a screening tool. It does not measure body fat directly and can misclassify very muscular people, older adults and some ethnic groups. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy weight at 4 ft 8 in?

Roughly 83 to 111 lb for a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9.

It is a rough screening measure; body composition and age matter.
