Floor Joist Calculator

Calculate how many joists a floor or deck needs for a given spacing.

How it works

Joists = floor(length x 12 / spacing) + 1, which includes the first and last joist. Standard spacings are 12, 16 and 24 inches on center. Joist size and span depend on lumber species and code tables.

Frequently asked questions

What spacing is most common?

16 inches on center for floors.

How do I size the joists?

Use span tables from your local building code or a span calculator for your lumber species.
