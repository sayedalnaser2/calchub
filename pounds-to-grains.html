Pounds to Grains Converter

Convert pounds to grains instantly. 1 lb = 7,000 gr.

How it works

To convert pounds to grains, multiply the number of pounds by 7,000. One pound equals 7,000 grains, and one grain equals 0.00014285714 pounds. The table shows common values.

Pounds to Grains formula

Grains = pounds × 7,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 7,000 = 87,500 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 0.00014285714 (or divide by 7,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
