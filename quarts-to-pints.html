Quarts to Pints Converter

Convert quarts to pints instantly. 1 qt = 2 pt.

How it works

To convert quarts to pints, multiply the number of quarts by 2. One quart equals 2 pints, and one pint equals 0.5 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Pints formula

Pints = quarts × 2

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 2 = 25 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 0.5 (or divide by 2).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
