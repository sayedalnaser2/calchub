Bar to Torr Converter

Convert bar to torr instantly. 1 bar = 750.06168 Torr.

How it works

To convert bar to torr, multiply the number of bar by 750.06168. One bar equals 750.06168 torr, and one torr equals 0.0013332237 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to Torr formula

Torr = bar × 750.06168

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 750.06168 = 9,375.771 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 0.0013332237 (or divide by 750.06168).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
