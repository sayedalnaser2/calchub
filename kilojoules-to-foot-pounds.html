Kilojoules to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert kilojoules to foot-pounds instantly. 1 kJ = 737.56215 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to foot-pounds, multiply the number of kilojoules by 737.56215. One kilojoule equals 737.56215 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 0.0013558179 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = kilojoules × 737.56215

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 737.56215 = 9,219.5269 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 0.0013558179 (or divide by 737.56215).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
