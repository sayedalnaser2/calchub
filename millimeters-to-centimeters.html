How it works

To convert millimeters to centimeters, multiply the number of millimeters by 0.1. To convert centimeters to millimeters, multiply by 10 (or divide by 0.1). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Millimeters to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = millimeters × 0.1

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.1 = 1.25 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 10 (or divide by 0.1).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
