Carats to Grains Converter

Convert carats to grains instantly. 1 ct = 3.0864717 gr.

How it works

To convert carats to grains, multiply the number of carats by 3.0864717. One carat equals 3.0864717 grains, and one grain equals 0.32399455 carats. The table shows common values.

Carats to Grains formula

Grains = carats × 3.0864717

Worked example: convert 12.5 carats to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 3.0864717 = 38.580896 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 0.32399455 (or divide by 3.0864717).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
