Fluid Ounces to Milliliters Converter

Convert fluid ounces to milliliters instantly. 1 fl oz = 29.57353 ml.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to milliliters, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 29.57353. One fluid ounce equals 29.57353 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.033814023 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = fluid ounces × 29.57353

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 29.57353 = 369.66912 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.033814023 (or divide by 29.57353).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
