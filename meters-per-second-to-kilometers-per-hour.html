Meters per Second to Kilometers per Hour Converter

Convert meters per second to kilometers per hour instantly. 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h.

How it works

To convert meters per second to kilometers per hour, multiply the number of meters per second by 3.6. One meter per second equals 3.6 kilometers per hour, and one kilometer per hour equals 0.27777778 meters per second. The table shows common values.

Meters per Second to Kilometers per Hour formula

Kilometers per hour = meters per second × 3.6

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters per second to kilometers per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 3.6 = 45 km/h. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per hour by 0.27777778 (or divide by 3.6).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
