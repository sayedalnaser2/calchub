Revolutions per Minute to Megahertz Converter

Convert revolutions per minute to megahertz instantly. 1 rpm = 1.66667e-08 MHz.

How it works

To convert revolutions per minute to megahertz, multiply the number of revolutions per minute by 1.66667e-08. One revolution per minute equals 1.66667e-08 megahertz, and one megahertz equals 60,000,000 revolutions per minute. The table shows common values.

Revolutions per Minute to Megahertz formula

Megahertz = revolutions per minute × 1.66667e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 revolutions per minute to megahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1.66667e-08 = 2.08333e-07 MHz. To go the other way, multiply megahertz by 60,000,000 (or divide by 1.66667e-08).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
