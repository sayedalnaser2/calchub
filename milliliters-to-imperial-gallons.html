Milliliters to Imperial Gallons Converter

Convert milliliters to imperial gallons instantly. 1 ml = 0.00021996925 imp gal.

How it works

To convert milliliters to imperial gallons, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.00021996925. One milliliter equals 0.00021996925 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 4,546.09 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = milliliters × 0.00021996925

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00021996925 = 0.0027496156 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 4,546.09 (or divide by 0.00021996925).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
