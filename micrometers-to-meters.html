Micrometers to Meters Converter

Convert micrometers to meters instantly. 1 µm = 1e-06 m.

How it works

To convert micrometers to meters, multiply the number of micrometers by 1e-06. One micrometer equals 1e-06 meters, and one meter equals 1,000,000 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Meters formula

Meters = micrometers × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
