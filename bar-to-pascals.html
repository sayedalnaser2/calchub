Bar to Pascals Converter

Convert bar to pascals instantly. 1 bar = 100,000 Pa.

How it works

To convert bar to pascals, multiply the number of bar by 100,000. One bar equals 100,000 pascals, and one pascal equals 1e-05 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to Pascals formula

Pascals = bar × 100,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 100,000 = 1,250,000 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 1e-05 (or divide by 100,000).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
