How it works

To convert grams to kilograms, multiply the number of grams by 0.001. To convert kilograms to grams, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Grams to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = grams × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
