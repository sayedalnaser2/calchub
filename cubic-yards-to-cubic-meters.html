Cubic Yards to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert cubic yards to cubic meters instantly. 1 yd³ = 0.76455486 m³.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to cubic meters, multiply the number of cubic yards by 0.76455486. One cubic yard equals 0.76455486 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 1.3079506 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = cubic yards × 0.76455486

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.76455486 = 9.5569357 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 1.3079506 (or divide by 0.76455486).
