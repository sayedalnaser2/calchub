How it works

To convert cubic yards to cubic meters, multiply the number of cubic yards by 0.76455486. To convert cubic meters to cubic yards, multiply by 1.3079506 (or divide by 0.76455486). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Yards to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = cubic yards × 0.76455486

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.76455486 = 9.5569357 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 1.3079506 (or divide by 0.76455486).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
