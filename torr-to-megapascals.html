How it works

To convert torr to megapascals, multiply the number of torr by 0.00013332237. One torr equals 0.00013332237 megapascals, and one megapascal equals 7,500.6168 torr. The table shows common values.

Torr to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = torr × 0.00013332237

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00013332237 = 0.0016665296 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 7,500.6168 (or divide by 0.00013332237).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
