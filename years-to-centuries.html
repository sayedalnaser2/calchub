How it works

To convert years to centuries, multiply the number of years by 0.01. To convert centuries to years, multiply by 100 (or divide by 0.01). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Years to Centuries formula

Centuries = years × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to centuries. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 centuries. To go the other way, multiply centuries by 100 (or divide by 0.01).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
