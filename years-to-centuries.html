Years to Centuries Converter

Convert years to centuries instantly. 1 years = 0.01 centuries.

How it works

To convert years to centuries, multiply the number of years by 0.01. One year equals 0.01 centuries, and one century equals 100 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Centuries formula

Centuries = years × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to centuries. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 centuries. To go the other way, multiply centuries by 100 (or divide by 0.01).
