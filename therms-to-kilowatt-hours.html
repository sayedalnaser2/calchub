How it works

To convert therms to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of therms by 29.300111. To convert kilowatt hours to therms, multiply by 0.034129563 (or divide by 29.300111). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Therms to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = therms × 29.300111

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
