How it works

To convert therms to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of therms by 29.300111. One therm equals 29.300111 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 0.034129563 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = therms × 29.300111

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 29.300111 = 366.25139 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 0.034129563 (or divide by 29.300111).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
