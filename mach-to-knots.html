How it works

To convert mach to knots, multiply the number of mach by 661.47084. One Mach equals 661.47084 knots, and one knot equals 0.0015117824 mach. The table shows common values.

Mach to Knots formula

Knots = mach × 661.47084

Worked example: convert 12.5 mach to knots. Multiply 12.5 × 661.47084 = 8,268.3855 kn. To go the other way, multiply knots by 0.0015117824 (or divide by 661.47084).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
