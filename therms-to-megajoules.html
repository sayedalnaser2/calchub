How it works

To convert therms to megajoules, multiply the number of therms by 105.4804. To convert megajoules to therms, multiply by 0.0094804343 (or divide by 105.4804). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Therms to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = therms × 105.4804

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 105.4804 = 1,318.505 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 0.0094804343 (or divide by 105.4804).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
