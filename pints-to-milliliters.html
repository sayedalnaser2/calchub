Pints to Milliliters Converter

Convert pints to milliliters instantly. 1 pt = 473.17647 ml.

How it works

To convert pints to milliliters, multiply the number of pints by 473.17647. One pint equals 473.17647 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.0021133764 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = pints × 473.17647

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 473.17647 = 5,914.7059 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.0021133764 (or divide by 473.17647).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
