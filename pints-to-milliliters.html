How it works

To convert pints to milliliters, multiply the number of pints by 473.17647. To convert milliliters to pints, multiply by 0.0021133764 (or divide by 473.17647). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pints to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = pints × 473.17647

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
