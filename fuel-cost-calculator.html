Fuel Cost Calculator

Solve trip fuel cost, distance, mpg or fuel price from any three values.

Type in any 3 fields and the rest are calculated.

How it works

Fuel used is distance divided by fuel economy. Multiply by the price per gallon for the trip cost.

Formula

Cost = distance / mpg x price per gallon. Enter any three of the four values.

Frequently asked questions

How can I lower fuel costs?

Keep tires inflated, avoid hard acceleration, remove roof racks and slow down on highways.
