Convert kilojoules to therms instantly. 1 kJ = 9.48043e-06 therms.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to therms, multiply the number of kilojoules by 9.48043e-06. One kilojoule equals 9.48043e-06 therms, and one therm equals 105,480.4 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Therms formula

Therms = kilojoules × 9.48043e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 9.48043e-06 = 0.00011850543 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 105,480.4 (or divide by 9.48043e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
