Kilobits to Gigabytes Converter

Convert kilobits to gigabytes instantly. 1 Kb = 1.25e-07 GB.

How it works

To convert kilobits to gigabytes, multiply the number of kilobits by 1.25e-07. One kilobit equals 1.25e-07 gigabytes, and one gigabyte equals 8,000,000 kilobits. The table shows common values.

Kilobits to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = kilobits × 1.25e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobits to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.25e-07 = 1.5625e-06 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 8,000,000 (or divide by 1.25e-07).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
