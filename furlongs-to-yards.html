Furlongs to Yards Converter

Convert furlongs to yards instantly. 1 furlongs = 220 yd.

How it works

To convert furlongs to yards, multiply the number of furlongs by 220. One furlong equals 220 yards, and one yard equals 0.0045454545 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Yards formula

Yards = furlongs × 220

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 220 = 2,750 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 0.0045454545 (or divide by 220).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
