Convert torr to bar instantly. 1 Torr = 0.0013332237 bar.

How it works

To convert torr to bar, multiply the number of torr by 0.0013332237. One torr equals 0.0013332237 bar, and one bar equals 750.06168 torr. The table shows common values.

Torr to Bar formula

Bar = torr × 0.0013332237

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013332237 = 0.016665296 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 750.06168 (or divide by 0.0013332237).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
