Cubic Inches to Cups Converter

How it works

To convert cubic inches to cups, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.069264069. One cubic inch equals 0.069264069 cups, and one cup equals 14.4375 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Cups formula

Cups = cubic inches × 0.069264069

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 0.069264069 = 0.86580087 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 14.4375 (or divide by 0.069264069).
