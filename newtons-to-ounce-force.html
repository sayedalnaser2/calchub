How it works

To convert newtons to ounce-force, multiply the number of newtons by 3.5969431. One newton equals 3.5969431 ounce-force, and one ounce-force equals 0.27801385 newtons. The table shows common values.

Newtons to Ounce-Force formula

Ounce-force = newtons × 3.5969431

Worked example: convert 12.5 newtons to ounce-force. Multiply 12.5 × 3.5969431 = 44.961789 ozf. To go the other way, multiply ounce-force by 0.27801385 (or divide by 3.5969431).
