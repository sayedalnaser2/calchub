How it works

To convert millimeters to nanometers, multiply the number of millimeters by 1,000,000. To convert nanometers to millimeters, multiply by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Millimeters to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = millimeters × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
