Cubic Inches to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert cubic inches to fluid ounces instantly. 1 in³ = 0.55411255 fl oz.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to fluid ounces, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.55411255. One cubic inch equals 0.55411255 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 1.8046875 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = cubic inches × 0.55411255

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 0.55411255 = 6.9264069 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 1.8046875 (or divide by 0.55411255).
