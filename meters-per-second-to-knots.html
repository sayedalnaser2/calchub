Meters per Second to Knots Converter

Convert meters per second to knots instantly. 1 m/s = 1.9438445 kn.

How it works

To convert meters per second to knots, multiply the number of meters per second by 1.9438445. One meter per second equals 1.9438445 knots, and one knot equals 0.51444444 meters per second. The table shows common values.

Meters per Second to Knots formula

Knots = meters per second × 1.9438445

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters per second to knots. Multiply 12.5 × 1.9438445 = 24.298056 kn. To go the other way, multiply knots by 0.51444444 (or divide by 1.9438445).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
