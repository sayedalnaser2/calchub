How it works

Horsepower = torque (lb-ft) x RPM / 5252. The constant 5252 comes from converting rotational power to the horsepower unit, 33,000 ft-lb per minute, divided by 2π.

Formula

Power = torque x angular speed. In US units, horsepower = torque (lb-ft) x RPM / 5252.

Frequently asked questions

Why do torque and horsepower curves cross at 5252 RPM?

Because of the formula, they are numerically equal at exactly 5252 RPM.

Is this wheel or crank horsepower?

It gives power at wherever torque was measured, crank for engine dyno figures.
