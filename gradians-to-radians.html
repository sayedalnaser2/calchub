Gradians to Radians Converter

Convert gradians to radians instantly. 1 grad = 0.015707963 rad.

How it works

To convert gradians to radians, multiply the number of gradians by 0.015707963. One gradian equals 0.015707963 radians, and one radian equals 63.661977 gradians. The table shows common values.

Gradians to Radians formula

Radians = gradians × 0.015707963

Worked example: convert 12.5 gradians to radians. Multiply 12.5 × 0.015707963 = 0.19634954 rad. To go the other way, multiply radians by 63.661977 (or divide by 0.015707963).
