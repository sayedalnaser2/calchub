Caffeine Half-Life Calculator

Estimate how much caffeine is left in your body hours after a drink.

How it works

Caffeine decays exponentially: remaining = dose x 0.5^(hours / half-life). The average half-life is about 5 hours but ranges from 2 to 10 depending on genetics, pregnancy, smoking and medications.

Frequently asked questions

How much caffeine is in a cup of coffee?

Roughly 80-120 mg for a brewed 8 oz cup.

How much is too much?

Up to 400 mg a day is generally considered safe for most healthy adults.
