PPI Calculator (Pixel Density)

Calculate screen pixel density and physical size.

How it works

PPI = √(width² + height²) / diagonal. Higher PPI means sharper images.

Frequently asked questions

What PPI looks sharp?

Around 110 at arm's length for monitors, 300+ for phones.
