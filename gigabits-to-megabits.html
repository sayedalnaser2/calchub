Gigabits to Megabits Converter

Convert gigabits to megabits instantly. 1 Gb = 1,000 Mb.

How it works

To convert gigabits to megabits, multiply the number of gigabits by 1,000. One gigabit equals 1,000 megabits, and one megabit equals 0.001 gigabits. The table shows common values.

Gigabits to Megabits formula

Megabits = gigabits × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabits to megabits. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 Mb. To go the other way, multiply megabits by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
