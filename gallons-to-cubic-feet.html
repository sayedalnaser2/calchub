How it works

To convert gallons to cubic feet, multiply the number of gallons by 0.13368056. To convert cubic feet to gallons, multiply by 7.4805195 (or divide by 0.13368056). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Gallons to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = gallons × 0.13368056

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.13368056 = 1.6710069 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 7.4805195 (or divide by 0.13368056).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
