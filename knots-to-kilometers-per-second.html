Knots to Kilometers per Second Converter

Convert knots to kilometers per second instantly. 1 kn = 0.00051444444 km/s.

How it works

To convert knots to kilometers per second, multiply the number of knots by 0.00051444444. One knot equals 0.00051444444 kilometers per second, and one kilometer per second equals 1,943.8445 knots. The table shows common values.

Knots to Kilometers per Second formula

Kilometers per second = knots × 0.00051444444

Worked example: convert 12.5 knots to kilometers per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00051444444 = 0.0064305556 km/s. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per second by 1,943.8445 (or divide by 0.00051444444).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
