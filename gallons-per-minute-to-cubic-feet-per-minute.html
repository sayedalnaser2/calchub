How it works

To convert gallons per minute to cubic feet per minute, multiply the number of gallons per minute by 0.13368056. To convert cubic feet per minute to gallons per minute, multiply by 7.4805195 (or divide by 0.13368056). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Gallons per Minute to Cubic Feet per Minute formula

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
