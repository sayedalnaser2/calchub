Gallons per Minute to Cubic Feet per Minute Converter

Convert gallons per minute to cubic feet per minute instantly. 1 gal/min = 0.13368056 cfm.

How it works

To convert gallons per minute to cubic feet per minute, multiply the number of gallons per minute by 0.13368056. One gallon per minute equals 0.13368056 cubic feet per minute, and one cubic foot per minute equals 7.4805195 gallons per minute. The table shows common values.

Gallons per Minute to Cubic Feet per Minute formula

Cubic feet per minute = gallons per minute × 0.13368056

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons per minute to cubic feet per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 0.13368056 = 1.6710069 cfm. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet per minute by 7.4805195 (or divide by 0.13368056).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
