How it works

To convert imperial gallons to cubic meters, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 0.00454609. To convert cubic meters to imperial gallons, multiply by 219.96925 (or divide by 0.00454609). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Imperial Gallons to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = imperial gallons × 0.00454609

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00454609 = 0.056826125 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 219.96925 (or divide by 0.00454609).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
