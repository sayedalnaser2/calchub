Yards to Mils Converter

Convert yards to mils instantly. 1 yd = 36,000 mil.

How it works

To convert yards to mils, multiply the number of yards by 36,000. One yard equals 36,000 mils, and one mil equals 2.77778e-05 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Mils formula

Mils = yards × 36,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 36,000 = 450,000 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 2.77778e-05 (or divide by 36,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
