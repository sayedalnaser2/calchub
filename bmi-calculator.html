How it works

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. A BMI of 18.5-24.9 is classed as healthy, 25-29.9 as overweight and 30 or more as obesity.

Formula

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) squared. Leave the BMI blank to calculate it, or enter a target BMI and a height to find the matching weight.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate?

BMI is a quick screening tool. It does not distinguish muscle from fat, so athletes can score high without excess body fat.

Does BMI differ for children?

Yes. Children and teens use age and sex percentiles rather than the adult cutoffs shown here.
