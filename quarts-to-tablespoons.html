Quarts to Tablespoons Converter

Convert quarts to tablespoons instantly. 1 qt = 64 tbsp.

How it works

To convert quarts to tablespoons, multiply the number of quarts by 64. One quart equals 64 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.015625 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = quarts × 64

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 64 = 800 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.015625 (or divide by 64).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
