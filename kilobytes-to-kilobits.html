Kilobytes to Kilobits Converter

Convert kilobytes to kilobits instantly. 1 KB = 8 Kb.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to kilobits, multiply the number of kilobytes by 8. One kilobyte equals 8 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 0.125 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = kilobytes × 8

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 8 = 100 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 0.125 (or divide by 8).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
