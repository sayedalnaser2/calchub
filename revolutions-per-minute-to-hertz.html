Revolutions per Minute to Hertz Converter

Convert revolutions per minute to hertz instantly. 1 rpm = 0.016666667 Hz.

How it works

To convert revolutions per minute to hertz, multiply the number of revolutions per minute by 0.016666667. One revolution per minute equals 0.016666667 hertz, and one hertz equals 60 revolutions per minute. The table shows common values.

Revolutions per Minute to Hertz formula

Hertz = revolutions per minute × 0.016666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 revolutions per minute to hertz. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016666667 = 0.20833333 Hz. To go the other way, multiply hertz by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
