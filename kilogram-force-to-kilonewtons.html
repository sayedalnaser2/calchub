Kilogram-Force to Kilonewtons Converter

Convert kilogram-force to kilonewtons instantly. 1 kgf = 0.00980665 kN.

How it works

To convert kilogram-force to kilonewtons, multiply the number of kilogram-force by 0.00980665. One kilogram-force equals 0.00980665 kilonewtons, and one kilonewton equals 101.97162 kilogram-force. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force to Kilonewtons formula

Kilonewtons = kilogram-force × 0.00980665

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force to kilonewtons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00980665 = 0.12258313 kN. To go the other way, multiply kilonewtons by 101.97162 (or divide by 0.00980665).
