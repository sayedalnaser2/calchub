Furlongs to Miles Converter

Convert furlongs to miles instantly. 1 furlongs = 0.125 mi.

How it works

To convert furlongs to miles, multiply the number of furlongs by 0.125. One furlong equals 0.125 miles, and one mile equals 8 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Miles formula

Miles = furlongs × 0.125

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.125 = 1.5625 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 8 (or divide by 0.125).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
