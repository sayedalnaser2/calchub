How it works

To convert grams to grains, multiply the number of grams by 15.432358. One gram equals 15.432358 grains, and one grain equals 0.06479891 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Grains formula

Grains = grams × 15.432358

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 15.432358 = 192.90448 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 0.06479891 (or divide by 15.432358).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
