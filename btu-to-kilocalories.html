How it works

To convert BTU to kilocalories, multiply the number of BTU by 0.2521644. To convert kilocalories to BTU, multiply by 3.9656668 (or divide by 0.2521644). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

BTU to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = BTU × 0.2521644

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.2521644 = 3.152055 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 3.9656668 (or divide by 0.2521644).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
