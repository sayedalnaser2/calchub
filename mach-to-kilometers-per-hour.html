Mach to Kilometers per Hour Converter

Convert mach to kilometers per hour instantly. 1 Mach = 1,225.044 km/h.

How it works

To convert mach to kilometers per hour, multiply the number of mach by 1,225.044. One Mach equals 1,225.044 kilometers per hour, and one kilometer per hour equals 0.00081629721 mach. The table shows common values.

Mach to Kilometers per Hour formula

Kilometers per hour = mach × 1,225.044

Worked example: convert 12.5 mach to kilometers per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 1,225.044 = 15,313.05 km/h. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per hour by 0.00081629721 (or divide by 1,225.044).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
