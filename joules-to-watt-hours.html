Joules to Watt Hours Converter

Convert joules to watt hours instantly. 1 J = 0.00027777778 Wh.

How it works

To convert joules to watt hours, multiply the number of joules by 0.00027777778. One joule equals 0.00027777778 watt hours, and one watt hour equals 3,600 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = joules × 0.00027777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00027777778 = 0.0034722222 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 3,600 (or divide by 0.00027777778).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
