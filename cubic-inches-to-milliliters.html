Cubic Inches to Milliliters Converter

Convert cubic inches to milliliters instantly. 1 in³ = 16.387064 ml.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to milliliters, multiply the number of cubic inches by 16.387064. One cubic inch equals 16.387064 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.061023744 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = cubic inches × 16.387064

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 16.387064 = 204.8383 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.061023744 (or divide by 16.387064).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
