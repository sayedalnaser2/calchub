How it works

To convert gradians to turns, multiply the number of gradians by 0.0025. To convert turns to gradians, multiply by 400 (or divide by 0.0025). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Gradians to Turns formula

Turns = gradians × 0.0025

Worked example: convert 12.5 gradians to turns. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0025 = 0.03125 turns. To go the other way, multiply turns by 400 (or divide by 0.0025).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
