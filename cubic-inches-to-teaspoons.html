Cubic Inches to Teaspoons Converter

Convert cubic inches to teaspoons instantly. 1 in³ = 3.3246753 tsp.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to teaspoons, multiply the number of cubic inches by 3.3246753. One cubic inch equals 3.3246753 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.30078125 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = cubic inches × 3.3246753

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 3.3246753 = 41.558442 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.30078125 (or divide by 3.3246753).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
