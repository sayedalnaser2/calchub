How it works

To convert newtons to kips, multiply the number of newtons by 0.00022480894. One newton equals 0.00022480894 kips, and one kip equals 4,448.2216 newtons. The table shows common values.

Newtons to Kips formula

Kips = newtons × 0.00022480894

Worked example: convert 12.5 newtons to kips. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00022480894 = 0.0028101118 kip. To go the other way, multiply kips by 4,448.2216 (or divide by 0.00022480894).
