Meters to Light Years Converter

Convert meters to light years instantly. 1 m = 1.057e-16 ly.

How it works

To convert meters to light years, multiply the number of meters by 1.057e-16. One meter equals 1.057e-16 light years, and one light year equals 9.46073e+15 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Light Years formula

Light years = meters × 1.057e-16

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 1.057e-16 = 1.32125e-15 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 9.46073e+15 (or divide by 1.057e-16).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
