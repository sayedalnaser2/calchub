Quarts to Oil Barrels Converter

Convert quarts to oil barrels instantly. 1 qt = 0.005952381 bbl.

How it works

To convert quarts to oil barrels, multiply the number of quarts by 0.005952381. One quart equals 0.005952381 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 168 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = quarts × 0.005952381

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to oil barrels. Multiply 12.5 × 0.005952381 = 0.074404762 bbl. To go the other way, multiply oil barrels by 168 (or divide by 0.005952381).
