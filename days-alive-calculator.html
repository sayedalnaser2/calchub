How Many Days Old Am I? Calculator

Find how many days, weeks, hours and minutes you have been alive.

How it works

Days alive is today's date minus your birth date. Dividing by 7, 30.4375 (average month) or 365.2425 (average year) gives the other units.

Frequently asked questions

When will I turn 10,000 days old?

About 27 years and 4 months after birth.

Does it include leap days?

Yes, it counts the actual calendar days.
