Kilocalories to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert kilocalories to foot-pounds instantly. 1 kcal = 3,085.96 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to foot-pounds, multiply the number of kilocalories by 3,085.96. One kilocalorie equals 3,085.96 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 0.00032404827 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = kilocalories × 3,085.96

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,085.96 = 38,574.5 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 0.00032404827 (or divide by 3,085.96).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
