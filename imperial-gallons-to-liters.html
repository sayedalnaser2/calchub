How it works

To convert imperial gallons to liters, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 4.54609. To convert liters to imperial gallons, multiply by 0.21996925 (or divide by 4.54609). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Imperial Gallons to Liters formula

Liters = imperial gallons × 4.54609

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 4.54609 = 56.826125 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.21996925 (or divide by 4.54609).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
