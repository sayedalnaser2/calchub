How it works

To convert cubic feet per minute to liters per minute, multiply the number of cubic feet per minute by 28.316847. One cubic foot per minute equals 28.316847 liters per minute, and one liter per minute equals 0.035314667 cubic feet per minute. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet per Minute to Liters per Minute formula

Liters per minute = cubic feet per minute × 28.316847

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet per minute to liters per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 28.316847 = 353.96058 L/min. To go the other way, multiply liters per minute by 0.035314667 (or divide by 28.316847).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
