Square Kilometers to Square Yards Converter

Convert square kilometers to square yards instantly. 1 km² = 1,195,990 yd².

How it works

To convert square kilometers to square yards, multiply the number of square kilometers by 1,195,990. One square kilometer equals 1,195,990 square yards, and one square yard equals 8.36127e-07 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Square Yards formula

Square yards = square kilometers × 1,195,990

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to square yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1,195,990 = 14,949,876 yd². To go the other way, multiply square yards by 8.36127e-07 (or divide by 1,195,990).
