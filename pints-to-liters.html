How it works

To convert pints to liters, multiply the number of pints by 0.47317647. To convert liters to pints, multiply by 2.1133764 (or divide by 0.47317647). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pints to Liters formula

Liters = pints × 0.47317647

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.47317647 = 5.9147059 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 2.1133764 (or divide by 0.47317647).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
