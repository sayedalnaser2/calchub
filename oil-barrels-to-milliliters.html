How it works

To convert oil barrels to milliliters, multiply the number of oil barrels by 158,987.29. To convert milliliters to oil barrels, multiply by 6.28981e-06 (or divide by 158,987.29). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
