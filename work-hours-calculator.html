Work Hours Calculator

Calculate hours worked between a start and end time, minus unpaid breaks.

How it works

Hours worked = end time - start time - break. If the end time is earlier than the start (overnight shift), the calculator adds 24 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide by 60; 30 minutes is 0.5.

Does it handle night shifts?

Yes, when the end is earlier than the start it assumes the next day.
