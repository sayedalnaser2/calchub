Foot-Pounds to Megajoules Converter

Convert foot-pounds to megajoules instantly. 1 ft-lb = 1.35582e-06 MJ.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to megajoules, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 1.35582e-06. One foot-pound equals 1.35582e-06 megajoules, and one megajoule equals 737,562.15 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = foot-pounds × 1.35582e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1.35582e-06 = 1.69477e-05 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 737,562.15 (or divide by 1.35582e-06).
