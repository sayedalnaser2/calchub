Feet to Millimeters Converter

Convert feet to millimeters instantly. 1 ft = 304.8 mm.

How it works

To convert feet to millimeters, multiply the number of feet by 304.8. One foot equals 304.8 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 0.0032808399 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = feet × 304.8

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 304.8 = 3,810 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 0.0032808399 (or divide by 304.8).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
