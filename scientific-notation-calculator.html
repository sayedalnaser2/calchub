Scientific Notation Converter

Convert numbers to scientific and engineering notation.

How it works

Scientific notation writes a number as m x 10^n with 1 <= |m| < 10. Engineering notation restricts the exponent to multiples of three.

Frequently asked questions

Why use scientific notation?

It makes very large or very small numbers easier to read and compare.
