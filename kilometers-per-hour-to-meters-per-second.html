Kilometers per Hour to Meters per Second Converter

Convert kilometers per hour to meters per second instantly. 1 km/h = 0.27777778 m/s.

How it works

To convert kilometers per hour to meters per second, multiply the number of kilometers per hour by 0.27777778. One kilometer per hour equals 0.27777778 meters per second, and one meter per second equals 3.6 kilometers per hour. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Hour to Meters per Second formula

Meters per second = kilometers per hour × 0.27777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per hour to meters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.27777778 = 3.4722222 m/s. To go the other way, multiply meters per second by 3.6 (or divide by 0.27777778).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
