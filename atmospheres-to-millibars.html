How it works

To convert atmospheres to millibars, multiply the number of atmospheres by 1,013.25. To convert millibars to atmospheres, multiply by 0.00098692327 (or divide by 1,013.25). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Atmospheres to Millibars formula

Millibars = atmospheres × 1,013.25

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 1,013.25 = 12,665.625 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.00098692327 (or divide by 1,013.25).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
