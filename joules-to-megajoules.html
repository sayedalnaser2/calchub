Joules to Megajoules Converter

Convert joules to megajoules instantly. 1 J = 1e-06 MJ.

How it works

To convert joules to megajoules, multiply the number of joules by 1e-06. One joule equals 1e-06 megajoules, and one megajoule equals 1,000,000 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = joules × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
