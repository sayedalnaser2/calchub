How it works

To convert pound-force to kips, multiply the number of pound-force by 0.001. To convert kips to pound-force, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pound-Force to Kips formula

Kips = pound-force × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-force to kips. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kip. To go the other way, multiply kips by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).
