Pound-Force to Kips Converter

Convert pound-force to kips instantly. 1 lbf = 0.001 kip.

How it works

To convert pound-force to kips, multiply the number of pound-force by 0.001. One pound-force equals 0.001 kips, and one kip equals 1,000 pound-force. The table shows common values.

Pound-Force to Kips formula

Kips = pound-force × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-force to kips. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kip. To go the other way, multiply kips by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).
