Carats to Milligrams Converter

Convert carats to milligrams instantly. 1 ct = 200 mg.

How it works

To convert carats to milligrams, multiply the number of carats by 200. One carat equals 200 milligrams, and one milligram equals 0.005 carats. The table shows common values.

Carats to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = carats × 200

Worked example: convert 12.5 carats to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 200 = 2,500 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 0.005 (or divide by 200).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
