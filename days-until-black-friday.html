How Many Days Until Black Friday?

Live countdown showing the days, weeks and hours left until Black Friday.

How it works

The countdown compares today's date with the next occurrence of Black Friday. If the holiday has passed this year it counts to next year's date. Moving holidays such as Easter or Thanksgiving are calculated from their calendar rules.
