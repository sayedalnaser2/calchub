Estimate daily protein needs for your goal.

How it works

Needs are expressed in grams per kilogram of body weight. The RDA minimum is 0.8 g/kg, while active people often aim for 1.2-2.0 g/kg.

Frequently asked questions

Can I eat too much protein?

Most healthy people tolerate it well, but those with kidney disease should ask their doctor.
