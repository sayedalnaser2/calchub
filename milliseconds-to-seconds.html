Milliseconds to Seconds Converter

Convert milliseconds to seconds instantly. 1 ms = 0.001 s.

How it works

To convert milliseconds to seconds, multiply the number of milliseconds by 0.001. One millisecond equals 0.001 seconds, and one second equals 1,000 milliseconds. The table shows common values.

Milliseconds to Seconds formula

Seconds = milliseconds × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliseconds to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).
