Millibars to Kilopascals Converter

Convert millibars to kilopascals instantly. 1 mbar = 0.1 kPa.

How it works

To convert millibars to kilopascals, multiply the number of millibars by 0.1. One millibar equals 0.1 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 10 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = millibars × 0.1

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.1 = 1.25 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 10 (or divide by 0.1).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
