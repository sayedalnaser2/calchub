Self-Employment Tax Calculator

Estimate Social Security and Medicare tax on freelance or business income.

How it works

Self-employed people pay both halves of Social Security (12.4%) and Medicare (2.9%) on 92.35% of net earnings. Half of the tax is deductible. Figures use 2025 US federal rules and are estimates only.

Frequently asked questions

Do I also owe income tax?

Yes. SE tax is separate from federal and state income tax.
