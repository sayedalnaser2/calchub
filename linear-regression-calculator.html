Linear Regression Calculator

Fit a least-squares line and find the correlation of two data lists.

How it works

Least squares chooses the line minimizing squared vertical errors. The correlation r (between -1 and 1) measures the strength of the linear relationship.

Frequently asked questions

Does correlation mean causation?

No. Two variables can move together without one causing the other.
