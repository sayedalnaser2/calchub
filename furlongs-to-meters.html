How it works

To convert furlongs to meters, multiply the number of furlongs by 201.168. To convert meters to furlongs, multiply by 0.0049709695 (or divide by 201.168). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Furlongs to Meters formula

Meters = furlongs × 201.168

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 201.168 = 2,514.6 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 0.0049709695 (or divide by 201.168).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
