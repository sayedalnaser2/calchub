How it works

Pressure = force / area. One pascal is one newton per square meter. Standard atmospheric pressure is 101,325 Pa.

Formula

Pressure P = F / A. Enter any two of pressure, force and area.

Frequently asked questions

Why do sharp knives cut better?

The same force over a smaller area gives higher pressure.

How do I convert to psi?

Divide pascals by 6894.757.
