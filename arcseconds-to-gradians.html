Arcseconds to Gradians Converter

Convert arcseconds to gradians instantly. 1 arcsec = 0.00030864198 grad.

How it works

To convert arcseconds to gradians, multiply the number of arcseconds by 0.00030864198. One arcsecond equals 0.00030864198 gradians, and one gradian equals 3,240 arcseconds. The table shows common values.

Arcseconds to Gradians formula

Gradians = arcseconds × 0.00030864198

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcseconds to gradians. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00030864198 = 0.0038580247 grad. To go the other way, multiply gradians by 3,240 (or divide by 0.00030864198).
