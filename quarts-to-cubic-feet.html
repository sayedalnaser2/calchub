How it works

To convert quarts to cubic feet, multiply the number of quarts by 0.033420139. To convert cubic feet to quarts, multiply by 29.922078 (or divide by 0.033420139). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Quarts to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = quarts × 0.033420139

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
