Quarts to Cubic Feet Converter

Convert quarts to cubic feet instantly. 1 qt = 0.033420139 ft³.

How it works

To convert quarts to cubic feet, multiply the number of quarts by 0.033420139. One quart equals 0.033420139 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 29.922078 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = quarts × 0.033420139

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.033420139 = 0.41775174 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 29.922078 (or divide by 0.033420139).
