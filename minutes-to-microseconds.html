How it works

To convert minutes to microseconds, multiply the number of minutes by 60,000,000. To convert microseconds to minutes, multiply by 1.66667e-08 (or divide by 60,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Minutes to Microseconds formula

Microseconds = minutes × 60,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to microseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 60,000,000 = 750,000,000 µs. To go the other way, multiply microseconds by 1.66667e-08 (or divide by 60,000,000).
