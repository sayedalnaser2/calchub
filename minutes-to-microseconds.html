Minutes to Microseconds Converter

Convert minutes to microseconds instantly. 1 min = 60,000,000 µs.

How it works

To convert minutes to microseconds, multiply the number of minutes by 60,000,000. One minute equals 60,000,000 microseconds, and one microsecond equals 1.66667e-08 minutes. The table shows common values.

Minutes to Microseconds formula

Microseconds = minutes × 60,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to microseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 60,000,000 = 750,000,000 µs. To go the other way, multiply microseconds by 1.66667e-08 (or divide by 60,000,000).
