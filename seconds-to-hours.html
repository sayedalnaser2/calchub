Seconds to Hours Converter

Convert seconds to hours instantly. 1 s = 0.00027777778 h.

How it works

To convert seconds to hours, multiply the number of seconds by 0.00027777778. One second equals 0.00027777778 hours, and one hour equals 3,600 seconds. The table shows common values.

Seconds to Hours formula

Hours = seconds × 0.00027777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00027777778 = 0.0034722222 h. To go the other way, multiply hours by 3,600 (or divide by 0.00027777778).
