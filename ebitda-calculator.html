EBITDA Calculator

Calculate EBITDA from net income, interest, taxes and depreciation.

How it works

EBITDA adds interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization back to net income as a proxy for operating cash flow.

Frequently asked questions

Why do investors use EBITDA?

It lets them compare operating performance across companies with different financing and tax situations.
