How it works

To convert cups to pints, multiply the number of cups by 0.5. To convert pints to cups, multiply by 2 (or divide by 0.5). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cups to Pints formula

Pints = cups × 0.5

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.5 = 6.25 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 2 (or divide by 0.5).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
