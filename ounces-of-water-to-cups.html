Ounces of Water to Cups

Convert ounces of water to cups. 1 cup of water weighs about 237 g.

How it works

Water is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 237 g per US cup, convert ounces to cups by multiplying by 0.1196.

1 US cup of water weighs about 237 g (8.4 oz), 1 tablespoon about 14.8 g and 1 teaspoon about 4.9 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
