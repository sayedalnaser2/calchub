Feet to Fathoms Converter

Convert feet to fathoms instantly. 1 ft = 0.16666667 fathoms.

How it works

To convert feet to fathoms, multiply the number of feet by 0.16666667. One foot equals 0.16666667 fathoms, and one fathom equals 6 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = feet × 0.16666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.16666667 = 2.0833333 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 6 (or divide by 0.16666667).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
