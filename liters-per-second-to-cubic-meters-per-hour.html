Liters per Second to Cubic Meters per Hour Converter

Convert liters per second to cubic meters per hour instantly. 1 L/s = 3.6 m³/h.

How it works

To convert liters per second to cubic meters per hour, multiply the number of liters per second by 3.6. One liter per second equals 3.6 cubic meters per hour, and one cubic meter per hour equals 0.27777778 liters per second. The table shows common values.

Liters per Second to Cubic Meters per Hour formula

Cubic meters per hour = liters per second × 3.6

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per second to cubic meters per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 3.6 = 45 m³/h. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters per hour by 0.27777778 (or divide by 3.6).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
