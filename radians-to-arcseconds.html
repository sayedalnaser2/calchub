How it works

To convert radians to arcseconds, multiply the number of radians by 206,264.81. To convert arcseconds to radians, multiply by 4.84814e-06 (or divide by 206,264.81). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Radians to Arcseconds formula

Arcseconds = radians × 206,264.81

Worked example: convert 12.5 radians to arcseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 206,264.81 = 2,578,310.1 arcsec. To go the other way, multiply arcseconds by 4.84814e-06 (or divide by 206,264.81).
