Miles per Hour to Mach Converter

Convert miles per hour to mach instantly. 1 mph = 0.001313703 Mach.

How it works

To convert miles per hour to mach, multiply the number of miles per hour by 0.001313703. One mile per hour equals 0.001313703 mach, and one Mach equals 761.20705 miles per hour. The table shows common values.

Miles per Hour to Mach formula

Mach = miles per hour × 0.001313703

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles per hour to mach. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001313703 = 0.016421288 Mach. To go the other way, multiply mach by 761.20705 (or divide by 0.001313703).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
