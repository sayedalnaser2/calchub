Gibibytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert gibibytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 GiB = 1,073,741.8 KB.

How it works

To convert gibibytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of gibibytes by 1,073,741.8. One gibibyte equals 1,073,741.8 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 9.31323e-07 gibibytes. The table shows common values.

Gibibytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = gibibytes × 1,073,741.8

Worked example: convert 12.5 gibibytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,073,741.8 = 13,421,773 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 9.31323e-07 (or divide by 1,073,741.8).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
