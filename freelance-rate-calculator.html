Freelance Hourly Rate Calculator

Work out the hourly rate you need to charge to hit an income goal.

How it works

Revenue must cover business expenses plus enough pre-tax income to leave your desired take-home pay after tax. Dividing by billable hours (not total hours, since you also do admin and sales) gives the rate.

Frequently asked questions

Why billable hours instead of 40?

Freelancers spend time on admin, marketing and gaps between projects that cannot be billed.

Should I add benefits?

Yes. Include health insurance and retirement savings in expenses or income goals.
