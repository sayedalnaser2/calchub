How it works

To convert fathoms to centimeters, multiply the number of fathoms by 182.88. To convert centimeters to fathoms, multiply by 0.0054680665 (or divide by 182.88). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Fathoms to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = fathoms × 182.88

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 182.88 = 2,286 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 0.0054680665 (or divide by 182.88).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
