Kilometers to Millimeters Converter

Convert kilometers to millimeters instantly. 1 km = 1,000,000 mm.

How it works

To convert kilometers to millimeters, multiply the number of kilometers by 1,000,000. One kilometer equals 1,000,000 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 1e-06 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = kilometers × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
