How it works

To convert ounces to kilograms, multiply the number of ounces by 0.028349523. To convert kilograms to ounces, multiply by 35.273962 (or divide by 0.028349523). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Ounces to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = ounces × 0.028349523

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
