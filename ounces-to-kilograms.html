How it works

To convert ounces to kilograms, multiply the number of ounces by 0.028349523. One ounce equals 0.028349523 kilograms, and one kilogram equals 35.273962 ounces. The table shows common values.

Ounces to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = ounces × 0.028349523

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.028349523 = 0.35436904 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 35.273962 (or divide by 0.028349523).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
