How it works

To convert nautical miles to yards, multiply the number of nautical miles by 2,025.3718. One nautical mile equals 2,025.3718 yards, and one yard equals 0.0004937365 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Yards formula

Yards = nautical miles × 2,025.3718

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 2,025.3718 = 25,317.148 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 0.0004937365 (or divide by 2,025.3718).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
