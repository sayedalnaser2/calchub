Therms to Calories Converter

Convert therms to calories instantly. 1 therms = 25,210,421 cal.

How it works

To convert therms to calories, multiply the number of therms by 25,210,421. One therm equals 25,210,421 calories, and one calorie equals 3.96661e-08 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to Calories formula

Calories = therms × 25,210,421

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 25,210,421 = 315,130,260 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 3.96661e-08 (or divide by 25,210,421).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
