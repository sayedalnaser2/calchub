Square Millimeters to Square Feet Converter

Convert square millimeters to square feet instantly. 1 mm² = 1.07639e-05 ft².

How it works

To convert square millimeters to square feet, multiply the number of square millimeters by 1.07639e-05. One square millimeter equals 1.07639e-05 square feet, and one square foot equals 92,903.04 square millimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Millimeters to Square Feet formula

Square feet = square millimeters × 1.07639e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 square millimeters to square feet. Multiply 12.5 × 1.07639e-05 = 0.00013454888 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 92,903.04 (or divide by 1.07639e-05).
