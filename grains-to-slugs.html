How it works

To convert grains to slugs, multiply the number of grains by 4.44014e-06. One grain equals 4.44014e-06 slugs, and one slug equals 225,218.34 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Slugs formula

Slugs = grains × 4.44014e-06

About weight and mass units
