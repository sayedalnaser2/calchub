Newton Meters to Pound-Inches Converter

Convert newton meters to pound-inches instantly. 1 N·m = 8.8507458 lb-in.

How it works

To convert newton meters to pound-inches, multiply the number of newton meters by 8.8507458. One newton meter equals 8.8507458 pound-inches, and one pound-inch equals 0.11298483 newton meters. The table shows common values.

Newton Meters to Pound-Inches formula

Pound-inches = newton meters × 8.8507458

Worked example: convert 12.5 newton meters to pound-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 8.8507458 = 110.63432 lb-in. To go the other way, multiply pound-inches by 0.11298483 (or divide by 8.8507458).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
