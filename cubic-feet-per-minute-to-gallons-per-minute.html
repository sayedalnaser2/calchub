Cubic Feet per Minute to Gallons per Minute Converter

Convert cubic feet per minute to gallons per minute instantly. 1 cfm = 7.4805195 gal/min.

How it works

To convert cubic feet per minute to gallons per minute, multiply the number of cubic feet per minute by 7.4805195. One cubic foot per minute equals 7.4805195 gallons per minute, and one gallon per minute equals 0.13368056 cubic feet per minute. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet per Minute to Gallons per Minute formula

Gallons per minute = cubic feet per minute × 7.4805195

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet per minute to gallons per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 7.4805195 = 93.506494 gal/min. To go the other way, multiply gallons per minute by 0.13368056 (or divide by 7.4805195).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
