Quarts to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert quarts to fluid ounces instantly. 1 qt = 32 fl oz.

How it works

To convert quarts to fluid ounces, multiply the number of quarts by 32. One quart equals 32 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.03125 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = quarts × 32

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 32 = 400 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.03125 (or divide by 32).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
