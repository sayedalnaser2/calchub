Nanometers to Yards Converter

Convert nanometers to yards instantly. 1 nm = 1.09361e-09 yd.

How it works

To convert nanometers to yards, multiply the number of nanometers by 1.09361e-09. One nanometer equals 1.09361e-09 yards, and one yard equals 914,400,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Yards formula

Yards = nanometers × 1.09361e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1.09361e-09 = 1.36702e-08 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 914,400,000 (or divide by 1.09361e-09).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
