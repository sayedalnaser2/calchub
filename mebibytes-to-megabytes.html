Mebibytes to Megabytes Converter

Convert mebibytes to megabytes instantly. 1 MiB = 1.048576 MB.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to megabytes, multiply the number of mebibytes by 1.048576. One mebibyte equals 1.048576 megabytes, and one megabyte equals 0.95367432 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Megabytes formula

Megabytes = mebibytes × 1.048576

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to megabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.048576 = 13.1072 MB. To go the other way, multiply megabytes by 0.95367432 (or divide by 1.048576).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
