How it works

To convert mmHg to millibars, multiply the number of mmHg by 1.3332239. To convert millibars to mmHg, multiply by 0.75006158 (or divide by 1.3332239). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

mmHg to Millibars formula

Millibars = mmHg × 1.3332239

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3332239 = 16.665298 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.75006158 (or divide by 1.3332239).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
