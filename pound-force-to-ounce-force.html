Pound-Force to Ounce-Force Converter

Convert pound-force to ounce-force instantly. 1 lbf = 16 ozf.

How it works

To convert pound-force to ounce-force, multiply the number of pound-force by 16. One pound-force equals 16 ounce-force, and one ounce-force equals 0.0625 pound-force. The table shows common values.

Pound-Force to Ounce-Force formula

Ounce-force = pound-force × 16

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-force to ounce-force. Multiply 12.5 × 16 = 200 ozf. To go the other way, multiply ounce-force by 0.0625 (or divide by 16).
