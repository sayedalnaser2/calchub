Kilogram-Force Meters to Newton Meters Converter

Convert kilogram-force meters to newton meters instantly. 1 kgf·m = 9.80665 N·m.

How it works

To convert kilogram-force meters to newton meters, multiply the number of kilogram-force meters by 9.80665. One kilogram-force meter equals 9.80665 newton meters, and one newton meter equals 0.10197162 kilogram-force meters. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force Meters to Newton Meters formula

Newton meters = kilogram-force meters × 9.80665

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force meters to newton meters. Multiply 12.5 × 9.80665 = 122.58312 N·m. To go the other way, multiply newton meters by 0.10197162 (or divide by 9.80665).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
