Fathoms to Miles Converter

Convert fathoms to miles instantly. 1 fathom = 0.0011363636 mi.

How it works

To convert fathoms to miles, multiply the number of fathoms by 0.0011363636. One fathom equals 0.0011363636 miles, and one mile equals 880 fathoms. The table shows common values.

Fathoms to Miles formula

Miles = fathoms × 0.0011363636

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0011363636 = 0.014204545 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 880 (or divide by 0.0011363636).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
