Ounces of Ketchup to Cups

Convert ounces of ketchup to cups. 1 cup of ketchup weighs about 240 g.

How it works

Ketchup is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 240 g per US cup, convert ounces to cups by multiplying by 0.1181.

1 US cup of ketchup weighs about 240 g (8.5 oz), 1 tablespoon about 15.0 g and 1 teaspoon about 5.0 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
