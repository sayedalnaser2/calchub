Convert milliwatts to BTU per hour instantly. 1 mW = 0.0034121416 BTU/h.

How it works

To convert milliwatts to BTU per hour, multiply the number of milliwatts by 0.0034121416. One milliwatt equals 0.0034121416 BTU per hour, and one BTU per hour equals 293.07107 milliwatts. The table shows common values.

Milliwatts to BTU per Hour formula

Btu per hour = milliwatts × 0.0034121416

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliwatts to BTU per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0034121416 = 0.04265177 BTU/h. To go the other way, multiply BTU per hour by 293.07107 (or divide by 0.0034121416).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
