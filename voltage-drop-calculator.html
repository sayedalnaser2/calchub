Voltage Drop Calculator

Estimate voltage drop in a copper wire run.

How it works

Drop = 2 x length x resistance per foot x current. Copper wire resistance depends on gauge. Aim for 3% or less.

Frequently asked questions

Why use thicker wire?

It has lower resistance, so less drop and heat.
