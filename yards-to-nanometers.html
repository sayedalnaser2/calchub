Yards to Nanometers Converter

Convert yards to nanometers instantly. 1 yd = 914,400,000 nm.

How it works

To convert yards to nanometers, multiply the number of yards by 914,400,000. One yard equals 914,400,000 nanometers, and one nanometer equals 1.09361e-09 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = yards × 914,400,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 914,400,000 = 11,430,000,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 1.09361e-09 (or divide by 914,400,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
