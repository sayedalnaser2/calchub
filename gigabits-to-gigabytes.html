Gigabits to Gigabytes Converter

Convert gigabits to gigabytes instantly. 1 Gb = 0.125 GB.

How it works

To convert gigabits to gigabytes, multiply the number of gigabits by 0.125. One gigabit equals 0.125 gigabytes, and one gigabyte equals 8 gigabits. The table shows common values.

Gigabits to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = gigabits × 0.125

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabits to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.125 = 1.5625 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 8 (or divide by 0.125).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
