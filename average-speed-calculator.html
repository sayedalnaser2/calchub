Average Speed Calculator (Two Legs)

Calculate the true average speed for a trip made at two different speeds.

How it works

Average speed is total distance divided by total time, not the average of the speeds. Driving the same distance at 30 and 60 gives 40 average, because more time is spent at the slow speed.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't it just (30+60)/2?

Because you spend more time at the lower speed.

What if times are equal instead?

Then the simple average of the speeds is correct.
