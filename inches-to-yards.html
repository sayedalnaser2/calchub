How it works

To convert inches to yards, multiply the number of inches by 0.027777778. To convert yards to inches, multiply by 36 (or divide by 0.027777778). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Inches to Yards formula

Yards = inches × 0.027777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.027777778 = 0.34722222 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 36 (or divide by 0.027777778).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
