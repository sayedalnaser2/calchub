How it works

To convert metric tons to ounces, multiply the number of metric tons by 35273.962. One metric ton equals 35273.962 ounces, and one ounce equals 2.83495e-05 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Ounces formula

Ounces = metric tons × 35273.962

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 35273.962 = 440924.52 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 2.83495e-05 (or divide by 35273.962).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
