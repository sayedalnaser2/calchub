Molarity Calculator

Calculate molarity from mass, molar mass and volume.

How it works

Molarity = moles of solute / liters of solution, where moles = mass / molar mass.

Frequently asked questions

Molarity vs molality?

Molarity uses solution volume; molality uses solvent mass.
