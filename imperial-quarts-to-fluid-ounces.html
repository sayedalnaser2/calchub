Convert imperial quarts to fluid ounces instantly. 1 imp qt = 38.430398 fl oz.

How it works

To convert imperial quarts to fluid ounces, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 38.430398. One imperial quart equals 38.430398 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.026021068 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = imperial quarts × 38.430398

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 38.430398 = 480.37997 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.026021068 (or divide by 38.430398).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
