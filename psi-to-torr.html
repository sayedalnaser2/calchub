How it works

To convert psi to torr, multiply the number of psi by 51.714933. One psi equals 51.714933 torr, and one torr equals 0.019336775 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Torr formula

Torr = psi × 51.714933

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 51.714933 = 646.43666 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 0.019336775 (or divide by 51.714933).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
