Square Centimeters to Square Inches Converter

Convert square centimeters to square inches instantly. 1 cm² = 0.15500031 in².

How it works

To convert square centimeters to square inches, multiply the number of square centimeters by 0.15500031. One square centimeter equals 0.15500031 square inches, and one square inch equals 6.4516 square centimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Centimeters to Square Inches formula

Square inches = square centimeters × 0.15500031

Worked example: convert 12.5 square centimeters to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.15500031 = 1.9375039 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 6.4516 (or divide by 0.15500031).
