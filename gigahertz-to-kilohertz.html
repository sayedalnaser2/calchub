Gigahertz to Kilohertz Converter

Convert gigahertz to kilohertz instantly. 1 GHz = 1,000,000 kHz.

How it works

To convert gigahertz to kilohertz, multiply the number of gigahertz by 1,000,000. One gigahertz equals 1,000,000 kilohertz, and one kilohertz equals 1e-06 gigahertz. The table shows common values.

Gigahertz to Kilohertz formula

Kilohertz = gigahertz × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigahertz to kilohertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 kHz. To go the other way, multiply kilohertz by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
