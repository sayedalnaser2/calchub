How it works

To convert teaspoons to imperial pints, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.0086736894. One teaspoon equals 0.0086736894 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 115.29119 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = teaspoons × 0.0086736894

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0086736894 = 0.10842112 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 115.29119 (or divide by 0.0086736894).
