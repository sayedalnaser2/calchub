Square Kilometers to Square Centimeters Converter

Convert square kilometers to square centimeters instantly. 1 km² = 10,000,000,000 cm².

How it works

To convert square kilometers to square centimeters, multiply the number of square kilometers by 10,000,000,000. One square kilometer equals 10,000,000,000 square centimeters, and one square centimeter equals 1e-10 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square kilometers × 10,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 10,000,000,000 = 125,000,000,000 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 1e-10 (or divide by 10,000,000,000).
