How it works

To convert cubic inches to imperial quarts, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.014418601. To convert imperial quarts to cubic inches, multiply by 69.354858 (or divide by 0.014418601). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Inches to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = cubic inches × 0.014418601

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
