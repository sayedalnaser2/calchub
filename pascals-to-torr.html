How it works

To convert pascals to torr, multiply the number of pascals by 0.0075006168. To convert torr to pascals, multiply by 133.32237 (or divide by 0.0075006168). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pascals to Torr formula

Torr = pascals × 0.0075006168

Worked example: convert 12.5 pascals to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0075006168 = 0.09375771 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 133.32237 (or divide by 0.0075006168).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
