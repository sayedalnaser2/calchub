Nautical Miles to Centimeters Converter

Convert nautical miles to centimeters instantly. 1 nmi = 185,200 cm.

How it works

To convert nautical miles to centimeters, multiply the number of nautical miles by 185,200. One nautical mile equals 185,200 centimeters, and one centimeter equals 5.39957e-06 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = nautical miles × 185,200

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 185,200 = 2,315,000 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 5.39957e-06 (or divide by 185,200).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
