Fluid Ounces to Gallons Converter

Convert fluid ounces to gallons instantly. 1 fl oz = 0.0078125 gal.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to gallons, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 0.0078125. One fluid ounce equals 0.0078125 gallons, and one gallon equals 128 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Gallons formula

Gallons = fluid ounces × 0.0078125

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0078125 = 0.09765625 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 128 (or divide by 0.0078125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
