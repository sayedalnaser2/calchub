Convert imperial pints to quarts instantly. 1 imp pt = 0.60047496 qt.

How it works

To convert imperial pints to quarts, multiply the number of imperial pints by 0.60047496. One imperial pint equals 0.60047496 quarts, and one quart equals 1.6653484 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Quarts formula

Quarts = imperial pints × 0.60047496

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.60047496 = 7.505937 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 1.6653484 (or divide by 0.60047496).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
