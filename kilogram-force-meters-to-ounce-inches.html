How it works

To convert kilogram-force meters to ounce-inches, multiply the number of kilogram-force meters by 1,388.7387. One kilogram-force meter equals 1,388.7387 ounce-inches, and one ounce-inch equals 0.00072007788 kilogram-force meters. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force Meters to Ounce-Inches formula

Ounce-inches = kilogram-force meters × 1,388.7387

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
