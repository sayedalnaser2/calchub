How it works

To convert imperial gallons to pints, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 9.6075994. One imperial gallon equals 9.6075994 pints, and one pint equals 0.10408427 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Pints formula

Pints = imperial gallons × 9.6075994

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 9.6075994 = 120.09499 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 0.10408427 (or divide by 9.6075994).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
