How it works

To convert imperial gallons to gallons, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 1.2009499. One imperial gallon equals 1.2009499 gallons, and one gallon equals 0.83267418 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons = imperial gallons × 1.2009499

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
