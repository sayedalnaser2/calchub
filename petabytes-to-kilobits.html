Petabytes to Kilobits Converter

Convert petabytes to kilobits instantly. 1 PB = 8,000,000,000,000 Kb.

How it works

To convert petabytes to kilobits, multiply the number of petabytes by 8,000,000,000,000. One petabyte equals 8,000,000,000,000 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 1.25e-13 petabytes. The table shows common values.

Petabytes to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = petabytes × 8,000,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 petabytes to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,000,000,000,000 = 100,000,000,000,000 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 1.25e-13 (or divide by 8,000,000,000,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
