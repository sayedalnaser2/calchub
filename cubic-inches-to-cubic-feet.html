How it works

To convert cubic inches to cubic feet, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.0005787037. To convert cubic feet to cubic inches, multiply by 1,728 (or divide by 0.0005787037). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Inches to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = cubic inches × 0.0005787037

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0005787037 = 0.0072337963 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 1,728 (or divide by 0.0005787037).
