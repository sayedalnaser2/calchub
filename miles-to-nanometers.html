Miles to Nanometers Converter

Convert miles to nanometers instantly. 1 mi = 1,609,344,000,000 nm.

How it works

To convert miles to nanometers, multiply the number of miles by 1,609,344,000,000. One mile equals 1,609,344,000,000 nanometers, and one nanometer equals 6.21371e-13 miles. The table shows common values.

Miles to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = miles × 1,609,344,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1,609,344,000,000 = 20,116,800,000,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 6.21371e-13 (or divide by 1,609,344,000,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
