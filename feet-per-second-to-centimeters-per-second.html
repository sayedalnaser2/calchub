Feet per Second to Centimeters per Second Converter

Convert feet per second to centimeters per second instantly. 1 ft/s = 30.48 cm/s.

How it works

To convert feet per second to centimeters per second, multiply the number of feet per second by 30.48. One foot per second equals 30.48 centimeters per second, and one centimeter per second equals 0.032808399 feet per second. The table shows common values.

Feet per Second to Centimeters per Second formula

Centimeters per second = feet per second × 30.48

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet per second to centimeters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 30.48 = 381 cm/s. To go the other way, multiply centimeters per second by 0.032808399 (or divide by 30.48).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
