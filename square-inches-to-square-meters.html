Convert square inches to square meters instantly. 1 in² = 0.00064516 m².

How it works

To convert square inches to square meters, multiply the number of square inches by 0.00064516. One square inch equals 0.00064516 square meters, and one square meter equals 1,550.0031 square inches. The table shows common values.

Square Inches to Square Meters formula

Square meters = square inches × 0.00064516

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00064516 = 0.0080645 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 1,550.0031 (or divide by 0.00064516).
