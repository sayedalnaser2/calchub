Fence Calculator

Estimate posts, rails and pickets for a fence.

How it works

Posts = sections + 1. Pickets = fence length in inches / (picket width + gap). Rails = sections x rails per section.

Frequently asked questions

How far apart should posts be?

6-8 feet is common for wood fences.
