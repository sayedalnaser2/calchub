Megabytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert megabytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 MB = 1000 KB.

How it works

To convert megabytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of megabytes by 1000. One megabyte equals 1000 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 0.001 megabytes. The table shows common values.

Megabytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = megabytes × 1000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1000 = 12500 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 0.001 (or divide by 1000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
