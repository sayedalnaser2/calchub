Megabytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert megabytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 MB = 1,000 KB.

How it works

To convert megabytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of megabytes by 1,000. One megabyte equals 1,000 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 0.001 megabytes. The table shows common values.

Megabytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = megabytes × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
