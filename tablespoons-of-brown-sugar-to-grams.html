Convert tablespoons of brown sugar (packed) to grams. 1 cup of brown sugar (packed) weighs about 213 g.

How it works

Brown Sugar (packed) is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 213 g per US cup, convert tablespoons to grams by multiplying by 13.31.
