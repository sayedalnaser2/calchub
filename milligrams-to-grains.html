How it works

To convert milligrams to grains, multiply the number of milligrams by 0.015432358. One milligram equals 0.015432358 grains, and one grain equals 64.79891 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Grains formula

Grains = milligrams × 0.015432358

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 0.015432358 = 0.19290448 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 64.79891 (or divide by 0.015432358).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
