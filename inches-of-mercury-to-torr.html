How it works

To convert inches of mercury to torr, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 25.400006. To convert torr to inches of mercury, multiply by 0.039370069 (or divide by 25.400006). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Inches of Mercury to Torr formula

Torr = inches of mercury × 25.400006

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 25.400006 = 317.50008 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 0.039370069 (or divide by 25.400006).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
