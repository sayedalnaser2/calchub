Gigajoules to Joules Converter

Convert gigajoules to joules instantly. 1 GJ = 1,000,000,000 J.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to joules, multiply the number of gigajoules by 1,000,000,000. One gigajoule equals 1,000,000,000 joules, and one joule equals 1e-09 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Joules formula

Joules = gigajoules × 1,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 1e-09 (or divide by 1,000,000,000).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
