Nanometers to Micrometers Converter

Convert nanometers to micrometers instantly. 1 nm = 0.001 µm.

How it works

To convert nanometers to micrometers, multiply the number of nanometers by 0.001. One nanometer equals 0.001 micrometers, and one micrometer equals 1,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = nanometers × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
