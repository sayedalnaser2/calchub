Square Millimeters to Square Meters Converter

Convert square millimeters to square meters instantly. 1 mm² = 1e-06 m².

How it works

To convert square millimeters to square meters, multiply the number of square millimeters by 1e-06. One square millimeter equals 1e-06 square meters, and one square meter equals 1,000,000 square millimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Millimeters to Square Meters formula

Square meters = square millimeters × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 square millimeters to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).
