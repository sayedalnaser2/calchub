Watt Hours to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert watt hours to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 Wh = 0.001 kWh.

How it works

To convert watt hours to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of watt hours by 0.001. One watt hour equals 0.001 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 1,000 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = watt hours × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
