Gallons per Minute to Cubic Meters per Second Converter

Convert gallons per minute to cubic meters per second instantly. 1 gal/min = 6.30902e-05 m³/s.

How it works

To convert gallons per minute to cubic meters per second, multiply the number of gallons per minute by 6.30902e-05. One gallon per minute equals 6.30902e-05 cubic meters per second, and one cubic meter per second equals 15,850.323 gallons per minute. The table shows common values.

Gallons per Minute to Cubic Meters per Second formula

Cubic meters per second = gallons per minute × 6.30902e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons per minute to cubic meters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 6.30902e-05 = 0.00078862745 m³/s. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters per second by 15,850.323 (or divide by 6.30902e-05).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
