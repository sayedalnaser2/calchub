How it works

To convert gallons per minute to cubic meters per second, multiply the number of gallons per minute by 6.30902e-05. To convert cubic meters per second to gallons per minute, multiply by 15,850.323 (or divide by 6.30902e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
