Kilopascals to Pascals Converter

Convert kilopascals to pascals instantly. 1 kPa = 1000 Pa.

How it works

To convert kilopascals to pascals, multiply the number of kilopascals by 1000. One kilopascal equals 1000 pascals, and one pascal equals 0.001 kilopascals. The table shows common values.

Kilopascals to Pascals formula

Pascals = kilopascals × 1000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 1000 = 12500 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 0.001 (or divide by 1000).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
