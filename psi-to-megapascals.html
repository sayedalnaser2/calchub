PSI to Megapascals Converter

Convert psi to megapascals instantly. 1 psi = 0.0068947573 MPa.

How it works

To convert psi to megapascals, multiply the number of psi by 0.0068947573. One psi equals 0.0068947573 megapascals, and one megapascal equals 145.03774 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = psi × 0.0068947573

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0068947573 = 0.086184466 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 145.03774 (or divide by 0.0068947573).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
