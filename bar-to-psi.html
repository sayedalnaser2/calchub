Bar to PSI Converter

Convert bar to psi instantly. 1 bar = 14.503774 psi.

How it works

To convert bar to psi, multiply the number of bar by 14.503774. One bar equals 14.503774 psi, and one psi equals 0.068947573 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to PSI formula

Psi = bar × 14.503774

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to psi. Multiply 12.5 × 14.503774 = 181.29717 psi. To go the other way, multiply psi by 0.068947573 (or divide by 14.503774).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
