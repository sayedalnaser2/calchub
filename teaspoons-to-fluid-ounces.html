How it works

To convert teaspoons to fluid ounces, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.16666667. To convert fluid ounces to teaspoons, multiply by 6 (or divide by 0.16666667). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Teaspoons to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = teaspoons × 0.16666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 0.16666667 = 2.0833333 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 6 (or divide by 0.16666667).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
