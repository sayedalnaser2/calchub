Kibibytes to Gibibytes Converter

Convert kibibytes to gibibytes instantly. 1 KiB = 9.53674e-07 GiB.

How it works

To convert kibibytes to gibibytes, multiply the number of kibibytes by 9.53674e-07. One kibibyte equals 9.53674e-07 gibibytes, and one gibibyte equals 1,048,576 kibibytes. The table shows common values.

Kibibytes to Gibibytes formula

Gibibytes = kibibytes × 9.53674e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 kibibytes to gibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 9.53674e-07 = 1.19209e-05 GiB. To go the other way, multiply gibibytes by 1,048,576 (or divide by 9.53674e-07).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
