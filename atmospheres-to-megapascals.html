Atmospheres to Megapascals Converter

Convert atmospheres to megapascals instantly. 1 atm = 0.101325 MPa.

How it works

To convert atmospheres to megapascals, multiply the number of atmospheres by 0.101325. One atmosphere equals 0.101325 megapascals, and one megapascal equals 9.8692327 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Atmospheres to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = atmospheres × 0.101325

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.101325 = 1.2665625 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 9.8692327 (or divide by 0.101325).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
