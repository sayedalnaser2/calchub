How it works

To convert cups to imperial gallons, multiply the number of cups by 0.052042137. To convert imperial gallons to cups, multiply by 19.215199 (or divide by 0.052042137). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cups to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = cups × 0.052042137

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
