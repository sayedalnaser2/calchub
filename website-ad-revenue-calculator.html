Website Ad Revenue Calculator (RPM)

Estimate monthly and yearly ad income from page views and RPM.

How it works

Revenue = page views / 1000 x RPM. RPM varies enormously by niche, country and ad network, from under $2 to over $30 for finance topics.

Frequently asked questions

What RPM should I assume?

Start conservatively with $2-$10 and replace it with your real figure once ads run.

Do page views equal sessions?

No. RPM is usually quoted per 1,000 page views; check your ad network's definition.
