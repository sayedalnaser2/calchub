Fluid Ounces to Liters Converter

Convert fluid ounces to liters instantly. 1 fl oz = 0.02957353 L.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to liters, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 0.02957353. One fluid ounce equals 0.02957353 liters, and one liter equals 33.814023 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Liters formula

Liters = fluid ounces × 0.02957353

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.02957353 = 0.36966912 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 33.814023 (or divide by 0.02957353).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
