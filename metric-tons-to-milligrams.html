How it works

To convert metric tons to milligrams, multiply the number of metric tons by 1e+09. One metric ton equals 1e+09 milligrams, and one milligram equals 1e-09 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = metric tons × 1e+09

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 1e+09 = 1.25e+10 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 1e-09 (or divide by 1e+09).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
