Metric Tons to Milligrams Converter

Convert metric tons to milligrams instantly. 1 t = 1,000,000,000 mg.

How it works

To convert metric tons to milligrams, multiply the number of metric tons by 1,000,000,000. One metric ton equals 1,000,000,000 milligrams, and one milligram equals 1e-09 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = metric tons × 1,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 1e-09 (or divide by 1,000,000,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
