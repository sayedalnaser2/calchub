How it works

To convert inches of mercury to millibars, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 33.86389. To convert millibars to inches of mercury, multiply by 0.02952998 (or divide by 33.86389). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Inches of Mercury to Millibars formula

Millibars = inches of mercury × 33.86389

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 33.86389 = 423.29862 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.02952998 (or divide by 33.86389).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
