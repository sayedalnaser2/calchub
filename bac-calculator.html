Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) Calculator

Estimate BAC from drinks, body weight and time.

How it works

The Widmark formula divides alcohol consumed by body water, then subtracts about 0.015% per hour. It is only an estimate. Never drive after drinking.

Frequently asked questions

Is this safe to rely on for driving?

No. Many factors affect BAC. If you have been drinking, do not drive.
