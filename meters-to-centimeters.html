Meters to Centimeters Converter

Convert meters to centimeters instantly. 1 m = 100 cm.

How it works

To convert meters to centimeters, multiply the number of meters by 100. One meter equals 100 centimeters, and one centimeter equals 0.01 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = meters × 100

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 100 = 1,250 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 0.01 (or divide by 100).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
