Months to Weeks Converter

Convert months to weeks instantly. 1 month = 4.348125 weeks.

How it works

To convert months to weeks, multiply the number of months by 4.348125. One month equals 4.348125 weeks, and one week equals 0.22998419 months. The table shows common values.

Months to Weeks formula

Weeks = months × 4.348125

Worked example: convert 12.5 months to weeks. Multiply 12.5 × 4.348125 = 54.351562 weeks. To go the other way, multiply weeks by 0.22998419 (or divide by 4.348125).
