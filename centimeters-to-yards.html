Centimeters to Yards Converter

Convert centimeters to yards instantly. 1 cm = 0.010936133 yd.

How it works

To convert centimeters to yards, multiply the number of centimeters by 0.010936133. One centimeter equals 0.010936133 yards, and one yard equals 91.44 centimeters. The table shows common values.

Centimeters to Yards formula

Yards = centimeters × 0.010936133

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.010936133 = 0.13670166 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 91.44 (or divide by 0.010936133).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
