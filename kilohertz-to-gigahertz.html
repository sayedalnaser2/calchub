Kilohertz to Gigahertz Converter

Convert kilohertz to gigahertz instantly. 1 kHz = 1e-06 GHz.

How it works

To convert kilohertz to gigahertz, multiply the number of kilohertz by 1e-06. One kilohertz equals 1e-06 gigahertz, and one gigahertz equals 1,000,000 kilohertz. The table shows common values.

Kilohertz to Gigahertz formula

Gigahertz = kilohertz × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilohertz to gigahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 GHz. To go the other way, multiply gigahertz by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
