How it works

To convert torr to inches of mercury, multiply the number of torr by 0.039370069. One torr equals 0.039370069 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 25.400006 torr. The table shows common values.

Torr to Inches Of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = torr × 0.039370069

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 0.039370069 = 0.49212586 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 25.400006 (or divide by 0.039370069).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
