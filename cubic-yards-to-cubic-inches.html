Cubic Yards to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert cubic yards to cubic inches instantly. 1 yd³ = 46,656 in³.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to cubic inches, multiply the number of cubic yards by 46,656. One cubic yard equals 46,656 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 2.14335e-05 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = cubic yards × 46,656

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 46,656 = 583,200 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 2.14335e-05 (or divide by 46,656).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
