Metric Horsepower to Milliwatts Converter

Convert metric horsepower to milliwatts instantly. 1 PS = 735,498.75 mW.

How it works

To convert metric horsepower to milliwatts, multiply the number of metric horsepower by 735,498.75. One metric horsepower equals 735,498.75 milliwatts, and one milliwatt equals 1.35962e-06 metric horsepower. The table shows common values.

Metric Horsepower to Milliwatts formula

Milliwatts = metric horsepower × 735,498.75

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric horsepower to milliwatts. Multiply 12.5 × 735,498.75 = 9,193,734.4 mW. To go the other way, multiply milliwatts by 1.35962e-06 (or divide by 735,498.75).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
