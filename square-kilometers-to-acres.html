Square Kilometers to Acres Converter

Convert square kilometers to acres instantly. 1 km² = 247.10538 acres.

How it works

To convert square kilometers to acres, multiply the number of square kilometers by 247.10538. One square kilometer equals 247.10538 acres, and one acre equals 0.0040468564 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Acres formula

Acres = square kilometers × 247.10538

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 247.10538 = 3,088.8173 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 0.0040468564 (or divide by 247.10538).
