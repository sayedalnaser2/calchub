Convert mebibytes to gibibytes instantly. 1 MiB = 0.0009765625 GiB.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to gibibytes, multiply the number of mebibytes by 0.0009765625. One mebibyte equals 0.0009765625 gibibytes, and one gibibyte equals 1,024 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Gibibytes formula

Gibibytes = mebibytes × 0.0009765625

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to gibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0009765625 = 0.012207031 GiB. To go the other way, multiply gibibytes by 1,024 (or divide by 0.0009765625).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
