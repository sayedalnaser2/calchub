How it works

To convert gallons to imperial quarts, multiply the number of gallons by 3.3306967. To convert imperial quarts to gallons, multiply by 0.30023748 (or divide by 3.3306967). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
