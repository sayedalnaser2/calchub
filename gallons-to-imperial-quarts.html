How it works

To convert gallons to imperial quarts, multiply the number of gallons by 3.3306967. One gallon equals 3.3306967 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 0.30023748 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = gallons × 3.3306967

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 3.3306967 = 41.633709 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 0.30023748 (or divide by 3.3306967).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
