Torr to Atmospheres Converter

Convert torr to atmospheres instantly. 1 Torr = 0.0013157895 atm.

How it works

To convert torr to atmospheres, multiply the number of torr by 0.0013157895. One torr equals 0.0013157895 atmospheres, and one atmosphere equals 760 torr. The table shows common values.

Torr to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = torr × 0.0013157895

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to atmospheres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013157895 = 0.016447368 atm. To go the other way, multiply atmospheres by 760 (or divide by 0.0013157895).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
