Cubic Meters per Second to Liters per Second Converter

Convert cubic meters per second to liters per second instantly. 1 m³/s = 1,000 L/s.

How it works

To convert cubic meters per second to liters per second, multiply the number of cubic meters per second by 1,000. One cubic meter per second equals 1,000 liters per second, and one liter per second equals 0.001 cubic meters per second. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters per Second to Liters per Second formula

Liters per second = cubic meters per second × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters per second to liters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 L/s. To go the other way, multiply liters per second by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
