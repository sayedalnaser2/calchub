How it works

Volume = pi x r^2 x h / 3, one third of a cylinder with the same base and height. Slant height = sqrt(r^2 + h^2). Lateral area = pi x r x slant height.

Formula

V = pi x r squared x h / 3. Enter any two of radius, height and volume.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a cone one third of a cylinder?

It follows from integral calculus and can be shown by filling a cone with water three times.

What is slant height?

The distance along the surface from the apex to the base edge.
