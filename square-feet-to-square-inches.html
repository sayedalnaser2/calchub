How it works

To convert square feet to square inches, multiply the number of square feet by 144. One square foot equals 144 square inches, and one square inch equals 0.0069444444 square feet. The table shows common values.

Square Feet to Square Inches formula

Square inches = square feet × 144

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 144 = 1,800 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 0.0069444444 (or divide by 144).
