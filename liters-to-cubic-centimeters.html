Liters to Cubic Centimeters Converter

Convert liters to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 L = 1,000 cm³.

How it works

To convert liters to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of liters by 1,000. One liter equals 1,000 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.001 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = liters × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
