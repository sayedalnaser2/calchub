How it works

To convert liters to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of liters by 1,000. To convert cubic centimeters to liters, multiply by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Liters to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = liters × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
