Liters per Minute to Liters per Second Converter

Convert liters per minute to liters per second instantly. 1 L/min = 0.016666667 L/s.

How it works

To convert liters per minute to liters per second, multiply the number of liters per minute by 0.016666667. One liter per minute equals 0.016666667 liters per second, and one liter per second equals 60 liters per minute. The table shows common values.

Liters per Minute to Liters per Second formula

Liters per second = liters per minute × 0.016666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per minute to liters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016666667 = 0.20833333 L/s. To go the other way, multiply liters per second by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
