Inches Of Mercury to Kilopascals Converter

Convert inches of mercury to kilopascals instantly. 1 inHg = 3.386389 kPa.

How it works

To convert inches of mercury to kilopascals, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 3.386389. One inch of mercury equals 3.386389 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 0.2952998 inches of mercury. The table shows common values.

Inches Of Mercury to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = inches of mercury × 3.386389

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 3.386389 = 42.329863 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 0.2952998 (or divide by 3.386389).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
