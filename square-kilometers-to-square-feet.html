Square Kilometers to Square Feet Converter

Convert square kilometers to square feet instantly. 1 km² = 10,763,910 ft².

How it works

To convert square kilometers to square feet, multiply the number of square kilometers by 10,763,910. One square kilometer equals 10,763,910 square feet, and one square foot equals 9.2903e-08 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Square Feet formula

Square feet = square kilometers × 10,763,910

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to square feet. Multiply 12.5 × 10,763,910 = 134,548,880 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 9.2903e-08 (or divide by 10,763,910).
