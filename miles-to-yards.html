Miles to Yards Converter

Convert miles to yards instantly. 1 mi = 1,760 yd.

How it works

To convert miles to yards, multiply the number of miles by 1,760. One mile equals 1,760 yards, and one yard equals 0.00056818182 miles. The table shows common values.

Miles to Yards formula

Yards = miles × 1,760

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1,760 = 22,000 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 0.00056818182 (or divide by 1,760).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
