Half Marathon Pace for a 2:45:00 Finish Time

To run a Half Marathon in 2:45:00 you need 12:35 per mile (7:49 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Half Marathon is 21.0975 km (13.11 miles), so 2:45:00 works out to 12:35 per mile or 7:49 per kilometer.
