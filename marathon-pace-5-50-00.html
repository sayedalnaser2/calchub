Marathon Pace for a 5:50:00 Finish Time

To run a Marathon in 5:50:00 you need 13:21 per mile (8:18 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Marathon is 42.195 km (26.22 miles), so 5:50:00 works out to 13:21 per mile or 8:18 per kilometer.
