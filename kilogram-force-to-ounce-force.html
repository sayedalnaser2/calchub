How it works

To convert kilogram-force to ounce-force, multiply the number of kilogram-force by 35.273962. One kilogram-force equals 35.273962 ounce-force, and one ounce-force equals 0.028349523 kilogram-force. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force to Ounce-Force formula

Ounce-force = kilogram-force × 35.273962

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force to ounce-force. Multiply 12.5 × 35.273962 = 440.92453 ozf. To go the other way, multiply ounce-force by 0.028349523 (or divide by 35.273962).
