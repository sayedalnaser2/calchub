Kilocalories to Joules Converter

Convert kilocalories to joules instantly. 1 kcal = 4184 J.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to joules, multiply the number of kilocalories by 4184. One kilocalorie equals 4184 joules, and one joule equals 0.00023900574 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Joules formula

Joules = kilocalories × 4184

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 4184 = 52300 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 0.00023900574 (or divide by 4184).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
