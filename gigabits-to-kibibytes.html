Gigabits to Kibibytes Converter

Convert gigabits to kibibytes instantly. 1 Gb = 122,070.31 KiB.

How it works

To convert gigabits to kibibytes, multiply the number of gigabits by 122,070.31. One gigabit equals 122,070.31 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 8.192e-06 gigabits. The table shows common values.

Gigabits to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = gigabits × 122,070.31

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabits to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 122,070.31 = 1,525,878.9 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 8.192e-06 (or divide by 122,070.31).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
