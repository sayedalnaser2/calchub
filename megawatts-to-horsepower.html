Megawatts to Horsepower Converter

Convert megawatts to horsepower instantly. 1 MW = 1,341.0221 hp.

How it works

To convert megawatts to horsepower, multiply the number of megawatts by 1,341.0221. One megawatt equals 1,341.0221 horsepower, and one horsepower equals 0.00074569987 megawatts. The table shows common values.

Megawatts to Horsepower formula

Horsepower = megawatts × 1,341.0221

Worked example: convert 12.5 megawatts to horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 1,341.0221 = 16,762.776 hp. To go the other way, multiply horsepower by 0.00074569987 (or divide by 1,341.0221).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
