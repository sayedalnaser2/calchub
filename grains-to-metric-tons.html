How it works

To convert grains to metric tons, multiply the number of grains by 6.47989e-08. To convert metric tons to grains, multiply by 15,432,358 (or divide by 6.47989e-08). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
