Grains to Metric Tons Converter

Convert grains to metric tons instantly. 1 gr = 6.47989e-08 t.

How it works

To convert grains to metric tons, multiply the number of grains by 6.47989e-08. One grain equals 6.47989e-08 metric tons, and one metric ton equals 15,432,358 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Metric Tons formula

Metric tons = grains × 6.47989e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to metric tons. Multiply 12.5 × 6.47989e-08 = 8.09986e-07 t. To go the other way, multiply metric tons by 15,432,358 (or divide by 6.47989e-08).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
