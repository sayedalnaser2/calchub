How it works

To convert ounces to long tons, multiply the number of ounces by 2.79018e-05. One ounce equals 2.79018e-05 long tons, and one long ton equals 35,840 ounces. The table shows common values.

Ounces to Long Tons formula

Long tons = ounces × 2.79018e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to long tons. Multiply 12.5 × 2.79018e-05 = 0.00034877232 long tons. To go the other way, multiply long tons by 35,840 (or divide by 2.79018e-05).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
