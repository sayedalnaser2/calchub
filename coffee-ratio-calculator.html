Coffee to Water Ratio Calculator

Find how much coffee to use for a given amount of water.

How it works

Coffee weight = water weight / ratio (1 ml of water weighs about 1 g). The Specialty Coffee Association recommends roughly 1:15 to 1:18. One level tablespoon of ground coffee is about 5 g.

Frequently asked questions

What ratio does a French press use?

Around 1:12 to 1:15.

Why weigh coffee?

Scoops vary with grind and roast; weight is consistent.
