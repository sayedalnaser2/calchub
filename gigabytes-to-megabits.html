Gigabytes to Megabits Converter

Convert gigabytes to megabits instantly. 1 GB = 8,000 Mb.

How it works

To convert gigabytes to megabits, multiply the number of gigabytes by 8,000. One gigabyte equals 8,000 megabits, and one megabit equals 0.000125 gigabytes. The table shows common values.

Gigabytes to Megabits formula

Megabits = gigabytes × 8,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabytes to megabits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,000 = 100,000 Mb. To go the other way, multiply megabits by 0.000125 (or divide by 8,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
