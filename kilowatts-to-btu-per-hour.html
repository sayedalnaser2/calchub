Convert kilowatts to BTU per hour instantly. 1 kW = 3412.1416 BTU/h.

How it works

To convert kilowatts to BTU per hour, multiply the number of kilowatts by 3412.1416. One kilowatt equals 3412.1416 BTU per hour, and one BTU per hour equals 0.00029307107 kilowatts. The table shows common values.

Kilowatts to BTU per Hour formula

Btu per hour = kilowatts × 3412.1416

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatts to BTU per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 3412.1416 = 42651.77 BTU/h. To go the other way, multiply BTU per hour by 0.00029307107 (or divide by 3412.1416).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
