Micrometers to Yards Converter

Convert micrometers to yards instantly. 1 µm = 1.09361e-06 yd.

How it works

To convert micrometers to yards, multiply the number of micrometers by 1.09361e-06. One micrometer equals 1.09361e-06 yards, and one yard equals 914,400 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Yards formula

Yards = micrometers × 1.09361e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1.09361e-06 = 1.36702e-05 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 914,400 (or divide by 1.09361e-06).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
