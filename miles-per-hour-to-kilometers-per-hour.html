How it works

To convert miles per hour to kilometers per hour, multiply the number of miles per hour by 1.609344. To convert kilometers per hour to miles per hour, multiply by 0.62137119 (or divide by 1.609344). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles per Hour to Kilometers per Hour formula

Kilometers per hour = miles per hour × 1.609344

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles per hour to kilometers per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 1.609344 = 20.1168 km/h. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per hour by 0.62137119 (or divide by 1.609344).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
