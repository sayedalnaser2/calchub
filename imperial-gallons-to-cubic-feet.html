Imperial Gallons to Cubic Feet Converter

Convert imperial gallons to cubic feet instantly. 1 imp gal = 0.16054365 ft³.

How it works

To convert imperial gallons to cubic feet, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 0.16054365. One imperial gallon equals 0.16054365 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 6.2288355 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = imperial gallons × 0.16054365

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.16054365 = 2.0067957 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 6.2288355 (or divide by 0.16054365).
