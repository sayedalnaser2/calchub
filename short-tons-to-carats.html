To convert short tons to carats, multiply the number of short tons by 4,535,923.7. One short ton equals 4,535,923.7 carats, and one carat equals 2.20462e-07 short tons. The table shows common values.

Carats = short tons × 4,535,923.7

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
