How it works

To convert mmHg to torr, multiply the number of mmHg by 1.0000001. One mmHg equals 1.0000001 torr, and one torr equals 0.99999986 mmHg. The table shows common values.

mmHg to Torr formula

Torr = mmHg × 1.0000001

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 1.0000001 = 12.500002 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 0.99999986 (or divide by 1.0000001).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
