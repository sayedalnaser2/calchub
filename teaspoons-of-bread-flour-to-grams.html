Teaspoons of Bread Flour to Grams

Convert teaspoons of bread flour to grams. 1 cup of bread flour weighs about 120 g.

How it works

Bread Flour is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 120 g per US cup, convert teaspoons to grams by multiplying by 2.5.

1 US cup of bread flour weighs about 120 g (4.2 oz), 1 tablespoon about 7.5 g and 1 teaspoon about 2.5 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
