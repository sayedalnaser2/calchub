Therms to Joules Converter

Convert therms to joules instantly. 1 therms = 105,480,400 J.

How it works

To convert therms to joules, multiply the number of therms by 105,480,400. One therm equals 105,480,400 joules, and one joule equals 9.48043e-09 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to Joules formula

Joules = therms × 105,480,400

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 105,480,400 = 1,318,505,000 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 9.48043e-09 (or divide by 105,480,400).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
