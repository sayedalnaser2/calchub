Convert acres to square meters instantly. 1 acres = 4,046.8564 m².

How it works

To convert acres to square meters, multiply the number of acres by 4,046.8564. One acre equals 4,046.8564 square meters, and one square meter equals 0.00024710538 acres. The table shows common values.

Acres to Square Meters formula

Square meters = acres × 4,046.8564

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 4,046.8564 = 50,585.705 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 0.00024710538 (or divide by 4,046.8564).
