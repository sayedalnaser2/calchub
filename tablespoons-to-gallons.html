How it works

To convert tablespoons to gallons, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.00390625. To convert gallons to tablespoons, multiply by 256 (or divide by 0.00390625). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Tablespoons to Gallons formula

Gallons = tablespoons × 0.00390625

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00390625 = 0.048828125 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 256 (or divide by 0.00390625).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
