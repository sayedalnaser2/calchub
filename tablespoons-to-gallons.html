Tablespoons to Gallons Converter

Convert tablespoons to gallons instantly. 1 tbsp = 0.00390625 gal.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to gallons, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.00390625. One tablespoon equals 0.00390625 gallons, and one gallon equals 256 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Gallons formula

Gallons = tablespoons × 0.00390625

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00390625 = 0.048828125 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 256 (or divide by 0.00390625).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
