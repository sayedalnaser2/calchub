Mach to Feet per Second Converter

Convert mach to feet per second instantly. 1 Mach = 1,116.437 ft/s.

How it works

To convert mach to feet per second, multiply the number of mach by 1,116.437. One Mach equals 1,116.437 feet per second, and one foot per second equals 0.0008957066 mach. The table shows common values.

Mach to Feet per Second formula

Feet per second = mach × 1,116.437

Worked example: convert 12.5 mach to feet per second. Multiply 12.5 × 1,116.437 = 13,955.463 ft/s. To go the other way, multiply feet per second by 0.0008957066 (or divide by 1,116.437).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
