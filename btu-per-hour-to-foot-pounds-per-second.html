BTU per Hour to Foot-Pounds per Second Converter

Convert BTU per hour to foot-pounds per second instantly. 1 BTU/h = 0.21615813 ft-lb/s.

How it works

To convert BTU per hour to foot-pounds per second, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 0.21615813. One BTU per hour equals 0.21615813 foot-pounds per second, and one foot-pound per second equals 4.6262429 BTU per hour. The table shows common values.

BTU per Hour to Foot-Pounds per Second formula

Foot-pounds per second = BTU per hour × 0.21615813

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU per hour to foot-pounds per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.21615813 = 2.7019766 ft-lb/s. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds per second by 4.6262429 (or divide by 0.21615813).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
