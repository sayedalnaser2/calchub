Gallons to Teaspoons Converter

Convert gallons to teaspoons instantly. 1 gal = 768 tsp.

How it works

To convert gallons to teaspoons, multiply the number of gallons by 768. One gallon equals 768 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.0013020833 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = gallons × 768

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 768 = 9600 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.0013020833 (or divide by 768).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
