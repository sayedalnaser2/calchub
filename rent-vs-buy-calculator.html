Rent vs Buy Calculator

Compare the net cost of renting with buying a home over a set number of years.

How it works

Net cost of buying is the down payment plus mortgage payments plus yearly tax, insurance and upkeep, minus the equity you hold at the end (future value less the remaining loan). Renting totals the rent, raised each year. It ignores tax benefits, selling costs and investment returns on the down payment, so treat it as a rough guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include closing and selling costs?

No. Selling costs are often 5-8% of the price and would favor renting over short periods.

Why compare over several years?

Buying usually pays off only after several years because of up-front and selling costs.
