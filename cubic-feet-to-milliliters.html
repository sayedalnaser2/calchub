How it works

To convert cubic feet to milliliters, multiply the number of cubic feet by 28,316.847. To convert milliliters to cubic feet, multiply by 3.53147e-05 (or divide by 28,316.847). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Feet to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = cubic feet × 28,316.847

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 28,316.847 = 353,960.58 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 3.53147e-05 (or divide by 28,316.847).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
