Nanometers to Fathoms Converter

Convert nanometers to fathoms instantly. 1 nm = 5.46807e-10 fathoms.

How it works

To convert nanometers to fathoms, multiply the number of nanometers by 5.46807e-10. One nanometer equals 5.46807e-10 fathoms, and one fathom equals 1,828,800,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = nanometers × 5.46807e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 5.46807e-10 = 6.83508e-09 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 1,828,800,000 (or divide by 5.46807e-10).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
