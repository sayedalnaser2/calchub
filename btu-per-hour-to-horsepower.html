BTU per Hour to Horsepower Converter

Convert BTU per hour to horsepower instantly. 1 BTU/h = 0.00039301478 hp.

How it works

To convert BTU per hour to horsepower, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 0.00039301478. One BTU per hour equals 0.00039301478 horsepower, and one horsepower equals 2,544.4336 BTU per hour. The table shows common values.

BTU per Hour to Horsepower formula

Horsepower = BTU per hour × 0.00039301478

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU per hour to horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00039301478 = 0.0049126847 hp. To go the other way, multiply horsepower by 2,544.4336 (or divide by 0.00039301478).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
