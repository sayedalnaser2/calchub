Parallelogram Area Calculator

Calculate parallelogram area from base and height, or from two sides and an angle.

How it works

Area = base x height. If you know two adjacent sides and the angle between them, area = a x b x sin(angle).

Frequently asked questions

Is a rectangle a parallelogram?

Yes, with a 90 degree angle.

Why use the perpendicular height?

The slanted side is longer than the height, which would overstate the area.
