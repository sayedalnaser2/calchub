How it works

To convert cubic yards to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cubic yards by 764,554.86. To convert cubic centimeters to cubic yards, multiply by 1.30795e-06 (or divide by 764,554.86). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Yards to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cubic yards × 764,554.86

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 764,554.86 = 9,556,935.7 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 1.30795e-06 (or divide by 764,554.86).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
