Cubic Yards to Cubic Centimeters Converter

Convert cubic yards to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 yd³ = 764,554.86 cm³.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cubic yards by 764,554.86. One cubic yard equals 764,554.86 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 1.30795e-06 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cubic yards × 764,554.86

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 764,554.86 = 9,556,935.7 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 1.30795e-06 (or divide by 764,554.86).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
