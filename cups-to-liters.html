How it works

To convert cups to liters, multiply the number of cups by 0.23658824. To convert liters to cups, multiply by 4.2267528 (or divide by 0.23658824). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cups to Liters formula

Liters = cups × 0.23658824

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.23658824 = 2.957353 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 4.2267528 (or divide by 0.23658824).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
