Liters to Imperial Pints Converter

Convert liters to imperial pints instantly. 1 L = 1.759754 imp pt.

How it works

To convert liters to imperial pints, multiply the number of liters by 1.759754. One liter equals 1.759754 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.56826125 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = liters × 1.759754

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 1.759754 = 21.996925 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.56826125 (or divide by 1.759754).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
