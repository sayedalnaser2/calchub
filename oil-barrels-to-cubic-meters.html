Oil Barrels to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert oil barrels to cubic meters instantly. 1 bbl = 0.15898729 m³.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to cubic meters, multiply the number of oil barrels by 0.15898729. One oil barrel equals 0.15898729 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 6.2898108 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = oil barrels × 0.15898729

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.15898729 = 1.9873412 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 6.2898108 (or divide by 0.15898729).
