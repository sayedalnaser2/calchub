How it works

To convert mils to centimeters, multiply the number of mils by 0.00254. To convert centimeters to mils, multiply by 393.70079 (or divide by 0.00254). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Mils to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = mils × 0.00254

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00254 = 0.03175 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 393.70079 (or divide by 0.00254).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
