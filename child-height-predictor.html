Child Height Predictor

Estimate adult height from parents' heights.

How it works

The mid-parental method averages parents' heights and adds 6.5 cm for boys or subtracts 6.5 cm for girls. Actual heights vary by about 8.5 cm either way.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

It is a population average, not a guarantee for one child.
