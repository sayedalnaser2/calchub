Bar to Kilopascals Converter

Convert bar to kilopascals instantly. 1 bar = 100 kPa.

How it works

To convert bar to kilopascals, multiply the number of bar by 100. One bar equals 100 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 0.01 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = bar × 100

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 100 = 1,250 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 0.01 (or divide by 100).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
