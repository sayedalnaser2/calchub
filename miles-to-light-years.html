How it works

To convert miles to light years, multiply the number of miles by 1.70108e-13. To convert light years to miles, multiply by 5,878,625,400,000 (or divide by 1.70108e-13). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles to Light Years formula

Light years = miles × 1.70108e-13

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 1.70108e-13 = 2.12635e-12 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 5,878,625,400,000 (or divide by 1.70108e-13).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
