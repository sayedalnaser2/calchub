Calories Burned Calculator

Estimate calories burned during exercise using MET values.

How it works

Calories = MET x 3.5 x body weight (kg) / 200 x minutes. MET values rate the energy cost of activities relative to rest.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

It is an average. Fitness, intensity and body composition change the real figure.
