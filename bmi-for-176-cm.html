BMI for 176 cm (5 ft 9.3 in)

Check your BMI at 176 cm and see the healthy weight range for this height.

How it works

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) squared. At 176 cm, a healthy BMI of 18.5-24.9 corresponds to 57-77 kg.

BMI is a screening tool. It does not measure body fat directly and can misclassify very muscular people, older adults and some ethnic groups. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy weight at 176 cm?
