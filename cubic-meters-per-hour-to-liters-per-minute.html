Cubic Meters per Hour to Liters per Minute Converter

Convert cubic meters per hour to liters per minute instantly. 1 m³/h = 16.666667 L/min.

How it works

To convert cubic meters per hour to liters per minute, multiply the number of cubic meters per hour by 16.666667. One cubic meter per hour equals 16.666667 liters per minute, and one liter per minute equals 0.06 cubic meters per hour. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters per Hour to Liters per Minute formula

Liters per minute = cubic meters per hour × 16.666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters per hour to liters per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 16.666667 = 208.33333 L/min. To go the other way, multiply liters per minute by 0.06 (or divide by 16.666667).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
