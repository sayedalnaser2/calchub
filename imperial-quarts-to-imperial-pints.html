Imperial Quarts to Imperial Pints Converter

Convert imperial quarts to imperial pints instantly. 1 imp qt = 2 imp pt.

How it works

To convert imperial quarts to imperial pints, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 2. One imperial quart equals 2 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.5 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = imperial quarts × 2

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 2 = 25 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.5 (or divide by 2).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
