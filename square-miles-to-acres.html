Square Miles to Acres Converter

Convert square miles to acres instantly. 1 mi² = 640 acres.

How it works

To convert square miles to acres, multiply the number of square miles by 640. One square mile equals 640 acres, and one acre equals 0.0015625 square miles. The table shows common values.

Square Miles to Acres formula

Acres = square miles × 640

Worked example: convert 12.5 square miles to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 640 = 8,000 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 0.0015625 (or divide by 640).
