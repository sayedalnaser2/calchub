Pyramid Volume Calculator

Calculate the volume and slant heights of a rectangular pyramid.

How it works

Volume = base area x height / 3. The four triangular faces have slant heights sqrt(h^2 + (w/2)^2) and sqrt(h^2 + (l/2)^2).

Frequently asked questions

What if the base is a square?

Enter the same number for length and width.

Does this work for the Great Pyramid?

Yes, with base 230.4 m and original height 146.6 m the volume is about 2.6 million cubic meters.
