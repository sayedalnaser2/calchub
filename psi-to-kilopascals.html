How it works

To convert psi to kilopascals, multiply the number of psi by 6.8947573. To convert kilopascals to psi, multiply by 0.14503774 (or divide by 6.8947573). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
