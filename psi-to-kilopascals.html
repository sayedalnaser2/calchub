PSI to Kilopascals Converter

Convert psi to kilopascals instantly. 1 psi = 6.8947573 kPa.

How it works

To convert psi to kilopascals, multiply the number of psi by 6.8947573. One psi equals 6.8947573 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 0.14503774 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = psi × 6.8947573

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 6.8947573 = 86.184466 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 0.14503774 (or divide by 6.8947573).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
