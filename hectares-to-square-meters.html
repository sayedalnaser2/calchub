Hectares to Square Meters Converter

Convert hectares to square meters instantly. 1 ha = 10,000 m².

How it works

To convert hectares to square meters, multiply the number of hectares by 10,000. One hectare equals 10,000 square meters, and one square meter equals 0.0001 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Square Meters formula

Square meters = hectares × 10,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 10,000 = 125,000 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 0.0001 (or divide by 10,000).
