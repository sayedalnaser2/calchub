How it works

To convert cubic yards to liters, multiply the number of cubic yards by 764.55486. To convert liters to cubic yards, multiply by 0.0013079506 (or divide by 764.55486). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Yards to Liters formula

Liters = cubic yards × 764.55486

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 764.55486 = 9,556.9357 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.0013079506 (or divide by 764.55486).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
