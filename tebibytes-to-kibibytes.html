Convert tebibytes to kibibytes instantly. 1 TiB = 1,073,741,800 KiB.

How it works

To convert tebibytes to kibibytes, multiply the number of tebibytes by 1,073,741,800. One tebibyte equals 1,073,741,800 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 9.31323e-10 tebibytes. The table shows common values.

Tebibytes to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = tebibytes × 1,073,741,800

Worked example: convert 12.5 tebibytes to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,073,741,800 = 13,421,773,000 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 9.31323e-10 (or divide by 1,073,741,800).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
