Calories to Kilojoules Converter

Convert calories to kilojoules instantly. 1 cal = 0.004184 kJ.

How it works

To convert calories to kilojoules, multiply the number of calories by 0.004184. One calorie equals 0.004184 kilojoules, and one kilojoule equals 239.00574 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = calories × 0.004184

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.004184 = 0.0523 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 239.00574 (or divide by 0.004184).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
