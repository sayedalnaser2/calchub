Meters to Kilometers Converter

Convert meters to kilometers instantly. 1 m = 0.001 km.

How it works

To convert meters to kilometers, multiply the number of meters by 0.001. One meter equals 0.001 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 1000 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = meters × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 1000 (or divide by 0.001).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
