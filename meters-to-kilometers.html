How it works

To convert meters to kilometers, multiply the number of meters by 0.001. To convert kilometers to meters, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Meters to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = meters × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
