Carpet Calculator

Estimate carpet needed in square feet and square yards, with waste.

How it works

Room area is length times width. Waste covers cuts and pattern matching. Carpet is priced per square yard, so square feet are divided by 9. Rolls usually come in 12 ft widths.

Frequently asked questions

How much waste should I add?

10% is typical, 15% or more for patterned carpet or awkward rooms.

Does this include padding and labor?

No. Add padding and installation separately.
