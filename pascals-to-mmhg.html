Pascals to mmHg Converter

Convert pascals to mmHg instantly. 1 Pa = 0.0075006158 mmHg.

How it works

To convert pascals to mmHg, multiply the number of pascals by 0.0075006158. One pascal equals 0.0075006158 mmHg, and one mmHg equals 133.32239 pascals. The table shows common values.

Pascals to mmHg formula

Mmhg = pascals × 0.0075006158

Worked example: convert 12.5 pascals to mmHg. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0075006158 = 0.093757697 mmHg. To go the other way, multiply mmHg by 133.32239 (or divide by 0.0075006158).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
