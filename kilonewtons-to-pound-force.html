How it works

To convert kilonewtons to pound-force, multiply the number of kilonewtons by 224.80894. One kilonewton equals 224.80894 pound-force, and one pound-force equals 0.0044482216 kilonewtons. The table shows common values.

Kilonewtons to Pound-Force formula

Pound-force = kilonewtons × 224.80894

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilonewtons to pound-force. Multiply 12.5 × 224.80894 = 2810.1118 lbf. To go the other way, multiply pound-force by 0.0044482216 (or divide by 224.80894).
