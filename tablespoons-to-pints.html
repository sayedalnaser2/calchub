Tablespoons to Pints Converter

Convert tablespoons to pints instantly. 1 tbsp = 0.03125 pt.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to pints, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.03125. One tablespoon equals 0.03125 pints, and one pint equals 32 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Pints formula

Pints = tablespoons × 0.03125

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.03125 = 0.390625 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 32 (or divide by 0.03125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
