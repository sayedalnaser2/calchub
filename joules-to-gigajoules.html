Joules to Gigajoules Converter

Convert joules to gigajoules instantly. 1 J = 1e-09 GJ.

How it works

To convert joules to gigajoules, multiply the number of joules by 1e-09. One joule equals 1e-09 gigajoules, and one gigajoule equals 1,000,000,000 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = joules × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
