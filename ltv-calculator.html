Loan-to-Value (LTV) Calculator

Calculate the loan-to-value ratio of a mortgage.

How it works

LTV = loan balance / property value. Lenders use it to price risk. PMI is typically required above 80%.

Frequently asked questions

How do I lower LTV?

Pay down the loan or wait for the property value to rise.
