Credit Card Interest Calculator

Estimate the interest charged on a credit card balance.

How it works

Most card issuers compute a daily periodic rate (APR / 365) and charge it on your balance each day. Interest for a cycle is balance x daily rate x days. Compounding daily over a year produces the final figure.

Frequently asked questions

How can I avoid credit card interest?

Pay the full statement balance by the due date every month.

Is APR the same as the rate I pay?

APR is the yearly rate; with daily compounding the effective rate is slightly higher.
