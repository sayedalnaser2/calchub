How it works

To convert knots to feet per second, multiply the number of knots by 1.6878099. To convert feet per second to knots, multiply by 0.5924838 (or divide by 1.6878099). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Knots to Feet per Second formula

Feet per second = knots × 1.6878099

Worked example: convert 12.5 knots to feet per second. Multiply 12.5 × 1.6878099 = 21.097623 ft/s. To go the other way, multiply feet per second by 0.5924838 (or divide by 1.6878099).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
