How it works

To convert nautical miles to meters, multiply the number of nautical miles by 1,852. To convert meters to nautical miles, multiply by 0.0005399568 (or divide by 1,852). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Nautical Miles to Meters formula

Meters = nautical miles × 1,852

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,852 = 23,150 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 0.0005399568 (or divide by 1,852).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
