How it works

To convert bar to millibars, multiply the number of bar by 1,000. To convert millibars to bar, multiply by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Bar to Millibars formula

Millibars = bar × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
