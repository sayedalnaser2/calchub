How it works

To convert pounds per cubic foot to pounds per gallon, multiply the number of pounds per cubic foot by 0.13368056. One pound per cubic foot equals 0.13368056 pounds per gallon, and one pound per gallon equals 7.4805195 pounds per cubic foot. The table shows common values.

Pounds per Cubic Foot to Pounds per Gallon formula

Pounds per gallon = pounds per cubic foot × 0.13368056
