Cubic Centimeters to Oil Barrels Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to oil barrels instantly. 1 cm³ = 6.28981e-06 bbl.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to oil barrels, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 6.28981e-06. One cubic centimeter equals 6.28981e-06 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 158,987.29 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = cubic centimeters × 6.28981e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to oil barrels. Multiply 12.5 × 6.28981e-06 = 7.86226e-05 bbl. To go the other way, multiply oil barrels by 158,987.29 (or divide by 6.28981e-06).
