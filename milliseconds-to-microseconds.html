Milliseconds to Microseconds Converter

Convert milliseconds to microseconds instantly. 1 ms = 1,000 µs.

How it works

To convert milliseconds to microseconds, multiply the number of milliseconds by 1,000. One millisecond equals 1,000 microseconds, and one microsecond equals 0.001 milliseconds. The table shows common values.

Milliseconds to Microseconds formula

Microseconds = milliseconds × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliseconds to microseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 µs. To go the other way, multiply microseconds by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
