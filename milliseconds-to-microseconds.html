How it works

To convert milliseconds to microseconds, multiply the number of milliseconds by 1,000. To convert microseconds to milliseconds, multiply by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Milliseconds to Microseconds formula

Microseconds = milliseconds × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliseconds to microseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 µs. To go the other way, multiply microseconds by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
