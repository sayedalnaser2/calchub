Typing Speed (WPM) Calculator

Calculate words per minute and accuracy from a typing test.

How it works

By convention one word is five characters. Gross WPM = (characters / 5) / minutes. Net WPM subtracts uncorrected errors per minute.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good typing speed?

The average is around 40 WPM; 65-75 WPM is considered strong.

Why five characters per word?

It standardizes tests regardless of actual word length.
