How it works

To convert watt hours to gigajoules, multiply the number of watt hours by 3.6e-06. To convert gigajoules to watt hours, multiply by 277,777.78 (or divide by 3.6e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Watt Hours to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = watt hours × 3.6e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 3.6e-06 = 4.5e-05 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 277,777.78 (or divide by 3.6e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
