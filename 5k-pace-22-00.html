5K Pace for a 22:00 Finish Time

To run a 5K in 22:00 you need 7:05 per mile (4:24 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A 5K is 5 km (3.11 miles), so 22:00 works out to 7:05 per mile or 4:24 per kilometer.
