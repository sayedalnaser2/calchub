Pascals to Millibars Converter

Convert pascals to millibars instantly. 1 Pa = 0.01 mbar.

How it works

To convert pascals to millibars, multiply the number of pascals by 0.01. One pascal equals 0.01 millibars, and one millibar equals 100 pascals. The table shows common values.

Pascals to Millibars formula

Millibars = pascals × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 pascals to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 100 (or divide by 0.01).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
