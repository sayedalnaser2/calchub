Kilowatts to Watts Converter

Convert kilowatts to watts instantly. 1 kW = 1,000 W.

How it works

To convert kilowatts to watts, multiply the number of kilowatts by 1,000. One kilowatt equals 1,000 watts, and one watt equals 0.001 kilowatts. The table shows common values.

Kilowatts to Watts formula

Watts = kilowatts × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatts to watts. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 W. To go the other way, multiply watts by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
