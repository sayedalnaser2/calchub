How it works

To convert square miles to hectares, multiply the number of square miles by 258.99881. To convert hectares to square miles, multiply by 0.0038610216 (or divide by 258.99881). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Miles to Hectares formula

Hectares = square miles × 258.99881

Worked example: convert 12.5 square miles to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 258.99881 = 3,237.4851 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 0.0038610216 (or divide by 258.99881).
