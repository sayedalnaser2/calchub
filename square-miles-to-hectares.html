Convert square miles to hectares instantly. 1 mi² = 258.99881 ha.

How it works

To convert square miles to hectares, multiply the number of square miles by 258.99881. One square mile equals 258.99881 hectares, and one hectare equals 0.0038610216 square miles. The table shows common values.

Square Miles to Hectares formula

Hectares = square miles × 258.99881

Worked example: convert 12.5 square miles to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 258.99881 = 3,237.4851 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 0.0038610216 (or divide by 258.99881).
