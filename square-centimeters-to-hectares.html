Square Centimeters to Hectares Converter

Convert square centimeters to hectares instantly. 1 cm² = 1e-08 ha.

How it works

To convert square centimeters to hectares, multiply the number of square centimeters by 1e-08. One square centimeter equals 1e-08 hectares, and one hectare equals 100,000,000 square centimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Centimeters to Hectares formula

Hectares = square centimeters × 1e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 square centimeters to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-08 = 1.25e-07 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 100,000,000 (or divide by 1e-08).
