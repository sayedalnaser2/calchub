How it works

To convert feet per second to miles per hour, multiply the number of feet per second by 0.68181818. One foot per second equals 0.68181818 miles per hour, and one mile per hour equals 1.4666667 feet per second. The table shows common values.

Feet per Second to Miles per Hour formula

Miles per hour = feet per second × 0.68181818

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet per second to miles per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 0.68181818 = 8.5227273 mph. To go the other way, multiply miles per hour by 1.4666667 (or divide by 0.68181818).
