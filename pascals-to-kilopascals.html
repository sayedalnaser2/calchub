Pascals to Kilopascals Converter

Convert pascals to kilopascals instantly. 1 Pa = 0.001 kPa.

How it works

To convert pascals to kilopascals, multiply the number of pascals by 0.001. One pascal equals 0.001 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 1,000 pascals. The table shows common values.

Pascals to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = pascals × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 pascals to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
