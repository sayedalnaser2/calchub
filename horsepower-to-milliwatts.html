How it works

To convert horsepower to milliwatts, multiply the number of horsepower by 745,699.87. To convert milliwatts to horsepower, multiply by 1.34102e-06 (or divide by 745,699.87). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Horsepower to Milliwatts formula

Milliwatts = horsepower × 745,699.87

Worked example: convert 12.5 horsepower to milliwatts. Multiply 12.5 × 745,699.87 = 9,321,248.4 mW. To go the other way, multiply milliwatts by 1.34102e-06 (or divide by 745,699.87).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
