Kilogram-Force to Dynes Converter

Convert kilogram-force to dynes instantly. 1 kgf = 980,665 dyn.

How it works

To convert kilogram-force to dynes, multiply the number of kilogram-force by 980,665. One kilogram-force equals 980,665 dynes, and one dyne equals 1.01972e-06 kilogram-force. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force to Dynes formula

Dynes = kilogram-force × 980,665

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force to dynes. Multiply 12.5 × 980,665 = 12,258,312 dyn. To go the other way, multiply dynes by 1.01972e-06 (or divide by 980,665).

Force is measured in newtons in the SI system. Pound-force and kilogram-force are common in engineering and everyday weight contexts.
