1/2 as a Decimal and Percent

1/2 as a decimal is 0.5. See it as a percent and as inches in mm.

How it works

Divide the numerator by the denominator: 1 / 2 = 0.5. As a percent that is 50.00%. If the fraction is a length in inches, it equals 12.70 mm.
