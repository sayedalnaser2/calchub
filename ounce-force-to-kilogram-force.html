How it works

To convert ounce-force to kilogram-force, multiply the number of ounce-force by 0.028349523. One ounce-force equals 0.028349523 kilogram-force, and one kilogram-force equals 35.273962 ounce-force. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Force to Kilogram-Force formula

Kilogram-force = ounce-force × 0.028349523
