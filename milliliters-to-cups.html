Milliliters to Cups Converter

Convert milliliters to cups instantly. 1 ml = 0.0042267528 cup.

How it works

To convert milliliters to cups, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.0042267528. One milliliter equals 0.0042267528 cups, and one cup equals 236.58824 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Cups formula

Cups = milliliters × 0.0042267528

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0042267528 = 0.05283441 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 236.58824 (or divide by 0.0042267528).
