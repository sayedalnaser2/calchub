How it works

To convert square inches to square millimeters, multiply the number of square inches by 645.16. To convert square millimeters to square inches, multiply by 0.0015500031 (or divide by 645.16). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Inches to Square Millimeters formula

Square millimeters = square inches × 645.16

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to square millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 645.16 = 8,064.5 mm². To go the other way, multiply square millimeters by 0.0015500031 (or divide by 645.16).
