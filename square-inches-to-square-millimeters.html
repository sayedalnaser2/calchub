Square Inches to Square Millimeters Converter

Convert square inches to square millimeters instantly. 1 in² = 645.16 mm².

How it works

To convert square inches to square millimeters, multiply the number of square inches by 645.16. One square inch equals 645.16 square millimeters, and one square millimeter equals 0.0015500031 square inches. The table shows common values.

Square Inches to Square Millimeters formula

Square millimeters = square inches × 645.16

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to square millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 645.16 = 8,064.5 mm². To go the other way, multiply square millimeters by 0.0015500031 (or divide by 645.16).
