How it works

To convert pound-feet to newton meters, multiply the number of pound-feet by 1.3558179. To convert newton meters to pound-feet, multiply by 0.73756215 (or divide by 1.3558179). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pound-Feet to Newton Meters formula

Newton meters = pound-feet × 1.3558179

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
