Pound-Feet to Newton Meters Converter

Convert pound-feet to newton meters instantly. 1 lb-ft = 1.3558179 N·m.

How it works

To convert pound-feet to newton meters, multiply the number of pound-feet by 1.3558179. One pound-foot equals 1.3558179 newton meters, and one newton meter equals 0.73756215 pound-feet. The table shows common values.

Pound-Feet to Newton Meters formula

Newton meters = pound-feet × 1.3558179

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-feet to newton meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3558179 = 16.947724 N·m. To go the other way, multiply newton meters by 0.73756215 (or divide by 1.3558179).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
