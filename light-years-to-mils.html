Light Years to Mils Converter

Convert light years to mils instantly. 1 ly = 3.7247e+20 mil.

How it works

To convert light years to mils, multiply the number of light years by 3.7247e+20. One light year equals 3.7247e+20 mils, and one mil equals 2.68478e-21 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Mils formula

Mils = light years × 3.7247e+20

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 3.7247e+20 = 4.65587e+21 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 2.68478e-21 (or divide by 3.7247e+20).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
