Imperial Gallons to Tablespoons Converter

Convert imperial gallons to tablespoons instantly. 1 imp gal = 307.44318 tbsp.

How it works

To convert imperial gallons to tablespoons, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 307.44318. One imperial gallon equals 307.44318 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.0032526335 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = imperial gallons × 307.44318

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 307.44318 = 3,843.0398 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.0032526335 (or divide by 307.44318).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
