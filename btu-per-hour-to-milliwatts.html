BTU per Hour to Milliwatts Converter

Convert BTU per hour to milliwatts instantly. 1 BTU/h = 293.07107 mW.

How it works

To convert BTU per hour to milliwatts, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 293.07107. One BTU per hour equals 293.07107 milliwatts, and one milliwatt equals 0.0034121416 BTU per hour. The table shows common values.

BTU per Hour to Milliwatts formula

Milliwatts = BTU per hour × 293.07107

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU per hour to milliwatts. Multiply 12.5 × 293.07107 = 3,663.3884 mW. To go the other way, multiply milliwatts by 0.0034121416 (or divide by 293.07107).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
