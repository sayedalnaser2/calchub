How it works

Moles = mass / molar mass. One mole contains 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles (Avogadro's number). Water has a molar mass of about 18.015 g/mol.

Formula

Moles n = mass / molar mass. One mole contains 6.02214076 x 10^23 particles.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find molar mass?

Add the atomic masses of all atoms in the formula.

What is a mole?

The amount of substance containing exactly the Avogadro number of entities.
