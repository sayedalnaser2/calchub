Kilobits to Petabytes Converter

Convert kilobits to petabytes instantly. 1 Kb = 1.25e-13 PB.

How it works

To convert kilobits to petabytes, multiply the number of kilobits by 1.25e-13. One kilobit equals 1.25e-13 petabytes, and one petabyte equals 8,000,000,000,000 kilobits. The table shows common values.

Kilobits to Petabytes formula

Petabytes = kilobits × 1.25e-13

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobits to petabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.25e-13 = 1.5625e-12 PB. To go the other way, multiply petabytes by 8,000,000,000,000 (or divide by 1.25e-13).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
