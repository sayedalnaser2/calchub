How it works

To convert degrees to turns, multiply the number of degrees by 0.0027777778. To convert turns to degrees, multiply by 360 (or divide by 0.0027777778). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Degrees to Turns formula

Turns = degrees × 0.0027777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 degrees to turns. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0027777778 = 0.034722222 turns. To go the other way, multiply turns by 360 (or divide by 0.0027777778).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
