Furlongs to Kilometers Converter

Convert furlongs to kilometers instantly. 1 furlong = 0.201168 km.

How it works

To convert furlongs to kilometers, multiply the number of furlongs by 0.201168. One furlong equals 0.201168 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 4.9709695 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = furlongs × 0.201168

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.201168 = 2.5146 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 4.9709695 (or divide by 0.201168).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
