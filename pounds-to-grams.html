How it works

To convert pounds to grams, multiply the number of pounds by 453.59237. To convert grams to pounds, multiply by 0.0022046226 (or divide by 453.59237). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pounds to Grams formula

Grams = pounds × 453.59237

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to grams. Multiply 12.5 × 453.59237 = 5,669.9046 g. To go the other way, multiply grams by 0.0022046226 (or divide by 453.59237).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
