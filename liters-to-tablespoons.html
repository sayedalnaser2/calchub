Liters to Tablespoons Converter

Convert liters to tablespoons instantly. 1 L = 67.628045 tbsp.

How it works

To convert liters to tablespoons, multiply the number of liters by 67.628045. One liter equals 67.628045 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.014786765 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = liters × 67.628045

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 67.628045 = 845.35057 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.014786765 (or divide by 67.628045).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
