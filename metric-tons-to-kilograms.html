Metric Tons to Kilograms Converter

Convert metric tons to kilograms instantly. 1 t = 1000 kg.

How it works

To convert metric tons to kilograms, multiply the number of metric tons by 1000. One metric ton equals 1000 kilograms, and one kilogram equals 0.001 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = metric tons × 1000

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 1000 = 12500 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 0.001 (or divide by 1000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
