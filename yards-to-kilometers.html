Yards to Kilometers Converter

Convert yards to kilometers instantly. 1 yd = 0.0009144 km.

How it works

To convert yards to kilometers, multiply the number of yards by 0.0009144. One yard equals 0.0009144 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 1,093.6133 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = yards × 0.0009144

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0009144 = 0.01143 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 1,093.6133 (or divide by 0.0009144).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
