Convert a decimal number to a simplified fraction.

How it works

A terminating decimal equals digits over a power of ten. The calculator finds the smallest denominator that matches the decimal exactly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a repeating decimal?

For repeating decimals, set x equal to the decimal, multiply to align the repeat and subtract.
