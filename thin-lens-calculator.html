Thin Lens Equation Calculator

Find image distance and magnification for a thin lens or mirror.

How it works

The thin lens equation 1/f = 1/do + 1/di relates focal length f, object distance do and image distance di. Magnification m = -di/do. A positive di means a real image on the opposite side of the lens.

Frequently asked questions

What is a virtual image?

One that cannot be projected on a screen, seen when the object is inside the focal length.

Does it work for mirrors?

Yes, with the same equation and sign convention.
