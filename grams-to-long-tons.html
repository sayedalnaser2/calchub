How it works

To convert grams to long tons, multiply the number of grams by 9.84207e-07. One gram equals 9.84207e-07 long tons, and one long ton equals 1,016,046.9 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Long Tons formula

Long tons = grams × 9.84207e-07

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
