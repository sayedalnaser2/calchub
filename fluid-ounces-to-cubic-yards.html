How it works

To convert fluid ounces to cubic yards, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 3.86807e-05. To convert cubic yards to fluid ounces, multiply by 25,852.675 (or divide by 3.86807e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Fluid Ounces to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = fluid ounces × 3.86807e-05

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
