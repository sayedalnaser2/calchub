Fluid Ounces to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert fluid ounces to cubic meters instantly. 1 fl oz = 2.95735e-05 m³.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to cubic meters, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 2.95735e-05. One fluid ounce equals 2.95735e-05 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 33,814.023 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = fluid ounces × 2.95735e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 2.95735e-05 = 0.00036966912 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 33,814.023 (or divide by 2.95735e-05).
