How it works

To convert feet per second squared to standard gravities, multiply the number of feet per second squared by 0.03108095. One foot per second squared equals 0.03108095 standard gravities, and one standard gravity equals 32.174049 feet per second squared. The table shows common values.

Feet per Second Squared to Standard Gravities formula

Standard gravities = feet per second squared × 0.03108095

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet per second squared to standard gravities. Multiply 12.5 × 0.03108095 = 0.38851188 g. To go the other way, multiply standard gravities by 32.174049 (or divide by 0.03108095).
