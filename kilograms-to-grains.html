Kilograms to Grains Converter

Convert kilograms to grains instantly. 1 kg = 15,432.358 gr.

How it works

To convert kilograms to grains, multiply the number of kilograms by 15,432.358. One kilogram equals 15,432.358 grains, and one grain equals 6.47989e-05 kilograms. The table shows common values.

Kilograms to Grains formula

Grains = kilograms × 15,432.358

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 15,432.358 = 192,904.48 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 6.47989e-05 (or divide by 15,432.358).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
