Millimeters to Miles Converter

Convert millimeters to miles instantly. 1 mm = 6.21371e-07 mi.

How it works

To convert millimeters to miles, multiply the number of millimeters by 6.21371e-07. One millimeter equals 6.21371e-07 miles, and one mile equals 1.60934e+06 millimeters. The table shows common values.

Millimeters to Miles formula

Miles = millimeters × 6.21371e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 6.21371e-07 = 7.76714e-06 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1.60934e+06 (or divide by 6.21371e-07).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
