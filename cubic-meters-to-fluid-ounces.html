Cubic Meters to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert cubic meters to fluid ounces instantly. 1 m³ = 33,814.023 fl oz.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to fluid ounces, multiply the number of cubic meters by 33,814.023. One cubic meter equals 33,814.023 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 2.95735e-05 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = cubic meters × 33,814.023

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 33,814.023 = 422,675.28 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 2.95735e-05 (or divide by 33,814.023).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
