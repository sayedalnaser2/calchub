Credit Card Minimum Payment Calculator

See how long minimum payments take to clear a card balance.

How it works

Many cards set the minimum at 1% of the balance plus that month's interest, or a floor such as $25. Minimums shrink as the balance falls, which stretches payoff for years.

Frequently asked questions

How do I escape the minimum trap?

Pay a fixed amount above the minimum every month.
