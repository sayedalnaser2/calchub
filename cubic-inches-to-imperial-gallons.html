How it works

To convert cubic inches to imperial gallons, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.0036046501. One cubic inch equals 0.0036046501 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 277.41943 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = cubic inches × 0.0036046501

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0036046501 = 0.045058127 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 277.41943 (or divide by 0.0036046501).

About volume units
