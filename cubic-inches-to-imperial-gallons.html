How it works

To convert cubic inches to imperial gallons, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.0036046501. To convert imperial gallons to cubic inches, multiply by 277.41943 (or divide by 0.0036046501). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Inches to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = cubic inches × 0.0036046501

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0036046501 = 0.045058127 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 277.41943 (or divide by 0.0036046501).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
