TDEE Calculator

Estimate your total daily energy expenditure from BMR and activity level.

How it works

BMR is estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation: 10 x kg + 6.25 x cm - 5 x age, plus 5 for men or minus 161 for women. TDEE multiplies BMR by an activity factor. It is an estimate and not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is calories burned at rest. TDEE adds the calories you burn moving and exercising.

How accurate is it?

Within roughly 10%. Track your weight for a few weeks and adjust.
