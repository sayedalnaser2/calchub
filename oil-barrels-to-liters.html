Convert oil barrels to liters instantly. 1 bbl = 158.98729 L.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to liters, multiply the number of oil barrels by 158.98729. One oil barrel equals 158.98729 liters, and one liter equals 0.0062898108 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Liters formula

Liters = oil barrels × 158.98729

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 158.98729 = 1,987.3412 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.0062898108 (or divide by 158.98729).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
