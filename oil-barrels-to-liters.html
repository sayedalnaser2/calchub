How it works

To convert oil barrels to liters, multiply the number of oil barrels by 158.98729. To convert liters to oil barrels, multiply by 0.0062898108 (or divide by 158.98729). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Oil Barrels to Liters formula

Liters = oil barrels × 158.98729

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 158.98729 = 1,987.3412 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.0062898108 (or divide by 158.98729).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
