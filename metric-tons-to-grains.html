Metric Tons to Grains Converter

Convert metric tons to grains instantly. 1 t = 15,432,358 gr.

How it works

To convert metric tons to grains, multiply the number of metric tons by 15,432,358. One metric ton equals 15,432,358 grains, and one grain equals 6.47989e-08 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Grains formula

Grains = metric tons × 15,432,358

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 15,432,358 = 192,904,480 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 6.47989e-08 (or divide by 15,432,358).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
