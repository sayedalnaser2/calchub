Kilojoules to Gigajoules Converter

Convert kilojoules to gigajoules instantly. 1 kJ = 1e-06 GJ.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to gigajoules, multiply the number of kilojoules by 1e-06. One kilojoule equals 1e-06 gigajoules, and one gigajoule equals 1,000,000 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = kilojoules × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
