Pascals to Bar Converter

Convert pascals to bar instantly. 1 Pa = 1e-05 bar.

How it works

To convert pascals to bar, multiply the number of pascals by 1e-05. One pascal equals 1e-05 bar, and one bar equals 100000 pascals. The table shows common values.

Pascals to Bar formula

Bar = pascals × 1e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 pascals to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-05 = 0.000125 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 100000 (or divide by 1e-05).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
