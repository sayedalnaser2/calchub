How it works

To convert liters per second to cubic meters per second, multiply the number of liters per second by 0.001. To convert cubic meters per second to liters per second, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Liters per Second to Cubic Meters per Second formula

Cubic meters per second = liters per second × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per second to cubic meters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 m³/s. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters per second by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
