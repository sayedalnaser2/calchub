Convert meters to feet instantly. 1 m = 3.2808399 ft.

How it works

To convert meters to feet, multiply the number of meters by 3.2808399. One meter equals 3.2808399 feet, and one foot equals 0.3048 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Feet formula

Feet = meters × 3.2808399

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3.2808399 = 41.010499 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 0.3048 (or divide by 3.2808399).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
