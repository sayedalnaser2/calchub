Nanoseconds to Seconds Converter

Convert nanoseconds to seconds instantly. 1 ns = 1e-09 s.

How it works

To convert nanoseconds to seconds, multiply the number of nanoseconds by 1e-09. One nanosecond equals 1e-09 seconds, and one second equals 1,000,000,000 nanoseconds. The table shows common values.

Nanoseconds to Seconds formula

Seconds = nanoseconds × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanoseconds to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).
