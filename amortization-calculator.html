Amortization Calculator

See your loan payoff time and interest saved, with optional extra monthly payments.

How it works

Each month interest is charged on the remaining balance and the rest of the payment reduces principal. Extra payments cut the balance sooner, which lowers all future interest.

Frequently asked questions

Do extra payments really save that much?

Yes. Early in a loan most of each payment is interest, so extra principal payments compound into large savings.

Should extra payments go to principal?

Tell your lender to apply them to principal only, otherwise they may be held as a prepayment of next month.
