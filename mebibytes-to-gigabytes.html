How it works

To convert mebibytes to gigabytes, multiply the number of mebibytes by 0.001048576. One mebibyte equals 0.001048576 gigabytes, and one gigabyte equals 953.67432 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = mebibytes × 0.001048576

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001048576 = 0.0131072 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 953.67432 (or divide by 0.001048576).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
