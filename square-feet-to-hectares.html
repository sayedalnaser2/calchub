Square Feet to Hectares Converter

Convert square feet to hectares instantly. 1 ft² = 9.2903e-06 ha.

How it works

To convert square feet to hectares, multiply the number of square feet by 9.2903e-06. One square foot equals 9.2903e-06 hectares, and one hectare equals 107,639.1 square feet. The table shows common values.

Square Feet to Hectares formula

Hectares = square feet × 9.2903e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 9.2903e-06 = 0.0001161288 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 107,639.1 (or divide by 9.2903e-06).
