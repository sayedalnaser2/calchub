Cubic Meters per Second to Gallons per Minute Converter

Convert cubic meters per second to gallons per minute instantly. 1 m³/s = 15,850.323 gal/min.

How it works

To convert cubic meters per second to gallons per minute, multiply the number of cubic meters per second by 15,850.323. One cubic meter per second equals 15,850.323 gallons per minute, and one gallon per minute equals 6.30902e-05 cubic meters per second. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters per Second to Gallons per Minute formula

Gallons per minute = cubic meters per second × 15,850.323

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters per second to gallons per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 15,850.323 = 198,129.04 gal/min. To go the other way, multiply gallons per minute by 6.30902e-05 (or divide by 15,850.323).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
