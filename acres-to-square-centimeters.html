How it works

To convert acres to square centimeters, multiply the number of acres by 40,468,564. To convert square centimeters to acres, multiply by 2.47105e-08 (or divide by 40,468,564). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Acres to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = acres × 40,468,564

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 40,468,564 = 505,857,050 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 2.47105e-08 (or divide by 40,468,564).
