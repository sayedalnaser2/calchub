Convert imperial pints to teaspoons instantly. 1 imp pt = 115.29119 tsp.

How it works

To convert imperial pints to teaspoons, multiply the number of imperial pints by 115.29119. One imperial pint equals 115.29119 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.0086736894 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = imperial pints × 115.29119

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 115.29119 = 1,441.1399 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.0086736894 (or divide by 115.29119).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
