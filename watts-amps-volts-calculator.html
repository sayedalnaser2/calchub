Convert between watts, amps, volts and power factor for DC and AC circuits.

How it works

I = P / (V x PF). For three-phase, I = P / (√3 x V x PF).

Formula

Real power P = V x I x power factor. For DC circuits the power factor is 1. Enter any three values.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a 15 A circuit limit loads?

15 A at 120 V is 1,800 W; continuous loads should stay under 80%.
