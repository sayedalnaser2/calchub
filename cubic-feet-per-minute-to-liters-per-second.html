How it works

To convert cubic feet per minute to liters per second, multiply the number of cubic feet per minute by 0.47194744. One cubic foot per minute equals 0.47194744 liters per second, and one liter per second equals 2.11888 cubic feet per minute. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet per Minute to Liters per Second formula

Liters per second = cubic feet per minute × 0.47194744

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet per minute to liters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.47194744 = 5.899343 L/s. To go the other way, multiply liters per second by 2.11888 (or divide by 0.47194744).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
