Square Kilometers to Square Millimeters Converter

Convert square kilometers to square millimeters instantly. 1 km² = 1,000,000,000,000 mm².

How it works

To convert square kilometers to square millimeters, multiply the number of square kilometers by 1,000,000,000,000. One square kilometer equals 1,000,000,000,000 square millimeters, and one square millimeter equals 1e-12 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Square Millimeters formula

Square millimeters = square kilometers × 1,000,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to square millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000,000 mm². To go the other way, multiply square millimeters by 1e-12 (or divide by 1,000,000,000,000).
