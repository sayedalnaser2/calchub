Short Tons to Pounds Converter

Convert short tons to pounds instantly. 1 short ton = 2,000 lb.

How it works

To convert short tons to pounds, multiply the number of short tons by 2,000. One short ton equals 2,000 pounds, and one pound equals 0.0005 short tons. The table shows common values.

Short Tons to Pounds formula

Pounds = short tons × 2,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 short tons to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 2,000 = 25,000 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 0.0005 (or divide by 2,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
