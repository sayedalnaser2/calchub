Kilopascals to Torr Converter

Convert kilopascals to torr instantly. 1 kPa = 7.5006168 Torr.

How it works

To convert kilopascals to torr, multiply the number of kilopascals by 7.5006168. One kilopascal equals 7.5006168 torr, and one torr equals 0.13332237 kilopascals. The table shows common values.

Kilopascals to Torr formula

Torr = kilopascals × 7.5006168

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 7.5006168 = 93.75771 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 0.13332237 (or divide by 7.5006168).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
