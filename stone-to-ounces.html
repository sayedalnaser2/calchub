Stone to Ounces Converter

Convert stone to ounces instantly. 1 st = 224 oz.

How it works

To convert stone to ounces, multiply the number of stone by 224. One stone equals 224 ounces, and one ounce equals 0.0044642857 stone. The table shows common values.

Stone to Ounces formula

Ounces = stone × 224

Worked example: convert 12.5 stone to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 224 = 2,800 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 0.0044642857 (or divide by 224).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
