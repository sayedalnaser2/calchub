Years to Milliseconds Converter

Convert years to milliseconds instantly. 1 years = 31,556,952,000 ms.

How it works

To convert years to milliseconds, multiply the number of years by 31,556,952,000. One year equals 31,556,952,000 milliseconds, and one millisecond equals 3.16887e-11 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Milliseconds formula

Milliseconds = years × 31,556,952,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to milliseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 31,556,952,000 = 394,461,900,000 ms. To go the other way, multiply milliseconds by 3.16887e-11 (or divide by 31,556,952,000).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
