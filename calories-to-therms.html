Calories to Therms Converter

Convert calories to therms instantly. 1 cal = 3.96661e-08 therms.

How it works

To convert calories to therms, multiply the number of calories by 3.96661e-08. One calorie equals 3.96661e-08 therms, and one therm equals 25,210,421 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Therms formula

Therms = calories × 3.96661e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 3.96661e-08 = 4.95827e-07 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 25,210,421 (or divide by 3.96661e-08).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
