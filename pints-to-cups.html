Pints to Cups Converter

Convert pints to cups instantly. 1 pt = 2 cup.

How it works

To convert pints to cups, multiply the number of pints by 2. One pint equals 2 cups, and one cup equals 0.5 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Cups formula

Cups = pints × 2

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 2 = 25 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.5 (or divide by 2).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
