Tablespoons to Quarts Converter

Convert tablespoons to quarts instantly. 1 tbsp = 0.015625 qt.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to quarts, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.015625. One tablespoon equals 0.015625 quarts, and one quart equals 64 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Quarts formula

Quarts = tablespoons × 0.015625

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.015625 = 0.1953125 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 64 (or divide by 0.015625).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
