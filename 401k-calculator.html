401(k) Calculator

Project your 401(k) balance with employer match and salary growth.

How it works

Each year your contribution plus the employer match is added, then the balance grows by the expected return. Salary growth raises later contributions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 2025 contribution limit?

The employee limit is $23,500, with extra catch-up for age 50+. Check the IRS for current figures.

Always get the full match?

Yes. An employer match is an immediate return on your contribution.
