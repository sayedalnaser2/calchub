Light Years to Meters Converter

Convert light years to meters instantly. 1 ly = 9.46073e+15 m.

How it works

To convert light years to meters, multiply the number of light years by 9.46073e+15. One light year equals 9.46073e+15 meters, and one meter equals 1.057e-16 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Meters formula

Meters = light years × 9.46073e+15

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 9.46073e+15 = 1.18259e+17 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 1.057e-16 (or divide by 9.46073e+15).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
