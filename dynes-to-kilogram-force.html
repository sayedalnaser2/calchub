Dynes to Kilogram-Force Converter

Convert dynes to kilogram-force instantly. 1 dyn = 1.01972e-06 kgf.

How it works

To convert dynes to kilogram-force, multiply the number of dynes by 1.01972e-06. One dyne equals 1.01972e-06 kilogram-force, and one kilogram-force equals 980,665 dynes. The table shows common values.

Dynes to Kilogram-Force formula

Kilogram-force = dynes × 1.01972e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 dynes to kilogram-force. Multiply 12.5 × 1.01972e-06 = 1.27465e-05 kgf. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force by 980,665 (or divide by 1.01972e-06).
