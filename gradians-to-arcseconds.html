Gradians to Arcseconds Converter

Convert gradians to arcseconds instantly. 1 grad = 3,240 arcsec.

How it works

To convert gradians to arcseconds, multiply the number of gradians by 3,240. One gradian equals 3,240 arcseconds, and one arcsecond equals 0.00030864198 gradians. The table shows common values.

Gradians to Arcseconds formula

Arcseconds = gradians × 3,240

Worked example: convert 12.5 gradians to arcseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,240 = 40,500 arcsec. To go the other way, multiply arcseconds by 0.00030864198 (or divide by 3,240).
