How it works

To convert gradians to arcseconds, multiply the number of gradians by 3,240. To convert arcseconds to gradians, multiply by 0.00030864198 (or divide by 3,240). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Gradians to Arcseconds formula

Arcseconds = gradians × 3,240

Worked example: convert 12.5 gradians to arcseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,240 = 40,500 arcsec. To go the other way, multiply arcseconds by 0.00030864198 (or divide by 3,240).
