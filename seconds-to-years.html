Seconds to Years Converter

Convert seconds to years instantly. 1 s = 3.16887e-08 years.

How it works

To convert seconds to years, multiply the number of seconds by 3.16887e-08. One second equals 3.16887e-08 years, and one year equals 3.1557e+07 seconds. The table shows common values.

Seconds to Years formula

Years = seconds × 3.16887e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to years. Multiply 12.5 × 3.16887e-08 = 3.96109e-07 years. To go the other way, multiply years by 3.1557e+07 (or divide by 3.16887e-08).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
