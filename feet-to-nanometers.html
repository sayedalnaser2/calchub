How it works

To convert feet to nanometers, multiply the number of feet by 304,800,000. To convert nanometers to feet, multiply by 3.28084e-09 (or divide by 304,800,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Feet to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = feet × 304,800,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 304,800,000 = 3,810,000,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 3.28084e-09 (or divide by 304,800,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
