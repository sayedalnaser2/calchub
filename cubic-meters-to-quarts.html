Cubic Meters to Quarts Converter

Convert cubic meters to quarts instantly. 1 m³ = 1,056.6882 qt.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to quarts, multiply the number of cubic meters by 1,056.6882. One cubic meter equals 1,056.6882 quarts, and one quart equals 0.00094635295 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Quarts formula

Quarts = cubic meters × 1,056.6882

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 1,056.6882 = 13,208.603 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 0.00094635295 (or divide by 1,056.6882).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
