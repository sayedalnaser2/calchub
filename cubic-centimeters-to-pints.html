How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to pints, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.0021133764. One cubic centimeter equals 0.0021133764 pints, and one pint equals 473.17647 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Pints formula

Pints = cubic centimeters × 0.0021133764

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0021133764 = 0.026417205 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 473.17647 (or divide by 0.0021133764).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
