Kilometers per Second to Knots Converter

Convert kilometers per second to knots instantly. 1 km/s = 1,943.8445 kn.

How it works

To convert kilometers per second to knots, multiply the number of kilometers per second by 1,943.8445. One kilometer per second equals 1,943.8445 knots, and one knot equals 0.00051444444 kilometers per second. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Second to Knots formula

Knots = kilometers per second × 1,943.8445

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per second to knots. Multiply 12.5 × 1,943.8445 = 24,298.056 kn. To go the other way, multiply knots by 0.00051444444 (or divide by 1,943.8445).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
