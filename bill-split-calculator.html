Bill Split Calculator with Tip

Split a restaurant bill and tip evenly between any number of people.

How it works

The tip is the bill times the tip percentage. Add it to the bill and divide the total by the number of people.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

15-20% is customary for table service in the US.

Should I tip on the pre-tax amount?

Many people do, but tipping on the post-tax total is also common.
