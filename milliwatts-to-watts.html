Milliwatts to Watts Converter

Convert milliwatts to watts instantly. 1 mW = 0.001 W.

How it works

To convert milliwatts to watts, multiply the number of milliwatts by 0.001. One milliwatt equals 0.001 watts, and one watt equals 1,000 milliwatts. The table shows common values.

Milliwatts to Watts formula

Watts = milliwatts × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliwatts to watts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 W. To go the other way, multiply watts by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
