Weeks to Hours Converter

Convert weeks to hours instantly. 1 week = 168 h.

How it works

To convert weeks to hours, multiply the number of weeks by 168. One week equals 168 hours, and one hour equals 0.005952381 weeks. The table shows common values.

Weeks to Hours formula

Hours = weeks × 168

Worked example: convert 12.5 weeks to hours. Multiply 12.5 × 168 = 2,100 h. To go the other way, multiply hours by 0.005952381 (or divide by 168).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
