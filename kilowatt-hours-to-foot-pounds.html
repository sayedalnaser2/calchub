Kilowatt Hours to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert kilowatt hours to foot-pounds instantly. 1 kWh = 2,655,223.7 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to foot-pounds, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 2,655,223.7. One kilowatt hour equals 2,655,223.7 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 3.76616e-07 kilowatt hours. The table shows common values.

Kilowatt Hours to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = kilowatt hours × 2,655,223.7

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 2,655,223.7 = 33,190,297 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 3.76616e-07 (or divide by 2,655,223.7).
