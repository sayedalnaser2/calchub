How it works

To convert pints to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of pints by 473.17647. One pint equals 473.17647 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.0021133764 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = pints × 473.17647

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 473.17647 = 5,914.7059 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.0021133764 (or divide by 473.17647).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
