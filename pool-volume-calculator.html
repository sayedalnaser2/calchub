Pool Volume Calculator

Calculate the gallons of water in a pool.

How it works

Volume in cubic feet x 7.48 gives US gallons. Use the average of the shallow and deep ends for depth.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find average depth?

Add shallow and deep ends and divide by two (for sloped bottoms).
