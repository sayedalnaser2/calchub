Arcseconds to Degrees Converter

Convert arcseconds to degrees instantly. 1 arcsec = 0.00027777778 °.

How it works

To convert arcseconds to degrees, multiply the number of arcseconds by 0.00027777778. One arcsecond equals 0.00027777778 degrees, and one degree equals 3,600 arcseconds. The table shows common values.

Arcseconds to Degrees formula

Degrees = arcseconds × 0.00027777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcseconds to degrees. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00027777778 = 0.0034722222 °. To go the other way, multiply degrees by 3,600 (or divide by 0.00027777778).
