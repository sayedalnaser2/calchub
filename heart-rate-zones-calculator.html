Heart Rate Zones Calculator

Calculate your five training heart rate zones with the Karvonen method.

How it works

Max heart rate is estimated as 220 minus age. The Karvonen method uses heart rate reserve (max minus resting): target = resting + reserve x intensity. Individual maximums vary by up to 10-20 bpm.

Frequently asked questions

Which zone burns the most fat?

Zone 2 uses the highest proportion of fat, but total calories matter more for weight loss.

Is 220 - age accurate?

It is a rough average; a tested max is better.
