How it works

To convert BTU to gigajoules, multiply the number of BTU by 1.05506e-06. To convert gigajoules to BTU, multiply by 947,817.12 (or divide by 1.05506e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

BTU to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = BTU × 1.05506e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1.05506e-06 = 1.31882e-05 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 947,817.12 (or divide by 1.05506e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
