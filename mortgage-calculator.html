How your mortgage payment is calculated

Your monthly principal and interest depends on the loan amount (home price minus down payment), the interest rate and the term. Lenders usually add property tax and homeowners insurance into the monthly payment (called escrow). If your down payment is under 20%, you may also pay private mortgage insurance (PMI), which this estimate does not include.

Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford?

A common guideline is keeping total housing costs under about 28% of gross monthly income, and total debts under 36%.

Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?

A 15-year loan has higher monthly payments but far less total interest. A 30-year loan has lower payments and more flexibility. Try both terms above to compare.

Does a larger down payment help?

Yes. It lowers the loan amount, the monthly payment and total interest, and 20% or more usually avoids PMI.
