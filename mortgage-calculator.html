Mortgage Calculator

Estimate your monthly mortgage payment including property tax and home insurance.

How it works

Monthly principal and interest comes from the loan amount (price minus down payment), the interest rate and the term. Property tax and homeowners insurance are added as one-twelfth of the yearly cost, which is how most lenders collect them in escrow. Private mortgage insurance (PMI) is not included.

Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford?

A common guideline is to keep housing costs under about 28% of gross monthly income and total debts under 36%.

Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?

A 15-year loan has higher payments but far less total interest. A 30-year loan has lower payments and more flexibility. Compare both above.

Does a bigger down payment help?

Yes. It lowers the loan amount, the payment and total interest, and 20% or more usually avoids PMI.
