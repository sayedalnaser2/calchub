Gallons to Quarts Converter

Convert gallons to quarts instantly. 1 gal = 4 qt.

How it works

To convert gallons to quarts, multiply the number of gallons by 4. One gallon equals 4 quarts, and one quart equals 0.25 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Quarts formula

Quarts = gallons × 4

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 4 = 50 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 0.25 (or divide by 4).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
