Convert cubic inches to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 in³ = 16.387064 cm³.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cubic inches by 16.387064. One cubic inch equals 16.387064 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.061023744 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cubic inches × 16.387064

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 16.387064 = 204.8383 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.061023744 (or divide by 16.387064).
