How it works

To convert cubic inches to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cubic inches by 16.387064. To convert cubic centimeters to cubic inches, multiply by 0.061023744 (or divide by 16.387064). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Inches to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cubic inches × 16.387064

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 16.387064 = 204.8383 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.061023744 (or divide by 16.387064).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
