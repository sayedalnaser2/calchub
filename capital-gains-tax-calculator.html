Capital Gains Tax Calculator

Estimate federal tax on short-term or long-term investment gains.

How it works

Short-term gains are taxed as ordinary income. Long-term gains use 0%, 15% or 20% depending on income. The net investment income tax and state taxes are not included. Figures use 2025 US federal rules and are estimates only.
