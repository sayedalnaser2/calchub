Cups to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert cups to cubic inches instantly. 1 cup = 14.4375 in³.

How it works

To convert cups to cubic inches, multiply the number of cups by 14.4375. One cup equals 14.4375 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.069264069 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = cups × 14.4375

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 14.4375 = 180.46875 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.069264069 (or divide by 14.4375).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
