How it works

To convert cups to cubic inches, multiply the number of cups by 14.4375. To convert cubic inches to cups, multiply by 0.069264069 (or divide by 14.4375). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cups to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = cups × 14.4375

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 14.4375 = 180.46875 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.069264069 (or divide by 14.4375).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
