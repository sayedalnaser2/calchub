Hours to Nanoseconds Converter

Convert hours to nanoseconds instantly. 1 h = 3,600,000,000,000 ns.

How it works

To convert hours to nanoseconds, multiply the number of hours by 3,600,000,000,000. One hour equals 3,600,000,000,000 nanoseconds, and one nanosecond equals 2.77778e-13 hours. The table shows common values.

Hours to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = hours × 3,600,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600,000,000,000 = 45,000,000,000,000 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 2.77778e-13 (or divide by 3,600,000,000,000).
