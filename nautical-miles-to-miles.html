Nautical Miles to Miles Converter

Convert nautical miles to miles instantly. 1 nmi = 1.1507794 mi.

How it works

To convert nautical miles to miles, multiply the number of nautical miles by 1.1507794. One nautical mile equals 1.1507794 miles, and one mile equals 0.86897624 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Miles formula

Miles = nautical miles × 1.1507794

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 1.1507794 = 14.384743 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 0.86897624 (or divide by 1.1507794).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
