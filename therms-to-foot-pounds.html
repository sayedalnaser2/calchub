How it works

To convert therms to foot-pounds, multiply the number of therms by 77,798,351. One therm equals 77,798,351 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 1.28537e-08 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = therms × 77,798,351
