Kilocalories to Megajoules Converter

Convert kilocalories to megajoules instantly. 1 kcal = 0.004184 MJ.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to megajoules, multiply the number of kilocalories by 0.004184. One kilocalorie equals 0.004184 megajoules, and one megajoule equals 239.00574 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = kilocalories × 0.004184

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.004184 = 0.0523 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 239.00574 (or divide by 0.004184).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
