Capacitor Energy Calculator

Calculate the energy and charge stored in a capacitor.

How it works

Energy E = 1/2 C V^2 and charge Q = C V. Capacitance in microfarads is converted to farads by multiplying by 10^-6.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the voltage squared?

Energy grows with both charge and voltage, giving a squared term.

Is discharged energy fully usable?

Not always; circuits stop working below a minimum voltage.
