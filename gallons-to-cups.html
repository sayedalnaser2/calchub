Gallons to Cups Converter

Convert gallons to cups instantly. 1 gal = 16 cup.

How it works

To convert gallons to cups, multiply the number of gallons by 16. One gallon equals 16 cups, and one cup equals 0.0625 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Cups formula

Cups = gallons × 16

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 16 = 200 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.0625 (or divide by 16).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
