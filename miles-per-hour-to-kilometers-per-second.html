Miles per Hour to Kilometers per Second Converter

Convert miles per hour to kilometers per second instantly. 1 mph = 0.00044704 km/s.

How it works

To convert miles per hour to kilometers per second, multiply the number of miles per hour by 0.00044704. One mile per hour equals 0.00044704 kilometers per second, and one kilometer per second equals 2,236.9363 miles per hour. The table shows common values.

Miles per Hour to Kilometers per Second formula

Kilometers per second = miles per hour × 0.00044704

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles per hour to kilometers per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00044704 = 0.005588 km/s. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per second by 2,236.9363 (or divide by 0.00044704).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
