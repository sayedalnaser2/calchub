How it works

To convert square meters to square kilometers, multiply the number of square meters by 1e-06. To convert square kilometers to square meters, multiply by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Meters to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = square meters × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).
