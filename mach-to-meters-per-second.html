Mach to Meters per Second Converter

Convert mach to meters per second instantly. 1 Mach = 340.29 m/s.

How it works

To convert mach to meters per second, multiply the number of mach by 340.29. One Mach equals 340.29 meters per second, and one meter per second equals 0.00293867 mach. The table shows common values.

Mach to Meters per Second formula

Meters per second = mach × 340.29

Worked example: convert 12.5 mach to meters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 340.29 = 4,253.625 m/s. To go the other way, multiply meters per second by 0.00293867 (or divide by 340.29).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
