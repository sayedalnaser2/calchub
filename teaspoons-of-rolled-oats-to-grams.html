Teaspoons of Rolled Oats to Grams

Convert teaspoons of rolled oats to grams. 1 cup of rolled oats weighs about 90 g.

How it works

Rolled Oats is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 90 g per US cup, convert teaspoons to grams by multiplying by 1.875.

1 US cup of rolled oats weighs about 90 g (3.2 oz), 1 tablespoon about 5.6 g and 1 teaspoon about 1.9 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
