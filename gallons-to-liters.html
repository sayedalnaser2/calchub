How it works

To convert gallons to liters, multiply the number of gallons by 3.7854118. To convert liters to gallons, multiply by 0.26417205 (or divide by 3.7854118). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Gallons to Liters formula

Liters = gallons × 3.7854118

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 3.7854118 = 47.317647 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.26417205 (or divide by 3.7854118).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
