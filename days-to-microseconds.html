Days to Microseconds Converter

Convert days to microseconds instantly. 1 days = 86,400,000,000 µs.

How it works

To convert days to microseconds, multiply the number of days by 86,400,000,000. One day equals 86,400,000,000 microseconds, and one microsecond equals 1.15741e-11 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Microseconds formula

Microseconds = days × 86,400,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to microseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 86,400,000,000 = 1,080,000,000,000 µs. To go the other way, multiply microseconds by 1.15741e-11 (or divide by 86,400,000,000).
