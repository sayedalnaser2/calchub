Arcminutes to Degrees Converter

Convert arcminutes to degrees instantly. 1 arcmin = 0.016666667 °.

How it works

To convert arcminutes to degrees, multiply the number of arcminutes by 0.016666667. One arcminute equals 0.016666667 degrees, and one degree equals 60 arcminutes. The table shows common values.

Arcminutes to Degrees formula

Degrees = arcminutes × 0.016666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcminutes to degrees. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016666667 = 0.20833333 °. To go the other way, multiply degrees by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667).
