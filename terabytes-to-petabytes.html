Terabytes to Petabytes Converter

Convert terabytes to petabytes instantly. 1 TB = 0.001 PB.

How it works

To convert terabytes to petabytes, multiply the number of terabytes by 0.001. One terabyte equals 0.001 petabytes, and one petabyte equals 1,000 terabytes. The table shows common values.

Terabytes to Petabytes formula

Petabytes = terabytes × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 terabytes to petabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 PB. To go the other way, multiply petabytes by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
