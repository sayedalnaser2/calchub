Calories to Megajoules Converter

Convert calories to megajoules instantly. 1 cal = 4.184e-06 MJ.

How it works

To convert calories to megajoules, multiply the number of calories by 4.184e-06. One calorie equals 4.184e-06 megajoules, and one megajoule equals 239,005.74 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = calories × 4.184e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 4.184e-06 = 5.23e-05 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 239,005.74 (or divide by 4.184e-06).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
