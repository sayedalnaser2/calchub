BTU per Hour to Watts Converter

Convert BTU per hour to watts instantly. 1 BTU/h = 0.29307107 W.

How it works

To convert BTU per hour to watts, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 0.29307107. One BTU per hour equals 0.29307107 watts, and one watt equals 3.4121416 BTU per hour. The table shows common values.

BTU per Hour to Watts formula

Watts = BTU per hour × 0.29307107

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU per hour to watts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.29307107 = 3.6633884 W. To go the other way, multiply watts by 3.4121416 (or divide by 0.29307107).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
