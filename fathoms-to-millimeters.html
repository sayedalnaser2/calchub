How it works

To convert fathoms to millimeters, multiply the number of fathoms by 1,828.8. To convert millimeters to fathoms, multiply by 0.00054680665 (or divide by 1,828.8). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Fathoms to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = fathoms × 1,828.8

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,828.8 = 22,860 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 0.00054680665 (or divide by 1,828.8).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
