Gigajoules to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert gigajoules to foot-pounds instantly. 1 GJ = 737,562,150 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to foot-pounds, multiply the number of gigajoules by 737,562,150. One gigajoule equals 737,562,150 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 1.35582e-09 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = gigajoules × 737,562,150

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 737,562,150 = 9,219,526,900 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 1.35582e-09 (or divide by 737,562,150).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
