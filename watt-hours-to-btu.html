Watt Hours to BTU Converter

Convert watt hours to BTU instantly. 1 Wh = 3.4121416 BTU.

How it works

To convert watt hours to BTU, multiply the number of watt hours by 3.4121416. One watt hour equals 3.4121416 BTU, and one BTU equals 0.29307107 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to BTU formula

Btu = watt hours × 3.4121416

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 3.4121416 = 42.65177 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 0.29307107 (or divide by 3.4121416).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
