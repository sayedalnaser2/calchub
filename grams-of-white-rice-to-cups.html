Convert grams of white rice (uncooked) to cups. 1 cup of white rice (uncooked) weighs about 185 g.

How it works

White Rice (uncooked) is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 185 g per US cup, convert grams to cups by multiplying by 0.005405.
