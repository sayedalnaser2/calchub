Millibars to Pascals Converter

Convert millibars to pascals instantly. 1 mbar = 100 Pa.

How it works

To convert millibars to pascals, multiply the number of millibars by 100. One millibar equals 100 pascals, and one pascal equals 0.01 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to Pascals formula

Pascals = millibars × 100

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 100 = 1,250 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 0.01 (or divide by 100).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
