Years to Seconds Converter

Convert years to seconds instantly. 1 years = 31,556,952 s.

How it works

To convert years to seconds, multiply the number of years by 31,556,952. One year equals 31,556,952 seconds, and one second equals 3.16887e-08 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Seconds formula

Seconds = years × 31,556,952

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 31,556,952 = 394,461,900 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 3.16887e-08 (or divide by 31,556,952).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
