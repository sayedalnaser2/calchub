Years to Seconds Converter

Convert years to seconds instantly. 1 years = 3.1557e+07 s.

How it works

To convert years to seconds, multiply the number of years by 3.1557e+07. One year equals 3.1557e+07 seconds, and one second equals 3.16887e-08 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Seconds formula

Seconds = years × 3.1557e+07

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3.1557e+07 = 3.94462e+08 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 3.16887e-08 (or divide by 3.1557e+07).
