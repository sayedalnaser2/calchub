Watt Hours to Kilojoules Converter

Convert watt hours to kilojoules instantly. 1 Wh = 3.6 kJ.

How it works

To convert watt hours to kilojoules, multiply the number of watt hours by 3.6. One watt hour equals 3.6 kilojoules, and one kilojoule equals 0.27777778 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = watt hours × 3.6

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 3.6 = 45 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 0.27777778 (or divide by 3.6).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
