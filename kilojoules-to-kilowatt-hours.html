Kilojoules to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert kilojoules to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 kJ = 0.00027777778 kWh.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of kilojoules by 0.00027777778. One kilojoule equals 0.00027777778 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 3,600 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = kilojoules × 0.00027777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00027777778 = 0.0034722222 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 3,600 (or divide by 0.00027777778).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
