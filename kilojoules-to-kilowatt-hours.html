How it works

To convert kilojoules to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of kilojoules by 0.00027777778. To convert kilowatt hours to kilojoules, multiply by 3,600 (or divide by 0.00027777778). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilojoules to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = kilojoules × 0.00027777778

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
