Micrograms to Stone Converter

Convert micrograms to stone instantly. 1 µg = 1.57473e-10 st.

How it works

To convert micrograms to stone, multiply the number of micrograms by 1.57473e-10. One microgram equals 1.57473e-10 stone, and one stone equals 6,350,293,200 micrograms. The table shows common values.

Micrograms to Stone formula

Stone = micrograms × 1.57473e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrograms to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 1.57473e-10 = 1.96841e-09 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 6,350,293,200 (or divide by 1.57473e-10).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
