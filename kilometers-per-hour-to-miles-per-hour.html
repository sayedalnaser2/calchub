Kilometers per Hour to Miles per Hour Converter

Convert kilometers per hour to miles per hour instantly. 1 km/h = 0.62137119 mph.

How it works

To convert kilometers per hour to miles per hour, multiply the number of kilometers per hour by 0.62137119. One kilometer per hour equals 0.62137119 miles per hour, and one mile per hour equals 1.609344 kilometers per hour. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Hour to Miles per Hour formula

Miles per hour = kilometers per hour × 0.62137119

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per hour to miles per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 0.62137119 = 7.7671399 mph. To go the other way, multiply miles per hour by 1.609344 (or divide by 0.62137119).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
