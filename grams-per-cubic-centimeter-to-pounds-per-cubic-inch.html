How it works

To convert grams per cubic centimeter to pounds per cubic inch, multiply the number of grams per cubic centimeter by 0.036127292. One gram per cubic centimeter equals 0.036127292 pounds per cubic inch, and one pound per cubic inch equals 27.679905 grams per cubic centimeter. The table shows common values.

Grams per Cubic Centimeter to Pounds per Cubic Inch formula

Pounds per cubic inch = grams per cubic centimeter × 0.036127292
