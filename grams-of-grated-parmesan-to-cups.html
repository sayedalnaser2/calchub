Grams of Grated Parmesan to Cups

Convert grams of grated parmesan to cups. 1 cup of grated parmesan weighs about 100 g.

How it works

Grated Parmesan is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 100 g per US cup, convert grams to cups by multiplying by 0.01.

1 US cup of grated parmesan weighs about 100 g (3.5 oz), 1 tablespoon about 6.2 g and 1 teaspoon about 2.1 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
