Cubic Meters to Liters Converter

Convert cubic meters to liters instantly. 1 m³ = 1,000 L.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to liters, multiply the number of cubic meters by 1,000. One cubic meter equals 1,000 liters, and one liter equals 0.001 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Liters formula

Liters = cubic meters × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
