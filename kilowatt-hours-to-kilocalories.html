Kilowatt Hours to Kilocalories Converter

Convert kilowatt hours to kilocalories instantly. 1 kWh = 860.42065 kcal.

How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to kilocalories, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 860.42065. One kilowatt hour equals 860.42065 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 0.0011622222 kilowatt hours. The table shows common values.

Kilowatt Hours to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = kilowatt hours × 860.42065

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 860.42065 = 10,755.258 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 0.0011622222 (or divide by 860.42065).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
