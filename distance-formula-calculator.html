Distance Formula Calculator

Find the distance between two points in 2D or 3D.

How it works

Distance = √((x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)² + (z₂-z₁)²). Leave z at 0 for 2D.

Frequently asked questions

Is it the Pythagorean theorem?

Yes, extended to coordinates.
