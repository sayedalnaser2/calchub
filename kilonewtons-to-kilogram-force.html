Convert kilonewtons to kilogram-force instantly. 1 kN = 101.97162 kgf.

How it works

To convert kilonewtons to kilogram-force, multiply the number of kilonewtons by 101.97162. One kilonewton equals 101.97162 kilogram-force, and one kilogram-force equals 0.00980665 kilonewtons. The table shows common values.

Kilonewtons to Kilogram-Force formula

Kilogram-force = kilonewtons × 101.97162

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilonewtons to kilogram-force. Multiply 12.5 × 101.97162 = 1,274.6453 kgf. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force by 0.00980665 (or divide by 101.97162).
