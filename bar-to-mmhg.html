How it works

To convert bar to mmHg, multiply the number of bar by 750.06158. One bar equals 750.06158 mmHg, and one mmHg equals 0.0013332239 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to mmHg formula

Mmhg = bar × 750.06158

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to mmHg. Multiply 12.5 × 750.06158 = 9,375.7697 mmHg. To go the other way, multiply mmHg by 0.0013332239 (or divide by 750.06158).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
