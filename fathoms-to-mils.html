Fathoms to Mils Converter

Convert fathoms to mils instantly. 1 fathoms = 72,000 mil.

How it works

To convert fathoms to mils, multiply the number of fathoms by 72,000. One fathom equals 72,000 mils, and one mil equals 1.38889e-05 fathoms. The table shows common values.

Fathoms to Mils formula

Mils = fathoms × 72,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 72,000 = 900,000 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 1.38889e-05 (or divide by 72,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
