How it works

To convert kilopascals to millibars, multiply the number of kilopascals by 10. To convert millibars to kilopascals, multiply by 0.1 (or divide by 10). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilopascals to Millibars formula

Millibars = kilopascals × 10

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 10 = 125 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.1 (or divide by 10).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
