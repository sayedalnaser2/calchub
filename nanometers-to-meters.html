Nanometers to Meters Converter

Convert nanometers to meters instantly. 1 nm = 1e-09 m.

How it works

To convert nanometers to meters, multiply the number of nanometers by 1e-09. One nanometer equals 1e-09 meters, and one meter equals 1,000,000,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Meters formula

Meters = nanometers × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
