Kilograms per Cubic Meter to Pounds per Cubic Inch Converter

Convert kilograms per cubic meter to pounds per cubic inch instantly. 1 kg/m³ = 3.61273e-05 lb/in³.

How it works

To convert kilograms per cubic meter to pounds per cubic inch, multiply the number of kilograms per cubic meter by 3.61273e-05. One kilogram per cubic meter equals 3.61273e-05 pounds per cubic inch, and one pound per cubic inch equals 27,679.905 kilograms per cubic meter. The table shows common values.

Kilograms per Cubic Meter to Pounds per Cubic Inch formula

Pounds per cubic inch = kilograms per cubic meter × 3.61273e-05
