How it works

To convert cubic feet to imperial gallons, multiply the number of cubic feet by 6.2288355. To convert imperial gallons to cubic feet, multiply by 0.16054365 (or divide by 6.2288355). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Feet to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = cubic feet × 6.2288355

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 6.2288355 = 77.860443 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 0.16054365 (or divide by 6.2288355).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
