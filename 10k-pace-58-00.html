10K Pace for a 58:00 Finish Time

To run a 10K in 58:00 you need 9:20 per mile (5:48 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A 10K is 10 km (6.21 miles), so 58:00 works out to 9:20 per mile or 5:48 per kilometer.
