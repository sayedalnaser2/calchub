Years to Minutes Converter

Convert years to minutes instantly. 1 years = 525,949.2 min.

How it works

To convert years to minutes, multiply the number of years by 525,949.2. One year equals 525,949.2 minutes, and one minute equals 1.90132e-06 years. The table shows common values.

Years to Minutes formula

Minutes = years × 525,949.2

Worked example: convert 12.5 years to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 525,949.2 = 6,574,365 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 1.90132e-06 (or divide by 525,949.2).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
