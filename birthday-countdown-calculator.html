Birthday Countdown Calculator

Count the days until your next birthday and see the age you will turn.

How it works

The calculator finds the next occurrence of your birth month and day on or after today, then counts the days between.

Frequently asked questions

What if I was born on February 29?

In non-leap years the date rolls to March 1 in this calculator.

Does it update daily?

Yes, it always uses today's date.
