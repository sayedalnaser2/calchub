Inches of Mercury to Megapascals Converter

Convert inches of mercury to megapascals instantly. 1 inHg = 0.003386389 MPa.

How it works

To convert inches of mercury to megapascals, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 0.003386389. One inch of mercury equals 0.003386389 megapascals, and one megapascal equals 295.2998 inches of mercury. The table shows common values.

Inches of Mercury to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = inches of mercury × 0.003386389

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.003386389 = 0.042329863 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 295.2998 (or divide by 0.003386389).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
