Kilowatt Hours to Gigajoules Converter

Convert kilowatt hours to gigajoules instantly. 1 kWh = 0.0036 GJ.

How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to gigajoules, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 0.0036. One kilowatt hour equals 0.0036 gigajoules, and one gigajoule equals 277.77778 kilowatt hours. The table shows common values.

Kilowatt Hours to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = kilowatt hours × 0.0036

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0036 = 0.045 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 277.77778 (or divide by 0.0036).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
