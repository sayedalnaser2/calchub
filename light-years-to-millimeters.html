Light Years to Millimeters Converter

Convert light years to millimeters instantly. 1 ly = 9.46073e+18 mm.

How it works

To convert light years to millimeters, multiply the number of light years by 9.46073e+18. One light year equals 9.46073e+18 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 1.057e-19 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = light years × 9.46073e+18

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 9.46073e+18 = 1.18259e+20 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 1.057e-19 (or divide by 9.46073e+18).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
