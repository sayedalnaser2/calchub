Convert atmospheres to inches of mercury instantly. 1 atm = 29.921252 inHg.

How it works

To convert atmospheres to inches of mercury, multiply the number of atmospheres by 29.921252. One atmosphere equals 29.921252 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 0.033421061 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Atmospheres to Inches of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = atmospheres × 29.921252

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 29.921252 = 374.01566 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 0.033421061 (or divide by 29.921252).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
