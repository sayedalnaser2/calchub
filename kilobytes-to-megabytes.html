Kilobytes to Megabytes Converter

Convert kilobytes to megabytes instantly. 1 KB = 0.001 MB.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to megabytes, multiply the number of kilobytes by 0.001. One kilobyte equals 0.001 megabytes, and one megabyte equals 1000 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Megabytes formula

Megabytes = kilobytes × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to megabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 MB. To go the other way, multiply megabytes by 1000 (or divide by 0.001).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
