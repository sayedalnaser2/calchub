Conception Date Calculator

Estimate when you conceived from your due date.

How it works

A pregnancy lasts about 266 days from conception, or 280 days from the last period. Subtracting from the due date gives an approximate conception date.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

Within a few days at best. Ultrasound dating is more precise.
