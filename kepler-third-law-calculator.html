Kepler's Third Law (Orbital Period) Calculator

Calculate an orbital period from distance to the central star.

How it works

Kepler's third law: T^2 = a^3 / M, with T in years, a in astronomical units and M in solar masses. Mars at 1.524 AU takes about 1.88 years to orbit the Sun.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AU?

The average Earth-Sun distance, about 149.6 million km.

Does the orbiting mass matter?

It matters only if comparable to the central mass.
