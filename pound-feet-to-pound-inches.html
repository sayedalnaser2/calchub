How it works

To convert pound-feet to pound-inches, multiply the number of pound-feet by 12. To convert pound-inches to pound-feet, multiply by 0.083333333 (or divide by 12). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pound-Feet to Pound-Inches formula

Pound-inches = pound-feet × 12

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-feet to pound-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 12 = 150 lb-in. To go the other way, multiply pound-inches by 0.083333333 (or divide by 12).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
