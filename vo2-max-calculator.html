VO2 Max Calculator

Estimate VO2 max from the Cooper 12-minute test or heart rate.

How it works

The Cooper test estimates VO2 max from the distance covered in 12 minutes. The heart-rate method uses the ratio of max to resting heart rate.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good VO2 max?

It depends on age and sex. Around 40-50 ml/kg/min is good for many adults.
