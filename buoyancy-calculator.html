Buoyancy Calculator

Find the buoyant force on an object and whether it floats.

How it works

Archimedes' principle: the buoyant force equals the weight of the fluid displaced, F = fluid density x volume x g. An object floats if its average density is below that of the fluid.

Frequently asked questions

What is the density of seawater?

About 1025 kg/m³.

Why do steel ships float?

Their hull encloses a lot of air, so average density is below water.
