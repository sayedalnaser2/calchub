How it works

To convert imperial quarts to teaspoons, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 230.58239. One imperial quart equals 230.58239 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.0043368447 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = imperial quarts × 230.58239

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 230.58239 = 2,882.2798 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.0043368447 (or divide by 230.58239).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
