Teaspoons of Cocoa Powder to Grams

Convert teaspoons of cocoa powder to grams. 1 cup of cocoa powder weighs about 84 g.

How it works

Cocoa Powder is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 84 g per US cup, convert teaspoons to grams by multiplying by 1.75.

1 US cup of cocoa powder weighs about 84 g (3.0 oz), 1 tablespoon about 5.2 g and 1 teaspoon about 1.8 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
