How it works

To convert psi to pascals, multiply the number of psi by 6894.7573. One psi equals 6894.7573 pascals, and one pascal equals 0.00014503774 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Pascals formula

Pascals = psi × 6894.7573

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 6894.7573 = 86184.466 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 0.00014503774 (or divide by 6894.7573).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
