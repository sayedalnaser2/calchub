Cubic Meters to Cubic Centimeters Converter

Convert cubic meters to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 m³ = 1,000,000 cm³.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cubic meters by 1,000,000. One cubic meter equals 1,000,000 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 1e-06 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cubic meters × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
