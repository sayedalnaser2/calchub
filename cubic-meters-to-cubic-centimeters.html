How it works

To convert cubic meters to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cubic meters by 1,000,000. To convert cubic centimeters to cubic meters, multiply by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Meters to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cubic meters × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
