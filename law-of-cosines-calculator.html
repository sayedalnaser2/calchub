Law of Cosines Calculator

Find the third side and all angles of a triangle from two sides and the included angle.

How it works

Law of cosines: c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab cos(C). Once c is known, the other angles follow from the law of cosines again or the law of sines.

Frequently asked questions

When do I use the law of cosines?

When you know two sides and the included angle (SAS) or all three sides (SSS).

How does it relate to Pythagoras?

When C = 90 degrees, cos C = 0 and it reduces to a^2 + b^2 = c^2.
