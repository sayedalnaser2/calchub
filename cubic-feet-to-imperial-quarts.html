How it works

To convert cubic feet to imperial quarts, multiply the number of cubic feet by 24.915342. To convert imperial quarts to cubic feet, multiply by 0.040135913 (or divide by 24.915342). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Feet to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = cubic feet × 24.915342

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
