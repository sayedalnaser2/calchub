How it works

To convert seconds to minutes, multiply the number of seconds by 0.016666667. To convert minutes to seconds, multiply by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Seconds to Minutes formula

Minutes = seconds × 0.016666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016666667 = 0.20833333 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667).
