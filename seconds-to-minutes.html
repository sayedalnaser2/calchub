Seconds to Minutes Converter

Convert seconds to minutes instantly. 1 s = 0.016666667 min.

How it works

To convert seconds to minutes, multiply the number of seconds by 0.016666667. One second equals 0.016666667 minutes, and one minute equals 60 seconds. The table shows common values.

Seconds to Minutes formula

Minutes = seconds × 0.016666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016666667 = 0.20833333 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667).
