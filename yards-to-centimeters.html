Yards to Centimeters Converter

Convert yards to centimeters instantly. 1 yd = 91.44 cm.

How it works

To convert yards to centimeters, multiply the number of yards by 91.44. One yard equals 91.44 centimeters, and one centimeter equals 0.010936133 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = yards × 91.44

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 91.44 = 1143 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 0.010936133 (or divide by 91.44).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
