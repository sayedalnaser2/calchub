Hours to Microseconds Converter

Convert hours to microseconds instantly. 1 h = 3,600,000,000 µs.

How it works

To convert hours to microseconds, multiply the number of hours by 3,600,000,000. One hour equals 3,600,000,000 microseconds, and one microsecond equals 2.77778e-10 hours. The table shows common values.

Hours to Microseconds formula

Microseconds = hours × 3,600,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to microseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600,000,000 = 45,000,000,000 µs. To go the other way, multiply microseconds by 2.77778e-10 (or divide by 3,600,000,000).
