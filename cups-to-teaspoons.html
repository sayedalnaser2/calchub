Cups to Teaspoons Converter

Convert cups to teaspoons instantly. 1 cup = 48 tsp.

How it works

To convert cups to teaspoons, multiply the number of cups by 48. One cup equals 48 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.020833333 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = cups × 48

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 48 = 600 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.020833333 (or divide by 48).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
