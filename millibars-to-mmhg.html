Millibars to mmHg Converter

Convert millibars to mmHg instantly. 1 mbar = 0.75006158 mmHg.

How it works

To convert millibars to mmHg, multiply the number of millibars by 0.75006158. One millibar equals 0.75006158 mmHg, and one mmHg equals 1.3332239 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to mmHg formula

Mmhg = millibars × 0.75006158

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to mmHg. Multiply 12.5 × 0.75006158 = 9.3757697 mmHg. To go the other way, multiply mmHg by 1.3332239 (or divide by 0.75006158).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
