Convert oil barrels to cups instantly. 1 bbl = 672 cup.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to cups, multiply the number of oil barrels by 672. One oil barrel equals 672 cups, and one cup equals 0.0014880952 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Cups formula

Cups = oil barrels × 672

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 672 = 8,400 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.0014880952 (or divide by 672).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
