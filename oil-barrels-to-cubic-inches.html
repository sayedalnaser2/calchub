Convert oil barrels to cubic inches instantly. 1 bbl = 9,702 in³.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to cubic inches, multiply the number of oil barrels by 9,702. One oil barrel equals 9,702 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.00010307153 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = oil barrels × 9,702

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 9,702 = 121,275 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.00010307153 (or divide by 9,702).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
