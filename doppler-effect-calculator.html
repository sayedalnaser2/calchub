Doppler Effect Calculator

Calculate the observed frequency when a source or observer is moving.

How it works

For sound: f' = f x (c + v_observer) / (c - v_source) when approaching. Use the opposite signs when moving apart. This is why an ambulance siren drops in pitch as it passes.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for light?

Light needs the relativistic Doppler formula.

What speed is Mach 1?

About 343 m/s in air at 20 °C.
