Therms to Gigajoules Converter

Convert therms to gigajoules instantly. 1 therms = 0.1054804 GJ.

How it works

To convert therms to gigajoules, multiply the number of therms by 0.1054804. One therm equals 0.1054804 gigajoules, and one gigajoule equals 9.4804343 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = therms × 0.1054804

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.1054804 = 1.318505 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 9.4804343 (or divide by 0.1054804).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
