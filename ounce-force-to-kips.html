Convert ounce-force to kips instantly. 1 ozf = 6.25e-05 kip.

How it works

To convert ounce-force to kips, multiply the number of ounce-force by 6.25e-05. One ounce-force equals 6.25e-05 kips, and one kip equals 16,000 ounce-force. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Force to Kips formula

Kips = ounce-force × 6.25e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounce-force to kips. Multiply 12.5 × 6.25e-05 = 0.00078125 kip. To go the other way, multiply kips by 16,000 (or divide by 6.25e-05).
