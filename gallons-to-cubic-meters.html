Gallons to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert gallons to cubic meters instantly. 1 gal = 0.0037854118 m³.

How it works

To convert gallons to cubic meters, multiply the number of gallons by 0.0037854118. One gallon equals 0.0037854118 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 264.17205 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = gallons × 0.0037854118

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0037854118 = 0.047317647 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 264.17205 (or divide by 0.0037854118).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
