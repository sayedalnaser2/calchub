Newton Millimeters to Pound-Inches Converter

Convert newton millimeters to pound-inches instantly. 1 N·mm = 0.0088507458 lb-in.

How it works

To convert newton millimeters to pound-inches, multiply the number of newton millimeters by 0.0088507458. One newton millimeter equals 0.0088507458 pound-inches, and one pound-inch equals 112.98483 newton millimeters. The table shows common values.

Newton Millimeters to Pound-Inches formula

Pound-inches = newton millimeters × 0.0088507458

Worked example: convert 12.5 newton millimeters to pound-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0088507458 = 0.11063432 lb-in. To go the other way, multiply pound-inches by 112.98483 (or divide by 0.0088507458).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
