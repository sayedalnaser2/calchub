Convert mmHg to psi instantly. 1 mmHg = 0.019336777 psi.

How it works

To convert mmHg to psi, multiply the number of mmHg by 0.019336777. One mmHg equals 0.019336777 psi, and one psi equals 51.714925 mmHg. The table shows common values.

mmHg to PSI formula

Psi = mmHg × 0.019336777

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to psi. Multiply 12.5 × 0.019336777 = 0.24170972 psi. To go the other way, multiply psi by 51.714925 (or divide by 0.019336777).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
