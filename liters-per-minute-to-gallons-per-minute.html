Liters per Minute to Gallons per Minute Converter

Convert liters per minute to gallons per minute instantly. 1 L/min = 0.26417205 gal/min.

How it works

To convert liters per minute to gallons per minute, multiply the number of liters per minute by 0.26417205. One liter per minute equals 0.26417205 gallons per minute, and one gallon per minute equals 3.7854118 liters per minute. The table shows common values.

Liters per Minute to Gallons per Minute formula

Gallons per minute = liters per minute × 0.26417205

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per minute to gallons per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 0.26417205 = 3.3021507 gal/min. To go the other way, multiply gallons per minute by 3.7854118 (or divide by 0.26417205).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
