Weeks to Days Converter

Convert weeks to days instantly. 1 weeks = 7 days.

How it works

To convert weeks to days, multiply the number of weeks by 7. One week equals 7 days, and one day equals 0.14285714 weeks. The table shows common values.

Weeks to Days formula

Days = weeks × 7

Worked example: convert 12.5 weeks to days. Multiply 12.5 × 7 = 87.5 days. To go the other way, multiply days by 0.14285714 (or divide by 7).
