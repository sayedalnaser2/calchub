Newton Millimeters to Ounce-Inches Converter

Convert newton millimeters to ounce-inches instantly. 1 N·mm = 0.14161193 oz-in.

How it works

To convert newton millimeters to ounce-inches, multiply the number of newton millimeters by 0.14161193. One newton millimeter equals 0.14161193 ounce-inches, and one ounce-inch equals 7.0615518 newton millimeters. The table shows common values.

Newton Millimeters to Ounce-Inches formula

Ounce-inches = newton millimeters × 0.14161193

Worked example: convert 12.5 newton millimeters to ounce-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.14161193 = 1.7701492 oz-in. To go the other way, multiply ounce-inches by 7.0615518 (or divide by 0.14161193).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
