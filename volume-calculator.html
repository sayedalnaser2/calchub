Volume Calculator

Calculate the volume of a sphere, cylinder, cone or cube.

How it works

Sphere: 4/3 πr³. Cylinder: πr²h. Cone: ⅓πr²h. Cube: a³. Height is ignored for the sphere and cube.

Frequently asked questions

What units does the volume use?

Cubic units matching your input. If you enter centimetres the result is in cm³, and 1,000 cm³ equals one litre.
