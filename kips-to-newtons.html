How it works

To convert kips to newtons, multiply the number of kips by 4,448.2216. To convert newtons to kips, multiply by 0.00022480894 (or divide by 4,448.2216). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kips to Newtons formula

Newtons = kips × 4,448.2216
