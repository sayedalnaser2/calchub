Furlongs to Fathoms Converter

Convert furlongs to fathoms instantly. 1 furlongs = 110 fathoms.

How it works

To convert furlongs to fathoms, multiply the number of furlongs by 110. One furlong equals 110 fathoms, and one fathom equals 0.0090909091 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = furlongs × 110

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 110 = 1,375 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 0.0090909091 (or divide by 110).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
