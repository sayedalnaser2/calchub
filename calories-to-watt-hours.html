Calories to Watt Hours Converter

Convert calories to watt hours instantly. 1 cal = 0.0011622222 Wh.

How it works

To convert calories to watt hours, multiply the number of calories by 0.0011622222. One calorie equals 0.0011622222 watt hours, and one watt hour equals 860.42065 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = calories × 0.0011622222

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0011622222 = 0.014527778 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 860.42065 (or divide by 0.0011622222).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
