Megawatts to Milliwatts Converter

Convert megawatts to milliwatts instantly. 1 MW = 1,000,000,000 mW.

How it works

To convert megawatts to milliwatts, multiply the number of megawatts by 1,000,000,000. One megawatt equals 1,000,000,000 milliwatts, and one milliwatt equals 1e-09 megawatts. The table shows common values.

Megawatts to Milliwatts formula

Milliwatts = megawatts × 1,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megawatts to milliwatts. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000 mW. To go the other way, multiply milliwatts by 1e-09 (or divide by 1,000,000,000).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
