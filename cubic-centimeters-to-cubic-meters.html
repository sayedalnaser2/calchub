Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to cubic meters instantly. 1 cm³ = 1e-06 m³.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to cubic meters, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 1e-06. One cubic centimeter equals 1e-06 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 1,000,000 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = cubic centimeters × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
