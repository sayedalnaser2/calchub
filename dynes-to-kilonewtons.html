Dynes to Kilonewtons Converter

Convert dynes to kilonewtons instantly. 1 dyn = 1e-08 kN.

How it works

To convert dynes to kilonewtons, multiply the number of dynes by 1e-08. One dyne equals 1e-08 kilonewtons, and one kilonewton equals 100,000,000 dynes. The table shows common values.

Dynes to Kilonewtons formula

Kilonewtons = dynes × 1e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 dynes to kilonewtons. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-08 = 1.25e-07 kN. To go the other way, multiply kilonewtons by 100,000,000 (or divide by 1e-08).
