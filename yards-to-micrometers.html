Yards to Micrometers Converter

Convert yards to micrometers instantly. 1 yd = 914,400 µm.

How it works

To convert yards to micrometers, multiply the number of yards by 914,400. One yard equals 914,400 micrometers, and one micrometer equals 1.09361e-06 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = yards × 914,400

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 914,400 = 11,430,000 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 1.09361e-06 (or divide by 914,400).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
