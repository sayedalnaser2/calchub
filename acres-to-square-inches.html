Acres to Square Inches Converter

Convert acres to square inches instantly. 1 acre = 6,272,640 in².

How it works

To convert acres to square inches, multiply the number of acres by 6,272,640. One acre equals 6,272,640 square inches, and one square inch equals 1.59423e-07 acres. The table shows common values.

Acres to Square Inches formula

Square inches = acres × 6,272,640

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 6,272,640 = 78,408,000 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 1.59423e-07 (or divide by 6,272,640).
