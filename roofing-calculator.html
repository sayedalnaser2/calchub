Roofing Calculator

Estimate roof area, roofing squares and shingle bundles from the house footprint and pitch.

How it works

Footprint area including overhangs is multiplied by sqrt(1 + (rise/12)^2) to account for the slope. One roofing square covers 100 sq ft, and most asphalt shingles come three bundles to a square. This assumes a simple gable roof.

Frequently asked questions

What is a roofing square?

A roofing square is 100 square feet of roof.

Does this cover hips and valleys?

Not exactly. Complex roofs need extra waste, so add 5-10% more.
