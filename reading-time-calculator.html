Reading Time Calculator

Estimate how long it takes to read a text based on its word count.

How it works

Reading time = words / words per minute. The average adult reads about 238 words per minute of non-fiction on paper; screens and technical text are slower.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a 1,000-word article?

About four minutes at an average pace.

What is a typical page word count?

About 250-300 words in a printed book.
