Running Pace Converter (min/mile, min/km, mph)

Convert running pace between minutes per mile, minutes per kilometer, mph and km/h.

How it works

Speed in mph = 3600 / seconds per mile. One mile is 1.609344 km, so pace per mile = pace per km x 1.609344. Race times assume a constant pace.

Frequently asked questions

What pace is a 4-hour marathon?

About 9:09 per mile or 5:41 per kilometer.

How do I read a pace?

8:30 per mile means each mile takes 8 minutes 30 seconds.
