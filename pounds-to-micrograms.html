How it works

To convert pounds to micrograms, multiply the number of pounds by 453,592,370. To convert micrograms to pounds, multiply by 2.20462e-09 (or divide by 453,592,370). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pounds to Micrograms formula

Micrograms = pounds × 453,592,370

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to micrograms. Multiply 12.5 × 453,592,370 = 5,669,904,600 µg. To go the other way, multiply micrograms by 2.20462e-09 (or divide by 453,592,370).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
