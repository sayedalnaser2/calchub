Nanoseconds to Microseconds Converter

Convert nanoseconds to microseconds instantly. 1 ns = 0.001 µs.

How it works

To convert nanoseconds to microseconds, multiply the number of nanoseconds by 0.001. One nanosecond equals 0.001 microseconds, and one microsecond equals 1,000 nanoseconds. The table shows common values.

Nanoseconds to Microseconds formula

Microseconds = nanoseconds × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanoseconds to microseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 µs. To go the other way, multiply microseconds by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).
