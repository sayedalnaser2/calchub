Cups of Maple Syrup to Grams

Convert cups of maple syrup to grams. 1 cup of maple syrup weighs about 322 g.

How it works

Maple Syrup is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 322 g per US cup, convert cups to grams by multiplying by 322.

1 US cup of maple syrup weighs about 322 g (11.4 oz), 1 tablespoon about 20.1 g and 1 teaspoon about 6.7 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
