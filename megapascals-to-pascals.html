Megapascals to Pascals Converter

Convert megapascals to pascals instantly. 1 MPa = 1,000,000 Pa.

How it works

To convert megapascals to pascals, multiply the number of megapascals by 1,000,000. One megapascal equals 1,000,000 pascals, and one pascal equals 1e-06 megapascals. The table shows common values.

Megapascals to Pascals formula

Pascals = megapascals × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megapascals to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
