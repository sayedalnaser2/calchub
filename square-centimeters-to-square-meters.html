Square Centimeters to Square Meters Converter

Convert square centimeters to square meters instantly. 1 cm² = 0.0001 m².

How it works

To convert square centimeters to square meters, multiply the number of square centimeters by 0.0001. One square centimeter equals 0.0001 square meters, and one square meter equals 10,000 square centimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Centimeters to Square Meters formula

Square meters = square centimeters × 0.0001

Worked example: convert 12.5 square centimeters to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0001 = 0.00125 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 10,000 (or divide by 0.0001).
