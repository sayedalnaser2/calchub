How it works

To convert torr to millibars, multiply the number of torr by 1.3332237. To convert millibars to torr, multiply by 0.75006168 (or divide by 1.3332237). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Torr to Millibars formula

Millibars = torr × 1.3332237

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3332237 = 16.665296 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.75006168 (or divide by 1.3332237).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
