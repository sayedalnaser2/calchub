BMI for 6 ft 4 in (193 cm)

Check your BMI at 6 ft 4 in and see the healthy weight range for this height.

How it works

BMI = 703 x weight (lb) / height (in) squared. At 6 ft 4 in (76 inches), a healthy BMI of 18.5-24.9 corresponds to 152-205 lb.

BMI is a screening tool. It does not measure body fat directly and can misclassify very muscular people, older adults and some ethnic groups. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy weight at 6 ft 4 in?

Roughly 152 to 205 lb for a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9.

Is BMI accurate?

It is a rough screening measure; body composition and age matter.
