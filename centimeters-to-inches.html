Centimeters to Inches Converter

Convert centimeters to inches instantly. 1 cm = 0.39370079 in.

How it works

To convert centimeters to inches, multiply the number of centimeters by 0.39370079. One centimeter equals 0.39370079 inches, and one inch equals 2.54 centimeters. The table shows common values.

Centimeters to Inches formula

Inches = centimeters × 0.39370079

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.39370079 = 4.9212598 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 2.54 (or divide by 0.39370079).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
