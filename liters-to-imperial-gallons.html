Liters to Imperial Gallons Converter

Convert liters to imperial gallons instantly. 1 L = 0.21996925 imp gal.

How it works

To convert liters to imperial gallons, multiply the number of liters by 0.21996925. One liter equals 0.21996925 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 4.54609 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = liters × 0.21996925

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.21996925 = 2.7496156 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 4.54609 (or divide by 0.21996925).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
