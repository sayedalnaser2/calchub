How it works

To convert ounce-force to dynes, multiply the number of ounce-force by 27,801.385. One ounce-force equals 27,801.385 dynes, and one dyne equals 3.59694e-05 ounce-force. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Force to Dynes formula

Dynes = ounce-force × 27,801.385

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounce-force to dynes. Multiply 12.5 × 27,801.385 = 347,517.31 dyn. To go the other way, multiply dynes by 3.59694e-05 (or divide by 27,801.385).
