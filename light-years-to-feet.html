Light Years to Feet Converter

Convert light years to feet instantly. 1 ly = 3.10391e+16 ft.

How it works

To convert light years to feet, multiply the number of light years by 3.10391e+16. One light year equals 3.10391e+16 feet, and one foot equals 3.22174e-17 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Feet formula

Feet = light years × 3.10391e+16

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3.10391e+16 = 3.87989e+17 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 3.22174e-17 (or divide by 3.10391e+16).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
