How it works

To convert micrograms to carats, multiply the number of micrograms by 5e-06. To convert carats to micrograms, multiply by 200,000 (or divide by 5e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Micrograms to Carats formula

Carats = micrograms × 5e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrograms to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 5e-06 = 6.25e-05 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 200,000 (or divide by 5e-06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
