Imperial Quarts to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert imperial quarts to cubic meters instantly. 1 imp qt = 0.0011365225 m³.

How it works

To convert imperial quarts to cubic meters, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 0.0011365225. One imperial quart equals 0.0011365225 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 879.87699 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = imperial quarts × 0.0011365225

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0011365225 = 0.014206531 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 879.87699 (or divide by 0.0011365225).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
