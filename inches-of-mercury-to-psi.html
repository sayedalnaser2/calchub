Inches Of Mercury to PSI Converter

Convert inches of mercury to psi instantly. 1 inHg = 0.4911542 psi.

How it works

To convert inches of mercury to psi, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 0.4911542. One inch of mercury equals 0.4911542 psi, and one psi equals 2.0360205 inches of mercury. The table shows common values.

Inches Of Mercury to PSI formula

Psi = inches of mercury × 0.4911542

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to psi. Multiply 12.5 × 0.4911542 = 6.1394275 psi. To go the other way, multiply psi by 2.0360205 (or divide by 0.4911542).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
