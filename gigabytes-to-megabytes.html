Gigabytes to Megabytes Converter

Convert gigabytes to megabytes instantly. 1 GB = 1,000 MB.

How it works

To convert gigabytes to megabytes, multiply the number of gigabytes by 1,000. One gigabyte equals 1,000 megabytes, and one megabyte equals 0.001 gigabytes. The table shows common values.

Gigabytes to Megabytes formula

Megabytes = gigabytes × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabytes to megabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 MB. To go the other way, multiply megabytes by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
