Pizza Calculator (How Many Pizzas to Order)

Work out how many pizzas to order for a party.

How it works

Total slices = adults x slices each + kids x slices each. Divide by slices per pizza and round up. A large pizza usually has 8 slices.

Frequently asked questions

How many slices does an adult eat?

Typically 2-4, depending on sides.

Should I order extra?

Rounding up usually covers it; add one more if the party is long.
