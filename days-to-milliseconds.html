Days to Milliseconds Converter

Convert days to milliseconds instantly. 1 day = 86,400,000 ms.

How it works

To convert days to milliseconds, multiply the number of days by 86,400,000. One day equals 86,400,000 milliseconds, and one millisecond equals 1.15741e-08 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Milliseconds formula

Milliseconds = days × 86,400,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to milliseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 86,400,000 = 1,080,000,000 ms. To go the other way, multiply milliseconds by 1.15741e-08 (or divide by 86,400,000).
