Feet to Kilometers Converter

Convert feet to kilometers instantly. 1 ft = 0.0003048 km.

How it works

To convert feet to kilometers, multiply the number of feet by 0.0003048. One foot equals 0.0003048 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 3,280.8399 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = feet × 0.0003048

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0003048 = 0.00381 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 3,280.8399 (or divide by 0.0003048).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
