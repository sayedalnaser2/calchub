Law of Sines Calculator

Solve a triangle from one side and two angles using the law of sines.

How it works

Law of sines: a / sin A = b / sin B = c / sin C = 2R. The angles of a triangle sum to 180 degrees, so the third angle is 180 - A - B.

Frequently asked questions

When is the law of sines ambiguous?

In the SSA case, two different triangles can sometimes fit the data.

What is R?

The radius of the circle passing through all three vertices.
