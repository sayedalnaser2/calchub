Convert numbers to Roman numerals and back.

How it works

Roman numerals use I, V, X, L, C, D and M. A smaller symbol before a larger one is subtracted (IV = 4).

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no zero?

The Roman system had no symbol for zero.
