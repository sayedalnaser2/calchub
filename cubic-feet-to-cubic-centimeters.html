Cubic Feet to Cubic Centimeters Converter

Convert cubic feet to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 ft³ = 28,316.847 cm³.

How it works

To convert cubic feet to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cubic feet by 28,316.847. One cubic foot equals 28,316.847 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 3.53147e-05 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cubic feet × 28,316.847

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 28,316.847 = 353,960.58 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 3.53147e-05 (or divide by 28,316.847).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
