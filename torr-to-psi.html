Convert torr to psi instantly. 1 Torr = 0.019336775 psi.

How it works

To convert torr to psi, multiply the number of torr by 0.019336775. One torr equals 0.019336775 psi, and one psi equals 51.714933 torr. The table shows common values.

Torr to PSI formula

Psi = torr × 0.019336775

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to psi. Multiply 12.5 × 0.019336775 = 0.24170968 psi. To go the other way, multiply psi by 51.714933 (or divide by 0.019336775).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
