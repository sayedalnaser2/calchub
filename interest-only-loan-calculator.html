Interest-Only Loan Calculator

Compare interest-only payments with the later full payment.

How it works

During the interest-only period you pay just the interest, so the balance does not fall. After it ends the full balance is repaid over the remaining term, making payments higher.

Frequently asked questions

Who uses interest-only loans?

Investors and borrowers expecting higher income later. The balance is not reduced during that period.
