Mils to Nanometers Converter

Convert mils to nanometers instantly. 1 mil = 25,400 nm.

How it works

To convert mils to nanometers, multiply the number of mils by 25,400. One mil equals 25,400 nanometers, and one nanometer equals 3.93701e-05 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = mils × 25,400

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 25,400 = 317,500 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 3.93701e-05 (or divide by 25,400).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
