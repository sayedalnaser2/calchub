How it works

To convert therms to BTU, multiply the number of therms by 99,976.129. One therm equals 99,976.129 BTU, and one BTU equals 1.00024e-05 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to BTU formula

Btu = therms × 99,976.129

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 99,976.129 = 1,249,701.6 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 1.00024e-05 (or divide by 99,976.129).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
