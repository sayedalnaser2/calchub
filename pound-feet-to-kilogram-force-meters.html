Pound-Feet to Kilogram-Force Meters Converter

Convert pound-feet to kilogram-force meters instantly. 1 lb-ft = 0.13825495 kgf·m.

How it works

To convert pound-feet to kilogram-force meters, multiply the number of pound-feet by 0.13825495. One pound-foot equals 0.13825495 kilogram-force meters, and one kilogram-force meter equals 7.2330139 pound-feet. The table shows common values.

Pound-Feet to Kilogram-Force Meters formula

Kilogram-force meters = pound-feet × 0.13825495

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-feet to kilogram-force meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.13825495 = 1.7281869 kgf·m. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force meters by 7.2330139 (or divide by 0.13825495).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
