Teaspoons to Pints Converter

Convert teaspoons to pints instantly. 1 tsp = 0.010416667 pt.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to pints, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.010416667. One teaspoon equals 0.010416667 pints, and one pint equals 96 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Pints formula

Pints = teaspoons × 0.010416667

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.010416667 = 0.13020833 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 96 (or divide by 0.010416667).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
