How it works

To convert cubic inches to cubic yards, multiply the number of cubic inches by 2.14335e-05. To convert cubic yards to cubic inches, multiply by 46,656 (or divide by 2.14335e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Inches to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = cubic inches × 2.14335e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 2.14335e-05 = 0.00026791838 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 46,656 (or divide by 2.14335e-05).
