Hourly to Salary Calculator

Convert an hourly wage into weekly, monthly and annual pay.

How it works

Annual pay is the hourly rate times hours per week times weeks worked. Monthly pay divides the annual figure by 12, and biweekly pay divides it by 26. Results are gross, before taxes and deductions.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $25 an hour per year?

At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, $25 an hour is $52,000 a year before taxes.

Should I subtract unpaid weeks?

Yes. Lower the weeks worked per year if you take unpaid time off.
