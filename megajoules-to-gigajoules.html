How it works

To convert megajoules to gigajoules, multiply the number of megajoules by 0.001. To convert gigajoules to megajoules, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Megajoules to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = megajoules × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
