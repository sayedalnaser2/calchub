How it works

To convert minutes to hours, multiply the number of minutes by 0.016666667. To convert hours to minutes, multiply by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Minutes to Hours formula

Hours = minutes × 0.016666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016666667 = 0.20833333 h. To go the other way, multiply hours by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667).
