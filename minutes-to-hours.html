Minutes to Hours Converter

Convert minutes to hours instantly. 1 min = 0.016666667 h.

How it works

To convert minutes to hours, multiply the number of minutes by 0.016666667. One minute equals 0.016666667 hours, and one hour equals 60 minutes. The table shows common values.

Minutes to Hours formula

Hours = minutes × 0.016666667

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016666667 = 0.20833333 h. To go the other way, multiply hours by 60 (or divide by 0.016666667).
