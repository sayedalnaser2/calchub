Dollar Cost Averaging Calculator

Find your average cost per share when investing a fixed amount at different prices.

How it works

Investing a fixed amount each period buys more shares when prices are low and fewer when high. Your average cost is total invested divided by total shares, which is below the simple average price whenever prices vary.

Frequently asked questions

Does dollar cost averaging guarantee profit?

No. It reduces timing risk but does not remove market risk.

Why is my average cost lower than the average price?

Because you buy more shares at low prices (a harmonic mean effect).
