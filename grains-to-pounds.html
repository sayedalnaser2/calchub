How it works

To convert grains to pounds, multiply the number of grains by 0.00014285714. One grain equals 0.00014285714 pounds, and one pound equals 7,000 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Pounds formula

Pounds = grains × 0.00014285714

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00014285714 = 0.0017857143 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 7,000 (or divide by 0.00014285714).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
