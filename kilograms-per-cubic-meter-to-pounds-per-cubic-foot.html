Kilograms per Cubic Meter to Pounds per Cubic Foot Converter

Convert kilograms per cubic meter to pounds per cubic foot instantly. 1 kg/m³ = 0.062427961 lb/ft³.

How it works

To convert kilograms per cubic meter to pounds per cubic foot, multiply the number of kilograms per cubic meter by 0.062427961. One kilogram per cubic meter equals 0.062427961 pounds per cubic foot, and one pound per cubic foot equals 16.018463 kilograms per cubic meter. The table shows common values.

Kilograms per Cubic Meter to Pounds per Cubic Foot formula

Pounds per cubic foot = kilograms per cubic meter × 0.062427961

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms per cubic meter to pounds per cubic foot. Multiply 12.5 × 0.062427961 = 0.78034951 lb/ft³. To go the other way, multiply pounds per cubic foot by 16.018463 (or divide by 0.062427961).
