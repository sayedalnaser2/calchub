Torr to Pascals Converter

Convert torr to pascals instantly. 1 Torr = 133.32237 Pa.

How it works

To convert torr to pascals, multiply the number of torr by 133.32237. One torr equals 133.32237 pascals, and one pascal equals 0.0075006168 torr. The table shows common values.

Torr to Pascals formula

Pascals = torr × 133.32237

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 133.32237 = 1,666.5296 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 0.0075006168 (or divide by 133.32237).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
