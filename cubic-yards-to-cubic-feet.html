Convert cubic yards to cubic feet instantly. 1 yd³ = 27 ft³.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to cubic feet, multiply the number of cubic yards by 27. One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 0.037037037 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = cubic yards × 27

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 27 = 337.5 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 0.037037037 (or divide by 27).
