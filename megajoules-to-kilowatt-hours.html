Megajoules to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert megajoules to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 MJ = 0.27777778 kWh.

How it works

To convert megajoules to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of megajoules by 0.27777778. One megajoule equals 0.27777778 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 3.6 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = megajoules × 0.27777778

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.27777778 = 3.4722222 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 3.6 (or divide by 0.27777778).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
