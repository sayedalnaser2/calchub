Megabits to Petabytes Converter

Convert megabits to petabytes instantly. 1 Mb = 1.25e-10 PB.

How it works

To convert megabits to petabytes, multiply the number of megabits by 1.25e-10. One megabit equals 1.25e-10 petabytes, and one petabyte equals 8,000,000,000 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Petabytes formula

Petabytes = megabits × 1.25e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to petabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.25e-10 = 1.5625e-09 PB. To go the other way, multiply petabytes by 8,000,000,000 (or divide by 1.25e-10).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
