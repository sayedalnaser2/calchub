Megabytes to Terabytes Converter

Convert megabytes to terabytes instantly. 1 MB = 1e-06 TB.

How it works

To convert megabytes to terabytes, multiply the number of megabytes by 1e-06. One megabyte equals 1e-06 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 1e+06 megabytes. The table shows common values.

Megabytes to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = megabytes × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabytes to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 1e+06 (or divide by 1e-06).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
