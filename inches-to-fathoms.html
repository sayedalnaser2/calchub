Inches to Fathoms Converter

Convert inches to fathoms instantly. 1 in = 0.013888889 fathoms.

How it works

To convert inches to fathoms, multiply the number of inches by 0.013888889. One inch equals 0.013888889 fathoms, and one fathom equals 72 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = inches × 0.013888889

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.013888889 = 0.17361111 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 72 (or divide by 0.013888889).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
