How it works

To convert inches to fathoms, multiply the number of inches by 0.013888889. To convert fathoms to inches, multiply by 72 (or divide by 0.013888889). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Fathoms = inches × 0.013888889

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
