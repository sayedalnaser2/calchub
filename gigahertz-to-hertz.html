Gigahertz to Hertz Converter

Convert gigahertz to hertz instantly. 1 GHz = 1,000,000,000 Hz.

How it works

To convert gigahertz to hertz, multiply the number of gigahertz by 1,000,000,000. One gigahertz equals 1,000,000,000 hertz, and one hertz equals 1e-09 gigahertz. The table shows common values.

Gigahertz to Hertz formula

Hertz = gigahertz × 1,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigahertz to hertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000 Hz. To go the other way, multiply hertz by 1e-09 (or divide by 1,000,000,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
