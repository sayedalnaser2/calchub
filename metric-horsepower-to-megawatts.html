Metric Horsepower to Megawatts Converter

Convert metric horsepower to megawatts instantly. 1 PS = 0.00073549875 MW.

How it works

To convert metric horsepower to megawatts, multiply the number of metric horsepower by 0.00073549875. One metric horsepower equals 0.00073549875 megawatts, and one megawatt equals 1,359.6216 metric horsepower. The table shows common values.

Metric Horsepower to Megawatts formula

Megawatts = metric horsepower × 0.00073549875

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric horsepower to megawatts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00073549875 = 0.0091937344 MW. To go the other way, multiply megawatts by 1,359.6216 (or divide by 0.00073549875).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
