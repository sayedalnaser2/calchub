Square Inches to Hectares Converter

Convert square inches to hectares instantly. 1 in² = 6.4516e-08 ha.

How it works

To convert square inches to hectares, multiply the number of square inches by 6.4516e-08. One square inch equals 6.4516e-08 hectares, and one hectare equals 15,500,031 square inches. The table shows common values.

Square Inches to Hectares formula

Hectares = square inches × 6.4516e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 6.4516e-08 = 8.0645e-07 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 15,500,031 (or divide by 6.4516e-08).
