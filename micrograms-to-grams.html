Micrograms to Grams Converter

Convert micrograms to grams instantly. 1 µg = 1e-06 g.

How it works

To convert micrograms to grams, multiply the number of micrograms by 1e-06. One microgram equals 1e-06 grams, and one gram equals 1,000,000 micrograms. The table shows common values.

Micrograms to Grams formula

Grams = micrograms × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrograms to grams. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 g. To go the other way, multiply grams by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
