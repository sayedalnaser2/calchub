Binomial Expansion Calculator

Expand (a + b)^n using binomial coefficients.

How it works

The binomial theorem expands (a+b)^n into terms with coefficients from row n of Pascal's triangle.

Frequently asked questions

What is Pascal's triangle?

A triangle where each entry is the sum of the two above it.
