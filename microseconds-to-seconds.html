Microseconds to Seconds Converter

Convert microseconds to seconds instantly. 1 µs = 1e-06 s.

How it works

To convert microseconds to seconds, multiply the number of microseconds by 1e-06. One microsecond equals 1e-06 seconds, and one second equals 1,000,000 microseconds. The table shows common values.

Microseconds to Seconds formula

Seconds = microseconds × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 microseconds to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).
