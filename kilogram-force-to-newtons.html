Kilogram-Force to Newtons Converter

Convert kilogram-force to newtons instantly. 1 kgf = 9.80665 N.

How it works

To convert kilogram-force to newtons, multiply the number of kilogram-force by 9.80665. One kilogram-force equals 9.80665 newtons, and one newton equals 0.10197162 kilogram-force. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force to Newtons formula

Newtons = kilogram-force × 9.80665

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force to newtons. Multiply 12.5 × 9.80665 = 122.58312 N. To go the other way, multiply newtons by 0.10197162 (or divide by 9.80665).
