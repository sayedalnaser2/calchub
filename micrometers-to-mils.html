Micrometers to Mils Converter

Convert micrometers to mils instantly. 1 µm = 0.039370079 mil.

How it works

To convert micrometers to mils, multiply the number of micrometers by 0.039370079. One micrometer equals 0.039370079 mils, and one mil equals 25.4 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Mils formula

Mils = micrometers × 0.039370079

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 0.039370079 = 0.49212598 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 25.4 (or divide by 0.039370079).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
