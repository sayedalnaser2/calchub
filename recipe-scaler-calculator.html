Recipe Scaler Calculator

Scale a recipe ingredient up or down for a different number of servings.

How it works

The scale factor is desired servings divided by original servings. Multiply every ingredient amount by that factor. Seasonings, baking time and pan size may need extra adjustment.

Frequently asked questions

Do cooking times scale too?

Not directly. Larger batches often need longer cooking but not proportionally.

Can I scale baking recipes?

Usually yes within about double or half, but check pan size and leavening.
