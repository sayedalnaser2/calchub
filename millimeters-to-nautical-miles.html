Millimeters to Nautical Miles Converter

Convert millimeters to nautical miles instantly. 1 mm = 5.39957e-07 nmi.

How it works

To convert millimeters to nautical miles, multiply the number of millimeters by 5.39957e-07. One millimeter equals 5.39957e-07 nautical miles, and one nautical mile equals 1,852,000 millimeters. The table shows common values.

Millimeters to Nautical Miles formula

Nautical miles = millimeters × 5.39957e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to nautical miles. Multiply 12.5 × 5.39957e-07 = 6.74946e-06 nmi. To go the other way, multiply nautical miles by 1,852,000 (or divide by 5.39957e-07).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
