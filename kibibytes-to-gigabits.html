Kibibytes to Gigabits Converter

Convert kibibytes to gigabits instantly. 1 KiB = 8.192e-06 Gb.

How it works

To convert kibibytes to gigabits, multiply the number of kibibytes by 8.192e-06. One kibibyte equals 8.192e-06 gigabits, and one gigabit equals 122,070.31 kibibytes. The table shows common values.

Kibibytes to Gigabits formula

Gigabits = kibibytes × 8.192e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kibibytes to gigabits. Multiply 12.5 × 8.192e-06 = 0.0001024 Gb. To go the other way, multiply gigabits by 122,070.31 (or divide by 8.192e-06).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
