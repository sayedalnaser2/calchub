Kilowatt Hours to BTU Converter

Convert kilowatt hours to BTU instantly. 1 kWh = 3,412.1416 BTU.

How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to BTU, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 3,412.1416. One kilowatt hour equals 3,412.1416 BTU, and one BTU equals 0.00029307107 kilowatt hours. The table shows common values.

Kilowatt Hours to BTU formula

Btu = kilowatt hours × 3,412.1416

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 3,412.1416 = 42,651.77 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 0.00029307107 (or divide by 3,412.1416).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
