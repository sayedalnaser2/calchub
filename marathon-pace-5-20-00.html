Marathon Pace for a 5:20:00 Finish Time

To run a Marathon in 5:20:00 you need 12:12 per mile (7:35 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Marathon is 42.195 km (26.22 miles), so 5:20:00 works out to 12:12 per mile or 7:35 per kilometer.
