Megajoules to Kilocalories Converter

Convert megajoules to kilocalories instantly. 1 MJ = 239.00574 kcal.

How it works

To convert megajoules to kilocalories, multiply the number of megajoules by 239.00574. One megajoule equals 239.00574 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 0.004184 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = megajoules × 239.00574

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 239.00574 = 2,987.5717 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 0.004184 (or divide by 239.00574).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
