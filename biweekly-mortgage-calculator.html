Biweekly Mortgage Calculator

See how biweekly payments pay off a mortgage faster.

How it works

Paying half the monthly amount every two weeks makes 26 half-payments, equal to 13 monthly payments a year, so one extra payment goes to principal.

Frequently asked questions

Does my lender allow it?

Some charge a fee. You can get the same result by paying one extra monthly payment a year.
