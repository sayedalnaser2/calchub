How it works

To convert cubic feet to cubic meters, multiply the number of cubic feet by 0.028316847. To convert cubic meters to cubic feet, multiply by 35.314667 (or divide by 0.028316847). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cubic Feet to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = cubic feet × 0.028316847

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.028316847 = 0.35396058 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 35.314667 (or divide by 0.028316847).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
