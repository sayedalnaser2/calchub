How it works

To convert cubic meters per second to cubic meters per hour, multiply the number of cubic meters per second by 3,600. One cubic meter per second equals 3,600 cubic meters per hour, and one cubic meter per hour equals 0.00027777778 cubic meters per second. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters per Second to Cubic Meters per Hour formula

Cubic meters per hour = cubic meters per second × 3,600

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters per second to cubic meters per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600 = 45,000 m³/h. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters per hour by 0.00027777778 (or divide by 3,600).
