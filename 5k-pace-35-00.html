5K Pace for a 35:00 Finish Time

To run a 5K in 35:00 you need 11:16 per mile (7:00 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A 5K is 5 km (3.11 miles), so 35:00 works out to 11:16 per mile or 7:00 per kilometer.
