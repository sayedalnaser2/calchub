Grams of Heavy Cream to Cups

Convert grams of heavy cream to cups. 1 cup of heavy cream weighs about 238 g.

How it works

Heavy Cream is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 238 g per US cup, convert grams to cups by multiplying by 0.004202.
