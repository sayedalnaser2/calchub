Atmospheres to Kilopascals Converter

Convert atmospheres to kilopascals instantly. 1 atm = 101.325 kPa.

How it works

To convert atmospheres to kilopascals, multiply the number of atmospheres by 101.325. One atmosphere equals 101.325 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 0.0098692327 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Atmospheres to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = atmospheres × 101.325

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 101.325 = 1,266.5625 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 0.0098692327 (or divide by 101.325).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
