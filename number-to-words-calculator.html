Number to Words Converter

Write any number out in English words, including cheque-style amounts.

How it works

Numbers are split into groups of three digits (thousands, millions and so on). Each group is spoken as hundreds, tens and units, followed by its scale word. Cheque-style output spells the cents separately.

Frequently asked questions

How do you write 1,000,000 in words?

One million.

Is 'and' used in numbers?

British English says 'one hundred and five'; American usage often omits 'and'.
