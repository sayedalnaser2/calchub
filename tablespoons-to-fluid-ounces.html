Tablespoons to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert tablespoons to fluid ounces instantly. 1 tbsp = 0.5 fl oz.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to fluid ounces, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.5. One tablespoon equals 0.5 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 2 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = tablespoons × 0.5

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 0.5 = 6.25 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 2 (or divide by 0.5).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
