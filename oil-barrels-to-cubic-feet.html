Oil Barrels to Cubic Feet Converter

Convert oil barrels to cubic feet instantly. 1 bbl = 5.6145833 ft³.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to cubic feet, multiply the number of oil barrels by 5.6145833. One oil barrel equals 5.6145833 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 0.17810761 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = oil barrels × 5.6145833

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 5.6145833 = 70.182292 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 0.17810761 (or divide by 5.6145833).
