Nanometers to Mils Converter

Convert nanometers to mils instantly. 1 nm = 3.93701e-05 mil.

How it works

To convert nanometers to mils, multiply the number of nanometers by 3.93701e-05. One nanometer equals 3.93701e-05 mils, and one mil equals 25,400 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Mils formula

Mils = nanometers × 3.93701e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 3.93701e-05 = 0.00049212598 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 25,400 (or divide by 3.93701e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
