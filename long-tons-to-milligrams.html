How it works

To convert long tons to milligrams, multiply the number of long tons by 1,016,046,900. One long ton equals 1,016,046,900 milligrams, and one milligram equals 9.84207e-10 long tons. The table shows common values.

Long Tons to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = long tons × 1,016,046,900

Worked example: convert 12.5 long tons to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 1,016,046,900 = 12,700,586,000 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 9.84207e-10 (or divide by 1,016,046,900).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
