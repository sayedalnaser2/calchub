How it works

To convert degrees to arcseconds, multiply the number of degrees by 3,600. To convert arcseconds to degrees, multiply by 0.00027777778 (or divide by 3,600). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Degrees to Arcseconds formula

Arcseconds = degrees × 3,600

Worked example: convert 12.5 degrees to arcseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600 = 45,000 arcsec. To go the other way, multiply arcseconds by 0.00027777778 (or divide by 3,600).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
