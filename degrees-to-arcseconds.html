Degrees to Arcseconds Converter

Convert degrees to arcseconds instantly. 1 ° = 3,600 arcsec.

How it works

To convert degrees to arcseconds, multiply the number of degrees by 3,600. One degree equals 3,600 arcseconds, and one arcsecond equals 0.00027777778 degrees. The table shows common values.

Degrees to Arcseconds formula

Arcseconds = degrees × 3,600

Worked example: convert 12.5 degrees to arcseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600 = 45,000 arcsec. To go the other way, multiply arcseconds by 0.00027777778 (or divide by 3,600).
