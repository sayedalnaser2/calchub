Liters to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert liters to cubic inches instantly. 1 L = 61.023744 in³.

How it works

To convert liters to cubic inches, multiply the number of liters by 61.023744. One liter equals 61.023744 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.016387064 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = liters × 61.023744

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 61.023744 = 762.7968 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.016387064 (or divide by 61.023744).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
