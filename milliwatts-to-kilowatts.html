Milliwatts to Kilowatts Converter

Convert milliwatts to kilowatts instantly. 1 mW = 1e-06 kW.

How it works

To convert milliwatts to kilowatts, multiply the number of milliwatts by 1e-06. One milliwatt equals 1e-06 kilowatts, and one kilowatt equals 1,000,000 milliwatts. The table shows common values.

Milliwatts to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = milliwatts × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliwatts to kilowatts. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 kW. To go the other way, multiply kilowatts by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
