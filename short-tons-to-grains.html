Short Tons to Grains Converter

Convert short tons to grains instantly. 1 short tons = 14,000,000 gr.

How it works

To convert short tons to grains, multiply the number of short tons by 14,000,000. One short ton equals 14,000,000 grains, and one grain equals 7.14286e-08 short tons. The table shows common values.

Short Tons to Grains formula

Grains = short tons × 14,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 short tons to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 14,000,000 = 175,000,000 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 7.14286e-08 (or divide by 14,000,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
