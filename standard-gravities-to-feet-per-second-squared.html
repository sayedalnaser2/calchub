Standard Gravities to Feet per Second Squared Converter

Convert standard gravities to feet per second squared instantly. 1 g = 32.174049 ft/s².

How it works

To convert standard gravities to feet per second squared, multiply the number of standard gravities by 32.174049. One standard gravity equals 32.174049 feet per second squared, and one foot per second squared equals 0.03108095 standard gravities. The table shows common values.

Standard Gravities to Feet per Second Squared formula

Feet per second squared = standard gravities × 32.174049

Worked example: convert 12.5 standard gravities to feet per second squared. Multiply 12.5 × 32.174049 = 402.17561 ft/s². To go the other way, multiply feet per second squared by 0.03108095 (or divide by 32.174049).
