Estimate GFR from creatinine using the CKD-EPI 2021 equation.

How it works

The CKD-EPI 2021 equation estimates kidney filtration from creatinine, age and sex. This is an educational estimate, not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is normal eGFR?

90 or more is typically normal. Persistent values under 60 may indicate kidney disease; talk to a doctor.
