Terabytes to Mebibytes Converter

Convert terabytes to mebibytes instantly. 1 TB = 953,674.32 MiB.

How it works

To convert terabytes to mebibytes, multiply the number of terabytes by 953,674.32. One terabyte equals 953,674.32 mebibytes, and one mebibyte equals 1.04858e-06 terabytes. The table shows common values.

Terabytes to Mebibytes formula

Mebibytes = terabytes × 953,674.32

Worked example: convert 12.5 terabytes to mebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 953,674.32 = 11,920,929 MiB. To go the other way, multiply mebibytes by 1.04858e-06 (or divide by 953,674.32).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
