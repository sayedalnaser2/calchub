Hectares to Square Feet Converter

Convert hectares to square feet instantly. 1 ha = 107639.1 ft².

How it works

To convert hectares to square feet, multiply the number of hectares by 107639.1. One hectare equals 107639.1 square feet, and one square foot equals 9.2903e-06 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Square Feet formula

Square feet = hectares × 107639.1

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square feet. Multiply 12.5 × 107639.1 = 1.34549e+06 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 9.2903e-06 (or divide by 107639.1).
