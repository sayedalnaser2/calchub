Kilometers per Second to Kilometers per Hour Converter

Convert kilometers per second to kilometers per hour instantly. 1 km/s = 3,600 km/h.

How it works

To convert kilometers per second to kilometers per hour, multiply the number of kilometers per second by 3,600. One kilometer per second equals 3,600 kilometers per hour, and one kilometer per hour equals 0.00027777778 kilometers per second. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Second to Kilometers per Hour formula

Kilometers per hour = kilometers per second × 3,600

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per second to kilometers per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600 = 45,000 km/h. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per hour by 0.00027777778 (or divide by 3,600).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
