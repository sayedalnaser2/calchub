Newton Millimeters to Pound-Feet Converter

Convert newton millimeters to pound-feet instantly. 1 N·mm = 0.00073756215 lb-ft.

How it works

To convert newton millimeters to pound-feet, multiply the number of newton millimeters by 0.00073756215. One newton millimeter equals 0.00073756215 pound-feet, and one pound-foot equals 1,355.8179 newton millimeters. The table shows common values.

Newton Millimeters to Pound-Feet formula

Pound-feet = newton millimeters × 0.00073756215

Worked example: convert 12.5 newton millimeters to pound-feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00073756215 = 0.0092195269 lb-ft. To go the other way, multiply pound-feet by 1,355.8179 (or divide by 0.00073756215).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
