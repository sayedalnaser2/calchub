$34 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

$34 an hour is $70,720 a year at 40 hours a week. Adjust hours and weeks for weekly, monthly and biweekly pay.

How it works

Annual pay is the hourly rate times hours per week times weeks per year. At $34 an hour and 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, that is $70,720. Monthly pay divides the annual figure by 12.

Take-home pay illustration

Gross pay of $70,720 a year is before taxes and deductions. If roughly 20% were withheld, take-home pay would be about $56,576 a year or $4,715 a month. Your real withholding depends on filing status, state and benefits.
