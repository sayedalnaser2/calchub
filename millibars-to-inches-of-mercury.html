Millibars to Inches Of Mercury Converter

Convert millibars to inches of mercury instantly. 1 mbar = 0.02952998 inHg.

How it works

To convert millibars to inches of mercury, multiply the number of millibars by 0.02952998. One millibar equals 0.02952998 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 33.86389 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to Inches Of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = millibars × 0.02952998

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 0.02952998 = 0.36912475 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 33.86389 (or divide by 0.02952998).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
