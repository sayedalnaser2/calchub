Square Centimeters to Square Feet Converter

Convert square centimeters to square feet instantly. 1 cm² = 0.001076391 ft².

How it works

To convert square centimeters to square feet, multiply the number of square centimeters by 0.001076391. One square centimeter equals 0.001076391 square feet, and one square foot equals 929.0304 square centimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Centimeters to Square Feet formula

Square feet = square centimeters × 0.001076391

Worked example: convert 12.5 square centimeters to square feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001076391 = 0.013454888 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 929.0304 (or divide by 0.001076391).
