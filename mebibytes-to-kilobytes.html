Mebibytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert mebibytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 MiB = 1,048.576 KB.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of mebibytes by 1,048.576. One mebibyte equals 1,048.576 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 0.00095367432 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = mebibytes × 1,048.576

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,048.576 = 13,107.2 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 0.00095367432 (or divide by 1,048.576).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
