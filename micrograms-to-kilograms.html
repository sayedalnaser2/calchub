Micrograms to Kilograms Converter

Convert micrograms to kilograms instantly. 1 µg = 1e-09 kg.

How it works

To convert micrograms to kilograms, multiply the number of micrograms by 1e-09. One microgram equals 1e-09 kilograms, and one kilogram equals 1,000,000,000 micrograms. The table shows common values.

Micrograms to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = micrograms × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrograms to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
