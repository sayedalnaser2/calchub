Megawatts to Kilowatts Converter

Convert megawatts to kilowatts instantly. 1 MW = 1,000 kW.

How it works

To convert megawatts to kilowatts, multiply the number of megawatts by 1,000. One megawatt equals 1,000 kilowatts, and one kilowatt equals 0.001 megawatts. The table shows common values.

Megawatts to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = megawatts × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megawatts to kilowatts. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 kW. To go the other way, multiply kilowatts by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
