Arcminutes to Arcseconds Converter

Convert arcminutes to arcseconds instantly. 1 arcmin = 60 arcsec.

How it works

To convert arcminutes to arcseconds, multiply the number of arcminutes by 60. One arcminute equals 60 arcseconds, and one arcsecond equals 0.016666667 arcminutes. The table shows common values.

Arcminutes to Arcseconds formula

Arcseconds = arcminutes × 60

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcminutes to arcseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 60 = 750 arcsec. To go the other way, multiply arcseconds by 0.016666667 (or divide by 60).
