BMI Prime Calculator

Calculate BMI prime, the ratio of your BMI to the upper healthy limit of 25.

How it works

BMI Prime = BMI / 25. Below 0.74 is underweight, 0.74-1.0 is healthy, above 1.0 is over the healthy upper limit.

Frequently asked questions

What does BMI Prime show?

How far you are from the healthy upper threshold as a simple ratio.
