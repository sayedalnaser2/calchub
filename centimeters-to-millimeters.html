Centimeters to Millimeters Converter

Convert centimeters to millimeters instantly. 1 cm = 10 mm.

How it works

To convert centimeters to millimeters, multiply the number of centimeters by 10. One centimeter equals 10 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 0.1 centimeters. The table shows common values.

Centimeters to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = centimeters × 10

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 10 = 125 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 0.1 (or divide by 10).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
