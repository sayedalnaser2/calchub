How it works

To convert tablespoons to liters, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.014786765. To convert liters to tablespoons, multiply by 67.628045 (or divide by 0.014786765). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Tablespoons to Liters formula

Liters = tablespoons × 0.014786765

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
