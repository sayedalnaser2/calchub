Convert tablespoons to liters instantly. 1 tbsp = 0.014786765 L.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to liters, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.014786765. One tablespoon equals 0.014786765 liters, and one liter equals 67.628045 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Liters formula

Liters = tablespoons × 0.014786765

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.014786765 = 0.18483456 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 67.628045 (or divide by 0.014786765).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
