Nautical Miles to Light Years Converter

Convert nautical miles to light years instantly. 1 nmi = 1.95757e-13 ly.

How it works

To convert nautical miles to light years, multiply the number of nautical miles by 1.95757e-13. One nautical mile equals 1.95757e-13 light years, and one light year equals 5,108,385,800,000 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Light Years formula

Light years = nautical miles × 1.95757e-13

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 1.95757e-13 = 2.44696e-12 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 5,108,385,800,000 (or divide by 1.95757e-13).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
