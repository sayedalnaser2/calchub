Furlongs to Feet Converter

Convert furlongs to feet instantly. 1 furlongs = 660 ft.

How it works

To convert furlongs to feet, multiply the number of furlongs by 660. One furlong equals 660 feet, and one foot equals 0.0015151515 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Feet formula

Feet = furlongs × 660

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 660 = 8,250 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 0.0015151515 (or divide by 660).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
