Roof Area and Shingle Calculator

Estimate roof area, squares and shingle bundles from pitch.

How it works

A sloped roof is larger than its footprint by sqrt(1 + (pitch/12)²). A roofing square is 100 sq ft, and three bundles of shingles usually cover one square.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 6/12 pitch?

The roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches of horizontal run, about 26.6 degrees.
