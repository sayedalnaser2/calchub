How it works

To convert meters to miles, multiply the number of meters by 0.00062137119. One meter equals 0.00062137119 miles, and one mile equals 1609.344 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Miles formula

Miles = meters × 0.00062137119

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00062137119 = 0.0077671399 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1609.344 (or divide by 0.00062137119).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
