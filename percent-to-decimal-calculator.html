Percent to Decimal and Fraction Calculator

Convert a percentage into a decimal and a simplified fraction.

How it works

Divide the percentage by 100 to get the decimal (37.5% = 0.375). The fraction is that decimal expressed over a power of ten and simplified: 0.375 = 3/8.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a decimal to a percent?

Multiply by 100.

What is a basis point?

One hundredth of a percentage point, so 1% = 100 basis points.
