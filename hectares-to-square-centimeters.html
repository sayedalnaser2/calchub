Hectares to Square Centimeters Converter

Convert hectares to square centimeters instantly. 1 ha = 100,000,000 cm².

How it works

To convert hectares to square centimeters, multiply the number of hectares by 100,000,000. One hectare equals 100,000,000 square centimeters, and one square centimeter equals 1e-08 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = hectares × 100,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 100,000,000 = 1,250,000,000 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 1e-08 (or divide by 100,000,000).
