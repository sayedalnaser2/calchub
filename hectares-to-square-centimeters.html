How it works

To convert hectares to square centimeters, multiply the number of hectares by 100,000,000. To convert square centimeters to hectares, multiply by 1e-08 (or divide by 100,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Hectares to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = hectares × 100,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 100,000,000 = 1,250,000,000 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 1e-08 (or divide by 100,000,000).
