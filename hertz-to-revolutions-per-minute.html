Hertz to Revolutions per Minute Converter

Convert hertz to revolutions per minute instantly. 1 Hz = 60 rpm.

How it works

To convert hertz to revolutions per minute, multiply the number of hertz by 60. One hertz equals 60 revolutions per minute, and one revolution per minute equals 0.016666667 hertz. The table shows common values.

Hertz to Revolutions per Minute formula

Revolutions per minute = hertz × 60

Worked example: convert 12.5 hertz to revolutions per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 60 = 750 rpm. To go the other way, multiply revolutions per minute by 0.016666667 (or divide by 60).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
