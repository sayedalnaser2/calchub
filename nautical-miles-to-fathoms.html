How it works

To convert nautical miles to fathoms, multiply the number of nautical miles by 1,012.6859. To convert fathoms to nautical miles, multiply by 0.000987473 (or divide by 1,012.6859). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Nautical Miles to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = nautical miles × 1,012.6859

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
