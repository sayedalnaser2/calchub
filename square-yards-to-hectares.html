How it works

To convert square yards to hectares, multiply the number of square yards by 8.36127e-05. To convert hectares to square yards, multiply by 11,959.9 (or divide by 8.36127e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Yards to Hectares formula

Hectares = square yards × 8.36127e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 8.36127e-05 = 0.0010451592 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 11,959.9 (or divide by 8.36127e-05).
