Data Storage Converter

Convert between bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes, terabytes and more.

How it works

KB, MB and GB use powers of 1000 as drive makers do. KiB, MiB and GiB use powers of 1024 as many operating systems do.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my 1 TB drive show less space?

Drives are sold in decimal units (1 TB = 10^12 bytes) while Windows reports binary units, so it shows about 931 GiB.
