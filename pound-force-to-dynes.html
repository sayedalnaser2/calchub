Pound-Force to Dynes Converter

Convert pound-force to dynes instantly. 1 lbf = 444,822.16 dyn.

How it works

To convert pound-force to dynes, multiply the number of pound-force by 444,822.16. One pound-force equals 444,822.16 dynes, and one dyne equals 2.24809e-06 pound-force. The table shows common values.

Pound-Force to Dynes formula

Dynes = pound-force × 444,822.16

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-force to dynes. Multiply 12.5 × 444,822.16 = 5,560,277 dyn. To go the other way, multiply dynes by 2.24809e-06 (or divide by 444,822.16).
