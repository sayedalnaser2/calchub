Gravitational Potential Energy Calculator

Calculate potential energy from mass, height and gravity.

How it works

Gravitational potential energy PE = m x g x h. Dropping an object converts it to kinetic energy, so v = sqrt(2 g h) ignoring air resistance.

Frequently asked questions

What is g on the Moon?

About 1.62 m/s².

Does mass matter for falling speed?

Not in a vacuum; all objects fall at the same rate.
