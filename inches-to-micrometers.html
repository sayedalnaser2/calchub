Inches to Micrometers Converter

Convert inches to micrometers instantly. 1 in = 25,400 µm.

How it works

To convert inches to micrometers, multiply the number of inches by 25,400. One inch equals 25,400 micrometers, and one micrometer equals 3.93701e-05 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = inches × 25,400

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 25,400 = 317,500 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 3.93701e-05 (or divide by 25,400).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
