Seconds to Decades Converter (Both Ways)

Convert seconds to decades and decades to seconds instantly. 1 s = 3.16887e-09 decades.

How it works

To convert seconds to decades, multiply the number of seconds by 3.16887e-09. To convert decades to seconds, multiply by 315,569,520 (or divide by 3.16887e-09). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Seconds to Decades formula

Decades = seconds × 3.16887e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to decades. Multiply 12.5 × 3.16887e-09 = 3.96109e-08 decades. To go the other way, multiply decades by 315,569,520 (or divide by 3.16887e-09).
