Megabits to Kilobits Converter

Convert megabits to kilobits instantly. 1 Mb = 1,000 Kb.

How it works

To convert megabits to kilobits, multiply the number of megabits by 1,000. One megabit equals 1,000 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 0.001 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = megabits × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
