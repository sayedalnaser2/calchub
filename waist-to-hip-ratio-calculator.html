Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator

Calculate WHR and what it says about health risk.

How it works

WHR is waist circumference divided by hip circumference. Higher ratios indicate more abdominal fat, linked with cardiovascular risk. This is an educational estimate, not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure?

Waist at the narrowest point, hips at the widest, tape level and snug.
