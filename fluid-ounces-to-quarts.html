Fluid Ounces to Quarts Converter

Convert fluid ounces to quarts instantly. 1 fl oz = 0.03125 qt.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to quarts, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 0.03125. One fluid ounce equals 0.03125 quarts, and one quart equals 32 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Quarts formula

Quarts = fluid ounces × 0.03125

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.03125 = 0.390625 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 32 (or divide by 0.03125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
