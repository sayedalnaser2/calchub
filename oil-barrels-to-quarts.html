Oil Barrels to Quarts Converter

Convert oil barrels to quarts instantly. 1 bbl = 168 qt.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to quarts, multiply the number of oil barrels by 168. One oil barrel equals 168 quarts, and one quart equals 0.005952381 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Quarts formula

Quarts = oil barrels × 168

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 168 = 2,100 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 0.005952381 (or divide by 168).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
