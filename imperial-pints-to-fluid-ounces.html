How it works

To convert imperial pints to fluid ounces, multiply the number of imperial pints by 19.215199. One imperial pint equals 19.215199 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.052042137 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = imperial pints × 19.215199

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 19.215199 = 240.18999 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.052042137 (or divide by 19.215199).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
