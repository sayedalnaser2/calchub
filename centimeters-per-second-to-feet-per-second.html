Centimeters per Second to Feet per Second Converter

Convert centimeters per second to feet per second instantly. 1 cm/s = 0.032808399 ft/s.

How it works

To convert centimeters per second to feet per second, multiply the number of centimeters per second by 0.032808399. One centimeter per second equals 0.032808399 feet per second, and one foot per second equals 30.48 centimeters per second. The table shows common values.

Centimeters per Second to Feet per Second formula

Feet per second = centimeters per second × 0.032808399

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters per second to feet per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.032808399 = 0.41010499 ft/s. To go the other way, multiply feet per second by 30.48 (or divide by 0.032808399).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
