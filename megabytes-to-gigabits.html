Megabytes to Gigabits Converter

Convert megabytes to gigabits instantly. 1 MB = 0.008 Gb.

How it works

To convert megabytes to gigabits, multiply the number of megabytes by 0.008. One megabyte equals 0.008 gigabits, and one gigabit equals 125 megabytes. The table shows common values.

Megabytes to Gigabits formula

Gigabits = megabytes × 0.008

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabytes to gigabits. Multiply 12.5 × 0.008 = 0.1 Gb. To go the other way, multiply gigabits by 125 (or divide by 0.008).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
