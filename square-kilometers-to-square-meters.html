Square Kilometers to Square Meters Converter

Convert square kilometers to square meters instantly. 1 km² = 1,000,000 m².

How it works

To convert square kilometers to square meters, multiply the number of square kilometers by 1,000,000. One square kilometer equals 1,000,000 square meters, and one square meter equals 1e-06 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Square Meters formula

Square meters = square kilometers × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).
