Megajoules to Calories Converter

Convert megajoules to calories instantly. 1 MJ = 239,005.74 cal.

How it works

To convert megajoules to calories, multiply the number of megajoules by 239,005.74. One megajoule equals 239,005.74 calories, and one calorie equals 4.184e-06 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to Calories formula

Calories = megajoules × 239,005.74

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 239,005.74 = 2,987,571.7 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 4.184e-06 (or divide by 239,005.74).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
