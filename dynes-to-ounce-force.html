Convert dynes to ounce-force instantly. 1 dyn = 3.59694e-05 ozf.

How it works

To convert dynes to ounce-force, multiply the number of dynes by 3.59694e-05. One dyne equals 3.59694e-05 ounce-force, and one ounce-force equals 27,801.385 dynes. The table shows common values.

Dynes to Ounce-Force formula

Ounce-force = dynes × 3.59694e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 dynes to ounce-force. Multiply 12.5 × 3.59694e-05 = 0.00044961789 ozf. To go the other way, multiply ounce-force by 27,801.385 (or divide by 3.59694e-05).
