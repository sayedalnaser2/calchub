Pound-Inches to Newton Millimeters Converter

Convert pound-inches to newton millimeters instantly. 1 lb-in = 112.98483 N·mm.

How it works

To convert pound-inches to newton millimeters, multiply the number of pound-inches by 112.98483. One pound-inch equals 112.98483 newton millimeters, and one newton millimeter equals 0.0088507458 pound-inches. The table shows common values.

Pound-Inches to Newton Millimeters formula

Newton millimeters = pound-inches × 112.98483

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-inches to newton millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 112.98483 = 1,412.3104 N·mm. To go the other way, multiply newton millimeters by 0.0088507458 (or divide by 112.98483).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
