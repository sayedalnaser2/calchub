Milliseconds to Minutes Converter

Convert milliseconds to minutes instantly. 1 ms = 1.66667e-05 min.

How it works

To convert milliseconds to minutes, multiply the number of milliseconds by 1.66667e-05. One millisecond equals 1.66667e-05 minutes, and one minute equals 60,000 milliseconds. The table shows common values.

Milliseconds to Minutes formula

Minutes = milliseconds × 1.66667e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliseconds to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.66667e-05 = 0.00020833333 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 60,000 (or divide by 1.66667e-05).
