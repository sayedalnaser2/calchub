Kilobytes to Megabits Converter

Convert kilobytes to megabits instantly. 1 KB = 0.008 Mb.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to megabits, multiply the number of kilobytes by 0.008. One kilobyte equals 0.008 megabits, and one megabit equals 125 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Megabits formula

Megabits = kilobytes × 0.008

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to megabits. Multiply 12.5 × 0.008 = 0.1 Mb. To go the other way, multiply megabits by 125 (or divide by 0.008).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
