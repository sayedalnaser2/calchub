Fraction Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions and get a simplified answer.

How it works

Fractions are combined using a common denominator for addition and subtraction, direct multiplication of tops and bottoms, or multiplication by the reciprocal for division. The result is reduced by the greatest common divisor.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Convert both to a common denominator, add the numerators, then simplify.

What is the reciprocal?

Swap numerator and denominator. Dividing by a fraction equals multiplying by its reciprocal.
