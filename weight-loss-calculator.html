Find how long it takes to reach your goal weight and the daily deficit needed.

How it works

About 7,700 kcal equals 1 kg of body fat. Dividing weekly loss by seven gives the daily deficit.

Frequently asked questions

What rate is safe?

0.25-1 kg per week (0.5-2 lb) is generally considered sustainable.
