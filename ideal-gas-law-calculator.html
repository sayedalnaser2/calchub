How it works

The ideal gas law PV = nRT relates pressure, volume, amount and temperature. The chosen variable is solved from the other three.

Formula

Ideal gas law: PV = nRT with R = 8.314462618 J/(mol·K). Enter any three of pressure, volume, moles and temperature.

Frequently asked questions

When is the ideal gas law inaccurate?

At high pressure and low temperature where real gases deviate.
