How it works

To convert meters to furlongs, multiply the number of meters by 0.0049709695. One meter equals 0.0049709695 furlongs, and one furlong equals 201.168 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = meters × 0.0049709695

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0049709695 = 0.062137119 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 201.168 (or divide by 0.0049709695).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
