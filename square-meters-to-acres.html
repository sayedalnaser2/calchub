How it works

To convert square meters to acres, multiply the number of square meters by 0.00024710538. One square meter equals 0.00024710538 acres, and one acre equals 4,046.8564 square meters. The table shows common values.

Square Meters to Acres formula

Acres = square meters × 0.00024710538

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00024710538 = 0.0030888173 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 4,046.8564 (or divide by 0.00024710538).
