Torr to Kilopascals Converter

Convert torr to kilopascals instantly. 1 Torr = 0.13332237 kPa.

How it works

To convert torr to kilopascals, multiply the number of torr by 0.13332237. One torr equals 0.13332237 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 7.5006168 torr. The table shows common values.

Torr to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = torr × 0.13332237

Worked example: convert 12.5 torr to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.13332237 = 1.6665296 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 7.5006168 (or divide by 0.13332237).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
