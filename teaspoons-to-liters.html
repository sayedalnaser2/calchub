How it works

To convert teaspoons to liters, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.0049289216. One teaspoon equals 0.0049289216 liters, and one liter equals 202.88414 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Liters formula

Liters = teaspoons × 0.0049289216

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0049289216 = 0.06161152 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 202.88414 (or divide by 0.0049289216).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
