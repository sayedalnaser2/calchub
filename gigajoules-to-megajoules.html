How it works

To convert gigajoules to megajoules, multiply the number of gigajoules by 1,000. One gigajoule equals 1,000 megajoules, and one megajoule equals 0.001 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = gigajoules × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
