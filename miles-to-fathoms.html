Miles to Fathoms Converter

Convert miles to fathoms instantly. 1 mi = 880 fathoms.

How it works

To convert miles to fathoms, multiply the number of miles by 880. One mile equals 880 fathoms, and one fathom equals 0.0011363636 miles. The table shows common values.

Miles to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = miles × 880

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 880 = 11,000 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 0.0011363636 (or divide by 880).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
