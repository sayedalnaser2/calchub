Kilometers per Hour to Feet per Second Converter

Convert kilometers per hour to feet per second instantly. 1 km/h = 0.91134442 ft/s.

How it works

To convert kilometers per hour to feet per second, multiply the number of kilometers per hour by 0.91134442. One kilometer per hour equals 0.91134442 feet per second, and one foot per second equals 1.09728 kilometers per hour. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Hour to Feet per Second formula

Feet per second = kilometers per hour × 0.91134442

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per hour to feet per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.91134442 = 11.391805 ft/s. To go the other way, multiply feet per second by 1.09728 (or divide by 0.91134442).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
