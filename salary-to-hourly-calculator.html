Salary to Hourly Calculator

Convert an annual salary into an hourly, daily and weekly wage.

How it works

Hourly wage = annual salary / (hours per week x weeks per year). 2,080 hours (40 x 52) is the standard full-time year.

Frequently asked questions

What about paid holidays?

Paid time off is included in the 52 weeks. Remove unpaid weeks to see an effective rate.
