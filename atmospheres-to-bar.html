Atmospheres to Bar Converter

Convert atmospheres to bar instantly. 1 atm = 1.01325 bar.

How it works

To convert atmospheres to bar, multiply the number of atmospheres by 1.01325. One atmosphere equals 1.01325 bar, and one bar equals 0.98692327 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Atmospheres to Bar formula

Bar = atmospheres × 1.01325

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 1.01325 = 12.665625 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 0.98692327 (or divide by 1.01325).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
