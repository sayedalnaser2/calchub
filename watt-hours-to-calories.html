Watt Hours to Calories Converter

Convert watt hours to calories instantly. 1 Wh = 860.42065 cal.

How it works

To convert watt hours to calories, multiply the number of watt hours by 860.42065. One watt hour equals 860.42065 calories, and one calorie equals 0.0011622222 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Calories formula

Calories = watt hours × 860.42065

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 860.42065 = 10,755.258 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 0.0011622222 (or divide by 860.42065).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
