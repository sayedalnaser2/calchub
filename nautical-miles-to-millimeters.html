Nautical Miles to Millimeters Converter

Convert nautical miles to millimeters instantly. 1 nmi = 1,852,000 mm.

How it works

To convert nautical miles to millimeters, multiply the number of nautical miles by 1,852,000. One nautical mile equals 1,852,000 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 5.39957e-07 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = nautical miles × 1,852,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,852,000 = 23,150,000 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 5.39957e-07 (or divide by 1,852,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
