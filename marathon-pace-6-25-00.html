Marathon Pace for a 6:25:00 Finish Time

To run a Marathon in 6:25:00 you need 14:41 per mile (9:07 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Marathon is 42.195 km (26.22 miles), so 6:25:00 works out to 14:41 per mile or 9:07 per kilometer.
