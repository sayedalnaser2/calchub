Kilowatt Hours to Therms Converter

Convert kilowatt hours to therms instantly. 1 kWh = 0.034129563 therms.

How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to therms, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 0.034129563. One kilowatt hour equals 0.034129563 therms, and one therm equals 29.300111 kilowatt hours. The table shows common values.

Kilowatt Hours to Therms formula

Therms = kilowatt hours × 0.034129563

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.034129563 = 0.42661954 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 29.300111 (or divide by 0.034129563).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
