Horsepower to Watts Converter

Convert horsepower to watts instantly. 1 hp = 745.69987 W.

How it works

To convert horsepower to watts, multiply the number of horsepower by 745.69987. One horsepower equals 745.69987 watts, and one watt equals 0.0013410221 horsepower. The table shows common values.

Horsepower to Watts formula

Watts = horsepower × 745.69987

Worked example: convert 12.5 horsepower to watts. Multiply 12.5 × 745.69987 = 9,321.2484 W. To go the other way, multiply watts by 0.0013410221 (or divide by 745.69987).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
