Inches to Mils Converter

Convert inches to mils instantly. 1 in = 1,000 mil.

How it works

To convert inches to mils, multiply the number of inches by 1,000. One inch equals 1,000 mils, and one mil equals 0.001 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Mils formula

Mils = inches × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
