Stone to Grains Converter

Convert stone to grains instantly. 1 st = 98,000 gr.

How it works

To convert stone to grains, multiply the number of stone by 98,000. One stone equals 98,000 grains, and one grain equals 1.02041e-05 stone. The table shows common values.

Stone to Grains formula

Grains = stone × 98,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 stone to grains. Multiply 12.5 × 98,000 = 1,225,000 gr. To go the other way, multiply grains by 1.02041e-05 (or divide by 98,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
