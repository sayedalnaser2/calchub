Kilogram-Force Meters to Pound-Feet Converter

Convert kilogram-force meters to pound-feet instantly. 1 kgf·m = 7.2330139 lb-ft.

How it works

To convert kilogram-force meters to pound-feet, multiply the number of kilogram-force meters by 7.2330139. One kilogram-force meter equals 7.2330139 pound-feet, and one pound-foot equals 0.13825495 kilogram-force meters. The table shows common values.

Kilogram-Force Meters to Pound-Feet formula

Pound-feet = kilogram-force meters × 7.2330139

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilogram-force meters to pound-feet. Multiply 12.5 × 7.2330139 = 90.412673 lb-ft. To go the other way, multiply pound-feet by 0.13825495 (or divide by 7.2330139).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
