How it works

To convert fathoms to micrometers, multiply the number of fathoms by 1,828,800. To convert micrometers to fathoms, multiply by 5.46807e-07 (or divide by 1,828,800). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Fathoms to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = fathoms × 1,828,800

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1,828,800 = 22,860,000 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 5.46807e-07 (or divide by 1,828,800).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
