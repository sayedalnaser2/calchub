Investing $700 a Month: How Much Will You Have?

$700 invested every month grows to $853,980 in 30 years at 7%.

How it works

The future value of regular deposits is M x ((1 + r)^k - 1) / r with a monthly rate r and k months. $700 a month for 30 years at 7% grows to about $853,980, of which $252,000 is your own money.
