To convert gibibytes to gigabytes, multiply the number of gibibytes by 1.0737418. One gibibyte equals 1.0737418 gigabytes, and one gigabyte equals 0.93132257 gibibytes. The table shows common values.

Gibibytes to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = gibibytes × 1.0737418

Worked example: convert 12.5 gibibytes to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.0737418 = 13.421773 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 0.93132257 (or divide by 1.0737418).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
