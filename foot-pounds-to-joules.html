Foot-Pounds to Joules Converter

Convert foot-pounds to joules instantly. 1 ft-lb = 1.3558179 J.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to joules, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 1.3558179. One foot-pound equals 1.3558179 joules, and one joule equals 0.73756215 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Joules formula

Joules = foot-pounds × 1.3558179

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3558179 = 16.947724 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 0.73756215 (or divide by 1.3558179).
