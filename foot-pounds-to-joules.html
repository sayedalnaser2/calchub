How it works

To convert foot-pounds to joules, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 1.3558179. To convert joules to foot-pounds, multiply by 0.73756215 (or divide by 1.3558179). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Foot-Pounds to Joules formula

Joules = foot-pounds × 1.3558179

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3558179 = 16.947724 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 0.73756215 (or divide by 1.3558179).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
