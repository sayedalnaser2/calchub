How it works

To convert imperial pints to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of imperial pints by 568.26125. One imperial pint equals 568.26125 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.001759754 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = imperial pints × 568.26125

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 568.26125 = 7,103.2656 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.001759754 (or divide by 568.26125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
