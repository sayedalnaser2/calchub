Cubic Inches to Pints Converter

Convert cubic inches to pints instantly. 1 in³ = 0.034632035 pt.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to pints, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.034632035. One cubic inch equals 0.034632035 pints, and one pint equals 28.875 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Pints formula

Pints = cubic inches × 0.034632035

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.034632035 = 0.43290043 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 28.875 (or divide by 0.034632035).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
