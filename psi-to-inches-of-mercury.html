How it works

To convert psi to inches of mercury, multiply the number of psi by 2.0360205. To convert inches of mercury to psi, multiply by 0.4911542 (or divide by 2.0360205). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

PSI to Inches of Mercury formula

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
