PSI to Inches of Mercury Converter

Convert psi to inches of mercury instantly. 1 psi = 2.0360205 inHg.

How it works

To convert psi to inches of mercury, multiply the number of psi by 2.0360205. One psi equals 2.0360205 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 0.4911542 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Inches of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = psi × 2.0360205

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 2.0360205 = 25.450256 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 0.4911542 (or divide by 2.0360205).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
