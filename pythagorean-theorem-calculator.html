How it works

For a right triangle a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse opposite the right angle.

Formula

a squared + b squared = c squared. Enter any two sides of a right triangle to find the third.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Pythagorean triple?

A set of three whole numbers satisfying a² + b² = c², such as 3-4-5 or 5-12-13.
