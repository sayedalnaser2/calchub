Petabytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert petabytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 PB = 1,000,000,000,000 KB.

How it works

To convert petabytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of petabytes by 1,000,000,000,000. One petabyte equals 1,000,000,000,000 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 1e-12 petabytes. The table shows common values.

Petabytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = petabytes × 1,000,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 petabytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000,000 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 1e-12 (or divide by 1,000,000,000,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
