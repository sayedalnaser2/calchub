Teaspoons to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert teaspoons to cubic inches instantly. 1 tsp = 0.30078125 in³.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to cubic inches, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.30078125. One teaspoon equals 0.30078125 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 3.3246753 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = teaspoons × 0.30078125

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.30078125 = 3.7597656 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 3.3246753 (or divide by 0.30078125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
