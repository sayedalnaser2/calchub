Gibibytes to Petabytes Converter

Convert gibibytes to petabytes instantly. 1 GiB = 1.07374e-06 PB.

How it works

To convert gibibytes to petabytes, multiply the number of gibibytes by 1.07374e-06. One gibibyte equals 1.07374e-06 petabytes, and one petabyte equals 931,322.57 gibibytes. The table shows common values.

Gibibytes to Petabytes formula

Petabytes = gibibytes × 1.07374e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 gibibytes to petabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.07374e-06 = 1.34218e-05 PB. To go the other way, multiply petabytes by 931,322.57 (or divide by 1.07374e-06).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
