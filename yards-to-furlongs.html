Yards to Furlongs Converter

Convert yards to furlongs instantly. 1 yd = 0.0045454545 furlongs.

How it works

To convert yards to furlongs, multiply the number of yards by 0.0045454545. One yard equals 0.0045454545 furlongs, and one furlong equals 220 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = yards × 0.0045454545

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0045454545 = 0.056818182 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 220 (or divide by 0.0045454545).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
