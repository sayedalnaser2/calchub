Feet to Yards Converter

Convert feet to yards instantly. 1 ft = 0.33333333 yd.

How it works

To convert feet to yards, multiply the number of feet by 0.33333333. One foot equals 0.33333333 yards, and one yard equals 3 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Yards formula

Yards = feet × 0.33333333

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.33333333 = 4.1666667 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 3 (or divide by 0.33333333).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
