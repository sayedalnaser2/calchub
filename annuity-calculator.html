Annuity Calculator

Find the future and present value of regular annuity payments.

How it works

An annuity is a series of equal payments. Future value shows what the payments grow to, and present value shows what they are worth today.

Frequently asked questions

Ordinary vs annuity due?

Ordinary pays at the end of each period; due pays at the start, so each payment earns one extra period of interest.
