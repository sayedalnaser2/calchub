How it works

To convert arcminutes to turns, multiply the number of arcminutes by 4.62963e-05. To convert turns to arcminutes, multiply by 21,600 (or divide by 4.62963e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Arcminutes to Turns formula

Turns = arcminutes × 4.62963e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcminutes to turns. Multiply 12.5 × 4.62963e-05 = 0.0005787037 turns. To go the other way, multiply turns by 21,600 (or divide by 4.62963e-05).
