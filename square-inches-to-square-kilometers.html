Square Inches to Square Kilometers Converter

Convert square inches to square kilometers instantly. 1 in² = 6.4516e-10 km².

How it works

To convert square inches to square kilometers, multiply the number of square inches by 6.4516e-10. One square inch equals 6.4516e-10 square kilometers, and one square kilometer equals 1,550,003,100 square inches. The table shows common values.

Square Inches to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = square inches × 6.4516e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 6.4516e-10 = 8.0645e-09 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 1,550,003,100 (or divide by 6.4516e-10).
