Revolutions per Minute to Gigahertz Converter

Convert revolutions per minute to gigahertz instantly. 1 rpm = 1.66667e-11 GHz.

How it works

To convert revolutions per minute to gigahertz, multiply the number of revolutions per minute by 1.66667e-11. One revolution per minute equals 1.66667e-11 gigahertz, and one gigahertz equals 60,000,000,000 revolutions per minute. The table shows common values.

Revolutions per Minute to Gigahertz formula

Gigahertz = revolutions per minute × 1.66667e-11

Worked example: convert 12.5 revolutions per minute to gigahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1.66667e-11 = 2.08333e-10 GHz. To go the other way, multiply gigahertz by 60,000,000,000 (or divide by 1.66667e-11).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
