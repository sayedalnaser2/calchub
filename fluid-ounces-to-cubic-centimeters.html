Fluid Ounces to Cubic Centimeters Converter

Convert fluid ounces to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 fl oz = 29.57353 cm³.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 29.57353. One fluid ounce equals 29.57353 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.033814023 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = fluid ounces × 29.57353

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 29.57353 = 369.66912 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.033814023 (or divide by 29.57353).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
