How it works

Pace is total time divided by distance. This tool reports it per kilometre and per mile, plus average speed.

Formula

pace = time / distance. Enter any two of distance, time and pace to solve the third. Pace is entered as decimal minutes; the table below shows it as minutes:seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good 10K pace?

Recreational runners often finish 10K in 50-70 minutes, a pace of about 5:00-7:00 per km.
