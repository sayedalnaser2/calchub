Knots to Miles per Hour Converter

Convert knots to miles per hour instantly. 1 kn = 1.1507794 mph.

How it works

To convert knots to miles per hour, multiply the number of knots by 1.1507794. One knot equals 1.1507794 miles per hour, and one mile per hour equals 0.86897624 knots. The table shows common values.

Knots to Miles per Hour formula

Miles per hour = knots × 1.1507794

Worked example: convert 12.5 knots to miles per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 1.1507794 = 14.384743 mph. To go the other way, multiply miles per hour by 0.86897624 (or divide by 1.1507794).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
