How it works

To convert decades to centuries, multiply the number of decades by 0.1. To convert centuries to decades, multiply by 10 (or divide by 0.1). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Decades to Centuries formula

Centuries = decades × 0.1

Worked example: convert 12.5 decades to centuries. Multiply 12.5 × 0.1 = 1.25 centuries. To go the other way, multiply centuries by 10 (or divide by 0.1).
