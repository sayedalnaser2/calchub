Mils to Furlongs Converter

Convert mils to furlongs instantly. 1 mil = 1.26263e-07 furlongs.

How it works

To convert mils to furlongs, multiply the number of mils by 1.26263e-07. One mil equals 1.26263e-07 furlongs, and one furlong equals 7,920,000 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = mils × 1.26263e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 1.26263e-07 = 1.57828e-06 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 7,920,000 (or divide by 1.26263e-07).

About length units
