Mils to Light Years Converter

Convert mils to light years instantly. 1 mil = 2.68478e-21 ly.

How it works

To convert mils to light years, multiply the number of mils by 2.68478e-21. One mil equals 2.68478e-21 light years, and one light year equals 3.7247e+20 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Light Years formula

Light years = mils × 2.68478e-21

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 2.68478e-21 = 3.35598e-20 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 3.7247e+20 (or divide by 2.68478e-21).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
