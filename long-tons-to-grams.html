How it works

To convert long tons to grams, multiply the number of long tons by 1,016,046.9. One long ton equals 1,016,046.9 grams, and one gram equals 9.84207e-07 long tons. The table shows common values.

Long Tons to Grams formula

Grams = long tons × 1,016,046.9

Worked example: convert 12.5 long tons to grams. Multiply 12.5 × 1,016,046.9 = 12,700,586 g. To go the other way, multiply grams by 9.84207e-07 (or divide by 1,016,046.9).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
