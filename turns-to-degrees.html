Turns to Degrees Converter

Convert turns to degrees instantly. 1 turns = 360 °.

How it works

To convert turns to degrees, multiply the number of turns by 360. One turn equals 360 degrees, and one degree equals 0.0027777778 turns. The table shows common values.

Turns to Degrees formula

Degrees = turns × 360

Worked example: convert 12.5 turns to degrees. Multiply 12.5 × 360 = 4,500 °. To go the other way, multiply degrees by 0.0027777778 (or divide by 360).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
