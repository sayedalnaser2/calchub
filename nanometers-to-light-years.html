Nanometers to Light Years Converter

Convert nanometers to light years instantly. 1 nm = 1.057e-25 ly.

How it works

To convert nanometers to light years, multiply the number of nanometers by 1.057e-25. One nanometer equals 1.057e-25 light years, and one light year equals 9.46073e+24 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Light Years formula

Light years = nanometers × 1.057e-25

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 1.057e-25 = 1.32125e-24 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 9.46073e+24 (or divide by 1.057e-25).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
