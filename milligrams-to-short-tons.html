How it works

To convert milligrams to short tons, multiply the number of milligrams by 1.10231e-09. One milligram equals 1.10231e-09 short tons, and one short ton equals 907,184,740 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Short Tons formula

Short tons = milligrams × 1.10231e-09

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
