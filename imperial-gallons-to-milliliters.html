How it works

To convert imperial gallons to milliliters, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 4,546.09. To convert milliliters to imperial gallons, multiply by 0.00021996925 (or divide by 4,546.09). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Imperial Gallons to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = imperial gallons × 4,546.09

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 4,546.09 = 56,826.125 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.00021996925 (or divide by 4,546.09).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
