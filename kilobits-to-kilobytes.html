Kilobits to Kilobytes Converter

Convert kilobits to kilobytes instantly. 1 Kb = 0.125 KB.

How it works

To convert kilobits to kilobytes, multiply the number of kilobits by 0.125. One kilobit equals 0.125 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 8 kilobits. The table shows common values.

Kilobits to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = kilobits × 0.125

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobits to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.125 = 1.5625 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 8 (or divide by 0.125).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
