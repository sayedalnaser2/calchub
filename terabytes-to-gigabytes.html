Terabytes to Gigabytes Converter

Convert terabytes to gigabytes instantly. 1 TB = 1,000 GB.

How it works

To convert terabytes to gigabytes, multiply the number of terabytes by 1,000. One terabyte equals 1,000 gigabytes, and one gigabyte equals 0.001 terabytes. The table shows common values.

Terabytes to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = terabytes × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 terabytes to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
