Megajoules to BTU Converter

Convert megajoules to BTU instantly. 1 MJ = 947.81712 BTU.

How it works

To convert megajoules to BTU, multiply the number of megajoules by 947.81712. One megajoule equals 947.81712 BTU, and one BTU equals 0.0010550559 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to BTU formula

Btu = megajoules × 947.81712

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 947.81712 = 11,847.714 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 0.0010550559 (or divide by 947.81712).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
