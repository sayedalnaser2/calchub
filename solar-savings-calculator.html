Solar Panel Savings Calculator

Estimate annual production, savings and payback of a solar system.

How it works

Production = size x peak sun hours x 365 x efficiency. Savings equal production times the electricity price. Real output declines slightly each year.

Frequently asked questions

Do rates matter?

Yes. Higher electricity prices shorten payback.
