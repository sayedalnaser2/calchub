Milliliters to Liters Converter

Convert milliliters to liters instantly. 1 ml = 0.001 L.

How it works

To convert milliliters to liters, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.001. One milliliter equals 0.001 liters, and one liter equals 1,000 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Liters formula

Liters = milliliters × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
