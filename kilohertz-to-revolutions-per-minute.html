Kilohertz to Revolutions per Minute Converter

Convert kilohertz to revolutions per minute instantly. 1 kHz = 60,000 rpm.

How it works

To convert kilohertz to revolutions per minute, multiply the number of kilohertz by 60,000. One kilohertz equals 60,000 revolutions per minute, and one revolution per minute equals 1.66667e-05 kilohertz. The table shows common values.

Kilohertz to Revolutions per Minute formula

Revolutions per minute = kilohertz × 60,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilohertz to revolutions per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 60,000 = 750,000 rpm. To go the other way, multiply revolutions per minute by 1.66667e-05 (or divide by 60,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
