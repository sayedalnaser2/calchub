Circle Sector Area and Arc Length Calculator

Calculate the area, arc length and chord of a circular sector.

How it works

Sector area = (angle/360) x pi x r^2. Arc length = (angle/360) x 2 x pi x r. The chord is 2 x r x sin(angle/2).

Frequently asked questions

What is a sector?

A pie-slice portion of a circle between two radii.

How do I use radians?

Multiply by 180/pi to convert to degrees first.
