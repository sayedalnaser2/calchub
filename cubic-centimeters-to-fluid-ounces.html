Cubic Centimeters to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to fluid ounces instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.033814023 fl oz.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to fluid ounces, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.033814023. One cubic centimeter equals 0.033814023 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 29.57353 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = cubic centimeters × 0.033814023

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 0.033814023 = 0.42267528 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 29.57353 (or divide by 0.033814023).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
