Milliliters to Quarts Converter

Convert milliliters to quarts instantly. 1 ml = 0.0010566882 qt.

How it works

To convert milliliters to quarts, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.0010566882. One milliliter equals 0.0010566882 quarts, and one quart equals 946.35295 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Quarts formula

Quarts = milliliters × 0.0010566882

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0010566882 = 0.013208603 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 946.35295 (or divide by 0.0010566882).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
