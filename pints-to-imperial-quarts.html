How it works

To convert pints to imperial quarts, multiply the number of pints by 0.41633709. One pint equals 0.41633709 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 2.4018999 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = pints × 0.41633709

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.41633709 = 5.2042137 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 2.4018999 (or divide by 0.41633709).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
