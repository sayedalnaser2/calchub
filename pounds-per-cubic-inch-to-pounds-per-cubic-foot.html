Pounds per Cubic Inch to Pounds per Cubic Foot Converter

Convert pounds per cubic inch to pounds per cubic foot instantly. 1 lb/in³ = 1,728 lb/ft³.

How it works

To convert pounds per cubic inch to pounds per cubic foot, multiply the number of pounds per cubic inch by 1,728. One pound per cubic inch equals 1,728 pounds per cubic foot, and one pound per cubic foot equals 0.0005787037 pounds per cubic inch. The table shows common values.

Pounds per Cubic Inch to Pounds per Cubic Foot formula

Pounds per cubic foot = pounds per cubic inch × 1,728

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds per cubic inch to pounds per cubic foot. Multiply 12.5 × 1,728 = 21,600 lb/ft³. To go the other way, multiply pounds per cubic foot by 0.0005787037 (or divide by 1,728).
