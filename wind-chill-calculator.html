Wind Chill Calculator

Find the wind chill (feels-like) temperature in cold, windy weather.

How it works

This uses the US National Weather Service formula: 35.74 + 0.6215T - 35.75V^0.16 + 0.4275T x V^0.16, with T in °F and V in mph. It is valid at or below 50 °F with winds of 3 mph or more.

Frequently asked questions

Does wind chill change the real temperature?

No. It describes how cold exposed skin feels. Objects do not cool below the air temperature.

When does frostbite risk rise?

Wind chills near -18 °F can cause frostbite on exposed skin in about 30 minutes.
