Ounces of Cake Flour to Cups

Convert ounces of cake flour to cups. 1 cup of cake flour weighs about 114 g.

How it works

Cake Flour is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 114 g per US cup, convert ounces to cups by multiplying by 0.2487.
