Inches to Kilometers Converter

Convert inches to kilometers instantly. 1 in = 2.54e-05 km.

How it works

To convert inches to kilometers, multiply the number of inches by 2.54e-05. One inch equals 2.54e-05 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 39370.079 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = inches × 2.54e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 2.54e-05 = 0.0003175 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 39370.079 (or divide by 2.54e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
