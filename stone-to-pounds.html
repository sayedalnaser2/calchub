Stone to Pounds Converter

Convert stone to pounds instantly. 1 st = 14 lb.

How it works

To convert stone to pounds, multiply the number of stone by 14. One stone equals 14 pounds, and one pound equals 0.071428571 stone. The table shows common values.

Stone to Pounds formula

Pounds = stone × 14

Worked example: convert 12.5 stone to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 14 = 175 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 0.071428571 (or divide by 14).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
