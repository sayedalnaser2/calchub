Turns to Radians Converter

Convert turns to radians instantly. 1 turn = 6.2831853 rad.

How it works

To convert turns to radians, multiply the number of turns by 6.2831853. One turn equals 6.2831853 radians, and one radian equals 0.15915494 turns. The table shows common values.

Turns to Radians formula

Radians = turns × 6.2831853

Worked example: convert 12.5 turns to radians. Multiply 12.5 × 6.2831853 = 78.539816 rad. To go the other way, multiply radians by 0.15915494 (or divide by 6.2831853).
