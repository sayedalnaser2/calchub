Watts to Kilowatts Converter

Convert watts to kilowatts instantly. 1 W = 0.001 kW.

How it works

To convert watts to kilowatts, multiply the number of watts by 0.001. One watt equals 0.001 kilowatts, and one kilowatt equals 1,000 watts. The table shows common values.

Watts to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = watts × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 watts to kilowatts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kW. To go the other way, multiply kilowatts by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
