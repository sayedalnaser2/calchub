Megapascals to Kilopascals Converter

Convert megapascals to kilopascals instantly. 1 MPa = 1,000 kPa.

How it works

To convert megapascals to kilopascals, multiply the number of megapascals by 1,000. One megapascal equals 1,000 kilopascals, and one kilopascal equals 0.001 megapascals. The table shows common values.

Megapascals to Kilopascals formula

Kilopascals = megapascals × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megapascals to kilopascals. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 kPa. To go the other way, multiply kilopascals by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
