Liters to Cubic Feet Converter

Convert liters to cubic feet instantly. 1 L = 0.035314667 ft³.

How it works

To convert liters to cubic feet, multiply the number of liters by 0.035314667. One liter equals 0.035314667 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 28.316847 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = liters × 0.035314667

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.035314667 = 0.44143333 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 28.316847 (or divide by 0.035314667).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
