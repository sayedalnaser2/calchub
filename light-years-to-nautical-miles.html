Light Years to Nautical Miles Converter

Convert light years to nautical miles instantly. 1 ly = 5,108,385,800,000 nmi.

How it works

To convert light years to nautical miles, multiply the number of light years by 5,108,385,800,000. One light year equals 5,108,385,800,000 nautical miles, and one nautical mile equals 1.95757e-13 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Nautical Miles formula

Nautical miles = light years × 5,108,385,800,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to nautical miles. Multiply 12.5 × 5,108,385,800,000 = 63,854,822,000,000 nmi. To go the other way, multiply nautical miles by 1.95757e-13 (or divide by 5,108,385,800,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
