Horsepower to Kilowatts Converter

Convert horsepower to kilowatts instantly. 1 hp = 0.74569987 kW.

How it works

To convert horsepower to kilowatts, multiply the number of horsepower by 0.74569987. One horsepower equals 0.74569987 kilowatts, and one kilowatt equals 1.3410221 horsepower. The table shows common values.

Horsepower to Kilowatts formula

Kilowatts = horsepower × 0.74569987

Worked example: convert 12.5 horsepower to kilowatts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.74569987 = 9.3212484 kW. To go the other way, multiply kilowatts by 1.3410221 (or divide by 0.74569987).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
