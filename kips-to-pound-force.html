Convert kips to pound-force instantly. 1 kip = 1,000 lbf.

How it works

To convert kips to pound-force, multiply the number of kips by 1,000. One kip equals 1,000 pound-force, and one pound-force equals 0.001 kips. The table shows common values.

Kips to Pound-Force formula

Pound-force = kips × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kips to pound-force. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 lbf. To go the other way, multiply pound-force by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
