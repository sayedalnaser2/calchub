Gibibytes to Kibibytes Converter

Convert gibibytes to kibibytes instantly. 1 GiB = 1,048,576 KiB.

How it works

To convert gibibytes to kibibytes, multiply the number of gibibytes by 1,048,576. One gibibyte equals 1,048,576 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 9.53674e-07 gibibytes. The table shows common values.

Gibibytes to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = gibibytes × 1,048,576

Worked example: convert 12.5 gibibytes to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,048,576 = 13,107,200 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 9.53674e-07 (or divide by 1,048,576).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
