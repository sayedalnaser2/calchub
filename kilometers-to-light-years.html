How it works

To convert kilometers to light years, multiply the number of kilometers by 1.057e-13. To convert light years to kilometers, multiply by 9,460,730,500,000 (or divide by 1.057e-13). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilometers to Light Years formula

Light years = kilometers × 1.057e-13

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 1.057e-13 = 1.32125e-12 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 9,460,730,500,000 (or divide by 1.057e-13).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
