EMI Calculator

Calculate your loan EMI (equated monthly installment) and total interest.

How it works

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1) where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months. It works for home, car and personal loans in any currency.

Frequently asked questions

What affects my EMI?

Loan amount, interest rate and tenure. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but raises the total interest.

Is it a fixed amount?

For fixed-rate loans yes. Floating-rate loans change the EMI or tenure when rates change.
