Percent Yield Calculator

Calculate percent yield of a chemical reaction from actual and theoretical yield.

How it works

Percent yield = actual yield / theoretical yield x 100. The theoretical yield is the maximum product predicted by stoichiometry; real reactions lose product to side reactions and handling.

Frequently asked questions

Can percent yield be above 100%?

Only through errors such as impurities or incomplete drying.

What is limiting reagent?

The reactant that runs out first and sets the theoretical yield.
