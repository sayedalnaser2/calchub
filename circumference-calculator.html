Circumference Calculator

Calculate circumference, diameter and area of a circle from its radius.

How it works

Circumference = 2 x pi x r = pi x d. Area = pi x r^2.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find circumference from diameter?

Multiply the diameter by pi.

What is pi?

The ratio of circumference to diameter, about 3.14159.
