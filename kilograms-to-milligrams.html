Convert kilograms to milligrams instantly. 1 kg = 1e+06 mg.

How it works

To convert kilograms to milligrams, multiply the number of kilograms by 1e+06. One kilogram equals 1e+06 milligrams, and one milligram equals 1e-06 kilograms. The table shows common values.

Kilograms to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = kilograms × 1e+06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 1e+06 = 1.25e+07 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 1e-06 (or divide by 1e+06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
