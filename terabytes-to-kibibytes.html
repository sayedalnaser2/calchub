Terabytes to Kibibytes Converter

Convert terabytes to kibibytes instantly. 1 TB = 976,562,500 KiB.

How it works

To convert terabytes to kibibytes, multiply the number of terabytes by 976,562,500. One terabyte equals 976,562,500 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 1.024e-09 terabytes. The table shows common values.

Terabytes to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = terabytes × 976,562,500

Worked example: convert 12.5 terabytes to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 976,562,500 = 12,207,031,000 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 1.024e-09 (or divide by 976,562,500).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
