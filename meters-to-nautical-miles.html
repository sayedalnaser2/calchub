How it works

To convert meters to nautical miles, multiply the number of meters by 0.0005399568. One meter equals 0.0005399568 nautical miles, and one nautical mile equals 1,852 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Nautical Miles formula

Nautical miles = meters × 0.0005399568

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to nautical miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0005399568 = 0.00674946 nmi. To go the other way, multiply nautical miles by 1,852 (or divide by 0.0005399568).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
