Millibars to Bar Converter

Convert millibars to bar instantly. 1 mbar = 0.001 bar.

How it works

To convert millibars to bar, multiply the number of millibars by 0.001. One millibar equals 0.001 bar, and one bar equals 1,000 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to Bar formula

Bar = millibars × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
