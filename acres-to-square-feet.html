Acres to Square Feet Converter

Convert acres to square feet instantly. 1 acre = 43,560 ft².

How it works

To convert acres to square feet, multiply the number of acres by 43,560. One acre equals 43,560 square feet, and one square foot equals 2.29568e-05 acres. The table shows common values.

Acres to Square Feet formula

Square feet = acres × 43,560

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square feet. Multiply 12.5 × 43,560 = 544,500 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 2.29568e-05 (or divide by 43,560).
