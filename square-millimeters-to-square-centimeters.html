Square Millimeters to Square Centimeters Converter

Convert square millimeters to square centimeters instantly. 1 mm² = 0.01 cm².

How it works

To convert square millimeters to square centimeters, multiply the number of square millimeters by 0.01. One square millimeter equals 0.01 square centimeters, and one square centimeter equals 100 square millimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Millimeters to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square millimeters × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 square millimeters to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 100 (or divide by 0.01).
