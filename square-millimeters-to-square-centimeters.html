How it works

To convert square millimeters to square centimeters, multiply the number of square millimeters by 0.01. To convert square centimeters to square millimeters, multiply by 100 (or divide by 0.01). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Millimeters to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square millimeters × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 square millimeters to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 100 (or divide by 0.01).
