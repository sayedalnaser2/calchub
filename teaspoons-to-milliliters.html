Convert teaspoons to milliliters instantly. 1 tsp = 4.9289216 ml.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to milliliters, multiply the number of teaspoons by 4.9289216. One teaspoon equals 4.9289216 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.20288414 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = teaspoons × 4.9289216

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 4.9289216 = 61.61152 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.20288414 (or divide by 4.9289216).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
