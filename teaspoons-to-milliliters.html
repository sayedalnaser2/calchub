How it works

To convert teaspoons to milliliters, multiply the number of teaspoons by 4.9289216. To convert milliliters to teaspoons, multiply by 0.20288414 (or divide by 4.9289216). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
