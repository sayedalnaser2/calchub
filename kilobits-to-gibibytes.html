Kilobits to Gibibytes Converter

Convert kilobits to gibibytes instantly. 1 Kb = 1.16415e-07 GiB.

How it works

To convert kilobits to gibibytes, multiply the number of kilobits by 1.16415e-07. One kilobit equals 1.16415e-07 gibibytes, and one gibibyte equals 8,589,934.6 kilobits. The table shows common values.

Kilobits to Gibibytes formula

Gibibytes = kilobits × 1.16415e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobits to gibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.16415e-07 = 1.45519e-06 GiB. To go the other way, multiply gibibytes by 8,589,934.6 (or divide by 1.16415e-07).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
