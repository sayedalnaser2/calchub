Teaspoons to Oil Barrels Converter

Convert teaspoons to oil barrels instantly. 1 tsp = 3.1002e-05 bbl.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to oil barrels, multiply the number of teaspoons by 3.1002e-05. One teaspoon equals 3.1002e-05 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 32,256 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = teaspoons × 3.1002e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to oil barrels. Multiply 12.5 × 3.1002e-05 = 0.0003875248 bbl. To go the other way, multiply oil barrels by 32,256 (or divide by 3.1002e-05).
