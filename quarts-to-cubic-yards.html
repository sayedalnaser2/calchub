How it works

To convert quarts to cubic yards, multiply the number of quarts by 0.0012377829. To convert cubic yards to quarts, multiply by 807.8961 (or divide by 0.0012377829). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Quarts to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = quarts × 0.0012377829

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
