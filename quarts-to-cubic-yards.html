Quarts to Cubic Yards Converter

Convert quarts to cubic yards instantly. 1 qt = 0.0012377829 yd³.

How it works

To convert quarts to cubic yards, multiply the number of quarts by 0.0012377829. One quart equals 0.0012377829 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 807.8961 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = quarts × 0.0012377829

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0012377829 = 0.015472287 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 807.8961 (or divide by 0.0012377829).
