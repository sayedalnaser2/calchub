How it works

To convert weeks to centuries, multiply the number of weeks by 0.00019165349. To convert centuries to weeks, multiply by 5,217.75 (or divide by 0.00019165349). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Weeks to Centuries formula

Centuries = weeks × 0.00019165349

Worked example: convert 12.5 weeks to centuries. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00019165349 = 0.0023956686 centuries. To go the other way, multiply centuries by 5,217.75 (or divide by 0.00019165349).
