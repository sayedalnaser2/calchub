How it works

To convert gigabytes to mebibytes, multiply the number of gigabytes by 953.67432. One gigabyte equals 953.67432 mebibytes, and one mebibyte equals 0.001048576 gigabytes. The table shows common values.

Gigabytes to Mebibytes formula

Mebibytes = gigabytes × 953.67432

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabytes to mebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 953.67432 = 11,920.929 MiB. To go the other way, multiply mebibytes by 0.001048576 (or divide by 953.67432).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
