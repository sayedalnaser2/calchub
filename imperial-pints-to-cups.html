How it works

To convert imperial pints to cups, multiply the number of imperial pints by 2.4018999. One imperial pint equals 2.4018999 cups, and one cup equals 0.41633709 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Cups formula

Cups = imperial pints × 2.4018999

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 2.4018999 = 30.023748 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.41633709 (or divide by 2.4018999).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
