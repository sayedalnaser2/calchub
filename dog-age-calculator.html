Dog Age Calculator

Convert your dog's age to human years.

How it works

A 2020 study suggests human age = 16 x ln(dog age) + 31, which fits dogs better than the old 'times 7' rule, though breed and size matter.

Frequently asked questions

Do big dogs age faster?

Yes. Large breeds tend to have shorter lifespans than small breeds.
