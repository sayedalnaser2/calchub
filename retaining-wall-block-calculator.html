Retaining Wall Block Calculator

Estimate how many blocks you need for a retaining wall.

How it works

Courses = wall height / block height. Blocks per course = wall length / block length. Multiply and add a waste allowance. Taller walls need drainage, a compacted base and often engineering.

Frequently asked questions

How tall can a DIY wall be?

Many areas require engineering for walls over 3-4 feet.

Do I need a base?

Yes. A compacted gravel base and drainage stone are essential.
