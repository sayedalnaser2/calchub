Cubic Inches to Liters Converter

Convert cubic inches to liters instantly. 1 in³ = 0.016387064 L.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to liters, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.016387064. One cubic inch equals 0.016387064 liters, and one liter equals 61.023744 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Liters formula

Liters = cubic inches × 0.016387064

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.016387064 = 0.2048383 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 61.023744 (or divide by 0.016387064).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
