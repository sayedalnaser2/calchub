APR Calculator

Find the true annual percentage rate of a loan including upfront fees.

How it works

The APR is the rate that makes the payment schedule equal the money you actually receive (loan minus fees). It is higher than the stated rate whenever there are upfront fees.

Frequently asked questions

APR vs interest rate?

The interest rate is the cost of the principal. APR adds certain fees so you can compare loans fairly.
