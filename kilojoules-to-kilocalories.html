Kilojoules to Kilocalories Converter

Convert kilojoules to kilocalories instantly. 1 kJ = 0.23900574 kcal.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to kilocalories, multiply the number of kilojoules by 0.23900574. One kilojoule equals 0.23900574 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 4.184 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = kilojoules × 0.23900574

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.23900574 = 2.9875717 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 4.184 (or divide by 0.23900574).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
