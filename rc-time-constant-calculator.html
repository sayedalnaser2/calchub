How it works

The time constant tau = R x C is the time for a capacitor to charge to 63.2% of the supply voltage (or discharge to 36.8%). After five time constants it is about 99.3% charged. The RC filter cutoff is 1 / (2 pi R C).

Formula

τ = R x C. Enter any two of time constant, resistance and capacitance.

Frequently asked questions

What is a low-pass filter?

An RC circuit that passes low frequencies and attenuates high ones.

How long to fully charge?

In theory never, but 5τ is practically full.
