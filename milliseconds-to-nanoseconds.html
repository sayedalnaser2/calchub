Milliseconds to Nanoseconds Converter

Convert milliseconds to nanoseconds instantly. 1 ms = 1,000,000 ns.

How it works

To convert milliseconds to nanoseconds, multiply the number of milliseconds by 1,000,000. One millisecond equals 1,000,000 nanoseconds, and one nanosecond equals 1e-06 milliseconds. The table shows common values.

Milliseconds to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = milliseconds × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliseconds to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).
