How it works

To convert milliseconds to nanoseconds, multiply the number of milliseconds by 1,000,000. To convert nanoseconds to milliseconds, multiply by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Milliseconds to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = milliseconds × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliseconds to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).
