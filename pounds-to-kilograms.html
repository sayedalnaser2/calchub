How it works

To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply the number of pounds by 0.45359237. To convert kilograms to pounds, multiply by 2.2046226 (or divide by 0.45359237). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pounds to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.45359237 = 5.6699046 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 2.2046226 (or divide by 0.45359237).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
