Tablespoons to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert tablespoons to cubic inches instantly. 1 tbsp = 0.90234375 in³.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to cubic inches, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.90234375. One tablespoon equals 0.90234375 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 1.1082251 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = tablespoons × 0.90234375

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.90234375 = 11.279297 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 1.1082251 (or divide by 0.90234375).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
