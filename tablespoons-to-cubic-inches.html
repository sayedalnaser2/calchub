How it works

To convert tablespoons to cubic inches, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.90234375. To convert cubic inches to tablespoons, multiply by 1.1082251 (or divide by 0.90234375). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Tablespoons to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = tablespoons × 0.90234375

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.90234375 = 11.279297 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 1.1082251 (or divide by 0.90234375).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
