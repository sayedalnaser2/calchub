How it works

To convert miles to meters, multiply the number of miles by 1,609.344. To convert meters to miles, multiply by 0.00062137119 (or divide by 1,609.344). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles to Meters formula

Meters = miles × 1,609.344

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,609.344 = 20,116.8 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 0.00062137119 (or divide by 1,609.344).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
