Teaspoons to Cubic Yards Converter

Convert teaspoons to cubic yards instantly. 1 tsp = 6.44679e-06 yd³.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to cubic yards, multiply the number of teaspoons by 6.44679e-06. One teaspoon equals 6.44679e-06 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 155,116.05 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = teaspoons × 6.44679e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 6.44679e-06 = 8.05848e-05 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 155,116.05 (or divide by 6.44679e-06).
