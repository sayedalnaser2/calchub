Convert cups to imperial pints instantly. 1 cup = 0.41633709 imp pt.

How it works

To convert cups to imperial pints, multiply the number of cups by 0.41633709. One cup equals 0.41633709 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 2.4018999 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = cups × 0.41633709

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.41633709 = 5.2042137 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 2.4018999 (or divide by 0.41633709).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
