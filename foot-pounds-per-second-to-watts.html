Foot-Pounds per Second to Watts Converter

Convert foot-pounds per second to watts instantly. 1 ft-lb/s = 1.3558179 W.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds per second to watts, multiply the number of foot-pounds per second by 1.3558179. One foot-pound per second equals 1.3558179 watts, and one watt equals 0.73756215 foot-pounds per second. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds per Second to Watts formula

Watts = foot-pounds per second × 1.3558179

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds per second to watts. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3558179 = 16.947724 W. To go the other way, multiply watts by 0.73756215 (or divide by 1.3558179).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
