How it works

To convert inches to nanometers, multiply the number of inches by 25,400,000. To convert nanometers to inches, multiply by 3.93701e-08 (or divide by 25,400,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Inches to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = inches × 25,400,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 25,400,000 = 317,500,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 3.93701e-08 (or divide by 25,400,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
