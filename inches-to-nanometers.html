Inches to Nanometers Converter

Convert inches to nanometers instantly. 1 in = 25,400,000 nm.

How it works

To convert inches to nanometers, multiply the number of inches by 25,400,000. One inch equals 25,400,000 nanometers, and one nanometer equals 3.93701e-08 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = inches × 25,400,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 25,400,000 = 317,500,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 3.93701e-08 (or divide by 25,400,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
