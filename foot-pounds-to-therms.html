Foot-Pounds to Therms Converter

Convert foot-pounds to therms instantly. 1 ft-lb = 1.28537e-08 therms.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to therms, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 1.28537e-08. One foot-pound equals 1.28537e-08 therms, and one therm equals 77,798,351 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Therms formula

Therms = foot-pounds × 1.28537e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 1.28537e-08 = 1.60672e-07 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 77,798,351 (or divide by 1.28537e-08).
