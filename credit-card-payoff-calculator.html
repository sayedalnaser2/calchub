Paying off credit card debt faster

Credit card interest compounds, so small increases in your monthly payment can shorten payoff by years. Try raising the payment above and watch total interest fall. Two common strategies: the avalanche method (pay the highest rate first) saves the most money; the snowball method (pay the smallest balance first) builds momentum.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a minimum payment take so long?

Minimums are often just slightly above the monthly interest, so very little reduces the balance.

Should I consider a balance transfer?

A 0% intro APR transfer can save interest if you pay it off before the promo ends and the transfer fee is reasonable.
