Rent Affordability Calculator

Find how much rent you can afford with the 30% rule.

How it works

A common guideline is spending no more than 30% of gross income on rent. Lenders also look at total debt-to-income, here taken as 36%.

Frequently asked questions

Do landlords use 3x rent?

Many require monthly income of at least three times the rent, which is about 33%.
