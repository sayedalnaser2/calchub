Leap Year Calculator

Check whether a year is a leap year and find the next one.

How it works

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except century years, which must also be divisible by 400. So 2000 was a leap year but 1900 was not.

Frequently asked questions

Why do we have leap years?

A solar year is about 365.2425 days, and the extra day keeps calendars in sync.

Is 2100 a leap year?

No. It is divisible by 100 but not by 400.
