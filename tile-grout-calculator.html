Grout Calculator

Estimate the grout needed for a tiled area.

How it works

Grout volume is the joint length times width times tile thickness. Density (about 1.86 lb per 16 cubic inches) converts it to weight, with 10% waste.

Frequently asked questions

Sanded or unsanded?

Sanded for joints wider than 1/8 inch; unsanded for narrow joints.
