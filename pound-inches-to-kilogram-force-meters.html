Pound-Inches to Kilogram-Force Meters Converter

Convert pound-inches to kilogram-force meters instantly. 1 lb-in = 0.011521246 kgf·m.

How it works

To convert pound-inches to kilogram-force meters, multiply the number of pound-inches by 0.011521246. One pound-inch equals 0.011521246 kilogram-force meters, and one kilogram-force meter equals 86.796166 pound-inches. The table shows common values.

Pound-Inches to Kilogram-Force Meters formula

Kilogram-force meters = pound-inches × 0.011521246

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-inches to kilogram-force meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.011521246 = 0.14401558 kgf·m. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force meters by 86.796166 (or divide by 0.011521246).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
