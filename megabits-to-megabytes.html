Megabits to Megabytes Converter

Convert megabits to megabytes instantly. 1 Mb = 0.125 MB.

How it works

To convert megabits to megabytes, multiply the number of megabits by 0.125. One megabit equals 0.125 megabytes, and one megabyte equals 8 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Megabytes formula

Megabytes = megabits × 0.125

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to megabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.125 = 1.5625 MB. To go the other way, multiply megabytes by 8 (or divide by 0.125).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
