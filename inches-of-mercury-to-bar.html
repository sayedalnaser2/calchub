Inches of Mercury to Bar Converter

Convert inches of mercury to bar instantly. 1 inHg = 0.03386389 bar.

How it works

To convert inches of mercury to bar, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 0.03386389. One inch of mercury equals 0.03386389 bar, and one bar equals 29.52998 inches of mercury. The table shows common values.

Inches of Mercury to Bar formula

Bar = inches of mercury × 0.03386389

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 0.03386389 = 0.42329862 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 29.52998 (or divide by 0.03386389).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
