Watts to Horsepower Converter

Convert watts to horsepower instantly. 1 W = 0.0013410221 hp.

How it works

To convert watts to horsepower, multiply the number of watts by 0.0013410221. One watt equals 0.0013410221 horsepower, and one horsepower equals 745.69987 watts. The table shows common values.

Watts to Horsepower formula

Horsepower = watts × 0.0013410221

Worked example: convert 12.5 watts to horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013410221 = 0.016762776 hp. To go the other way, multiply horsepower by 745.69987 (or divide by 0.0013410221).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
