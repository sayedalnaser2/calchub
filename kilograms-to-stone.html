How it works

To convert kilograms to stone, multiply the number of kilograms by 0.15747304. One kilogram equals 0.15747304 stone, and one stone equals 6.3502932 kilograms. The table shows common values.

Kilograms to Stone formula

Stone = kilograms × 0.15747304

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 0.15747304 = 1.9684131 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 6.3502932 (or divide by 0.15747304).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
