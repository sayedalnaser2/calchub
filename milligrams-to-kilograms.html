How it works

To convert milligrams to kilograms, multiply the number of milligrams by 1e-06. To convert kilograms to milligrams, multiply by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Milligrams to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = milligrams × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
