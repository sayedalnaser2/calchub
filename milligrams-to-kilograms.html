How it works

To convert milligrams to kilograms, multiply the number of milligrams by 1e-06. One milligram equals 1e-06 kilograms, and one kilogram equals 1e+06 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = milligrams × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 1e+06 (or divide by 1e-06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
