Light Years to Fathoms Converter

Convert light years to fathoms instantly. 1 ly = 5.17319e+15 fathoms.

How it works

To convert light years to fathoms, multiply the number of light years by 5.17319e+15. One light year equals 5.17319e+15 fathoms, and one fathom equals 1.93304e-16 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = light years × 5.17319e+15

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 5.17319e+15 = 6.46649e+16 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 1.93304e-16 (or divide by 5.17319e+15).
