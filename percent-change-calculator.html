How it works

Percent change = (new value - old value) / |old value| x 100. A positive result is an increase and a negative result is a decrease.

Formula

Percent change = (new - old) / old x 100. Enter any two of old value, new value and percent change.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't a 50% rise followed by a 50% fall back to the start?

The fall applies to the larger number. 100 up 50% is 150, and 150 down 50% is 75.
