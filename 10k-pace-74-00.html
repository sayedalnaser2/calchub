10K Pace for a 74:00 Finish Time

To run a 10K in 74:00 you need 11:55 per mile (7:24 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A 10K is 10 km (6.21 miles), so 74:00 works out to 11:55 per mile or 7:24 per kilometer.
