Sales Tax Calculator

Add sales tax, remove it from a total, or find the tax rate from any two values.

Type in any 2 fields and the rest are calculated.

How it works

Sales tax equals the pre-tax price multiplied by the tax rate. Add it to the price to get the total you pay.

Formula

Total = price x (1 + tax rate). Enter any two of price, rate and total to add tax, remove tax from a total, or find the rate.

Frequently asked questions

What is my sales tax rate?

It depends on your state, county and city. Check your local revenue department's website for the combined rate.
