How it works

To convert atmospheres to mmHg, multiply the number of atmospheres by 759.99989. One atmosphere equals 759.99989 mmHg, and one mmHg equals 0.0013157897 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Mmhg = atmospheres × 759.99989

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
