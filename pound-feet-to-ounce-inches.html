Pound-Feet to Ounce-Inches Converter

Convert pound-feet to ounce-inches instantly. 1 lb-ft = 192 oz-in.

How it works

To convert pound-feet to ounce-inches, multiply the number of pound-feet by 192. One pound-foot equals 192 ounce-inches, and one ounce-inch equals 0.0052083333 pound-feet. The table shows common values.

Pound-Feet to Ounce-Inches formula

Ounce-inches = pound-feet × 192

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-feet to ounce-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 192 = 2,400 oz-in. To go the other way, multiply ounce-inches by 0.0052083333 (or divide by 192).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
