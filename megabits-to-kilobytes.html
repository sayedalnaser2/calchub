Megabits to Kilobytes Converter

Convert megabits to kilobytes instantly. 1 Mb = 125 KB.

How it works

To convert megabits to kilobytes, multiply the number of megabits by 125. One megabit equals 125 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 0.008 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = megabits × 125

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 125 = 1,562.5 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 0.008 (or divide by 125).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
