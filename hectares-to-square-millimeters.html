Hectares to Square Millimeters Converter

Convert hectares to square millimeters instantly. 1 ha = 10,000,000,000 mm².

How it works

To convert hectares to square millimeters, multiply the number of hectares by 10,000,000,000. One hectare equals 10,000,000,000 square millimeters, and one square millimeter equals 1e-10 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Square Millimeters formula

Square millimeters = hectares × 10,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to square millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 10,000,000,000 = 125,000,000,000 mm². To go the other way, multiply square millimeters by 1e-10 (or divide by 10,000,000,000).
