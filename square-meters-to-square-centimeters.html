Square Meters to Square Centimeters Converter

Convert square meters to square centimeters instantly. 1 m² = 10,000 cm².

How it works

To convert square meters to square centimeters, multiply the number of square meters by 10,000. One square meter equals 10,000 square centimeters, and one square centimeter equals 0.0001 square meters. The table shows common values.

Square Meters to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square meters × 10,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 10,000 = 125,000 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 0.0001 (or divide by 10,000).
