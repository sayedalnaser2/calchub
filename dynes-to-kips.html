Dynes to Kips Converter

Convert dynes to kips instantly. 1 dyn = 2.24809e-09 kip.

How it works

To convert dynes to kips, multiply the number of dynes by 2.24809e-09. One dyne equals 2.24809e-09 kips, and one kip equals 444,822,160 dynes. The table shows common values.

Dynes to Kips formula

Kips = dynes × 2.24809e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 dynes to kips. Multiply 12.5 × 2.24809e-09 = 2.81011e-08 kip. To go the other way, multiply kips by 444,822,160 (or divide by 2.24809e-09).
