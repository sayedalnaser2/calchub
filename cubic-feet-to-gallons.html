Cubic Feet to Gallons Converter

Convert cubic feet to gallons instantly. 1 ft³ = 7.4805195 gal.

How it works

To convert cubic feet to gallons, multiply the number of cubic feet by 7.4805195. One cubic foot equals 7.4805195 gallons, and one gallon equals 0.13368056 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Gallons formula

Gallons = cubic feet × 7.4805195

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 7.4805195 = 93.506494 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 0.13368056 (or divide by 7.4805195).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
