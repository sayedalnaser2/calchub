Carats to Metric Tons Converter

Convert carats to metric tons instantly. 1 ct = 2e-07 t.

How it works

To convert carats to metric tons, multiply the number of carats by 2e-07. One carat equals 2e-07 metric tons, and one metric ton equals 5,000,000 carats. The table shows common values.

Carats to Metric Tons formula

Metric tons = carats × 2e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 carats to metric tons. Multiply 12.5 × 2e-07 = 2.5e-06 t. To go the other way, multiply metric tons by 5,000,000 (or divide by 2e-07).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
