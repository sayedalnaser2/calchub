Feet to Mils Converter

Convert feet to mils instantly. 1 ft = 12,000 mil.

How it works

To convert feet to mils, multiply the number of feet by 12,000. One foot equals 12,000 mils, and one mil equals 8.33333e-05 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Mils formula

Mils = feet × 12,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 12,000 = 150,000 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 8.33333e-05 (or divide by 12,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
