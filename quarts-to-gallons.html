How it works

To convert quarts to gallons, multiply the number of quarts by 0.25. To convert gallons to quarts, multiply by 4 (or divide by 0.25). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Quarts to Gallons formula

Gallons = quarts × 0.25

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.25 = 3.125 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 4 (or divide by 0.25).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
