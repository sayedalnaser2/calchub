Probability Calculator

Combine two event probabilities: and, or, neither.

How it works

For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B) and P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B).

Frequently asked questions

What if events are not independent?

Then you need the conditional probability P(B|A).
