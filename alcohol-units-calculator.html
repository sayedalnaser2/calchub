Alcohol Units and Standard Drinks Calculator

Convert a drink's volume and strength into UK units and US standard drinks.

How it works

UK units = volume (ml) x ABV / 1000, where one unit is 10 ml (8 g) of pure alcohol. A US standard drink contains 14 g of alcohol. Ethanol has a density of 0.789 g/ml and about 7 kcal per gram.

Frequently asked questions

What are the low-risk drinking guidelines?

UK guidance advises no more than 14 units a week.

Is a pint one unit?

No. A pint of 5% beer is about 2.8 units.
