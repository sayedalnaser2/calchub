How it works

To convert kilopascals to atmospheres, multiply the number of kilopascals by 0.0098692327. To convert atmospheres to kilopascals, multiply by 101.325 (or divide by 0.0098692327). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilopascals to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = kilopascals × 0.0098692327

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
