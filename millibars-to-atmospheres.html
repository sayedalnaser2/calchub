Millibars to Atmospheres Converter

Convert millibars to atmospheres instantly. 1 mbar = 0.00098692327 atm.

How it works

To convert millibars to atmospheres, multiply the number of millibars by 0.00098692327. One millibar equals 0.00098692327 atmospheres, and one atmosphere equals 1,013.25 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = millibars × 0.00098692327

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to atmospheres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00098692327 = 0.012336541 atm. To go the other way, multiply atmospheres by 1,013.25 (or divide by 0.00098692327).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
