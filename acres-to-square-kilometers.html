How it works

To convert acres to square kilometers, multiply the number of acres by 0.0040468564. One acre equals 0.0040468564 square kilometers, and one square kilometer equals 247.10538 acres. The table shows common values.

Acres to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = acres × 0.0040468564

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0040468564 = 0.050585705 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 247.10538 (or divide by 0.0040468564).
