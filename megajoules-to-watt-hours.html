Convert megajoules to watt hours instantly. 1 MJ = 277.77778 Wh.

How it works

To convert megajoules to watt hours, multiply the number of megajoules by 277.77778. One megajoule equals 277.77778 watt hours, and one watt hour equals 0.0036 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = megajoules × 277.77778

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 277.77778 = 3,472.2222 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 0.0036 (or divide by 277.77778).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
