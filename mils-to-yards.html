Mils to Yards Converter

Convert mils to yards instantly. 1 mil = 2.77778e-05 yd.

How it works

To convert mils to yards, multiply the number of mils by 2.77778e-05. One mil equals 2.77778e-05 yards, and one yard equals 36,000 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Yards formula

Yards = mils × 2.77778e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 2.77778e-05 = 0.00034722222 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 36,000 (or divide by 2.77778e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
