Feet to Meters Converter

Convert feet to meters instantly. 1 ft = 0.3048 m.

How it works

To convert feet to meters, multiply the number of feet by 0.3048. One foot equals 0.3048 meters, and one meter equals 3.2808399 feet. The table shows common values.

Feet to Meters formula

Meters = feet × 0.3048

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.3048 = 3.81 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 3.2808399 (or divide by 0.3048).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
