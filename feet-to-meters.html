How it works

To convert feet to meters, multiply the number of feet by 0.3048. To convert meters to feet, multiply by 3.2808399 (or divide by 0.3048). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Feet to Meters formula

Meters = feet × 0.3048

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.3048 = 3.81 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 3.2808399 (or divide by 0.3048).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
