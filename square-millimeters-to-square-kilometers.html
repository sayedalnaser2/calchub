Square Millimeters to Square Kilometers Converter

Convert square millimeters to square kilometers instantly. 1 mm² = 1e-12 km².

How it works

To convert square millimeters to square kilometers, multiply the number of square millimeters by 1e-12. One square millimeter equals 1e-12 square kilometers, and one square kilometer equals 1,000,000,000,000 square millimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Millimeters to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = square millimeters × 1e-12

Worked example: convert 12.5 square millimeters to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-12 = 1.25e-11 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 1,000,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-12).
