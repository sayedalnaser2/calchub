How it works

Gear ratio = driven teeth / driving teeth. Output speed equals input speed divided by the ratio, while torque rises by the ratio in an ideal, lossless system.

Formula

Gear ratio = driven teeth / driving teeth. Enter any two values.

Frequently asked questions

What does a higher ratio mean?

More torque and lower output speed.

Do real gears lose power?

Yes, typically a few percent to friction per mesh.
