How it works

To convert BTU to kilojoules, multiply the number of BTU by 1.0550559. To convert kilojoules to BTU, multiply by 0.94781712 (or divide by 1.0550559). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

BTU to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = BTU × 1.0550559

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1.0550559 = 13.188198 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 0.94781712 (or divide by 1.0550559).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
