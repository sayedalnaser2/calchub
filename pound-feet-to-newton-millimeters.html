How it works

To convert pound-feet to newton millimeters, multiply the number of pound-feet by 1,355.8179. To convert newton millimeters to pound-feet, multiply by 0.00073756215 (or divide by 1,355.8179). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
