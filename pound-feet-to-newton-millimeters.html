Pound-Feet to Newton Millimeters Converter

Convert pound-feet to newton millimeters instantly. 1 lb-ft = 1,355.8179 N·mm.

How it works

To convert pound-feet to newton millimeters, multiply the number of pound-feet by 1,355.8179. One pound-foot equals 1,355.8179 newton millimeters, and one newton millimeter equals 0.00073756215 pound-feet. The table shows common values.

Pound-Feet to Newton Millimeters formula

Newton millimeters = pound-feet × 1,355.8179

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-feet to newton millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,355.8179 = 16,947.724 N·mm. To go the other way, multiply newton millimeters by 0.00073756215 (or divide by 1,355.8179).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
