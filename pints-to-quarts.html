How it works

To convert pints to quarts, multiply the number of pints by 0.5. To convert quarts to pints, multiply by 2 (or divide by 0.5). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pints to Quarts formula

Quarts = pints × 0.5

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.5 = 6.25 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 2 (or divide by 0.5).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
