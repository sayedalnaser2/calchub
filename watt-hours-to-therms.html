Watt Hours to Therms Converter

Convert watt hours to therms instantly. 1 Wh = 3.41296e-05 therms.

How it works

To convert watt hours to therms, multiply the number of watt hours by 3.41296e-05. One watt hour equals 3.41296e-05 therms, and one therm equals 29,300.111 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Therms formula

Therms = watt hours × 3.41296e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 3.41296e-05 = 0.00042661954 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 29,300.111 (or divide by 3.41296e-05).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
