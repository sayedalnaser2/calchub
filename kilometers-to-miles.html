Kilometers to Miles Converter

Convert kilometers to miles instantly. 1 km = 0.62137119 mi.

How it works

To convert kilometers to miles, multiply the number of kilometers by 0.62137119. One kilometer equals 0.62137119 miles, and one mile equals 1.609344 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Miles formula

Miles = kilometers × 0.62137119

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.62137119 = 7.7671399 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1.609344 (or divide by 0.62137119).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
