Kilowatt Hours to Joules Converter

Convert kilowatt hours to joules instantly. 1 kWh = 3,600,000 J.

How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to joules, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 3,600,000. One kilowatt hour equals 3,600,000 joules, and one joule equals 2.77778e-07 kilowatt hours. The table shows common values.

Kilowatt Hours to Joules formula

Joules = kilowatt hours × 3,600,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600,000 = 45,000,000 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 2.77778e-07 (or divide by 3,600,000).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
