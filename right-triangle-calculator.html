Right Triangle Calculator

Find the hypotenuse, area, perimeter and angles of a right triangle from its two legs.

How it works

By the Pythagorean theorem c = sqrt(a^2 + b^2). Angle A = arctan(a/b) and the other acute angle is 90 degrees minus A.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 3-4-5 triangle?

The smallest integer right triangle: 3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2.

How do I find a missing leg?

Use a = sqrt(c^2 - b^2).
