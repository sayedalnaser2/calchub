Plywood Sheet Calculator

Calculate how many 4x8 sheets of plywood or OSB you need.

How it works

A 4x8 sheet covers 32 square feet. Sheets = area / 32, plus a waste allowance for cuts and offcuts.

Frequently asked questions

How much waste should I allow?

10% is typical, 15% for complex layouts.

What thickness should I use?

Subfloors are often 3/4 inch; roof sheathing 1/2 inch, depending on rafter spacing.
