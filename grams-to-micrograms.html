How it works

To convert grams to micrograms, multiply the number of grams by 1,000,000. To convert micrograms to grams, multiply by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Grams to Micrograms formula

Micrograms = grams × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to micrograms. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 µg. To go the other way, multiply micrograms by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
