How it works

To convert centimeters per second to knots, multiply the number of centimeters per second by 0.019438445. One centimeter per second equals 0.019438445 knots, and one knot equals 51.444444 centimeters per second. The table shows common values.

Centimeters per Second to Knots formula

Knots = centimeters per second × 0.019438445

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters per second to knots. Multiply 12.5 × 0.019438445 = 0.24298056 kn. To go the other way, multiply knots by 51.444444 (or divide by 0.019438445).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
