Cube Volume and Diagonal Calculator

Calculate volume, surface area and diagonals of a cube from its side length.

How it works

Volume = s^3, surface area = 6 s^2, face diagonal = s x sqrt(2), and space diagonal = s x sqrt(3).

Frequently asked questions

How do I find side from volume?

Take the cube root of the volume.

How many faces does a cube have?

Six.
