Mils to Millimeters Converter

Convert mils to millimeters instantly. 1 mil = 0.0254 mm.

How it works

To convert mils to millimeters, multiply the number of mils by 0.0254. One mil equals 0.0254 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 39.370079 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = mils × 0.0254

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0254 = 0.3175 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 39.370079 (or divide by 0.0254).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
