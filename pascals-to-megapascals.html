Pascals to Megapascals Converter

Convert pascals to megapascals instantly. 1 Pa = 1e-06 MPa.

How it works

To convert pascals to megapascals, multiply the number of pascals by 1e-06. One pascal equals 1e-06 megapascals, and one megapascal equals 1,000,000 pascals. The table shows common values.

Pascals to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = pascals × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 pascals to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
