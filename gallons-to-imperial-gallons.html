How it works

To convert gallons to imperial gallons, multiply the number of gallons by 0.83267418. One gallon equals 0.83267418 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 1.2009499 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = gallons × 0.83267418

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
