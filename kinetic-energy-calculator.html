How it works

Kinetic energy is KE = ½mv². Doubling the speed quadruples the energy.

Formula

Kinetic energy KE = 1/2 x m x v squared. Doubling speed quadruples the energy.

Frequently asked questions

Why does speed matter so much?

Energy grows with the square of velocity, which is why high-speed crashes are far more destructive.
