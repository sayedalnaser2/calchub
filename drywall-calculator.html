Drywall Calculator

Estimate sheets of drywall for walls and ceilings.

How it works

Total surface area is divided by the sheet area, plus 10% for cuts. Door and window openings are not deducted, to be safe.

Frequently asked questions

What thickness should I use?

1/2 inch for walls, 5/8 inch for ceilings or fire-rated assemblies.
