Oil Barrels to Pints Converter

Convert oil barrels to pints instantly. 1 bbl = 336 pt.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to pints, multiply the number of oil barrels by 336. One oil barrel equals 336 pints, and one pint equals 0.0029761905 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Pints formula

Pints = oil barrels × 336

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 336 = 4,200 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 0.0029761905 (or divide by 336).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
