P-Value Calculator

Find the p-value from a z-score for one- or two-tailed tests.

How it works

The p-value is the probability of a result at least as extreme as yours if the null hypothesis were true, computed from the standard normal distribution.

Frequently asked questions

What does p < 0.05 mean?

The result would be unlikely (under 5%) if there were no real effect, so it is called statistically significant.
