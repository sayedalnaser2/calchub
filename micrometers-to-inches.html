Micrometers to Inches Converter

Convert micrometers to inches instantly. 1 µm = 3.93701e-05 in.

How it works

To convert micrometers to inches, multiply the number of micrometers by 3.93701e-05. One micrometer equals 3.93701e-05 inches, and one inch equals 25,400 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Inches formula

Inches = micrometers × 3.93701e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 3.93701e-05 = 0.00049212598 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 25,400 (or divide by 3.93701e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
