Gallons per Minute to Cubic Meters per Hour Converter

Convert gallons per minute to cubic meters per hour instantly. 1 gal/min = 0.22712471 m³/h.

How it works

To convert gallons per minute to cubic meters per hour, multiply the number of gallons per minute by 0.22712471. One gallon per minute equals 0.22712471 cubic meters per hour, and one cubic meter per hour equals 4.4028675 gallons per minute. The table shows common values.

Gallons per Minute to Cubic Meters per Hour formula

Cubic meters per hour = gallons per minute × 0.22712471

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons per minute to cubic meters per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 0.22712471 = 2.8390588 m³/h. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters per hour by 4.4028675 (or divide by 0.22712471).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
