Light Years to Inches Converter

Convert light years to inches instantly. 1 ly = 3.7247e+17 in.

How it works

To convert light years to inches, multiply the number of light years by 3.7247e+17. One light year equals 3.7247e+17 inches, and one inch equals 2.68478e-18 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Inches formula

Inches = light years × 3.7247e+17

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 3.7247e+17 = 4.65587e+18 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 2.68478e-18 (or divide by 3.7247e+17).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
