How it works

To convert yards to meters, multiply the number of yards by 0.9144. To convert meters to yards, multiply by 1.0936133 (or divide by 0.9144). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Yards to Meters formula

Meters = yards × 0.9144

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.9144 = 11.43 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 1.0936133 (or divide by 0.9144).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
