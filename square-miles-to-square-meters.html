Square Miles to Square Meters Converter

Convert square miles to square meters instantly. 1 mi² = 2,589,988.1 m².

How it works

To convert square miles to square meters, multiply the number of square miles by 2,589,988.1. One square mile equals 2,589,988.1 square meters, and one square meter equals 3.86102e-07 square miles. The table shows common values.

Square Miles to Square Meters formula

Square meters = square miles × 2,589,988.1

Worked example: convert 12.5 square miles to square meters. Multiply 12.5 × 2,589,988.1 = 32,374,851 m². To go the other way, multiply square meters by 3.86102e-07 (or divide by 2,589,988.1).
