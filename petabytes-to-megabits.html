Petabytes to Megabits Converter

Convert petabytes to megabits instantly. 1 PB = 8,000,000,000 Mb.

How it works

To convert petabytes to megabits, multiply the number of petabytes by 8,000,000,000. One petabyte equals 8,000,000,000 megabits, and one megabit equals 1.25e-10 petabytes. The table shows common values.

Petabytes to Megabits formula

Megabits = petabytes × 8,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 petabytes to megabits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,000,000,000 = 100,000,000,000 Mb. To go the other way, multiply megabits by 1.25e-10 (or divide by 8,000,000,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
