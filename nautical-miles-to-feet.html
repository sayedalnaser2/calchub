How it works

To convert nautical miles to feet, multiply the number of nautical miles by 6,076.1155. One nautical mile equals 6,076.1155 feet, and one foot equals 0.00016457883 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Feet formula

Feet = nautical miles × 6,076.1155

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 6,076.1155 = 75,951.444 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 0.00016457883 (or divide by 6,076.1155).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
