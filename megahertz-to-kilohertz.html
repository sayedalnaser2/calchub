Convert megahertz to kilohertz instantly. 1 MHz = 1,000 kHz.

How it works

To convert megahertz to kilohertz, multiply the number of megahertz by 1,000. One megahertz equals 1,000 kilohertz, and one kilohertz equals 0.001 megahertz. The table shows common values.

Megahertz to Kilohertz formula

Kilohertz = megahertz × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megahertz to kilohertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 kHz. To go the other way, multiply kilohertz by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
