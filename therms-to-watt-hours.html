Convert therms to watt hours instantly. 1 therm = 29,300.111 Wh.

How it works

To convert therms to watt hours, multiply the number of therms by 29,300.111. One therm equals 29,300.111 watt hours, and one watt hour equals 3.41296e-05 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = therms × 29,300.111

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 29,300.111 = 366,251.39 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 3.41296e-05 (or divide by 29,300.111).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
