Imperial Pints to Liters Converter

Convert imperial pints to liters instantly. 1 imp pt = 0.56826125 L.

How it works

To convert imperial pints to liters, multiply the number of imperial pints by 0.56826125. One imperial pint equals 0.56826125 liters, and one liter equals 1.759754 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Liters formula

Liters = imperial pints × 0.56826125

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.56826125 = 7.1032656 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 1.759754 (or divide by 0.56826125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
