Convert imperial gallons to cubic inches instantly. 1 imp gal = 277.41943 in³.

How it works

To convert imperial gallons to cubic inches, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 277.41943. One imperial gallon equals 277.41943 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.0036046501 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = imperial gallons × 277.41943

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 277.41943 = 3,467.7429 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.0036046501 (or divide by 277.41943).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
