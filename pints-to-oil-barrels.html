How it works

To convert pints to oil barrels, multiply the number of pints by 0.0029761905. One pint equals 0.0029761905 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 336 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = pints × 0.0029761905

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to oil barrels. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0029761905 = 0.037202381 bbl. To go the other way, multiply oil barrels by 336 (or divide by 0.0029761905).
