How it works

To convert grains to carats, multiply the number of grains by 0.32399455. To convert carats to grains, multiply by 3.0864717 (or divide by 0.32399455). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Grains to Carats formula

Carats = grains × 0.32399455

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 0.32399455 = 4.0499319 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 3.0864717 (or divide by 0.32399455).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
