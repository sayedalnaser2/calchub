FIRE Calculator

Estimate your financial independence number and years until you reach it.

How it works

Your FIRE number is annual spending divided by the withdrawal rate (25x spending at 4%). The calculator grows your savings until it reaches that number.

Frequently asked questions

Is 4% safe?

It is a historical guideline for ~30-year retirements. Longer retirements may need a lower rate.
