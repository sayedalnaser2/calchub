How it works

To convert ounces to stone, multiply the number of ounces by 0.0044642857. To convert stone to ounces, multiply by 224 (or divide by 0.0044642857). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Ounces to Stone formula

Stone = ounces × 0.0044642857

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0044642857 = 0.055803571 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 224 (or divide by 0.0044642857).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
