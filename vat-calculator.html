VAT Calculator

Add or remove VAT, or find the rate, from any two values.

Type in any 2 fields and the rest are calculated.

How it works

To add VAT multiply by 1 + rate. To remove it divide by 1 + rate. The VAT amount is the difference.

Formula

Gross = net x (1 + VAT rate). Enter any two of net, rate and gross to add VAT, remove VAT, or find the rate.

Frequently asked questions

Standard VAT rates?

The UK is 20%; EU countries range roughly from 17% to 27%.
