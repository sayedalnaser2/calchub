How it works

To convert grams to stone, multiply the number of grams by 0.00015747304. One gram equals 0.00015747304 stone, and one stone equals 6,350.2932 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Stone formula

Stone = grams × 0.00015747304

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00015747304 = 0.0019684131 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 6,350.2932 (or divide by 0.00015747304).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
