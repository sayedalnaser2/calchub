Cubic Meters to Cubic Yards Converter

Convert cubic meters to cubic yards instantly. 1 m³ = 1.3079506 yd³.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to cubic yards, multiply the number of cubic meters by 1.3079506. One cubic meter equals 1.3079506 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 0.76455486 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = cubic meters × 1.3079506

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3079506 = 16.349383 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 0.76455486 (or divide by 1.3079506).
