Megabits to Gigabits Converter

Convert megabits to gigabits instantly. 1 Mb = 0.001 Gb.

How it works

To convert megabits to gigabits, multiply the number of megabits by 0.001. One megabit equals 0.001 gigabits, and one gigabit equals 1,000 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Gigabits formula

Gigabits = megabits × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to gigabits. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 Gb. To go the other way, multiply gigabits by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
