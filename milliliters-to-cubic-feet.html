Milliliters to Cubic Feet Converter

Convert milliliters to cubic feet instantly. 1 ml = 3.53147e-05 ft³.

How it works

To convert milliliters to cubic feet, multiply the number of milliliters by 3.53147e-05. One milliliter equals 3.53147e-05 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 28,316.847 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = milliliters × 3.53147e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3.53147e-05 = 0.00044143333 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 28,316.847 (or divide by 3.53147e-05).
