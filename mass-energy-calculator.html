Mass-Energy Equivalence (E = mc²) Calculator

Calculate the energy equivalent of a mass.

How it works

Einstein's relation E = mc² gives the rest energy of a mass. One gram corresponds to about 21.5 kilotons of TNT.

Frequently asked questions

Can all mass be converted?

No. Only a small fraction is released in nuclear reactions.
