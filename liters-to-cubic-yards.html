How it works

To convert liters to cubic yards, multiply the number of liters by 0.0013079506. One liter equals 0.0013079506 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 764.55486 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = liters × 0.0013079506

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013079506 = 0.016349383 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 764.55486 (or divide by 0.0013079506).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
