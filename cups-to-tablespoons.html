Cups to Tablespoons Converter

Convert cups to tablespoons instantly. 1 cup = 16 tbsp.

How it works

To convert cups to tablespoons, multiply the number of cups by 16. One cup equals 16 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.0625 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = cups × 16

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 16 = 200 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.0625 (or divide by 16).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
