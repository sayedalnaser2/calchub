Gallons to Pints Converter

Convert gallons to pints instantly. 1 gal = 8 pt.

How it works

To convert gallons to pints, multiply the number of gallons by 8. One gallon equals 8 pints, and one pint equals 0.125 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Pints formula

Pints = gallons × 8

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 8 = 100 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 0.125 (or divide by 8).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
