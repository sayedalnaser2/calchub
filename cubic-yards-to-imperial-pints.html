Cubic Yards to Imperial Pints Converter

Convert cubic yards to imperial pints instantly. 1 yd³ = 1,345.4285 imp pt.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to imperial pints, multiply the number of cubic yards by 1,345.4285. One cubic yard equals 1,345.4285 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.00074325765 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = cubic yards × 1,345.4285

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 1,345.4285 = 16,817.856 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.00074325765 (or divide by 1,345.4285).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
