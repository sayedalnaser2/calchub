How it works

Speed = distance / time. Enter the total time in hours and minutes.

Formula

speed = distance / time, so distance = speed x time and time = distance / speed. Enter any two and the third is solved.

Frequently asked questions

Rearrange: distance = speed x time and time = distance / speed.
