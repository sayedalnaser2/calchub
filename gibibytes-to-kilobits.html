Gibibytes to Kilobits Converter

Convert gibibytes to kilobits instantly. 1 GiB = 8,589,934.6 Kb.

How it works

To convert gibibytes to kilobits, multiply the number of gibibytes by 8,589,934.6. One gibibyte equals 8,589,934.6 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 1.16415e-07 gibibytes. The table shows common values.

Gibibytes to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = gibibytes × 8,589,934.6

Worked example: convert 12.5 gibibytes to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,589,934.6 = 107,374,180 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 1.16415e-07 (or divide by 8,589,934.6).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
