Petabytes to Kibibytes Converter

Convert petabytes to kibibytes instantly. 1 PB = 976,562,500,000 KiB.

How it works

To convert petabytes to kibibytes, multiply the number of petabytes by 976,562,500,000. One petabyte equals 976,562,500,000 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 1.024e-12 petabytes. The table shows common values.

Petabytes to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = petabytes × 976,562,500,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 petabytes to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 976,562,500,000 = 12,207,031,000,000 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 1.024e-12 (or divide by 976,562,500,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
