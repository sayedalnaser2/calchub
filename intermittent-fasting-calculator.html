Find your eating window for 16:8, 18:6 and other fasting schedules.

How it works

Your fast runs from the end of the eating window until your first meal. With a 16:8 schedule you eat within 8 hours and fast for 16. This is general information, not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

Who should not fast?

People who are pregnant, underweight, have diabetes or eating disorders should consult a doctor first.

Can I drink during the fast?

Water, black coffee and unsweetened tea are commonly allowed.
