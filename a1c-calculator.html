A1C to Average Glucose Calculator

Convert HbA1c to estimated average blood glucose.

How it works

eAG (mg/dL) = 28.7 x A1C - 46.7. This is an educational estimate, not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

What A1C is normal?

Below 5.7% is normal. 5.7-6.4% is prediabetes and 6.5% or higher suggests diabetes.
