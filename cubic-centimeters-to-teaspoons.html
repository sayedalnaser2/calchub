Cubic Centimeters to Teaspoons Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to teaspoons instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.20288414 tsp.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to teaspoons, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.20288414. One cubic centimeter equals 0.20288414 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 4.9289216 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = cubic centimeters × 0.20288414

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.20288414 = 2.5360517 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 4.9289216 (or divide by 0.20288414).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
