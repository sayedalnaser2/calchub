Calories to BTU Converter

Convert calories to BTU instantly. 1 cal = 0.0039656668 BTU.

How it works

To convert calories to BTU, multiply the number of calories by 0.0039656668. One calorie equals 0.0039656668 BTU, and one BTU equals 252.1644 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to BTU formula

Btu = calories × 0.0039656668

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0039656668 = 0.049570835 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 252.1644 (or divide by 0.0039656668).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
