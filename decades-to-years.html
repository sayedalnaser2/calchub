Decades to Years Converter

Convert decades to years instantly. 1 decade = 10 years.

How it works

To convert decades to years, multiply the number of decades by 10. One decade equals 10 years, and one year equals 0.1 decades. The table shows common values.

Decades to Years formula

Years = decades × 10

Worked example: convert 12.5 decades to years. Multiply 12.5 × 10 = 125 years. To go the other way, multiply years by 0.1 (or divide by 10).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
