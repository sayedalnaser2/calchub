To convert carats to slugs, multiply the number of carats by 1.37044e-05. One carat equals 1.37044e-05 slugs, and one slug equals 72,969.515 carats. The table shows common values.

Carats to Slugs formula

Slugs = carats × 1.37044e-05

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
