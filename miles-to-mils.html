How it works

To convert miles to mils, multiply the number of miles by 63,360,000. To convert mils to miles, multiply by 1.57828e-08 (or divide by 63,360,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles to Mils formula

Mils = miles × 63,360,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 63,360,000 = 792,000,000 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 1.57828e-08 (or divide by 63,360,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
