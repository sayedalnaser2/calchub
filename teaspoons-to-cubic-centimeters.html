Convert teaspoons to cubic centimeters instantly. 1 tsp = 4.9289216 cm³.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of teaspoons by 4.9289216. One teaspoon equals 4.9289216 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.20288414 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = teaspoons × 4.9289216

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 4.9289216 = 61.61152 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.20288414 (or divide by 4.9289216).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
