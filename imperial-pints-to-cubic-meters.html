How it works

To convert imperial pints to cubic meters, multiply the number of imperial pints by 0.00056826125. One imperial pint equals 0.00056826125 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 1,759.754 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = imperial pints × 0.00056826125

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00056826125 = 0.0071032656 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 1,759.754 (or divide by 0.00056826125).
