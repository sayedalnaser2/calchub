Inventory Turnover Calculator

Calculate how often inventory sells through and the average days to sell it.

How it works

Inventory turnover = cost of goods sold / average inventory. Days sales of inventory = 365 / turnover. Higher turnover generally means less cash tied up in stock.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good turnover ratio?

It varies by industry: grocers turn inventory many times a year, car dealers far fewer.

Can turnover be too high?

Yes, it can signal stockouts and lost sales.
