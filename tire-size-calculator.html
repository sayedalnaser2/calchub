Tire Size Calculator

Work out tire diameter, circumference and revolutions per mile from a tire size.

How it works

For a size such as 225/45R17, the sidewall equals width x aspect ratio. Overall diameter is the rim diameter plus two sidewalls. Circumference is pi times diameter, and a mile is 63,360 inches.

Frequently asked questions

Why does tire diameter matter?

A different diameter changes speedometer accuracy and gearing.

How much difference is safe?

Staying within about 3% of the original overall diameter is a common guideline.
