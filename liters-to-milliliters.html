Liters to Milliliters Converter

Convert liters to milliliters instantly. 1 L = 1,000 ml.

How it works

To convert liters to milliliters, multiply the number of liters by 1,000. One liter equals 1,000 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.001 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = liters × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.

Frequently asked questions

There are 0.001 liters in one milliliter.
