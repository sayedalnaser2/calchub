Liters to Gallons Converter

Convert liters to gallons instantly. 1 L = 0.26417205 gal.

How it works

To convert liters to gallons, multiply the number of liters by 0.26417205. One liter equals 0.26417205 gallons, and one gallon equals 3.7854118 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Gallons formula

Gallons = liters × 0.26417205

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.26417205 = 3.3021507 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 3.7854118 (or divide by 0.26417205).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
