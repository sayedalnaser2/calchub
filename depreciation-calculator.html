Depreciation Calculator

Calculate straight-line and double-declining depreciation.

How it works

Straight-line spreads cost minus salvage evenly over the life. Double-declining applies twice the straight-line rate to the remaining book value each year.

Frequently asked questions

Which method should I use?

Straight-line is simplest. Accelerated methods front-load deductions. Follow your accountant or tax rules.
