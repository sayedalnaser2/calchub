How it works

To convert watts to megawatts, multiply the number of watts by 1e-06. To convert megawatts to watts, multiply by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Watts to Megawatts formula

Megawatts = watts × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 watts to megawatts. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 MW. To go the other way, multiply megawatts by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
