Bar to Atmospheres Converter

Convert bar to atmospheres instantly. 1 bar = 0.98692327 atm.

How it works

To convert bar to atmospheres, multiply the number of bar by 0.98692327. One bar equals 0.98692327 atmospheres, and one atmosphere equals 1.01325 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = bar × 0.98692327

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to atmospheres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.98692327 = 12.336541 atm. To go the other way, multiply atmospheres by 1.01325 (or divide by 0.98692327).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
