Investing $2,500 a Month: How Much Will You Have?

$2,500 invested every month grows to $3,049,927 in 30 years at 7%.

How it works

The future value of regular deposits is M x ((1 + r)^k - 1) / r with a monthly rate r and k months. $2,500 a month for 30 years at 7% grows to about $3,049,927, of which $900,000 is your own money.
