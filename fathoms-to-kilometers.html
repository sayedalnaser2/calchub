How it works

To convert fathoms to kilometers, multiply the number of fathoms by 0.0018288. To convert kilometers to fathoms, multiply by 546.80665 (or divide by 0.0018288). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Fathoms to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = fathoms × 0.0018288

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0018288 = 0.02286 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 546.80665 (or divide by 0.0018288).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
