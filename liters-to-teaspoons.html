Liters to Teaspoons Converter

Convert liters to teaspoons instantly. 1 L = 202.88414 tsp.

How it works

To convert liters to teaspoons, multiply the number of liters by 202.88414. One liter equals 202.88414 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.0049289216 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = liters × 202.88414

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 202.88414 = 2,536.0517 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.0049289216 (or divide by 202.88414).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
