How it works

To convert grams per cubic centimeter to kilograms per cubic meter, multiply the number of grams per cubic centimeter by 1,000. One gram per cubic centimeter equals 1,000 kilograms per cubic meter, and one kilogram per cubic meter equals 0.001 grams per cubic centimeter. The table shows common values.

Grams per Cubic Centimeter to Kilograms per Cubic Meter formula

Kilograms per cubic meter = grams per cubic centimeter × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams per cubic centimeter to kilograms per cubic meter. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 kg/m³. To go the other way, multiply kilograms per cubic meter by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
