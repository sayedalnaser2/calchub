Convert grains to long tons instantly. 1 gr = 6.37755e-08 long tons.

How it works

To convert grains to long tons, multiply the number of grains by 6.37755e-08. One grain equals 6.37755e-08 long tons, and one long ton equals 15,680,000 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Long Tons formula

Long tons = grains × 6.37755e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to long tons. Multiply 12.5 × 6.37755e-08 = 7.97194e-07 long tons. To go the other way, multiply long tons by 15,680,000 (or divide by 6.37755e-08).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
