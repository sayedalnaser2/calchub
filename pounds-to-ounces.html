Pounds to Ounces Converter

Convert pounds to ounces instantly. 1 lb = 16 oz.

How it works

To convert pounds to ounces, multiply the number of pounds by 16. One pound equals 16 ounces, and one ounce equals 0.0625 pounds. The table shows common values.

Pounds to Ounces formula

Ounces = pounds × 16

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 16 = 200 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 0.0625 (or divide by 16).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
