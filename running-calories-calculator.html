Calories Burned Running Calculator

Estimate calories burned from your weight and running distance.

How it works

A common estimate is about 1.036 kcal per kilogram of body weight per kilometer run, regardless of pace. Real burn varies with terrain, wind and fitness.

Frequently asked questions

Does running faster burn more calories?

Per kilometer, the difference is small; per minute, faster running burns many more.

How accurate is this?

Within roughly 10-15%.
