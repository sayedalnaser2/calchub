Square Centimeters to Square Millimeters Converter

Convert square centimeters to square millimeters instantly. 1 cm² = 100 mm².

How it works

To convert square centimeters to square millimeters, multiply the number of square centimeters by 100. One square centimeter equals 100 square millimeters, and one square millimeter equals 0.01 square centimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Centimeters to Square Millimeters formula

Square millimeters = square centimeters × 100

Worked example: convert 12.5 square centimeters to square millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 100 = 1,250 mm². To go the other way, multiply square millimeters by 0.01 (or divide by 100).
