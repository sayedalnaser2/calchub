Megawatts to Watts Converter

Convert megawatts to watts instantly. 1 MW = 1,000,000 W.

How it works

To convert megawatts to watts, multiply the number of megawatts by 1,000,000. One megawatt equals 1,000,000 watts, and one watt equals 1e-06 megawatts. The table shows common values.

Megawatts to Watts formula

Watts = megawatts × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megawatts to watts. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 W. To go the other way, multiply watts by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
