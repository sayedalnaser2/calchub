Convert watt hours to kilocalories instantly. 1 Wh = 0.86042065 kcal.

How it works

To convert watt hours to kilocalories, multiply the number of watt hours by 0.86042065. One watt hour equals 0.86042065 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 1.1622222 watt hours. The table shows common values.

Watt Hours to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = watt hours × 0.86042065

Worked example: convert 12.5 watt hours to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.86042065 = 10.755258 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 1.1622222 (or divide by 0.86042065).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
