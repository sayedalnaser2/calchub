Z-Score Calculator

Convert a value to a z-score and percentile.

How it works

z = (x - mean) / standard deviation. The percentile uses the standard normal cumulative distribution.

Frequently asked questions

What does z = 2 mean?

The value is two standard deviations above the mean, around the 97.7th percentile.
