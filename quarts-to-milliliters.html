How it works

To convert quarts to milliliters, multiply the number of quarts by 946.35295. To convert milliliters to quarts, multiply by 0.0010566882 (or divide by 946.35295). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Quarts to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = quarts × 946.35295

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
