How it works

To convert quarts to milliliters, multiply the number of quarts by 946.35295. One quart equals 946.35295 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.0010566882 quarts. The table shows common values.

Quarts to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = quarts × 946.35295

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 946.35295 = 11829.412 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.0010566882 (or divide by 946.35295).
