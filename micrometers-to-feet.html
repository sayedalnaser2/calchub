Micrometers to Feet Converter

Convert micrometers to feet instantly. 1 µm = 3.28084e-06 ft.

How it works

To convert micrometers to feet, multiply the number of micrometers by 3.28084e-06. One micrometer equals 3.28084e-06 feet, and one foot equals 304,800 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Feet formula

Feet = micrometers × 3.28084e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3.28084e-06 = 4.10105e-05 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 304,800 (or divide by 3.28084e-06).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
