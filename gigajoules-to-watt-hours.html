Gigajoules to Watt Hours Converter

Convert gigajoules to watt hours instantly. 1 GJ = 277,777.78 Wh.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to watt hours, multiply the number of gigajoules by 277,777.78. One gigajoule equals 277,777.78 watt hours, and one watt hour equals 3.6e-06 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = gigajoules × 277,777.78

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 277,777.78 = 3,472,222.2 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 3.6e-06 (or divide by 277,777.78).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
