Investing $800 a Month: How Much Will You Have?

$800 invested every month grows to $975,977 in 30 years at 7%.

How it works

The future value of regular deposits is M x ((1 + r)^k - 1) / r with a monthly rate r and k months. $800 a month for 30 years at 7% grows to about $975,977, of which $288,000 is your own money.
