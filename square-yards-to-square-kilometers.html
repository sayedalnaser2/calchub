Square Yards to Square Kilometers Converter

Convert square yards to square kilometers instantly. 1 yd² = 8.36127e-07 km².

How it works

To convert square yards to square kilometers, multiply the number of square yards by 8.36127e-07. One square yard equals 8.36127e-07 square kilometers, and one square kilometer equals 1.19599e+06 square yards. The table shows common values.

Square Yards to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = square yards × 8.36127e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 8.36127e-07 = 1.04516e-05 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 1.19599e+06 (or divide by 8.36127e-07).
