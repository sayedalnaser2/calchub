Inches of Mercury to Atmospheres Converter

Convert inches of mercury to atmospheres instantly. 1 inHg = 0.033421061 atm.

How it works

To convert inches of mercury to atmospheres, multiply the number of inches of mercury by 0.033421061. One inch of mercury equals 0.033421061 atmospheres, and one atmosphere equals 29.921252 inches of mercury. The table shows common values.

Inches of Mercury to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = inches of mercury × 0.033421061

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches of mercury to atmospheres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.033421061 = 0.41776326 atm. To go the other way, multiply atmospheres by 29.921252 (or divide by 0.033421061).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
