Radians to Gradians Converter

Convert radians to gradians instantly. 1 rad = 63.661977 grad.

How it works

To convert radians to gradians, multiply the number of radians by 63.661977. One radian equals 63.661977 gradians, and one gradian equals 0.015707963 radians. The table shows common values.

Radians to Gradians formula

Gradians = radians × 63.661977

Worked example: convert 12.5 radians to gradians. Multiply 12.5 × 63.661977 = 795.77472 grad. To go the other way, multiply gradians by 0.015707963 (or divide by 63.661977).
