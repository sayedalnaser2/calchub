How it works

To convert radians to gradians, multiply the number of radians by 63.661977. To convert gradians to radians, multiply by 0.015707963 (or divide by 63.661977). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Radians to Gradians formula

Gradians = radians × 63.661977

Worked example: convert 12.5 radians to gradians. Multiply 12.5 × 63.661977 = 795.77472 grad. To go the other way, multiply gradians by 0.015707963 (or divide by 63.661977).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
