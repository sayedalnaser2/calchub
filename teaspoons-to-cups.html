Teaspoons to Cups Converter

Convert teaspoons to cups instantly. 1 tsp = 0.020833333 cup.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to cups, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.020833333. One teaspoon equals 0.020833333 cups, and one cup equals 48 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Cups formula

Cups = teaspoons × 0.020833333

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 0.020833333 = 0.26041667 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 48 (or divide by 0.020833333).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
