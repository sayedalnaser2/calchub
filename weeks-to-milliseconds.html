How it works

To convert weeks to milliseconds, multiply the number of weeks by 604,800,000. To convert milliseconds to weeks, multiply by 1.65344e-09 (or divide by 604,800,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Weeks to Milliseconds formula

Milliseconds = weeks × 604,800,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 weeks to milliseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 604,800,000 = 7,560,000,000 ms. To go the other way, multiply milliseconds by 1.65344e-09 (or divide by 604,800,000).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
