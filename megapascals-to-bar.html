Megapascals to Bar Converter

Convert megapascals to bar instantly. 1 MPa = 10 bar.

How it works

To convert megapascals to bar, multiply the number of megapascals by 10. One megapascal equals 10 bar, and one bar equals 0.1 megapascals. The table shows common values.

Megapascals to Bar formula

Bar = megapascals × 10

Worked example: convert 12.5 megapascals to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 10 = 125 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 0.1 (or divide by 10).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
