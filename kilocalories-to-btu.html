Kilocalories to BTU Converter

Convert kilocalories to BTU instantly. 1 kcal = 3.9656668 BTU.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to BTU, multiply the number of kilocalories by 3.9656668. One kilocalorie equals 3.9656668 BTU, and one BTU equals 0.2521644 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to BTU formula

Btu = kilocalories × 3.9656668

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 3.9656668 = 49.570835 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 0.2521644 (or divide by 3.9656668).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
