Wave Speed Calculator

Calculate wave speed from frequency and wavelength, plus the period.

How it works

Wave speed v = frequency x wavelength. The period is 1 / frequency. Sound travels about 343 m/s in air at 20 °C; light travels 299,792,458 m/s in vacuum.

Frequently asked questions

Does frequency change in a new medium?

No, frequency stays the same; wavelength and speed change.

What is the A4 note frequency?

440 Hz.
