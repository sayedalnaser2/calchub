Radians to Arcminutes Converter

Convert radians to arcminutes instantly. 1 rad = 3,437.7468 arcmin.

How it works

To convert radians to arcminutes, multiply the number of radians by 3,437.7468. One radian equals 3,437.7468 arcminutes, and one arcminute equals 0.00029088821 radians. The table shows common values.

Radians to Arcminutes formula

Arcminutes = radians × 3,437.7468

Worked example: convert 12.5 radians to arcminutes. Multiply 12.5 × 3,437.7468 = 42,971.835 arcmin. To go the other way, multiply arcminutes by 0.00029088821 (or divide by 3,437.7468).
