How it works

To convert radians to arcminutes, multiply the number of radians by 3,437.7468. To convert arcminutes to radians, multiply by 0.00029088821 (or divide by 3,437.7468). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Radians to Arcminutes formula

Arcminutes = radians × 3,437.7468

Worked example: convert 12.5 radians to arcminutes. Multiply 12.5 × 3,437.7468 = 42,971.835 arcmin. To go the other way, multiply arcminutes by 0.00029088821 (or divide by 3,437.7468).
