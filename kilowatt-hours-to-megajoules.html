How it works

To convert kilowatt hours to megajoules, multiply the number of kilowatt hours by 3.6. To convert megajoules to kilowatt hours, multiply by 0.27777778 (or divide by 3.6). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilowatt Hours to Megajoules formula

Megajoules = kilowatt hours × 3.6

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatt hours to megajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 3.6 = 45 MJ. To go the other way, multiply megajoules by 0.27777778 (or divide by 3.6).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
