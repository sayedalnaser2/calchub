Fathoms to Inches Converter

Convert fathoms to inches instantly. 1 fathom = 72 in.

How it works

To convert fathoms to inches, multiply the number of fathoms by 72. One fathom equals 72 inches, and one inch equals 0.013888889 fathoms. The table shows common values.

Fathoms to Inches formula

Inches = fathoms × 72

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 72 = 900 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 0.013888889 (or divide by 72).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
