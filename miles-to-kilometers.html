How it works

To convert miles to kilometers, multiply the number of miles by 1.609344. To convert kilometers to miles, multiply by 0.62137119 (or divide by 1.609344). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = miles × 1.609344

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1.609344 = 20.1168 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 0.62137119 (or divide by 1.609344).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
