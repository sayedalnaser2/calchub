Meters to Nanometers Converter

Convert meters to nanometers instantly. 1 m = 1,000,000,000 nm.

How it works

To convert meters to nanometers, multiply the number of meters by 1,000,000,000. One meter equals 1,000,000,000 nanometers, and one nanometer equals 1e-09 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = meters × 1,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 1e-09 (or divide by 1,000,000,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
