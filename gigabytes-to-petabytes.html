Gigabytes to Petabytes Converter

Convert gigabytes to petabytes instantly. 1 GB = 1e-06 PB.

How it works

To convert gigabytes to petabytes, multiply the number of gigabytes by 1e-06. One gigabyte equals 1e-06 petabytes, and one petabyte equals 1,000,000 gigabytes. The table shows common values.

Gigabytes to Petabytes formula

Petabytes = gigabytes × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabytes to petabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 PB. To go the other way, multiply petabytes by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
