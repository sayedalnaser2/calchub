Ellipse Area and Perimeter Calculator

Calculate the area and approximate perimeter of an ellipse from its semi-axes.

How it works

Area = pi x a x b. The perimeter has no exact closed form; Ramanujan's approximation pi(a+b)(1 + 3h/(10+sqrt(4-3h))), h = ((a-b)/(a+b))^2, is accurate to many digits.

Frequently asked questions

What is a semi-axis?

Half the longest (or shortest) diameter.

When is an ellipse a circle?

When a equals b; eccentricity is then 0.
