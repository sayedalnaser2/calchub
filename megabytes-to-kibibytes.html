Megabytes to Kibibytes Converter

Convert megabytes to kibibytes instantly. 1 MB = 976.5625 KiB.

How it works

To convert megabytes to kibibytes, multiply the number of megabytes by 976.5625. One megabyte equals 976.5625 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 0.001024 megabytes. The table shows common values.

Megabytes to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = megabytes × 976.5625

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabytes to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 976.5625 = 12,207.031 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 0.001024 (or divide by 976.5625).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
