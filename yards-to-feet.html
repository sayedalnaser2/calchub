Yards to Feet Converter

Convert yards to feet instantly. 1 yd = 3 ft.

How it works

To convert yards to feet, multiply the number of yards by 3. One yard equals 3 feet, and one foot equals 0.33333333 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Feet formula

Feet = yards × 3

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3 = 37.5 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 0.33333333 (or divide by 3).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
