Carats to Stone Converter

Convert carats to stone instantly. 1 ct = 3.14946e-05 st.

How it works

To convert carats to stone, multiply the number of carats by 3.14946e-05. One carat equals 3.14946e-05 stone, and one stone equals 31,751.466 carats. The table shows common values.

Carats to Stone formula

Stone = carats × 3.14946e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 carats to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 3.14946e-05 = 0.00039368261 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 31,751.466 (or divide by 3.14946e-05).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
