Petabytes to Megabytes Converter

Convert petabytes to megabytes instantly. 1 PB = 1,000,000,000 MB.

How it works

To convert petabytes to megabytes, multiply the number of petabytes by 1,000,000,000. One petabyte equals 1,000,000,000 megabytes, and one megabyte equals 1e-09 petabytes. The table shows common values.

Petabytes to Megabytes formula

Megabytes = petabytes × 1,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 petabytes to megabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000 MB. To go the other way, multiply megabytes by 1e-09 (or divide by 1,000,000,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
