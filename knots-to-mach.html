How it works

To convert knots to mach, multiply the number of knots by 0.0015117824. To convert mach to knots, multiply by 661.47084 (or divide by 0.0015117824). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Knots to Mach formula

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
