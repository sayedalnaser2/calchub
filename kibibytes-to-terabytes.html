Kibibytes to Terabytes Converter

Convert kibibytes to terabytes instantly. 1 KiB = 1.024e-09 TB.

How it works

To convert kibibytes to terabytes, multiply the number of kibibytes by 1.024e-09. One kibibyte equals 1.024e-09 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 976,562,500 kibibytes. The table shows common values.

Kibibytes to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = kibibytes × 1.024e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 kibibytes to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.024e-09 = 1.28e-08 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 976,562,500 (or divide by 1.024e-09).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
