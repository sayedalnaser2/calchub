Cubic Yards to Gallons Converter

Convert cubic yards to gallons instantly. 1 yd³ = 201.97403 gal.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to gallons, multiply the number of cubic yards by 201.97403. One cubic yard equals 201.97403 gallons, and one gallon equals 0.0049511317 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Gallons formula

Gallons = cubic yards × 201.97403

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 201.97403 = 2,524.6753 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 0.0049511317 (or divide by 201.97403).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
