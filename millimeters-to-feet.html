How it works

To convert millimeters to feet, multiply the number of millimeters by 0.0032808399. One millimeter equals 0.0032808399 feet, and one foot equals 304.8 millimeters. The table shows common values.

Millimeters to Feet formula

Feet = millimeters × 0.0032808399
