Square Centimeters to Square Yards Converter

Convert square centimeters to square yards instantly. 1 cm² = 0.000119599 yd².

How it works

To convert square centimeters to square yards, multiply the number of square centimeters by 0.000119599. One square centimeter equals 0.000119599 square yards, and one square yard equals 8,361.2736 square centimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Centimeters to Square Yards formula

Square yards = square centimeters × 0.000119599

Worked example: convert 12.5 square centimeters to square yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.000119599 = 0.0014949876 yd². To go the other way, multiply square yards by 8,361.2736 (or divide by 0.000119599).
