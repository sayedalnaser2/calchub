How it works

To convert imperial pints to cubic feet, multiply the number of imperial pints by 0.020067957. One imperial pint equals 0.020067957 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 49.830684 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = imperial pints × 0.020067957

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.020067957 = 0.25084946 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 49.830684 (or divide by 0.020067957).
