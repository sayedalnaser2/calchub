Stock Split Calculator

Calculate your new share count and price after a stock split.

How it works

In an a-for-b split each b old shares become a new shares and the price is divided by a/b, so total value does not change at the moment of the split.

Frequently asked questions

Does a split change my wealth?

Not by itself; the share count rises and the price falls proportionally.

What is a reverse split?

It reduces the share count and raises the price, e.g. 1-for-10.
