Asphalt Calculator (Tons Needed)

Estimate the tons of asphalt needed for a driveway or parking area.

How it works

Volume = length x width x thickness. Hot-mix asphalt weighs about 145 lb per cubic foot compacted, so tons = cubic feet x 145 / 2000. Add a margin for compaction and edges.

Frequently asked questions

How thick should a driveway be?

2-3 inches over a compacted gravel base for residential driveways.

Does it include the base?

No. Calculate the gravel base separately.
