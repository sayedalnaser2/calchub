How it works

To convert kilograms to ounces, multiply the number of kilograms by 35.273962. One kilogram equals 35.273962 ounces, and one ounce equals 0.028349523 kilograms. The table shows common values.

Kilograms to Ounces formula

Ounces = kilograms × 35.273962

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 35.273962 = 440.92452 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 0.028349523 (or divide by 35.273962).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
