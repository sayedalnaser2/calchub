How it works

To convert grams per cubic centimeter to pounds per gallon, multiply the number of grams per cubic centimeter by 8.3454045. One gram per cubic centimeter equals 8.3454045 pounds per gallon, and one pound per gallon equals 0.11982643 grams per cubic centimeter. The table shows common values.

Grams per Cubic Centimeter to Pounds per Gallon formula

Pounds per gallon = grams per cubic centimeter × 8.3454045
