Sleep Calculator

Find the best bedtime or wake-up time based on 90-minute sleep cycles.

How it works

Sleep moves in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels easier. The calculator allows 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Frequently asked questions

How many cycles do I need?

Most adults do best with 5-6 cycles (7.5-9 hours).
