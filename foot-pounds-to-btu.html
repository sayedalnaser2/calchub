Foot-Pounds to BTU Converter

Convert foot-pounds to BTU instantly. 1 ft-lb = 0.0012850675 BTU.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to BTU, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 0.0012850675. One foot-pound equals 0.0012850675 BTU, and one BTU equals 778.16926 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to BTU formula

Btu = foot-pounds × 0.0012850675

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0012850675 = 0.016063343 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 778.16926 (or divide by 0.0012850675).
