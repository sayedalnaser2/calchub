How it works

To convert meters per second to mach, multiply the number of meters per second by 0.00293867. To convert mach to meters per second, multiply by 340.29 (or divide by 0.00293867). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Meters per Second to Mach formula

Mach = meters per second × 0.00293867

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
