Convert imperial pints to tablespoons instantly. 1 imp pt = 38.430398 tbsp.

How it works

To convert imperial pints to tablespoons, multiply the number of imperial pints by 38.430398. One imperial pint equals 38.430398 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.026021068 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = imperial pints × 38.430398

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 38.430398 = 480.37997 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.026021068 (or divide by 38.430398).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
