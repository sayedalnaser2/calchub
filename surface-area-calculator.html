Surface Area Calculator

Calculate surface area of a sphere, cylinder, cone, cube or box.

How it works

Sphere: 4πr². Cylinder: 2πr(r+h). Cone: πr(r+slant). Cube: 6a². Box: 2(ab+bc+ac).

Frequently asked questions

Surface area vs volume?

Surface area measures the outside covering; volume measures the space inside.
