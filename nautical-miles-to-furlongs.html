How it works

To convert nautical miles to furlongs, multiply the number of nautical miles by 9.2062356. One nautical mile equals 9.2062356 furlongs, and one furlong equals 0.10862203 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = nautical miles × 9.2062356

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 9.2062356 = 115.07794 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 0.10862203 (or divide by 9.2062356).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
