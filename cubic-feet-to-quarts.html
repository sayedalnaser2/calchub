Cubic Feet to Quarts Converter

Convert cubic feet to quarts instantly. 1 ft³ = 29.922078 qt.

How it works

To convert cubic feet to quarts, multiply the number of cubic feet by 29.922078. One cubic foot equals 29.922078 quarts, and one quart equals 0.033420139 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Quarts formula

Quarts = cubic feet × 29.922078

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 29.922078 = 374.02597 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 0.033420139 (or divide by 29.922078).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
