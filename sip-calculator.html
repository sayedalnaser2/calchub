SIP Calculator

Project the future value of a monthly systematic investment plan.

How it works

A SIP invests a fixed amount at the start of each month. The future value is p x (((1+i)^n - 1) / i) x (1+i) with i the monthly return.

Frequently asked questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Market-linked returns vary, and the rate you enter is only an assumption.

Why start early?

Compounding gives the earliest contributions the most time to grow.
