Light Years to Miles Converter

Convert light years to miles instantly. 1 ly = 5,878,625,400,000 mi.

How it works

To convert light years to miles, multiply the number of light years by 5,878,625,400,000. One light year equals 5,878,625,400,000 miles, and one mile equals 1.70108e-13 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Miles formula

Miles = light years × 5,878,625,400,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 5,878,625,400,000 = 73,482,817,000,000 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1.70108e-13 (or divide by 5,878,625,400,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
