Liters per Second to Cubic Feet per Minute Converter

Convert liters per second to cubic feet per minute instantly. 1 L/s = 2.11888 cfm.

How it works

To convert liters per second to cubic feet per minute, multiply the number of liters per second by 2.11888. One liter per second equals 2.11888 cubic feet per minute, and one cubic foot per minute equals 0.47194744 liters per second. The table shows common values.

Liters per Second to Cubic Feet per Minute formula

Cubic feet per minute = liters per second × 2.11888

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per second to cubic feet per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 2.11888 = 26.486 cfm. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet per minute by 0.47194744 (or divide by 2.11888).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
