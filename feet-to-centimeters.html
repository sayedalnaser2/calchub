How it works

To convert feet to centimeters, multiply the number of feet by 30.48. To convert centimeters to feet, multiply by 0.032808399 (or divide by 30.48). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Feet to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = feet × 30.48

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 30.48 = 381 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 0.032808399 (or divide by 30.48).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
