Feet per Second to Kilometers per Hour Converter

Convert feet per second to kilometers per hour instantly. 1 ft/s = 1.09728 km/h.

How it works

To convert feet per second to kilometers per hour, multiply the number of feet per second by 1.09728. One foot per second equals 1.09728 kilometers per hour, and one kilometer per hour equals 0.91134442 feet per second. The table shows common values.

Feet per Second to Kilometers per Hour formula

Kilometers per hour = feet per second × 1.09728

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet per second to kilometers per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 1.09728 = 13.716 km/h. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per hour by 0.91134442 (or divide by 1.09728).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
