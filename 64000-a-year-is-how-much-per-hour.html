$64,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

$64,000 a year is $30.77 an hour at 40 hours a week. See monthly, biweekly and weekly pay.

How it works

Hourly pay is annual salary divided by the hours worked in a year. A full-time year is 40 hours x 52 weeks = 2,080 hours, so $64,000 / 2,080 = $30.77 an hour, before taxes.
