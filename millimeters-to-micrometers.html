Millimeters to Micrometers Converter

Convert millimeters to micrometers instantly. 1 mm = 1,000 µm.

How it works

To convert millimeters to micrometers, multiply the number of millimeters by 1,000. One millimeter equals 1,000 micrometers, and one micrometer equals 0.001 millimeters. The table shows common values.

Millimeters to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = millimeters × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
