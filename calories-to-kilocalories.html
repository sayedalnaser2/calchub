Calories to Kilocalories Converter

Convert calories to kilocalories instantly. 1 cal = 0.001 kcal.

How it works

To convert calories to kilocalories, multiply the number of calories by 0.001. One calorie equals 0.001 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 1,000 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = calories × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
