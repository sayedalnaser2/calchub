How it works

To convert stone to long tons, multiply the number of stone by 0.00625. One stone equals 0.00625 long tons, and one long ton equals 160 stone. The table shows common values.

Stone to Long Tons formula

Long tons = stone × 0.00625

Worked example: convert 12.5 stone to long tons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00625 = 0.078125 long tons. To go the other way, multiply long tons by 160 (or divide by 0.00625).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
