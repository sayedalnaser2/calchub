Convert cubic feet to fluid ounces instantly. 1 ft³ = 957.50649 fl oz.

How it works

To convert cubic feet to fluid ounces, multiply the number of cubic feet by 957.50649. One cubic foot equals 957.50649 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.0010443793 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = cubic feet × 957.50649

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 957.50649 = 11,968.831 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.0010443793 (or divide by 957.50649).
