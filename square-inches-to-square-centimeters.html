How it works

To convert square inches to square centimeters, multiply the number of square inches by 6.4516. To convert square centimeters to square inches, multiply by 0.15500031 (or divide by 6.4516). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Inches to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square inches × 6.4516

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 6.4516 = 80.645 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 0.15500031 (or divide by 6.4516).
