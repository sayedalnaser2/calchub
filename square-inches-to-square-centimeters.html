Square Inches to Square Centimeters Converter

Convert square inches to square centimeters instantly. 1 in² = 6.4516 cm².

How it works

To convert square inches to square centimeters, multiply the number of square inches by 6.4516. One square inch equals 6.4516 square centimeters, and one square centimeter equals 0.15500031 square inches. The table shows common values.

Square Inches to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square inches × 6.4516

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 6.4516 = 80.645 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 0.15500031 (or divide by 6.4516).
