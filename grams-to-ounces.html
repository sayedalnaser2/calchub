Grams to Ounces Converter

Convert grams to ounces instantly. 1 g = 0.035273962 oz.

How it works

To convert grams to ounces, multiply the number of grams by 0.035273962. One gram equals 0.035273962 ounces, and one ounce equals 28.349523 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Ounces formula

Ounces = grams × 0.035273962

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 0.035273962 = 0.44092452 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 28.349523 (or divide by 0.035273962).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
