Fathoms to Yards Converter

Convert fathoms to yards instantly. 1 fathom = 2 yd.

How it works

To convert fathoms to yards, multiply the number of fathoms by 2. One fathom equals 2 yards, and one yard equals 0.5 fathoms. The table shows common values.

Fathoms to Yards formula

Yards = fathoms × 2

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 2 = 25 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 0.5 (or divide by 2).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
