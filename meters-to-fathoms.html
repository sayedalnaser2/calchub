Meters to Fathoms Converter

Convert meters to fathoms instantly. 1 m = 0.54680665 fathoms.

How it works

To convert meters to fathoms, multiply the number of meters by 0.54680665. One meter equals 0.54680665 fathoms, and one fathom equals 1.8288 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = meters × 0.54680665

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.54680665 = 6.8350831 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 1.8288 (or divide by 0.54680665).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
