Cubic Meters to Milliliters Converter

Convert cubic meters to milliliters instantly. 1 m³ = 1,000,000 ml.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to milliliters, multiply the number of cubic meters by 1,000,000. One cubic meter equals 1,000,000 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 1e-06 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = cubic meters × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
