Extra Loan Payment Calculator

See how much time and interest an extra monthly payment saves on a loan.

How it works

The calculator computes the normal payment, then simulates paying that amount plus your extra each month. Extra payments go straight to principal, so the balance falls faster and less interest accrues.

Frequently asked questions

Do extra payments always save money?

Yes, as long as the loan has no prepayment penalty. Check your loan terms.

Is it better to pay extra or invest?

If the loan rate is higher than your expected after-tax investment return, paying extra usually wins.
