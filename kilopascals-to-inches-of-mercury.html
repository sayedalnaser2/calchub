Kilopascals to Inches of Mercury Converter

Convert kilopascals to inches of mercury instantly. 1 kPa = 0.2952998 inHg.

How it works

To convert kilopascals to inches of mercury, multiply the number of kilopascals by 0.2952998. One kilopascal equals 0.2952998 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 3.386389 kilopascals. The table shows common values.

Kilopascals to Inches of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = kilopascals × 0.2952998

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 0.2952998 = 3.6912475 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 3.386389 (or divide by 0.2952998).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
