Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Feet Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to cubic feet instantly. 1 cm³ = 3.53147e-05 ft³.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to cubic feet, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 3.53147e-05. One cubic centimeter equals 3.53147e-05 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 28,316.847 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = cubic centimeters × 3.53147e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3.53147e-05 = 0.00044143333 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 28,316.847 (or divide by 3.53147e-05).
