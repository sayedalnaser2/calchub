Square Kilometers to Square Inches Converter

Convert square kilometers to square inches instantly. 1 km² = 1.55e+09 in².

How it works

To convert square kilometers to square inches, multiply the number of square kilometers by 1.55e+09. One square kilometer equals 1.55e+09 square inches, and one square inch equals 6.4516e-10 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Square Inches formula

Square inches = square kilometers × 1.55e+09

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 1.55e+09 = 1.9375e+10 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 6.4516e-10 (or divide by 1.55e+09).
