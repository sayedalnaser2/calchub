How it works

To convert arcseconds to turns, multiply the number of arcseconds by 7.71605e-07. To convert turns to arcseconds, multiply by 1,296,000 (or divide by 7.71605e-07). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Arcseconds to Turns formula

Turns = arcseconds × 7.71605e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcseconds to turns. Multiply 12.5 × 7.71605e-07 = 9.64506e-06 turns. To go the other way, multiply turns by 1,296,000 (or divide by 7.71605e-07).
