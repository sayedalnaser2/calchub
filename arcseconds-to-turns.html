Arcseconds to Turns Converter

Convert arcseconds to turns instantly. 1 arcsec = 7.71605e-07 turns.

How it works

To convert arcseconds to turns, multiply the number of arcseconds by 7.71605e-07. One arcsecond equals 7.71605e-07 turns, and one turn equals 1,296,000 arcseconds. The table shows common values.

Arcseconds to Turns formula

Turns = arcseconds × 7.71605e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcseconds to turns. Multiply 12.5 × 7.71605e-07 = 9.64506e-06 turns. To go the other way, multiply turns by 1,296,000 (or divide by 7.71605e-07).
