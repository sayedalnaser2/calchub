Grains to Ounces Converter

Convert grains to ounces instantly. 1 gr = 0.0022857143 oz.

How it works

To convert grains to ounces, multiply the number of grains by 0.0022857143. One grain equals 0.0022857143 ounces, and one ounce equals 437.5 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Ounces formula

Ounces = grains × 0.0022857143

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0022857143 = 0.028571429 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 437.5 (or divide by 0.0022857143).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
