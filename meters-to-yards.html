Meters to Yards Converter

Convert meters to yards instantly. 1 m = 1.0936133 yd.

How it works

To convert meters to yards, multiply the number of meters by 1.0936133. One meter equals 1.0936133 yards, and one yard equals 0.9144 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Yards formula

Yards = meters × 1.0936133

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1.0936133 = 13.670166 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 0.9144 (or divide by 1.0936133).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
