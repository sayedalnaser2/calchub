How it works

To convert quarts to imperial gallons, multiply the number of quarts by 0.20816855. To convert imperial gallons to quarts, multiply by 4.8037997 (or divide by 0.20816855). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Quarts to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = quarts × 0.20816855

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.20816855 = 2.6021068 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 4.8037997 (or divide by 0.20816855).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
