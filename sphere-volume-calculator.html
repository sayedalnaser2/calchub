Sphere Volume and Surface Area Calculator

Calculate volume, surface area and diameter of a sphere from its radius.

How it works

Volume = 4/3 x pi x r^3. Surface area = 4 x pi x r^2. Use any unit; volume is in cubic units and area in square units.
