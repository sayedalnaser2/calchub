BTU to Therms Converter

Convert BTU to therms instantly. 1 BTU = 1.00024e-05 therms.

How it works

To convert BTU to therms, multiply the number of BTU by 1.00024e-05. One BTU equals 1.00024e-05 therms, and one therm equals 99,976.129 BTU. The table shows common values.

BTU to Therms formula

Therms = BTU × 1.00024e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 1.00024e-05 = 0.00012502985 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 99,976.129 (or divide by 1.00024e-05).
