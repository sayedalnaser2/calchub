How it works

To convert tablespoons to cups, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.0625. To convert cups to tablespoons, multiply by 16 (or divide by 0.0625). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Tablespoons to Cups formula

Cups = tablespoons × 0.0625

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0625 = 0.78125 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 16 (or divide by 0.0625).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
