Tebibytes to Megabytes Converter

Convert tebibytes to megabytes instantly. 1 TiB = 1,099,511.6 MB.

How it works

To convert tebibytes to megabytes, multiply the number of tebibytes by 1,099,511.6. One tebibyte equals 1,099,511.6 megabytes, and one megabyte equals 9.09495e-07 tebibytes. The table shows common values.

Tebibytes to Megabytes formula

Megabytes = tebibytes × 1,099,511.6

Worked example: convert 12.5 tebibytes to megabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,099,511.6 = 13,743,895 MB. To go the other way, multiply megabytes by 9.09495e-07 (or divide by 1,099,511.6).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
