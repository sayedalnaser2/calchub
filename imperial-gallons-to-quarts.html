Convert imperial gallons to quarts instantly. 1 imp gal = 4.8037997 qt.

How it works

To convert imperial gallons to quarts, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 4.8037997. One imperial gallon equals 4.8037997 quarts, and one quart equals 0.20816855 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Quarts formula

Quarts = imperial gallons × 4.8037997

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 4.8037997 = 60.047496 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 0.20816855 (or divide by 4.8037997).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
