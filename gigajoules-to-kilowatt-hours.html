Gigajoules to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert gigajoules to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 GJ = 277.77778 kWh.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of gigajoules by 277.77778. One gigajoule equals 277.77778 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 0.0036 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = gigajoules × 277.77778

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 277.77778 = 3,472.2222 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 0.0036 (or divide by 277.77778).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
