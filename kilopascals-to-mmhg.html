How it works

To convert kilopascals to mmHg, multiply the number of kilopascals by 7.5006158. One kilopascal equals 7.5006158 mmHg, and one mmHg equals 0.13332239 kilopascals. The table shows common values.

Kilopascals to mmHg formula

Mmhg = kilopascals × 7.5006158

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to mmHg. Multiply 12.5 × 7.5006158 = 93.757697 mmHg. To go the other way, multiply mmHg by 0.13332239 (or divide by 7.5006158).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
