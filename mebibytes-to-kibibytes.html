Mebibytes to Kibibytes Converter

Convert mebibytes to kibibytes instantly. 1 MiB = 1,024 KiB.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to kibibytes, multiply the number of mebibytes by 1,024. One mebibyte equals 1,024 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 0.0009765625 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = mebibytes × 1,024

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,024 = 12,800 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 0.0009765625 (or divide by 1,024).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
