How it works

To convert hours to centuries, multiply the number of hours by 1.14079e-06. To convert centuries to hours, multiply by 876,582 (or divide by 1.14079e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Hours to Centuries formula

Centuries = hours × 1.14079e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to centuries. Multiply 12.5 × 1.14079e-06 = 1.42599e-05 centuries. To go the other way, multiply centuries by 876,582 (or divide by 1.14079e-06).
