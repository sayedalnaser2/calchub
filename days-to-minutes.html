Days to Minutes Converter

Convert days to minutes instantly. 1 days = 1,440 min.

How it works

To convert days to minutes, multiply the number of days by 1,440. One day equals 1,440 minutes, and one minute equals 0.00069444444 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Minutes formula

Minutes = days × 1,440

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,440 = 18,000 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 0.00069444444 (or divide by 1,440).

About time units
