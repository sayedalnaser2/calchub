How it works

To convert yards to millimeters, multiply the number of yards by 914.4. To convert millimeters to yards, multiply by 0.0010936133 (or divide by 914.4). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Yards to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = yards × 914.4

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 914.4 = 11,430 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 0.0010936133 (or divide by 914.4).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
