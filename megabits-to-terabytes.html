Megabits to Terabytes Converter

Convert megabits to terabytes instantly. 1 Mb = 1.25e-07 TB.

How it works

To convert megabits to terabytes, multiply the number of megabits by 1.25e-07. One megabit equals 1.25e-07 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 8,000,000 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = megabits × 1.25e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.25e-07 = 1.5625e-06 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 8,000,000 (or divide by 1.25e-07).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
