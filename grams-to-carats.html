How it works

To convert grams to carats, multiply the number of grams by 5. To convert carats to grams, multiply by 0.2 (or divide by 5). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Grams to Carats formula

Carats = grams × 5

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 5 = 62.5 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 0.2 (or divide by 5).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
