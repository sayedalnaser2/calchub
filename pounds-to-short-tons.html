Pounds to Short Tons Converter

Convert pounds to short tons instantly. 1 lb = 0.0005 short tons.

How it works

To convert pounds to short tons, multiply the number of pounds by 0.0005. One pound equals 0.0005 short tons, and one short ton equals 2,000 pounds. The table shows common values.

Pounds to Short Tons formula

Short tons = pounds × 0.0005

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to short tons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0005 = 0.00625 short tons. To go the other way, multiply short tons by 2,000 (or divide by 0.0005).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
