Megapascals to Inches Of Mercury Converter

Convert megapascals to inches of mercury instantly. 1 MPa = 295.2998 inHg.

How it works

To convert megapascals to inches of mercury, multiply the number of megapascals by 295.2998. One megapascal equals 295.2998 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 0.003386389 megapascals. The table shows common values.

Megapascals to Inches Of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = megapascals × 295.2998

Worked example: convert 12.5 megapascals to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 295.2998 = 3,691.2475 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 0.003386389 (or divide by 295.2998).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
