Cubic Centimeters to Liters Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to liters instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.001 L.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to liters, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.001. One cubic centimeter equals 0.001 liters, and one liter equals 1,000 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Liters formula

Liters = cubic centimeters × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
