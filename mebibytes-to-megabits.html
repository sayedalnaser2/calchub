Mebibytes to Megabits Converter

Convert mebibytes to megabits instantly. 1 MiB = 8.388608 Mb.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to megabits, multiply the number of mebibytes by 8.388608. One mebibyte equals 8.388608 megabits, and one megabit equals 0.11920929 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Megabits formula

Megabits = mebibytes × 8.388608

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to megabits. Multiply 12.5 × 8.388608 = 104.8576 Mb. To go the other way, multiply megabits by 0.11920929 (or divide by 8.388608).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
