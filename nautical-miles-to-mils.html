Nautical Miles to Mils Converter

Convert nautical miles to mils instantly. 1 nmi = 72,913,386 mil.

How it works

To convert nautical miles to mils, multiply the number of nautical miles by 72,913,386. One nautical mile equals 72,913,386 mils, and one mil equals 1.37149e-08 nautical miles. The table shows common values.

Nautical Miles to Mils formula

Mils = nautical miles × 72,913,386

Worked example: convert 12.5 nautical miles to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 72,913,386 = 911,417,320 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 1.37149e-08 (or divide by 72,913,386).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
