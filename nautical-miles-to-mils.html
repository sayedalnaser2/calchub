How it works

To convert nautical miles to mils, multiply the number of nautical miles by 72,913,386. To convert mils to nautical miles, multiply by 1.37149e-08 (or divide by 72,913,386). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
