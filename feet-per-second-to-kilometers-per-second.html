How it works

To convert feet per second to kilometers per second, multiply the number of feet per second by 0.0003048. To convert kilometers per second to feet per second, multiply by 3,280.8399 (or divide by 0.0003048). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Feet per Second to Kilometers per Second formula

Kilometers per second = feet per second × 0.0003048

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet per second to kilometers per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0003048 = 0.00381 km/s. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per second by 3,280.8399 (or divide by 0.0003048).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
