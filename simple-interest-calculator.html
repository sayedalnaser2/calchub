How it works

Simple interest uses the formula I = P x r x t, where interest is calculated only on the original principal and never on accumulated interest.

Formula

Simple interest I = P x r x t. Enter any three of the four values.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is earned on the principal only. Compound interest is also earned on previously added interest, so it grows faster.
