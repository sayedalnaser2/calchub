Megabits to Tebibytes Converter

Convert megabits to tebibytes instantly. 1 Mb = 1.13687e-07 TiB.

How it works

To convert megabits to tebibytes, multiply the number of megabits by 1.13687e-07. One megabit equals 1.13687e-07 tebibytes, and one tebibyte equals 8,796,093 megabits. The table shows common values.

Megabits to Tebibytes formula

Tebibytes = megabits × 1.13687e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabits to tebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.13687e-07 = 1.42109e-06 TiB. To go the other way, multiply tebibytes by 8,796,093 (or divide by 1.13687e-07).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
