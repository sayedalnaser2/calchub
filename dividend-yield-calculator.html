Dividend Yield Calculator

Calculate dividend yield and annual dividend income.

How it works

Yield = annual dividend per share / share price. Income is dividend per share times the number of shares.

Frequently asked questions

Is a high yield good?

Not always. A very high yield can signal a falling price or an unsustainable dividend.
