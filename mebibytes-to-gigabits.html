Mebibytes to Gigabits Converter

Convert mebibytes to gigabits instantly. 1 MiB = 0.008388608 Gb.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to gigabits, multiply the number of mebibytes by 0.008388608. One mebibyte equals 0.008388608 gigabits, and one gigabit equals 119.20929 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Gigabits formula

Gigabits = mebibytes × 0.008388608

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to gigabits. Multiply 12.5 × 0.008388608 = 0.1048576 Gb. To go the other way, multiply gigabits by 119.20929 (or divide by 0.008388608).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
