How it works

To convert gibibytes to megabits, multiply the number of gibibytes by 8,589.9346. One gibibyte equals 8,589.9346 megabits, and one megabit equals 0.00011641532 gibibytes. The table shows common values.

Gibibytes to Megabits formula

Megabits = gibibytes × 8,589.9346

Worked example: convert 12.5 gibibytes to megabits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,589.9346 = 107,374.18 Mb. To go the other way, multiply megabits by 0.00011641532 (or divide by 8,589.9346).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
