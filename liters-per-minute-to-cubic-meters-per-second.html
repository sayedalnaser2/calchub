Liters per Minute to Cubic Meters per Second Converter

Convert liters per minute to cubic meters per second instantly. 1 L/min = 1.66667e-05 m³/s.

How it works

To convert liters per minute to cubic meters per second, multiply the number of liters per minute by 1.66667e-05. One liter per minute equals 1.66667e-05 cubic meters per second, and one cubic meter per second equals 60,000 liters per minute. The table shows common values.

Liters per Minute to Cubic Meters per Second formula

Cubic meters per second = liters per minute × 1.66667e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per minute to cubic meters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 1.66667e-05 = 0.00020833333 m³/s. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters per second by 60,000 (or divide by 1.66667e-05).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
