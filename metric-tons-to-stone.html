How it works

To convert metric tons to stone, multiply the number of metric tons by 157.47304. One metric ton equals 157.47304 stone, and one stone equals 0.0063502932 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Stone formula

Stone = metric tons × 157.47304

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 157.47304 = 1968.4131 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 0.0063502932 (or divide by 157.47304).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
