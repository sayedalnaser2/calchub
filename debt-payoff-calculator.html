Debt Payoff Calculator

See how long it takes to clear a balance and how much interest you will pay.

How it works

The payoff time uses the standard loan formula n = -ln(1 - r x B / P) / ln(1 + r), where r is the monthly rate, B the balance and P the payment. It assumes a fixed rate and no new charges.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a small payment take so long?

If the payment barely exceeds monthly interest, almost nothing reduces the balance.

How can I pay off debt faster?

Raise the monthly payment. Even a modest increase can cut years and a lot of interest.
