Milliliters to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert milliliters to fluid ounces instantly. 1 ml = 0.033814023 fl oz.

How it works

To convert milliliters to fluid ounces, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.033814023. One milliliter equals 0.033814023 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 29.57353 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = milliliters × 0.033814023

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 0.033814023 = 0.42267528 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 29.57353 (or divide by 0.033814023).
