Investing $300 a Month: How Much Will You Have?

$300 invested every month grows to $365,991 in 30 years at 7%.

How it works

The future value of regular deposits is M x ((1 + r)^k - 1) / r with a monthly rate r and k months. $300 a month for 30 years at 7% grows to about $365,991, of which $108,000 is your own money.
