Pound-Force to Kilonewtons Converter

Convert pound-force to kilonewtons instantly. 1 lbf = 0.0044482216 kN.

How it works

To convert pound-force to kilonewtons, multiply the number of pound-force by 0.0044482216. One pound-force equals 0.0044482216 kilonewtons, and one kilonewton equals 224.80894 pound-force. The table shows common values.

Pound-Force to Kilonewtons formula

Kilonewtons = pound-force × 0.0044482216

Worked example: convert 12.5 pound-force to kilonewtons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0044482216 = 0.05560277 kN. To go the other way, multiply kilonewtons by 224.80894 (or divide by 0.0044482216).
