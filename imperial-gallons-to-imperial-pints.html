How it works

To convert imperial gallons to imperial pints, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 8. To convert imperial pints to imperial gallons, multiply by 0.125 (or divide by 8). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Imperial Gallons to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = imperial gallons × 8

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 8 = 100 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.125 (or divide by 8).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
