Calories to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert calories to foot-pounds instantly. 1 cal = 3.08596 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert calories to foot-pounds, multiply the number of calories by 3.08596. One calorie equals 3.08596 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 0.32404827 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = calories × 3.08596

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 3.08596 = 38.5745 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 0.32404827 (or divide by 3.08596).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
