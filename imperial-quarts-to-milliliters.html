How it works

To convert imperial quarts to milliliters, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 1,136.5225. One imperial quart equals 1,136.5225 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.00087987699 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = imperial quarts × 1,136.5225

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,136.5225 = 14,206.531 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.00087987699 (or divide by 1,136.5225).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
