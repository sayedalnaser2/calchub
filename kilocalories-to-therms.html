Kilocalories to Therms Converter

Convert kilocalories to therms instantly. 1 kcal = 3.96661e-05 therms.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to therms, multiply the number of kilocalories by 3.96661e-05. One kilocalorie equals 3.96661e-05 therms, and one therm equals 25,210.421 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Therms formula

Therms = kilocalories × 3.96661e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 3.96661e-05 = 0.00049582671 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 25,210.421 (or divide by 3.96661e-05).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
