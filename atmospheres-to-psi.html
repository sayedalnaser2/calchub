Convert atmospheres to psi instantly. 1 atm = 14.695949 psi.

How it works

To convert atmospheres to psi, multiply the number of atmospheres by 14.695949. One atmosphere equals 14.695949 psi, and one psi equals 0.068045964 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Atmospheres to PSI formula

Psi = atmospheres × 14.695949

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to psi. Multiply 12.5 × 14.695949 = 183.69936 psi. To go the other way, multiply psi by 0.068045964 (or divide by 14.695949).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
