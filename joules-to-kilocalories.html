Convert joules to kilocalories instantly. 1 J = 0.00023900574 kcal.

How it works

To convert joules to kilocalories, multiply the number of joules by 0.00023900574. One joule equals 0.00023900574 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 4184 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = joules × 0.00023900574

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00023900574 = 0.0029875717 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 4184 (or divide by 0.00023900574).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
