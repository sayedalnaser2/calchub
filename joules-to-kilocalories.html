How it works

To convert joules to kilocalories, multiply the number of joules by 0.00023900574. To convert kilocalories to joules, multiply by 4,184 (or divide by 0.00023900574). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Joules to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = joules × 0.00023900574

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00023900574 = 0.0029875717 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 4,184 (or divide by 0.00023900574).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
