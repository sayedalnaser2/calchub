How it works

To convert long tons to carats, multiply the number of long tons by 5,080,234.5. One long ton equals 5,080,234.5 carats, and one carat equals 1.96841e-07 long tons. The table shows common values.

Long Tons to Carats formula

Carats = long tons × 5,080,234.5

Worked example: convert 12.5 long tons to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 5,080,234.5 = 63,502,932 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 1.96841e-07 (or divide by 5,080,234.5).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
