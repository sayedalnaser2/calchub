How it works

To convert teaspoons to quarts, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.0052083333. To convert quarts to teaspoons, multiply by 192 (or divide by 0.0052083333). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Teaspoons to Quarts formula

Quarts = teaspoons × 0.0052083333

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0052083333 = 0.065104167 qt. To go the other way, multiply quarts by 192 (or divide by 0.0052083333).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
