Meters per Second to Feet per Second Converter

Convert meters per second to feet per second instantly. 1 m/s = 3.2808399 ft/s.

How it works

To convert meters per second to feet per second, multiply the number of meters per second by 3.2808399. One meter per second equals 3.2808399 feet per second, and one foot per second equals 0.3048 meters per second. The table shows common values.

Meters per Second to Feet per Second formula

Feet per second = meters per second × 3.2808399

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters per second to feet per second. Multiply 12.5 × 3.2808399 = 41.010499 ft/s. To go the other way, multiply feet per second by 0.3048 (or divide by 3.2808399).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
