How it works

To convert cubic feet to imperial pints, multiply the number of cubic feet by 49.830684. One cubic foot equals 49.830684 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.020067957 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = cubic feet × 49.830684

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 49.830684 = 622.88355 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.020067957 (or divide by 49.830684).
