Convert furlongs to inches instantly. 1 furlong = 7,920 in.

How it works

To convert furlongs to inches, multiply the number of furlongs by 7,920. One furlong equals 7,920 inches, and one inch equals 0.00012626263 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Inches formula

Inches = furlongs × 7,920

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 7,920 = 99,000 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 0.00012626263 (or divide by 7,920).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
