Kilocalories to Kilojoules Converter

Convert kilocalories to kilojoules instantly. 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to kilojoules, multiply the number of kilocalories by 4.184. One kilocalorie equals 4.184 kilojoules, and one kilojoule equals 0.23900574 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = kilocalories × 4.184

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 4.184 = 52.3 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 0.23900574 (or divide by 4.184).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
