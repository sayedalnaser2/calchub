Milliwatts to Horsepower Converter

Convert milliwatts to horsepower instantly. 1 mW = 1.34102e-06 hp.

How it works

To convert milliwatts to horsepower, multiply the number of milliwatts by 1.34102e-06. One milliwatt equals 1.34102e-06 horsepower, and one horsepower equals 745,699.87 milliwatts. The table shows common values.

Milliwatts to Horsepower formula

Horsepower = milliwatts × 1.34102e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliwatts to horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 1.34102e-06 = 1.67628e-05 hp. To go the other way, multiply horsepower by 745,699.87 (or divide by 1.34102e-06).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
