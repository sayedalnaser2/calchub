Milliliters to Gallons Converter

Convert milliliters to gallons instantly. 1 ml = 0.00026417205 gal.

How it works

To convert milliliters to gallons, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.00026417205. One milliliter equals 0.00026417205 gallons, and one gallon equals 3,785.4118 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Gallons formula

Gallons = milliliters × 0.00026417205

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00026417205 = 0.0033021507 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 3,785.4118 (or divide by 0.00026417205).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
