Regular Polygon Calculator

Calculate area, perimeter and angles of a regular polygon.

How it works

Area = ½ x perimeter x apothem, where the apothem is s / (2 tan(π/n)). Interior angle = (n-2) x 180° / n.

Frequently asked questions

What is the apothem?

The distance from the center to the midpoint of a side.
