5K Pace for a 48:00 Finish Time

To run a 5K in 48:00 you need 15:27 per mile (9:36 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A 5K is 5 km (3.11 miles), so 48:00 works out to 15:27 per mile or 9:36 per kilometer.
