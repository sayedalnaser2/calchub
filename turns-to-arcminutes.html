Turns to Arcminutes Converter

Convert turns to arcminutes instantly. 1 turn = 21,600 arcmin.

How it works

To convert turns to arcminutes, multiply the number of turns by 21,600. One turn equals 21,600 arcminutes, and one arcminute equals 4.62963e-05 turns. The table shows common values.

Turns to Arcminutes formula

Arcminutes = turns × 21,600

Worked example: convert 12.5 turns to arcminutes. Multiply 12.5 × 21,600 = 270,000 arcmin. To go the other way, multiply arcminutes by 4.62963e-05 (or divide by 21,600).
