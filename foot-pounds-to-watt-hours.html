Foot-Pounds to Watt Hours Converter

Convert foot-pounds to watt hours instantly. 1 ft-lb = 0.0003766161 Wh.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to watt hours, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 0.0003766161. One foot-pound equals 0.0003766161 watt hours, and one watt hour equals 2,655.2237 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Watt Hours formula

Watt hours = foot-pounds × 0.0003766161

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to watt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0003766161 = 0.0047077012 Wh. To go the other way, multiply watt hours by 2,655.2237 (or divide by 0.0003766161).
