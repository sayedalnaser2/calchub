Nanometers to Nautical Miles Converter

Convert nanometers to nautical miles instantly. 1 nm = 5.39957e-13 nmi.

How it works

To convert nanometers to nautical miles, multiply the number of nanometers by 5.39957e-13. One nanometer equals 5.39957e-13 nautical miles, and one nautical mile equals 1,852,000,000,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Nautical Miles formula

Nautical miles = nanometers × 5.39957e-13

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to nautical miles. Multiply 12.5 × 5.39957e-13 = 6.74946e-12 nmi. To go the other way, multiply nautical miles by 1,852,000,000,000 (or divide by 5.39957e-13).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
