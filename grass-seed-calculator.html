Estimate pounds of grass seed for a new lawn or overseeding.

How it works

Seed = area / 1,000 x rate. Rates vary by species; follow your seed label.

Frequently asked questions

When should I seed?

Cool-season grasses: early fall. Warm-season grasses: late spring.
