Metric Tons to Grams Converter

Convert metric tons to grams instantly. 1 t = 1,000,000 g.

How it works

To convert metric tons to grams, multiply the number of metric tons by 1,000,000. One metric ton equals 1,000,000 grams, and one gram equals 1e-06 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Grams formula

Grams = metric tons × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to grams. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 g. To go the other way, multiply grams by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
