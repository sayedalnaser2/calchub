How it works

To convert tablespoons to imperial pints, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.026021068. One tablespoon equals 0.026021068 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 38.430398 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = tablespoons × 0.026021068

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 0.026021068 = 0.32526335 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 38.430398 (or divide by 0.026021068).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
