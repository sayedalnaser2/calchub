Kilobytes to Terabytes Converter

Convert kilobytes to terabytes instantly. 1 KB = 1e-09 TB.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to terabytes, multiply the number of kilobytes by 1e-09. One kilobyte equals 1e-09 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 1,000,000,000 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = kilobytes × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
