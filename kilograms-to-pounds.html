How it works

To convert kilograms to pounds, multiply the number of kilograms by 2.2046226. One kilogram equals 2.2046226 pounds, and one pound equals 0.45359237 kilograms. The table shows common values.

Kilograms to Pounds formula

Pounds = kilograms × 2.2046226

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 2.2046226 = 27.557783 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 0.45359237 (or divide by 2.2046226).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
