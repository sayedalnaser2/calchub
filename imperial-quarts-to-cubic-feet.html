Imperial Quarts to Cubic Feet Converter

Convert imperial quarts to cubic feet instantly. 1 imp qt = 0.040135913 ft³.

How it works

To convert imperial quarts to cubic feet, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 0.040135913. One imperial quart equals 0.040135913 cubic feet, and one cubic foot equals 24.915342 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Cubic Feet formula

Cubic feet = imperial quarts × 0.040135913

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to cubic feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.040135913 = 0.50169892 ft³. To go the other way, multiply cubic feet by 24.915342 (or divide by 0.040135913).
