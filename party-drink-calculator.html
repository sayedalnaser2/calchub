Party Drink Calculator

Estimate how many drinks, wine bottles and beers to buy for a party.

How it works

A common rule of thumb is two drinks per guest in the first hour and one per guest per hour afterwards. Adjust for your crowd, and always provide water and non-alcoholic options.

Frequently asked questions

How much ice do I need?

Plan about 1 pound of ice per guest.

Is this safe guidance?

It is a planning estimate, not a recommendation to drink.
