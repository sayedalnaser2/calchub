$4,500 a Month Is How Much a Year?

$4,500 a month is $54,000 a year, or $25.96 an hour at 40 hours a week.

How it works

Annual pay is monthly pay times 12. $4,500 x 12 = $54,000. Dividing by 2,080 working hours gives the hourly equivalent.
