Convert cubic meters to imperial pints instantly. 1 m³ = 1,759.754 imp pt.

How it works

To convert cubic meters to imperial pints, multiply the number of cubic meters by 1,759.754. One cubic meter equals 1,759.754 imperial pints, and one imperial pint equals 0.00056826125 cubic meters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Meters to Imperial Pints formula

Imperial pints = cubic meters × 1,759.754

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic meters to imperial pints. Multiply 12.5 × 1,759.754 = 21,996.925 imp pt. To go the other way, multiply imperial pints by 0.00056826125 (or divide by 1,759.754).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
