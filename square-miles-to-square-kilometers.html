Square Miles to Square Kilometers Converter

Convert square miles to square kilometers instantly. 1 mi² = 2.5899881 km².

How it works

To convert square miles to square kilometers, multiply the number of square miles by 2.5899881. One square mile equals 2.5899881 square kilometers, and one square kilometer equals 0.38610216 square miles. The table shows common values.

Square Miles to Square Kilometers formula

Square kilometers = square miles × 2.5899881

Worked example: convert 12.5 square miles to square kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 2.5899881 = 32.374851 km². To go the other way, multiply square kilometers by 0.38610216 (or divide by 2.5899881).
