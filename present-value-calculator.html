Present Value Calculator

Discount a future amount to what it is worth today.

How it works

PV = FV / (1 + r/n)^(n x t). A higher discount rate or longer time makes the present value smaller.

Frequently asked questions

What discount rate should I use?

Use your required return or the interest rate you could earn on a safe alternative.
