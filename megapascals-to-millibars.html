How it works

To convert megapascals to millibars, multiply the number of megapascals by 10,000. To convert millibars to megapascals, multiply by 0.0001 (or divide by 10,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Megapascals to Millibars formula

Millibars = megapascals × 10,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megapascals to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 10,000 = 125,000 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.0001 (or divide by 10,000).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
