PSI to Bar Converter

Convert psi to bar instantly. 1 psi = 0.068947573 bar.

How it works

To convert psi to bar, multiply the number of psi by 0.068947573. One psi equals 0.068947573 bar, and one bar equals 14.503774 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Bar formula

Bar = psi × 0.068947573

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 0.068947573 = 0.86184466 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 14.503774 (or divide by 0.068947573).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
