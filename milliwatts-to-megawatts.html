How it works

To convert milliwatts to megawatts, multiply the number of milliwatts by 1e-09. To convert megawatts to milliwatts, multiply by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Milliwatts to Megawatts formula

Megawatts = milliwatts × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliwatts to megawatts. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 MW. To go the other way, multiply megawatts by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
