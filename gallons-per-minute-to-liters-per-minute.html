Gallons per Minute to Liters per Minute Converter

Convert gallons per minute to liters per minute instantly. 1 gal/min = 3.7854118 L/min.

How it works

To convert gallons per minute to liters per minute, multiply the number of gallons per minute by 3.7854118. One gallon per minute equals 3.7854118 liters per minute, and one liter per minute equals 0.26417205 gallons per minute. The table shows common values.

Gallons per Minute to Liters per Minute formula

Liters per minute = gallons per minute × 3.7854118

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons per minute to liters per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 3.7854118 = 47.317647 L/min. To go the other way, multiply liters per minute by 0.26417205 (or divide by 3.7854118).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
