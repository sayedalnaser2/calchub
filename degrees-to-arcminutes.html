How it works

To convert degrees to arcminutes, multiply the number of degrees by 60. To convert arcminutes to degrees, multiply by 0.016666667 (or divide by 60). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Degrees to Arcminutes formula

Arcminutes = degrees × 60

Worked example: convert 12.5 degrees to arcminutes. Multiply 12.5 × 60 = 750 arcmin. To go the other way, multiply arcminutes by 0.016666667 (or divide by 60).
