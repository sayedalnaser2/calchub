Cubic Centimeters to Cups Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to cups instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.0042267528 cup.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to cups, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.0042267528. One cubic centimeter equals 0.0042267528 cups, and one cup equals 236.58824 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Cups formula

Cups = cubic centimeters × 0.0042267528

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0042267528 = 0.05283441 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 236.58824 (or divide by 0.0042267528).

About volume units
