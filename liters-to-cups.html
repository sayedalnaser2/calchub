Liters to Cups Converter

Convert liters to cups instantly. 1 L = 4.2267528 cup.

How it works

To convert liters to cups, multiply the number of liters by 4.2267528. One liter equals 4.2267528 cups, and one cup equals 0.23658824 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Cups formula

Cups = liters × 4.2267528

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 4.2267528 = 52.83441 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.23658824 (or divide by 4.2267528).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
