Micrometers to Nanometers Converter

Convert micrometers to nanometers instantly. 1 µm = 1,000 nm.

How it works

To convert micrometers to nanometers, multiply the number of micrometers by 1,000. One micrometer equals 1,000 nanometers, and one nanometer equals 0.001 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Nanometers formula

Nanometers = micrometers × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to nanometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 nm. To go the other way, multiply nanometers by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
