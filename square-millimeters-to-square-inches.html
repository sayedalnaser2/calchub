How it works

To convert square millimeters to square inches, multiply the number of square millimeters by 0.0015500031. One square millimeter equals 0.0015500031 square inches, and one square inch equals 645.16 square millimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Millimeters to Square Inches formula

Square inches = square millimeters × 0.0015500031

Worked example: convert 12.5 square millimeters to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0015500031 = 0.019375039 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 645.16 (or divide by 0.0015500031).
