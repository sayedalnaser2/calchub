Nanoseconds to Minutes Converter

Convert nanoseconds to minutes instantly. 1 ns = 1.66667e-11 min.

How it works

To convert nanoseconds to minutes, multiply the number of nanoseconds by 1.66667e-11. One nanosecond equals 1.66667e-11 minutes, and one minute equals 60,000,000,000 nanoseconds. The table shows common values.

Nanoseconds to Minutes formula

Minutes = nanoseconds × 1.66667e-11

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanoseconds to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.66667e-11 = 2.08333e-10 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 60,000,000,000 (or divide by 1.66667e-11).
