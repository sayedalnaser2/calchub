Convert hectares to acres instantly. 1 ha = 2.4710538 acres.

How it works

To convert hectares to acres, multiply the number of hectares by 2.4710538. One hectare equals 2.4710538 acres, and one acre equals 0.40468564 hectares. The table shows common values.

Hectares to Acres formula

Acres = hectares × 2.4710538

Worked example: convert 12.5 hectares to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 2.4710538 = 30.888173 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 0.40468564 (or divide by 2.4710538).
