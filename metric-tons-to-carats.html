Metric Tons to Carats Converter

Convert metric tons to carats instantly. 1 t = 5,000,000 ct.

How it works

To convert metric tons to carats, multiply the number of metric tons by 5,000,000. One metric ton equals 5,000,000 carats, and one carat equals 2e-07 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Carats formula

Carats = metric tons × 5,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 5,000,000 = 62,500,000 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 2e-07 (or divide by 5,000,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
