Grains to Short Tons Converter

Convert grains to short tons instantly. 1 gr = 7.14286e-08 short tons.

How it works

To convert grains to short tons, multiply the number of grains by 7.14286e-08. One grain equals 7.14286e-08 short tons, and one short ton equals 14,000,000 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Short Tons formula

Short tons = grains × 7.14286e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to short tons. Multiply 12.5 × 7.14286e-08 = 8.92857e-07 short tons. To go the other way, multiply short tons by 14,000,000 (or divide by 7.14286e-08).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
