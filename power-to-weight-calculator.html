Cycling Power-to-Weight Calculator

Calculate watts per kilogram for cycling performance.

How it works

Power-to-weight is watts divided by body mass in kilograms. It predicts climbing speed far better than raw watts. Levels shown are rough guides for sustained efforts.

Frequently asked questions

What is FTP?

Functional threshold power is the highest power you can sustain for about an hour.

How can I improve W/kg?

Increase power through training or reduce body mass sensibly.
