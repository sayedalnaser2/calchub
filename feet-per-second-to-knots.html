Feet per Second to Knots Converter

Convert feet per second to knots instantly. 1 ft/s = 0.5924838 kn.

How it works

To convert feet per second to knots, multiply the number of feet per second by 0.5924838. One foot per second equals 0.5924838 knots, and one knot equals 1.6878099 feet per second. The table shows common values.

Feet per Second to Knots formula

Knots = feet per second × 0.5924838

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet per second to knots. Multiply 12.5 × 0.5924838 = 7.4060475 kn. To go the other way, multiply knots by 1.6878099 (or divide by 0.5924838).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
