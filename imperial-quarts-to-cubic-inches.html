How it works

To convert imperial quarts to cubic inches, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 69.354858. One imperial quart equals 69.354858 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.014418601 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = imperial quarts × 69.354858

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 69.354858 = 866.93573 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.014418601 (or divide by 69.354858).

About volume units
