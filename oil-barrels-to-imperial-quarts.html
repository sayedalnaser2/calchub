How it works

To convert oil barrels to imperial quarts, multiply the number of oil barrels by 139.88926. One oil barrel equals 139.88926 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 0.0071485115 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = oil barrels × 139.88926

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 139.88926 = 1,748.6158 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 0.0071485115 (or divide by 139.88926).
