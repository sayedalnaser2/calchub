Metric Tons to Micrograms Converter

Convert metric tons to micrograms instantly. 1 t = 1,000,000,000,000 µg.

How it works

To convert metric tons to micrograms, multiply the number of metric tons by 1,000,000,000,000. One metric ton equals 1,000,000,000,000 micrograms, and one microgram equals 1e-12 metric tons. The table shows common values.

Metric Tons to Micrograms formula

Micrograms = metric tons × 1,000,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric tons to micrograms. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000,000 µg. To go the other way, multiply micrograms by 1e-12 (or divide by 1,000,000,000,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
