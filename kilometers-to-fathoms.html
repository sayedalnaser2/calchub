Kilometers to Fathoms Converter

Convert kilometers to fathoms instantly. 1 km = 546.80665 fathoms.

How it works

To convert kilometers to fathoms, multiply the number of kilometers by 546.80665. One kilometer equals 546.80665 fathoms, and one fathom equals 0.0018288 kilometers. The table shows common values.

Kilometers to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = kilometers × 546.80665

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 546.80665 = 6,835.0831 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 0.0018288 (or divide by 546.80665).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
