Ohm's Law Calculator

Find current and power from voltage and resistance.

How it works

Ohm's law: V = I x R. Power is P = V x I. Given voltage and resistance, current is I = V / R.

Frequently asked questions

What is Ohm's law?

It states that the current through a conductor is proportional to the voltage across it and inversely proportional to its resistance.
