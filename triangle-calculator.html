Triangle Angle Calculator (SSS)

Find all angles of a triangle from its three sides.

How it works

The law of cosines gives each angle from the three sides. Angles of a triangle always add to 180°.

Frequently asked questions

Can I solve a triangle with two sides and an angle?

Yes, with the law of sines or cosines; this tool handles the three-sides case.
