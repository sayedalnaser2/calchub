Convert imperial gallons to teaspoons instantly. 1 imp gal = 922.32954 tsp.

How it works

To convert imperial gallons to teaspoons, multiply the number of imperial gallons by 922.32954. One imperial gallon equals 922.32954 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.0010842112 imperial gallons. The table shows common values.

Imperial Gallons to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = imperial gallons × 922.32954

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial gallons to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 922.32954 = 11,529.119 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.0010842112 (or divide by 922.32954).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
