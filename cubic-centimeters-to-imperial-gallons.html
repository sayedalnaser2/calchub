Convert cubic centimeters to imperial gallons instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.00021996925 imp gal.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to imperial gallons, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.00021996925. One cubic centimeter equals 0.00021996925 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 4,546.09 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = cubic centimeters × 0.00021996925

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00021996925 = 0.0027496156 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 4,546.09 (or divide by 0.00021996925).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
