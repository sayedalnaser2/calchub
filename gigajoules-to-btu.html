Gigajoules to BTU Converter

Convert gigajoules to BTU instantly. 1 GJ = 947,817.12 BTU.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to BTU, multiply the number of gigajoules by 947,817.12. One gigajoule equals 947,817.12 BTU, and one BTU equals 1.05506e-06 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to BTU formula

Btu = gigajoules × 947,817.12

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 947,817.12 = 11,847,714 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 1.05506e-06 (or divide by 947,817.12).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
