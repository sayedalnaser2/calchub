Grams of Butter to Cups

Convert grams of butter to cups. 1 cup of butter weighs about 227 g.

How it works

Butter is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 227 g per US cup, convert grams to cups by multiplying by 0.004405.

1 US cup of butter weighs about 227 g (8.0 oz), 1 tablespoon about 14.2 g and 1 teaspoon about 4.7 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
