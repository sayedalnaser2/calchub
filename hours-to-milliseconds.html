Convert hours to milliseconds and milliseconds to hours instantly. 1 h = 3,600,000 ms.

How it works

To convert hours to milliseconds, multiply the number of hours by 3,600,000. To convert milliseconds to hours, multiply by 2.77778e-07 (or divide by 3,600,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Hours to Milliseconds formula

Milliseconds = hours × 3,600,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to milliseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3,600,000 = 45,000,000 ms. To go the other way, multiply milliseconds by 2.77778e-07 (or divide by 3,600,000).
