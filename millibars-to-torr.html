Millibars to Torr Converter

Convert millibars to torr instantly. 1 mbar = 0.75006168 Torr.

How it works

To convert millibars to torr, multiply the number of millibars by 0.75006168. One millibar equals 0.75006168 torr, and one torr equals 1.3332237 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to Torr formula

Torr = millibars × 0.75006168

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 0.75006168 = 9.375771 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 1.3332237 (or divide by 0.75006168).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
