Therms to Kilocalories Converter

Convert therms to kilocalories instantly. 1 therms = 25,210.421 kcal.

How it works

To convert therms to kilocalories, multiply the number of therms by 25,210.421. One therm equals 25,210.421 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 3.96661e-05 therms. The table shows common values.

Therms to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = therms × 25,210.421

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 25,210.421 = 315,130.26 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 3.96661e-05 (or divide by 25,210.421).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
