How it works

To convert furlongs to micrometers, multiply the number of furlongs by 201,168,000. To convert micrometers to furlongs, multiply by 4.97097e-09 (or divide by 201,168,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Furlongs to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = furlongs × 201,168,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 201,168,000 = 2,514,600,000 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 4.97097e-09 (or divide by 201,168,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
