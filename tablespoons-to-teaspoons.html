Tablespoons to Teaspoons Converter

Convert tablespoons to teaspoons instantly. 1 tbsp = 3 tsp.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to teaspoons, multiply the number of tablespoons by 3. One tablespoon equals 3 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 0.33333333 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = tablespoons × 3

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 3 = 37.5 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 0.33333333 (or divide by 3).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
