Federal Income Tax Calculator

Estimate your US federal income tax, marginal rate and effective rate.

How it works

The standard deduction is subtracted from income, then each slice is taxed at its bracket rate. The marginal rate applies only to the last dollars earned. Figures use 2025 US federal rules and are estimates only.

Frequently asked questions

Marginal vs effective rate?

Marginal is the rate on your next dollar. Effective is total tax divided by total income.

Does it include state tax?

No. State taxes vary widely.
