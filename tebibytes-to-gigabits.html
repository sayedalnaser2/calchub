Tebibytes to Gigabits Converter

Convert tebibytes to gigabits instantly. 1 TiB = 8,796.093 Gb.

How it works

To convert tebibytes to gigabits, multiply the number of tebibytes by 8,796.093. One tebibyte equals 8,796.093 gigabits, and one gigabit equals 0.00011368684 tebibytes. The table shows common values.

Tebibytes to Gigabits formula

Gigabits = tebibytes × 8,796.093

Worked example: convert 12.5 tebibytes to gigabits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,796.093 = 109,951.16 Gb. To go the other way, multiply gigabits by 0.00011368684 (or divide by 8,796.093).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
