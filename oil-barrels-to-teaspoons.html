Oil Barrels to Teaspoons Converter

Convert oil barrels to teaspoons instantly. 1 bbl = 32,256 tsp.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to teaspoons, multiply the number of oil barrels by 32,256. One oil barrel equals 32,256 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 3.1002e-05 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = oil barrels × 32,256

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 32,256 = 403,200 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 3.1002e-05 (or divide by 32,256).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
