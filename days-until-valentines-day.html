How Many Days Until Valentine's Day?

Live countdown showing the days, weeks and hours left until Valentine's Day.

How it works

The countdown compares today's date with the next occurrence of Valentine's Day. If the holiday has passed this year it counts to next year's date. Moving holidays such as Easter or Thanksgiving are calculated from their calendar rules.
