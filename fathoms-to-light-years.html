Fathoms to Light Years Converter

Convert fathoms to light years instantly. 1 fathoms = 1.93304e-16 ly.

How it works

To convert fathoms to light years, multiply the number of fathoms by 1.93304e-16. One fathom equals 1.93304e-16 light years, and one light year equals 5.17319e+15 fathoms. The table shows common values.

Fathoms to Light Years formula

Light years = fathoms × 1.93304e-16

Worked example: convert 12.5 fathoms to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 1.93304e-16 = 2.4163e-15 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 5.17319e+15 (or divide by 1.93304e-16).
