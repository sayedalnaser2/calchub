How it works

To convert mmHg to atmospheres, multiply the number of mmHg by 0.0013157897. One mmHg equals 0.0013157897 atmospheres, and one atmosphere equals 759.99989 mmHg. The table shows common values.

mmHg to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = mmHg × 0.0013157897

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to atmospheres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013157897 = 0.016447371 atm. To go the other way, multiply atmospheres by 759.99989 (or divide by 0.0013157897).
