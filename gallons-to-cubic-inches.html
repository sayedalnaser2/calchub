Gallons to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert gallons to cubic inches instantly. 1 gal = 231 in³.

How it works

To convert gallons to cubic inches, multiply the number of gallons by 231. One gallon equals 231 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.0043290043 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = gallons × 231

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 231 = 2,887.5 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.0043290043 (or divide by 231).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
