Teaspoons of Raisins to Grams

Convert teaspoons of raisins to grams. 1 cup of raisins weighs about 149 g.

How it works

Raisins is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 149 g per US cup, convert teaspoons to grams by multiplying by 3.104.
