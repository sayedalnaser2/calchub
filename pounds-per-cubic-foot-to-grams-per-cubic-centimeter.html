Pounds per Cubic Foot to Grams per Cubic Centimeter Converter

Convert pounds per cubic foot to grams per cubic centimeter instantly. 1 lb/ft³ = 0.016018463 g/cm³.

How it works

To convert pounds per cubic foot to grams per cubic centimeter, multiply the number of pounds per cubic foot by 0.016018463. One pound per cubic foot equals 0.016018463 grams per cubic centimeter, and one gram per cubic centimeter equals 62.427961 pounds per cubic foot. The table shows common values.

Pounds per Cubic Foot to Grams per Cubic Centimeter formula

Grams per cubic centimeter = pounds per cubic foot × 0.016018463
