How it works

To convert square inches to acres, multiply the number of square inches by 1.59423e-07. One square inch equals 1.59423e-07 acres, and one acre equals 6.27264e+06 square inches. The table shows common values.

Square Inches to Acres formula

Acres = square inches × 1.59423e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 1.59423e-07 = 1.99278e-06 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 6.27264e+06 (or divide by 1.59423e-07).
