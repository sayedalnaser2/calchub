How it works

To convert square inches to acres, multiply the number of square inches by 1.59423e-07. To convert acres to square inches, multiply by 6,272,640 (or divide by 1.59423e-07). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Inches to Acres formula

Acres = square inches × 1.59423e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 square inches to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 1.59423e-07 = 1.99278e-06 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 6,272,640 (or divide by 1.59423e-07).
