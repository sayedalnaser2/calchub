Foot-Pounds to Gigajoules Converter

Convert foot-pounds to gigajoules instantly. 1 ft-lb = 1.35582e-09 GJ.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to gigajoules, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 1.35582e-09. One foot-pound equals 1.35582e-09 gigajoules, and one gigajoule equals 737,562,150 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Gigajoules formula

Gigajoules = foot-pounds × 1.35582e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to gigajoules. Multiply 12.5 × 1.35582e-09 = 1.69477e-08 GJ. To go the other way, multiply gigajoules by 737,562,150 (or divide by 1.35582e-09).
