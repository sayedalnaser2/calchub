Micrometers to Centimeters Converter

Convert micrometers to centimeters instantly. 1 µm = 0.0001 cm.

How it works

To convert micrometers to centimeters, multiply the number of micrometers by 0.0001. One micrometer equals 0.0001 centimeters, and one centimeter equals 10,000 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = micrometers × 0.0001

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0001 = 0.00125 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 10,000 (or divide by 0.0001).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
