How it works

To convert kilonewtons to ounce-force, multiply the number of kilonewtons by 3,596.9431. One kilonewton equals 3,596.9431 ounce-force, and one ounce-force equals 0.00027801385 kilonewtons. The table shows common values.

Kilonewtons to Ounce-Force formula

Ounce-force = kilonewtons × 3,596.9431

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilonewtons to ounce-force. Multiply 12.5 × 3,596.9431 = 44,961.789 ozf. To go the other way, multiply ounce-force by 0.00027801385 (or divide by 3,596.9431).
