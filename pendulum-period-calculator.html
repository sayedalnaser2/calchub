Pendulum Period Calculator

Calculate the period and frequency of a simple pendulum.

How it works

For small angles the period is T = 2 x pi x sqrt(L / g), independent of mass and amplitude. A 1 m pendulum has a period of about 2 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Does mass affect the period?

No.

When is the formula inaccurate?

For swings beyond about 15 degrees, where the period grows slightly.
