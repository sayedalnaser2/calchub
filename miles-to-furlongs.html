Miles to Furlongs Converter

Convert miles to furlongs instantly. 1 mi = 8 furlongs.

How it works

To convert miles to furlongs, multiply the number of miles by 8. One mile equals 8 furlongs, and one furlong equals 0.125 miles. The table shows common values.

Miles to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = miles × 8

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 8 = 100 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 0.125 (or divide by 8).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
