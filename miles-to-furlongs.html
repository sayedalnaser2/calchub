How it works

To convert miles to furlongs, multiply the number of miles by 8. To convert furlongs to miles, multiply by 0.125 (or divide by 8). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Miles to Furlongs formula

Furlongs = miles × 8

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to furlongs. Multiply 12.5 × 8 = 100 furlongs. To go the other way, multiply furlongs by 0.125 (or divide by 8).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
