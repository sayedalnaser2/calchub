Kilojoules to Joules Converter

Convert kilojoules to joules instantly. 1 kJ = 1000 J.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to joules, multiply the number of kilojoules by 1000. One kilojoule equals 1000 joules, and one joule equals 0.001 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to Joules formula

Joules = kilojoules × 1000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 1000 = 12500 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 0.001 (or divide by 1000).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
