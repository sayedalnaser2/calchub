Square Feet to Square Miles Converter

Convert square feet to square miles instantly. 1 ft² = 3.58701e-08 mi².

How it works

To convert square feet to square miles, multiply the number of square feet by 3.58701e-08. One square foot equals 3.58701e-08 square miles, and one square mile equals 27,878,400 square feet. The table shows common values.

Square Feet to Square Miles formula

Square miles = square feet × 3.58701e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 square feet to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 3.58701e-08 = 4.48376e-07 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 27,878,400 (or divide by 3.58701e-08).
