Gigabits to Kilobits Converter

Convert gigabits to kilobits instantly. 1 Gb = 1,000,000 Kb.

How it works

To convert gigabits to kilobits, multiply the number of gigabits by 1,000,000. One gigabit equals 1,000,000 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 1e-06 gigabits. The table shows common values.

Gigabits to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = gigabits × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabits to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
