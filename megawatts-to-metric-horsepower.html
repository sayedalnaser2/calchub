Megawatts to Metric Horsepower Converter

Convert megawatts to metric horsepower instantly. 1 MW = 1,359.6216 PS.

How it works

To convert megawatts to metric horsepower, multiply the number of megawatts by 1,359.6216. One megawatt equals 1,359.6216 metric horsepower, and one metric horsepower equals 0.00073549875 megawatts. The table shows common values.

Megawatts to Metric Horsepower formula

Metric horsepower = megawatts × 1,359.6216

Worked example: convert 12.5 megawatts to metric horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 1,359.6216 = 16,995.27 PS. To go the other way, multiply metric horsepower by 0.00073549875 (or divide by 1,359.6216).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
