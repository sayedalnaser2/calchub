Ovulation Calculator

Estimate your fertile window and ovulation date from your cycle.

How it works

Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before the next period. Sperm can survive up to five days, so the fertile window runs from five days before ovulation to one day after.

Frequently asked questions

Is this accurate with irregular cycles?

Less so. Tracking basal body temperature or ovulation tests is more reliable.

Can I get pregnant outside the window?

Chances are low but timing varies cycle to cycle.
