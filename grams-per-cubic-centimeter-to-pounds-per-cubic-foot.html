Grams per Cubic Centimeter to Pounds per Cubic Foot Converter

Convert grams per cubic centimeter to pounds per cubic foot instantly. 1 g/cm³ = 62.427961 lb/ft³.

How it works

To convert grams per cubic centimeter to pounds per cubic foot, multiply the number of grams per cubic centimeter by 62.427961. One gram per cubic centimeter equals 62.427961 pounds per cubic foot, and one pound per cubic foot equals 0.016018463 grams per cubic centimeter. The table shows common values.

Grams per Cubic Centimeter to Pounds per Cubic Foot formula

Pounds per cubic foot = grams per cubic centimeter × 62.427961

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams per cubic centimeter to pounds per cubic foot. Multiply 12.5 × 62.427961 = 780.34951 lb/ft³. To go the other way, multiply pounds per cubic foot by 0.016018463 (or divide by 62.427961).
