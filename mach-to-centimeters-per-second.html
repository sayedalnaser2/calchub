Mach to Centimeters per Second Converter

Convert mach to centimeters per second instantly. 1 Mach = 34,029 cm/s.

How it works

To convert mach to centimeters per second, multiply the number of mach by 34,029. One Mach equals 34,029 centimeters per second, and one centimeter per second equals 2.93867e-05 mach. The table shows common values.

Mach to Centimeters per Second formula

Centimeters per second = mach × 34,029

Worked example: convert 12.5 mach to centimeters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 34,029 = 425,362.5 cm/s. To go the other way, multiply centimeters per second by 2.93867e-05 (or divide by 34,029).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
