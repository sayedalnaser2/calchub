How it works

To convert gallons per minute to liters per second, multiply the number of gallons per minute by 0.063090196. To convert liters per second to gallons per minute, multiply by 15.850323 (or divide by 0.063090196). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Gallons per Minute to Liters per Second formula

Liters per second = gallons per minute × 0.063090196

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons per minute to liters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.063090196 = 0.78862746 L/s. To go the other way, multiply liters per second by 15.850323 (or divide by 0.063090196).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
