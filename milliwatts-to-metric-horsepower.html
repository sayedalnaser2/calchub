How it works

To convert milliwatts to metric horsepower, multiply the number of milliwatts by 1.35962e-06. To convert metric horsepower to milliwatts, multiply by 735,498.75 (or divide by 1.35962e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
