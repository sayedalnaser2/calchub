Milliwatts to Metric Horsepower Converter

Convert milliwatts to metric horsepower instantly. 1 mW = 1.35962e-06 PS.

How it works

To convert milliwatts to metric horsepower, multiply the number of milliwatts by 1.35962e-06. One milliwatt equals 1.35962e-06 metric horsepower, and one metric horsepower equals 735,498.75 milliwatts. The table shows common values.

Milliwatts to Metric Horsepower formula

Metric horsepower = milliwatts × 1.35962e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliwatts to metric horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 1.35962e-06 = 1.69953e-05 PS. To go the other way, multiply metric horsepower by 735,498.75 (or divide by 1.35962e-06).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
