Cubic Yards to Imperial Quarts Converter

Convert cubic yards to imperial quarts instantly. 1 yd³ = 672.71423 imp qt.

How it works

To convert cubic yards to imperial quarts, multiply the number of cubic yards by 672.71423. One cubic yard equals 672.71423 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 0.0014865153 cubic yards. The table shows common values.

Cubic Yards to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = cubic yards × 672.71423

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic yards to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 672.71423 = 8,408.9279 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 0.0014865153 (or divide by 672.71423).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
