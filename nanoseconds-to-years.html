Nanoseconds to Years Converter

Convert nanoseconds to years instantly. 1 ns = 3.16887e-17 years.

How it works

To convert nanoseconds to years, multiply the number of nanoseconds by 3.16887e-17. One nanosecond equals 3.16887e-17 years, and one year equals 3.1557e+16 nanoseconds. The table shows common values.

Nanoseconds to Years formula

Years = nanoseconds × 3.16887e-17

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanoseconds to years. Multiply 12.5 × 3.16887e-17 = 3.96109e-16 years. To go the other way, multiply years by 3.1557e+16 (or divide by 3.16887e-17).
