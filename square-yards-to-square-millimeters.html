How it works

To convert square yards to square millimeters, multiply the number of square yards by 836,127.36. To convert square millimeters to square yards, multiply by 1.19599e-06 (or divide by 836,127.36). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Yards to Square Millimeters formula

Square millimeters = square yards × 836,127.36

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to square millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 836,127.36 = 10,451,592 mm². To go the other way, multiply square millimeters by 1.19599e-06 (or divide by 836,127.36).
