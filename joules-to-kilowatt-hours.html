Joules to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert joules to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 J = 2.77778e-07 kWh.

How it works

To convert joules to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of joules by 2.77778e-07. One joule equals 2.77778e-07 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 3,600,000 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = joules × 2.77778e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 2.77778e-07 = 3.47222e-06 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 3,600,000 (or divide by 2.77778e-07).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
