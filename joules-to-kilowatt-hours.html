How it works

To convert joules to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of joules by 2.77778e-07. To convert kilowatt hours to joules, multiply by 3,600,000 (or divide by 2.77778e-07). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Joules to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = joules × 2.77778e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 2.77778e-07 = 3.47222e-06 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 3,600,000 (or divide by 2.77778e-07).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
