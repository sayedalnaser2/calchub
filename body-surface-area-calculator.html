Body Surface Area Calculator

Calculate BSA using the Mosteller and DuBois formulas.

How it works

BSA is used in medicine to dose some drugs. Mosteller: √(height x weight / 3600). DuBois: 0.007184 x W^0.425 x H^0.725. This is an educational estimate, not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why does BSA matter?

Chemotherapy and some other doses are scaled to body surface area.
