Grams to Metric Tons Converter

Convert grams to metric tons instantly. 1 g = 1e-06 t.

How it works

To convert grams to metric tons, multiply the number of grams by 1e-06. One gram equals 1e-06 metric tons, and one metric ton equals 1,000,000 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Metric Tons formula

Metric tons = grams × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to metric tons. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 t. To go the other way, multiply metric tons by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
