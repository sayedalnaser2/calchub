Race Time Predictor

Predict your race times at other distances from a recent result.

How it works

The Riegel formula predicts T2 = T1 x (D2 / D1)^1.06. It works best for distances not too different from the known race and for well-trained runners.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it harder for the marathon?

Marathon performance depends on endurance training, so predictions from short races can be optimistic.
