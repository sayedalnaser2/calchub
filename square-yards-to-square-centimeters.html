Square Yards to Square Centimeters Converter

Convert square yards to square centimeters instantly. 1 yd² = 8,361.2736 cm².

How it works

To convert square yards to square centimeters, multiply the number of square yards by 8,361.2736. One square yard equals 8,361.2736 square centimeters, and one square centimeter equals 0.000119599 square yards. The table shows common values.

Square Yards to Square Centimeters formula

Square centimeters = square yards × 8,361.2736

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to square centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 8,361.2736 = 104,515.92 cm². To go the other way, multiply square centimeters by 0.000119599 (or divide by 8,361.2736).
