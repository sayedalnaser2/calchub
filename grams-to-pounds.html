Convert grams to pounds instantly. 1 g = 0.0022046226 lb.

How it works

To convert grams to pounds, multiply the number of grams by 0.0022046226. One gram equals 0.0022046226 pounds, and one pound equals 453.59237 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Pounds formula

Pounds = grams × 0.0022046226

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0022046226 = 0.027557783 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 453.59237 (or divide by 0.0022046226).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
