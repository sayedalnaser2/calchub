How it works

To convert short tons to stone, multiply the number of short tons by 142.85714. One short ton equals 142.85714 stone, and one stone equals 0.007 short tons. The table shows common values.

Stone = short tons × 142.85714

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
