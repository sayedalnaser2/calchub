CTR, CPC and CPM Calculator

Calculate click-through rate, cost per click and cost per thousand impressions.

How it works

CTR = clicks / impressions. CPC = cost / clicks. CPM = cost / impressions x 1000. These three metrics describe the efficiency of an ad campaign.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CTR?

Search ads often exceed 3%, display ads are usually well below 1%.

How are CPM and CPC related?

CPM = CPC x CTR x 1000.
