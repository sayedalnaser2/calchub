Centimeters per Second to Kilometers per Hour Converter

Convert centimeters per second to kilometers per hour instantly. 1 cm/s = 0.036 km/h.

How it works

To convert centimeters per second to kilometers per hour, multiply the number of centimeters per second by 0.036. One centimeter per second equals 0.036 kilometers per hour, and one kilometer per hour equals 27.777778 centimeters per second. The table shows common values.

Centimeters per Second to Kilometers per Hour formula

Kilometers per hour = centimeters per second × 0.036

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters per second to kilometers per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 0.036 = 0.45 km/h. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per hour by 27.777778 (or divide by 0.036).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
