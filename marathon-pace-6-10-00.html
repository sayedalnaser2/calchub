Marathon Pace for a 6:10:00 Finish Time

To run a Marathon in 6:10:00 you need 14:07 per mile (8:46 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Marathon is 42.195 km (26.22 miles), so 6:10:00 works out to 14:07 per mile or 8:46 per kilometer.
