Ounce-Inches to Newton Millimeters Converter

Convert ounce-inches to newton millimeters instantly. 1 oz-in = 7.0615518 N·mm.

How it works

To convert ounce-inches to newton millimeters, multiply the number of ounce-inches by 7.0615518. One ounce-inch equals 7.0615518 newton millimeters, and one newton millimeter equals 0.14161193 ounce-inches. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Inches to Newton Millimeters formula

Newton millimeters = ounce-inches × 7.0615518

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounce-inches to newton millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 7.0615518 = 88.269397 N·mm. To go the other way, multiply newton millimeters by 0.14161193 (or divide by 7.0615518).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
