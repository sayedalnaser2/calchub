Oil Barrels to Tablespoons Converter

Convert oil barrels to tablespoons instantly. 1 bbl = 10,752 tbsp.

How it works

To convert oil barrels to tablespoons, multiply the number of oil barrels by 10,752. One oil barrel equals 10,752 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 9.3006e-05 oil barrels. The table shows common values.

Oil Barrels to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = oil barrels × 10,752

Worked example: convert 12.5 oil barrels to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 10,752 = 134,400 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 9.3006e-05 (or divide by 10,752).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
