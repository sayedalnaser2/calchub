Atmospheres to Torr Converter

Convert atmospheres to torr instantly. 1 atm = 760 Torr.

How it works

To convert atmospheres to torr, multiply the number of atmospheres by 760. One atmosphere equals 760 torr, and one torr equals 0.0013157895 atmospheres. The table shows common values.

Atmospheres to Torr formula

Torr = atmospheres × 760

Worked example: convert 12.5 atmospheres to torr. Multiply 12.5 × 760 = 9,500 Torr. To go the other way, multiply torr by 0.0013157895 (or divide by 760).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
