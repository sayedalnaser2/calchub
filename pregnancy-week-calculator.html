Pregnancy Week Calculator

See how many weeks pregnant you are and which trimester.

How it works

Gestational age is counted from the first day of your last menstrual period. Trimesters: first up to week 13, second weeks 14-27, third from week 28.

Frequently asked questions

Why count from the last period?

Because the exact conception date is usually unknown.
