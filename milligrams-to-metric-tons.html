How it works

To convert milligrams to metric tons, multiply the number of milligrams by 1e-09. To convert metric tons to milligrams, multiply by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Milligrams to Metric Tons formula

Metric tons = milligrams × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to metric tons. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 t. To go the other way, multiply metric tons by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
