Speech Length Calculator

Estimate how many minutes a speech will take, or how many words you need for a target time.

How it works

Most people speak 120-150 words per minute when presenting. Time = words / pace, and words needed = minutes x pace.

Frequently asked questions

How many words is a 5-minute speech?

About 650 words at 130 wpm.

Should I allow for pauses?

Yes. Pauses, slides and audience reactions add time.
