Calories to Joules Converter

Convert calories to joules instantly. 1 cal = 4.184 J.

How it works

To convert calories to joules, multiply the number of calories by 4.184. One calorie equals 4.184 joules, and one joule equals 0.23900574 calories. The table shows common values.

Calories to Joules formula

Joules = calories × 4.184

Worked example: convert 12.5 calories to joules. Multiply 12.5 × 4.184 = 52.3 J. To go the other way, multiply joules by 0.23900574 (or divide by 4.184).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
