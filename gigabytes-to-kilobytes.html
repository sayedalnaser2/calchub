Gigabytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert gigabytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 GB = 1e+06 KB.

How it works

To convert gigabytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of gigabytes by 1e+06. One gigabyte equals 1e+06 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 1e-06 gigabytes. The table shows common values.

Gigabytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = gigabytes × 1e+06

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1e+06 = 1.25e+07 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 1e-06 (or divide by 1e+06).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
