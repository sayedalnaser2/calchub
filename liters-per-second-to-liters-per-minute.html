Convert liters per second to liters per minute instantly. 1 L/s = 60 L/min.

How it works

To convert liters per second to liters per minute, multiply the number of liters per second by 60. One liter per second equals 60 liters per minute, and one liter per minute equals 0.016666667 liters per second. The table shows common values.

Liters per Second to Liters per Minute formula

Liters per minute = liters per second × 60

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters per second to liters per minute. Multiply 12.5 × 60 = 750 L/min. To go the other way, multiply liters per minute by 0.016666667 (or divide by 60).

About flow rate units

Flow rate is the volume of fluid moving per unit of time. Liters per minute and gallons per minute are common for pumps and plumbing, and cfm for air handling.
