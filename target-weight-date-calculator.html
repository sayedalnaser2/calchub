Target Weight Date Calculator

Find when you will reach a goal weight at a steady weekly pace.

How it works

Weeks needed = weight difference / weekly change. A safe pace is generally 0.25-1 kg (0.5-2 lb) per week. Progress is rarely perfectly linear.

Frequently asked questions

What is a safe weekly weight loss?

About 0.5-1 kg (1-2 lb) per week is commonly recommended.

Does the date account for plateaus?

No. Treat it as an estimate.
