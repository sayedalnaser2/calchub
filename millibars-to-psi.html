Millibars to PSI Converter

Convert millibars to psi instantly. 1 mbar = 0.014503774 psi.

How it works

To convert millibars to psi, multiply the number of millibars by 0.014503774. One millibar equals 0.014503774 psi, and one psi equals 68.947573 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to PSI formula

Psi = millibars × 0.014503774

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to psi. Multiply 12.5 × 0.014503774 = 0.18129717 psi. To go the other way, multiply psi by 68.947573 (or divide by 0.014503774).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
