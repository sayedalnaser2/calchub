Mulch Calculator

Find how many cubic yards or bags of mulch you need.

How it works

Volume equals area times depth. Most beds use 2-3 inches of mulch. One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should mulch be?

2-3 inches. Deeper layers can suffocate plant roots.
