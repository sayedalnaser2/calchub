Imperial Pints to Imperial Quarts Converter

Convert imperial pints to imperial quarts instantly. 1 imp pt = 0.5 imp qt.

How it works

To convert imperial pints to imperial quarts, multiply the number of imperial pints by 0.5. One imperial pint equals 0.5 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 2 imperial pints. The table shows common values.

Imperial Pints to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = imperial pints × 0.5

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial pints to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.5 = 6.25 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 2 (or divide by 0.5).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
