How it works

The conversion formula is °F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32. The table lists reference points such as freezing and boiling.

About temperature scales

Celsius and Kelvin share the same step size, while a Fahrenheit degree is 5/9 as large. Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F, 273.15 K) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F, 373.15 K) at sea level.

What is the formula for Kelvin to Fahrenheit?
