Mils to Feet Converter

Convert mils to feet instantly. 1 mil = 8.33333e-05 ft.

How it works

To convert mils to feet, multiply the number of mils by 8.33333e-05. One mil equals 8.33333e-05 feet, and one foot equals 12,000 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Feet formula

Feet = mils × 8.33333e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 8.33333e-05 = 0.0010416667 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 12,000 (or divide by 8.33333e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
