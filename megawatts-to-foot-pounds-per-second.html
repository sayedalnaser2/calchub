Megawatts to Foot-Pounds per Second Converter

Convert megawatts to foot-pounds per second instantly. 1 MW = 737,562.15 ft-lb/s.

How it works

To convert megawatts to foot-pounds per second, multiply the number of megawatts by 737,562.15. One megawatt equals 737,562.15 foot-pounds per second, and one foot-pound per second equals 1.35582e-06 megawatts. The table shows common values.

Megawatts to Foot-Pounds per Second formula

Foot-pounds per second = megawatts × 737,562.15

Worked example: convert 12.5 megawatts to foot-pounds per second. Multiply 12.5 × 737,562.15 = 9,219,526.9 ft-lb/s. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds per second by 1.35582e-06 (or divide by 737,562.15).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
