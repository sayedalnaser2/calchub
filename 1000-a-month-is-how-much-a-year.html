$1,000 a Month Is How Much a Year?

$1,000 a month is $12,000 a year, or $5.77 an hour at 40 hours a week.

How it works

Annual pay is monthly pay times 12. $1,000 x 12 = $12,000. Dividing by 2,080 working hours gives the hourly equivalent.
