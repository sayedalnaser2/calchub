Microseconds to Nanoseconds Converter

Convert microseconds to nanoseconds instantly. 1 µs = 1,000 ns.

How it works

To convert microseconds to nanoseconds, multiply the number of microseconds by 1,000. One microsecond equals 1,000 nanoseconds, and one nanosecond equals 0.001 microseconds. The table shows common values.

Microseconds to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = microseconds × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 microseconds to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
