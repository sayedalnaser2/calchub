How it works

To convert microseconds to nanoseconds, multiply the number of microseconds by 1,000. To convert nanoseconds to microseconds, multiply by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Microseconds to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = microseconds × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 microseconds to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).
