How it works

To convert grains to micrograms, multiply the number of grains by 64,798.91. To convert micrograms to grains, multiply by 1.54324e-05 (or divide by 64,798.91). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Grains to Micrograms formula

Micrograms = grains × 64,798.91

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to micrograms. Multiply 12.5 × 64,798.91 = 809,986.37 µg. To go the other way, multiply micrograms by 1.54324e-05 (or divide by 64,798.91).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
