Milliliters to Teaspoons Converter

Convert milliliters to teaspoons instantly. 1 ml = 0.20288414 tsp.

How it works

To convert milliliters to teaspoons, multiply the number of milliliters by 0.20288414. One milliliter equals 0.20288414 teaspoons, and one teaspoon equals 4.9289216 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Teaspoons formula

Teaspoons = milliliters × 0.20288414

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to teaspoons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.20288414 = 2.5360517 tsp. To go the other way, multiply teaspoons by 4.9289216 (or divide by 0.20288414).
