Centimeters to Mils Converter

Convert centimeters to mils instantly. 1 cm = 393.70079 mil.

How it works

To convert centimeters to mils, multiply the number of centimeters by 393.70079. One centimeter equals 393.70079 mils, and one mil equals 0.00254 centimeters. The table shows common values.

Centimeters to Mils formula

Mils = centimeters × 393.70079

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 393.70079 = 4,921.2598 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 0.00254 (or divide by 393.70079).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
