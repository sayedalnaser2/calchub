Inches to Feet Converter

Convert inches to feet instantly. 1 in = 0.083333333 ft.

How it works

To convert inches to feet, multiply the number of inches by 0.083333333. One inch equals 0.083333333 feet, and one foot equals 12 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Feet formula

Feet = inches × 0.083333333

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 0.083333333 = 1.0416667 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 12 (or divide by 0.083333333).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
