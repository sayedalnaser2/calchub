How it works

To convert mmHg to bar, multiply the number of mmHg by 0.0013332239. One mmHg equals 0.0013332239 bar, and one bar equals 750.06158 mmHg. The table shows common values.

mmHg to Bar formula

Bar = mmHg × 0.0013332239

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013332239 = 0.016665298 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 750.06158 (or divide by 0.0013332239).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
