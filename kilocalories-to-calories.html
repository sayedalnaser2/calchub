Kilocalories to Calories Converter

Convert kilocalories to calories instantly. 1 kcal = 1,000 cal.

How it works

To convert kilocalories to calories, multiply the number of kilocalories by 1,000. One kilocalorie equals 1,000 calories, and one calorie equals 0.001 kilocalories. The table shows common values.

Kilocalories to Calories formula

Calories = kilocalories × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilocalories to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
