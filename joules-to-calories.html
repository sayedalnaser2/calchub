How it works

To convert joules to calories, multiply the number of joules by 0.23900574. To convert calories to joules, multiply by 4.184 (or divide by 0.23900574). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Joules to Calories formula

Calories = joules × 0.23900574

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.23900574 = 2.9875717 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 4.184 (or divide by 0.23900574).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
