How it works

To convert therms to kilojoules, multiply the number of therms by 105,480.4. To convert kilojoules to therms, multiply by 9.48043e-06 (or divide by 105,480.4). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Therms to Kilojoules formula

Kilojoules = therms × 105,480.4

Worked example: convert 12.5 therms to kilojoules. Multiply 12.5 × 105,480.4 = 1,318,505 kJ. To go the other way, multiply kilojoules by 9.48043e-06 (or divide by 105,480.4).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
