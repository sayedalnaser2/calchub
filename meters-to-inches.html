Convert meters to inches instantly. 1 m = 39.370079 in.

How it works

To convert meters to inches, multiply the number of meters by 39.370079. One meter equals 39.370079 inches, and one inch equals 0.0254 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Inches formula

Inches = meters × 39.370079

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 39.370079 = 492.12598 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 0.0254 (or divide by 39.370079).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
