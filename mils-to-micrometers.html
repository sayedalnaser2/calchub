How it works

To convert mils to micrometers, multiply the number of mils by 25.4. To convert micrometers to mils, multiply by 0.039370079 (or divide by 25.4). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Mils to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = mils × 25.4

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 25.4 = 317.5 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 0.039370079 (or divide by 25.4).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
