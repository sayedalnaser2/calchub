Kilometers per Second to Miles per Hour Converter

Convert kilometers per second to miles per hour instantly. 1 km/s = 2,236.9363 mph.

How it works

To convert kilometers per second to miles per hour, multiply the number of kilometers per second by 2,236.9363. One kilometer per second equals 2,236.9363 miles per hour, and one mile per hour equals 0.00044704 kilometers per second. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Second to Miles per Hour formula

Miles per hour = kilometers per second × 2,236.9363

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per second to miles per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 2,236.9363 = 27,961.704 mph. To go the other way, multiply miles per hour by 0.00044704 (or divide by 2,236.9363).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
