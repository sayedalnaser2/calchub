Millimeters to Light Years Converter

Convert millimeters to light years instantly. 1 mm = 1.057e-19 ly.

How it works

To convert millimeters to light years, multiply the number of millimeters by 1.057e-19. One millimeter equals 1.057e-19 light years, and one light year equals 9.46073e+18 millimeters. The table shows common values.

Millimeters to Light Years formula

Light years = millimeters × 1.057e-19

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 1.057e-19 = 1.32125e-18 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 9.46073e+18 (or divide by 1.057e-19).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
