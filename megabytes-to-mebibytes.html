Convert megabytes to mebibytes instantly. 1 MB = 0.95367432 MiB.

How it works

To convert megabytes to mebibytes, multiply the number of megabytes by 0.95367432. One megabyte equals 0.95367432 mebibytes, and one mebibyte equals 1.048576 megabytes. The table shows common values.

Megabytes to Mebibytes formula

Mebibytes = megabytes × 0.95367432

Worked example: convert 12.5 megabytes to mebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.95367432 = 11.920929 MiB. To go the other way, multiply mebibytes by 1.048576 (or divide by 0.95367432).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
