Pints to Tablespoons Converter

Convert pints to tablespoons instantly. 1 pt = 32 tbsp.

How it works

To convert pints to tablespoons, multiply the number of pints by 32. One pint equals 32 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.03125 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = pints × 32

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 32 = 400 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.03125 (or divide by 32).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
