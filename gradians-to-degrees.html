Gradians to Degrees Converter

Convert gradians to degrees instantly. 1 grad = 0.9 °.

How it works

To convert gradians to degrees, multiply the number of gradians by 0.9. One gradian equals 0.9 degrees, and one degree equals 1.1111111 gradians. The table shows common values.

Gradians to Degrees formula

Degrees = gradians × 0.9

Worked example: convert 12.5 gradians to degrees. Multiply 12.5 × 0.9 = 11.25 °. To go the other way, multiply degrees by 1.1111111 (or divide by 0.9).
