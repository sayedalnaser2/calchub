How it works

To convert terabytes to tebibytes, multiply the number of terabytes by 0.9094947. One terabyte equals 0.9094947 tebibytes, and one tebibyte equals 1.0995116 terabytes. The table shows common values.

Terabytes to Tebibytes formula

Tebibytes = terabytes × 0.9094947

Worked example: convert 12.5 terabytes to tebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.9094947 = 11.368684 TiB. To go the other way, multiply tebibytes by 1.0995116 (or divide by 0.9094947).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
