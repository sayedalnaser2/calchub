How it works

To convert milligrams to long tons, multiply the number of milligrams by 9.84207e-10. One milligram equals 9.84207e-10 long tons, and one long ton equals 1,016,046,900 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Long Tons formula

Long tons = milligrams × 9.84207e-10

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
