Foot-Pounds to Kilocalories Converter

Convert foot-pounds to kilocalories instantly. 1 ft-lb = 0.00032404827 kcal.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to kilocalories, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 0.00032404827. One foot-pound equals 0.00032404827 kilocalories, and one kilocalorie equals 3,085.96 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Kilocalories formula

Kilocalories = foot-pounds × 0.00032404827

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to kilocalories. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00032404827 = 0.0040506033 kcal. To go the other way, multiply kilocalories by 3,085.96 (or divide by 0.00032404827).

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
