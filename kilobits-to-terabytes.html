Kilobits to Terabytes Converter

Convert kilobits to terabytes instantly. 1 Kb = 1.25e-10 TB.

How it works

To convert kilobits to terabytes, multiply the number of kilobits by 1.25e-10. One kilobit equals 1.25e-10 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 8,000,000,000 kilobits. The table shows common values.

Kilobits to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = kilobits × 1.25e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobits to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1.25e-10 = 1.5625e-09 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 8,000,000,000 (or divide by 1.25e-10).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
