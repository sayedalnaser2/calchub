Kips to Ounce-Force Converter

Convert kips to ounce-force instantly. 1 kip = 16,000 ozf.

How it works

To convert kips to ounce-force, multiply the number of kips by 16,000. One kip equals 16,000 ounce-force, and one ounce-force equals 6.25e-05 kips. The table shows common values.

Kips to Ounce-Force formula

Ounce-force = kips × 16,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kips to ounce-force. Multiply 12.5 × 16,000 = 200,000 ozf. To go the other way, multiply ounce-force by 6.25e-05 (or divide by 16,000).
