Cups to Gallons Converter

Convert cups to gallons instantly. 1 cup = 0.0625 gal.

How it works

To convert cups to gallons, multiply the number of cups by 0.0625. One cup equals 0.0625 gallons, and one gallon equals 16 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Gallons formula

Gallons = cups × 0.0625

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0625 = 0.78125 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 16 (or divide by 0.0625).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
