How it works

To convert cups to gallons, multiply the number of cups by 0.0625. To convert gallons to cups, multiply by 16 (or divide by 0.0625). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Cups to Gallons formula

Gallons = cups × 0.0625

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0625 = 0.78125 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 16 (or divide by 0.0625).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
