How it works

To convert cubic feet to cups, multiply the number of cubic feet by 119.68831. One cubic foot equals 119.68831 cups, and one cup equals 0.0083550347 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Cups formula

Cups = cubic feet × 119.68831

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to cups. Multiply 12.5 × 119.68831 = 1,496.1039 cup. To go the other way, multiply cups by 0.0083550347 (or divide by 119.68831).
