APY Calculator

Convert an interest rate to APY and see the effect of compounding.

How it works

APY = (1 + r/n)^n - 1. It shows the real annual return including compounding within the year.

Frequently asked questions

APY vs APR?

APR ignores compounding within the year. APY includes it, so it is equal or higher.
