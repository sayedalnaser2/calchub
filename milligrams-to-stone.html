How it works

To convert milligrams to stone, multiply the number of milligrams by 1.57473e-07. One milligram equals 1.57473e-07 stone, and one stone equals 6.35029e+06 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Stone formula

Stone = milligrams × 1.57473e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 1.57473e-07 = 1.96841e-06 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 6.35029e+06 (or divide by 1.57473e-07).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
