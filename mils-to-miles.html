Mils to Miles Converter

Convert mils to miles instantly. 1 mil = 1.57828e-08 mi.

How it works

To convert mils to miles, multiply the number of mils by 1.57828e-08. One mil equals 1.57828e-08 miles, and one mile equals 63,360,000 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Miles formula

Miles = mils × 1.57828e-08

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 1.57828e-08 = 1.97285e-07 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 63,360,000 (or divide by 1.57828e-08).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
