Light Years to Yards Converter

Convert light years to yards instantly. 1 ly = 1.03464e+16 yd.

How it works

To convert light years to yards, multiply the number of light years by 1.03464e+16. One light year equals 1.03464e+16 yards, and one yard equals 9.66522e-17 light years. The table shows common values.

Light Years to Yards formula

Yards = light years × 1.03464e+16

Worked example: convert 12.5 light years to yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1.03464e+16 = 1.2933e+17 yd. To go the other way, multiply yards by 9.66522e-17 (or divide by 1.03464e+16).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
