How it works

To convert fluid ounces to imperial gallons, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 0.0065052671. To convert imperial gallons to fluid ounces, multiply by 153.72159 (or divide by 0.0065052671). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Fluid Ounces to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = fluid ounces × 0.0065052671

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
