How it works

To convert fluid ounces to imperial gallons, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 0.0065052671. One fluid ounce equals 0.0065052671 imperial gallons, and one imperial gallon equals 153.72159 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Imperial Gallons formula

Imperial gallons = fluid ounces × 0.0065052671

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to imperial gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0065052671 = 0.081315838 imp gal. To go the other way, multiply imperial gallons by 153.72159 (or divide by 0.0065052671).
