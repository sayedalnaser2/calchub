Heart Rate Reserve Calculator

Calculate heart rate reserve and target training heart rates.

How it works

Heart rate reserve is max minus resting heart rate. Target = resting + reserve x intensity.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find max heart rate?

A stress test is accurate. Otherwise 220 - age is a rough estimate.
