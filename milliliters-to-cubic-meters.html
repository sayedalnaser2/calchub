Milliliters to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert milliliters to cubic meters instantly. 1 ml = 1e-06 m³.

How it works

To convert milliliters to cubic meters, multiply the number of milliliters by 1e-06. One milliliter equals 1e-06 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 1,000,000 milliliters. The table shows common values.

Milliliters to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = milliliters × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliliters to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
