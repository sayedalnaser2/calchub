Miles per Hour to Meters per Second Converter

Convert miles per hour to meters per second instantly. 1 mph = 0.44704 m/s.

How it works

To convert miles per hour to meters per second, multiply the number of miles per hour by 0.44704. One mile per hour equals 0.44704 meters per second, and one meter per second equals 2.2369363 miles per hour. The table shows common values.

Miles per Hour to Meters per Second formula

Meters per second = miles per hour × 0.44704

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles per hour to meters per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.44704 = 5.588 m/s. To go the other way, multiply meters per second by 2.2369363 (or divide by 0.44704).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
