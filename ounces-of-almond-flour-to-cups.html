Ounces of Almond Flour to Cups

Convert ounces of almond flour to cups. 1 cup of almond flour weighs about 96 g.

How it works

Almond Flour is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 96 g per US cup, convert ounces to cups by multiplying by 0.2953.
