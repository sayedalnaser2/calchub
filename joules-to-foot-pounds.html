Joules to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert joules to foot-pounds instantly. 1 J = 0.73756215 ft-lb.

How it works

To convert joules to foot-pounds, multiply the number of joules by 0.73756215. One joule equals 0.73756215 foot-pounds, and one foot-pound equals 1.3558179 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Foot-Pounds formula

Foot-pounds = joules × 0.73756215

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to foot-pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 0.73756215 = 9.2195269 ft-lb. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds by 1.3558179 (or divide by 0.73756215).
