Carats to Micrograms Converter

Convert carats to micrograms instantly. 1 ct = 200,000 µg.

How it works

To convert carats to micrograms, multiply the number of carats by 200,000. One carat equals 200,000 micrograms, and one microgram equals 5e-06 carats. The table shows common values.

Carats to Micrograms formula

Micrograms = carats × 200,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 carats to micrograms. Multiply 12.5 × 200,000 = 2,500,000 µg. To go the other way, multiply micrograms by 5e-06 (or divide by 200,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
