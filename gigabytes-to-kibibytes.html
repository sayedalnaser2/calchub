Gigabytes to Kibibytes Converter

Convert gigabytes to kibibytes instantly. 1 GB = 976,562.5 KiB.

How it works

To convert gigabytes to kibibytes, multiply the number of gigabytes by 976,562.5. One gigabyte equals 976,562.5 kibibytes, and one kibibyte equals 1.024e-06 gigabytes. The table shows common values.

Gigabytes to Kibibytes formula

Kibibytes = gigabytes × 976,562.5

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabytes to kibibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 976,562.5 = 12,207,031 KiB. To go the other way, multiply kibibytes by 1.024e-06 (or divide by 976,562.5).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
