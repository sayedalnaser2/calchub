Foot-Pounds per Second to BTU per Hour Converter

Convert foot-pounds per second to BTU per hour instantly. 1 ft-lb/s = 4.6262429 BTU/h.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds per second to BTU per hour, multiply the number of foot-pounds per second by 4.6262429. One foot-pound per second equals 4.6262429 BTU per hour, and one BTU per hour equals 0.21615813 foot-pounds per second. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds per Second to BTU per Hour formula

Btu per hour = foot-pounds per second × 4.6262429

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds per second to BTU per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 4.6262429 = 57.828036 BTU/h. To go the other way, multiply BTU per hour by 0.21615813 (or divide by 4.6262429).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
