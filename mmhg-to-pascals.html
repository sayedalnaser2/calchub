Convert mmHg to pascals instantly. 1 mmHg = 133.32239 Pa.

How it works

To convert mmHg to pascals, multiply the number of mmHg by 133.32239. One mmHg equals 133.32239 pascals, and one pascal equals 0.0075006158 mmHg. The table shows common values.

mmHg to Pascals formula

Pascals = mmHg × 133.32239

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to pascals. Multiply 12.5 × 133.32239 = 1,666.5298 Pa. To go the other way, multiply pascals by 0.0075006158 (or divide by 133.32239).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
