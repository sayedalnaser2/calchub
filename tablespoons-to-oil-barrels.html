How it works

To convert tablespoons to oil barrels, multiply the number of tablespoons by 9.3006e-05. One tablespoon equals 9.3006e-05 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 10,752 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = tablespoons × 9.3006e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to oil barrels. Multiply 12.5 × 9.3006e-05 = 0.0011625744 bbl. To go the other way, multiply oil barrels by 10,752 (or divide by 9.3006e-05).
