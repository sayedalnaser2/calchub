Margin to Markup Converter

Convert between profit margin and markup percentages.

How it works

Markup is profit divided by cost; margin is profit divided by price. Margin = markup / (1 + markup) and markup = margin / (1 - margin).

Frequently asked questions

Why is margin always lower than markup?

Because margin divides profit by the larger selling price rather than the cost.

What markup gives a 50% margin?

100% markup (the selling price is double the cost).
