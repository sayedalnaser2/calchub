Half Marathon Pace for a 3:10:00 Finish Time

To run a Half Marathon in 3:10:00 you need 14:30 per mile (9:00 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A Half Marathon is 21.0975 km (13.11 miles), so 3:10:00 works out to 14:30 per mile or 9:00 per kilometer.
