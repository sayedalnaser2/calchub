Minutes to Seconds Converter

Convert minutes to seconds instantly. 1 min = 60 s.

How it works

To convert minutes to seconds, multiply the number of minutes by 60. One minute equals 60 seconds, and one second equals 0.016666667 minutes. The table shows common values.

Minutes to Seconds formula

Seconds = minutes × 60

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 60 = 750 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 0.016666667 (or divide by 60).
