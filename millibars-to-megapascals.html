Millibars to Megapascals Converter

Convert millibars to megapascals instantly. 1 mbar = 0.0001 MPa.

How it works

To convert millibars to megapascals, multiply the number of millibars by 0.0001. One millibar equals 0.0001 megapascals, and one megapascal equals 10,000 millibars. The table shows common values.

Millibars to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = millibars × 0.0001

Worked example: convert 12.5 millibars to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0001 = 0.00125 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 10,000 (or divide by 0.0001).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
