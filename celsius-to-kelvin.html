About temperature scales

Celsius and Kelvin share the same step size, while a Fahrenheit degree is 5/9 as large. Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F, 273.15 K) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F, 373.15 K) at sea level.

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula for Celsius to Kelvin?

K = °C + 273.15.

What is the formula for Kelvin to Celsius?

°C = K − 273.15.

What temperature is the same in Celsius and Fahrenheit?

-40 degrees is the same in both scales.
