Teaspoons of Whole Almonds to Grams

Convert teaspoons of whole almonds to grams. 1 cup of whole almonds weighs about 142 g.

How it works

Whole Almonds is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 142 g per US cup, convert teaspoons to grams by multiplying by 2.958.

1 US cup of whole almonds weighs about 142 g (5.0 oz), 1 tablespoon about 8.9 g and 1 teaspoon about 3.0 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
