Reverse Sales Tax Calculator

Find the pre-tax price and tax from a total that includes sales tax.

How it works

Pre-tax price = total / (1 + tax rate). The tax is the total minus the pre-tax price.

Frequently asked questions

Why not just subtract the percentage?

Because the tax was charged on the pre-tax price, not on the total.
