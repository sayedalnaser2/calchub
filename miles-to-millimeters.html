Miles to Millimeters Converter

Convert miles to millimeters instantly. 1 mi = 1,609,344 mm.

How it works

To convert miles to millimeters, multiply the number of miles by 1,609,344. One mile equals 1,609,344 millimeters, and one millimeter equals 6.21371e-07 miles. The table shows common values.

Miles to Millimeters formula

Millimeters = miles × 1,609,344

Worked example: convert 12.5 miles to millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,609,344 = 20,116,800 mm. To go the other way, multiply millimeters by 6.21371e-07 (or divide by 1,609,344).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
