Days to Seconds Converter

Convert days to seconds instantly. 1 day = 86,400 s.

How it works

To convert days to seconds, multiply the number of days by 86,400. One day equals 86,400 seconds, and one second equals 1.15741e-05 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Seconds formula

Seconds = days × 86,400

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 86,400 = 1,080,000 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 1.15741e-05 (or divide by 86,400).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
