Kilobytes to Petabytes Converter

Convert kilobytes to petabytes instantly. 1 KB = 1e-12 PB.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to petabytes, multiply the number of kilobytes by 1e-12. One kilobyte equals 1e-12 petabytes, and one petabyte equals 1,000,000,000,000 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Petabytes formula

Petabytes = kilobytes × 1e-12

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to petabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-12 = 1.25e-11 PB. To go the other way, multiply petabytes by 1,000,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-12).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
