How it works

To convert psi to millibars, multiply the number of psi by 68.947573. To convert millibars to psi, multiply by 0.014503774 (or divide by 68.947573). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

PSI to Millibars formula

Millibars = psi × 68.947573

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
