PSI to Millibars Converter

Convert psi to millibars instantly. 1 psi = 68.947573 mbar.

How it works

To convert psi to millibars, multiply the number of psi by 68.947573. One psi equals 68.947573 millibars, and one millibar equals 0.014503774 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Millibars formula

Millibars = psi × 68.947573

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to millibars. Multiply 12.5 × 68.947573 = 861.84466 mbar. To go the other way, multiply millibars by 0.014503774 (or divide by 68.947573).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
