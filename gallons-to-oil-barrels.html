Gallons to Oil Barrels Converter

Convert gallons to oil barrels instantly. 1 gal = 0.023809524 bbl.

How it works

To convert gallons to oil barrels, multiply the number of gallons by 0.023809524. One gallon equals 0.023809524 oil barrels, and one oil barrel equals 42 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Oil Barrels formula

Oil barrels = gallons × 0.023809524

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to oil barrels. Multiply 12.5 × 0.023809524 = 0.29761905 bbl. To go the other way, multiply oil barrels by 42 (or divide by 0.023809524).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
