Square Miles to Square Inches Converter

Convert square miles to square inches instantly. 1 mi² = 4,014,489,600 in².

How it works

To convert square miles to square inches, multiply the number of square miles by 4,014,489,600. One square mile equals 4,014,489,600 square inches, and one square inch equals 2.49098e-10 square miles. The table shows common values.

Square Miles to Square Inches formula

Square inches = square miles × 4,014,489,600

Worked example: convert 12.5 square miles to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 4,014,489,600 = 50,181,120,000 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 2.49098e-10 (or divide by 4,014,489,600).
