Kilobytes to Gigabytes Converter

Convert kilobytes to gigabytes instantly. 1 KB = 1e-06 GB.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to gigabytes, multiply the number of kilobytes by 1e-06. One kilobyte equals 1e-06 gigabytes, and one gigabyte equals 1e+06 kilobytes. The table shows common values.

Kilobytes to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = kilobytes × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 1e+06 (or divide by 1e-06).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
