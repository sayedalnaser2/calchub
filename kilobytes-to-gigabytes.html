Convert kilobytes to gigabytes and gigabytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 KB = 1e-06 GB.

How it works

To convert kilobytes to gigabytes, multiply the number of kilobytes by 1e-06. To convert gigabytes to kilobytes, multiply by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilobytes to Gigabytes formula

Gigabytes = kilobytes × 1e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobytes to gigabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-06 = 1.25e-05 GB. To go the other way, multiply gigabytes by 1,000,000 (or divide by 1e-06).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
