Closing Cost Calculator

Estimate buyer closing costs on a home purchase.

How it works

Buyer closing costs, including lender fees, title insurance, appraisal, prepaid taxes and escrow, commonly run about 2% to 5% of the purchase price. Actual costs depend on location and lender.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays closing costs?

Buyers and sellers both pay some. Sellers also pay agent commissions.

Can closing costs be negotiated?

Often partly. Ask lenders for a Loan Estimate and compare.
