Kilobits to Megabytes Converter

Convert kilobits to megabytes instantly. 1 Kb = 0.000125 MB.

How it works

To convert kilobits to megabytes, multiply the number of kilobits by 0.000125. One kilobit equals 0.000125 megabytes, and one megabyte equals 8,000 kilobits. The table shows common values.

Kilobits to Megabytes formula

Megabytes = kilobits × 0.000125

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilobits to megabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.000125 = 0.0015625 MB. To go the other way, multiply megabytes by 8,000 (or divide by 0.000125).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
