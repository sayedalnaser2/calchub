How it works

To convert imperial quarts to tablespoons, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 76.860795. One imperial quart equals 76.860795 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.013010534 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = imperial quarts × 76.860795

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 76.860795 = 960.75994 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.013010534 (or divide by 76.860795).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
