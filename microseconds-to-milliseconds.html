Microseconds to Milliseconds Converter

Convert microseconds to milliseconds instantly. 1 µs = 0.001 ms.

How it works

To convert microseconds to milliseconds, multiply the number of microseconds by 0.001. One microsecond equals 0.001 milliseconds, and one millisecond equals 1,000 microseconds. The table shows common values.

Microseconds to Milliseconds formula

Milliseconds = microseconds × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 microseconds to milliseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 ms. To go the other way, multiply milliseconds by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).
