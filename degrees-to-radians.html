How it works

To convert degrees to radians, multiply the number of degrees by 0.017453293. To convert radians to degrees, multiply by 57.29578 (or divide by 0.017453293). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Degrees to Radians formula

Radians = degrees × 0.017453293

Worked example: convert 12.5 degrees to radians. Multiply 12.5 × 0.017453293 = 0.21816616 rad. To go the other way, multiply radians by 57.29578 (or divide by 0.017453293).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
