Degrees to Radians Converter

Convert degrees to radians instantly. 1 ° = 0.017453293 rad.

How it works

To convert degrees to radians, multiply the number of degrees by 0.017453293. One degree equals 0.017453293 radians, and one radian equals 57.29578 degrees. The table shows common values.

Degrees to Radians formula

Radians = degrees × 0.017453293

Worked example: convert 12.5 degrees to radians. Multiply 12.5 × 0.017453293 = 0.21816616 rad. To go the other way, multiply radians by 57.29578 (or divide by 0.017453293).
