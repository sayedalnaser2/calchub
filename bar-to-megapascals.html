Bar to Megapascals Converter

Convert bar to megapascals instantly. 1 bar = 0.1 MPa.

How it works

To convert bar to megapascals, multiply the number of bar by 0.1. One bar equals 0.1 megapascals, and one megapascal equals 10 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to Megapascals formula

Megapascals = bar × 0.1

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to megapascals. Multiply 12.5 × 0.1 = 1.25 MPa. To go the other way, multiply megapascals by 10 (or divide by 0.1).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
