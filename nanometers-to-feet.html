Nanometers to Feet Converter

Convert nanometers to feet instantly. 1 nm = 3.28084e-09 ft.

How it works

To convert nanometers to feet, multiply the number of nanometers by 3.28084e-09. One nanometer equals 3.28084e-09 feet, and one foot equals 304,800,000 nanometers. The table shows common values.

Nanometers to Feet formula

Feet = nanometers × 3.28084e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanometers to feet. Multiply 12.5 × 3.28084e-09 = 4.10105e-08 ft. To go the other way, multiply feet by 304,800,000 (or divide by 3.28084e-09).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
