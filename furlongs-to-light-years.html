Furlongs to Light Years Converter

Convert furlongs to light years instantly. 1 furlongs = 2.12635e-14 ly.

How it works

To convert furlongs to light years, multiply the number of furlongs by 2.12635e-14. One furlong equals 2.12635e-14 light years, and one light year equals 47,029,003,000,000 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Light Years formula

Light years = furlongs × 2.12635e-14

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to light years. Multiply 12.5 × 2.12635e-14 = 2.65793e-13 ly. To go the other way, multiply light years by 47,029,003,000,000 (or divide by 2.12635e-14).

About length units
