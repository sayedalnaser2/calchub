Petabytes to Terabytes Converter

Convert petabytes to terabytes instantly. 1 PB = 1,000 TB.

How it works

To convert petabytes to terabytes, multiply the number of petabytes by 1,000. One petabyte equals 1,000 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 0.001 petabytes. The table shows common values.

Petabytes to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = petabytes × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 petabytes to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
