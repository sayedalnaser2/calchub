How it works

To convert mmHg to inches of mercury, multiply the number of mmHg by 0.039370075. One mmHg equals 0.039370075 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 25.400003 mmHg. The table shows common values.

mmHg to Inches Of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = mmHg × 0.039370075

Worked example: convert 12.5 mmHg to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 0.039370075 = 0.49212593 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 25.400003 (or divide by 0.039370075).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
