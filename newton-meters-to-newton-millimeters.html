Newton Meters to Newton Millimeters Converter

Convert newton meters to newton millimeters instantly. 1 N·m = 1,000 N·mm.

How it works

To convert newton meters to newton millimeters, multiply the number of newton meters by 1,000. One newton meter equals 1,000 newton millimeters, and one newton millimeter equals 0.001 newton meters. The table shows common values.

Newton Meters to Newton Millimeters formula

Newton millimeters = newton meters × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 newton meters to newton millimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 N·mm. To go the other way, multiply newton millimeters by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
