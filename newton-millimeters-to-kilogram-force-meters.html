Newton Millimeters to Kilogram-Force Meters Converter

Convert newton millimeters to kilogram-force meters instantly. 1 N·mm = 0.00010197162 kgf·m.

How it works

To convert newton millimeters to kilogram-force meters, multiply the number of newton millimeters by 0.00010197162. One newton millimeter equals 0.00010197162 kilogram-force meters, and one kilogram-force meter equals 9,806.65 newton millimeters. The table shows common values.

Newton Millimeters to Kilogram-Force Meters formula

Kilogram-force meters = newton millimeters × 0.00010197162

Worked example: convert 12.5 newton millimeters to kilogram-force meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00010197162 = 0.0012746453 kgf·m. To go the other way, multiply kilogram-force meters by 9,806.65 (or divide by 0.00010197162).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
