Seconds to Days Converter

Convert seconds to days instantly. 1 s = 1.15741e-05 days.

How it works

To convert seconds to days, multiply the number of seconds by 1.15741e-05. One second equals 1.15741e-05 days, and one day equals 86400 seconds. The table shows common values.

Seconds to Days formula

Days = seconds × 1.15741e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to days. Multiply 12.5 × 1.15741e-05 = 0.00014467593 days. To go the other way, multiply days by 86400 (or divide by 1.15741e-05).
