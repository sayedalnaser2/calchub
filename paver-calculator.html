Estimate pavers and base material for a patio.

How it works

Pavers = area / paver area plus waste. Base gravel and bedding sand are calculated from the area and depth.

Frequently asked questions

How thick should the base be?

4-6 inches of compacted gravel for patios; 8-12 inches for driveways.
