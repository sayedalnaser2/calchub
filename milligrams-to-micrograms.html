Milligrams to Micrograms Converter

Convert milligrams to micrograms instantly. 1 mg = 1,000 µg.

How it works

To convert milligrams to micrograms, multiply the number of milligrams by 1,000. One milligram equals 1,000 micrograms, and one microgram equals 0.001 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Micrograms formula

Micrograms = milligrams × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to micrograms. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 µg. To go the other way, multiply micrograms by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
