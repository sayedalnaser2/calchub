Degrees to Gradians Converter

Convert degrees to gradians instantly. 1 ° = 1.1111111 grad.

How it works

To convert degrees to gradians, multiply the number of degrees by 1.1111111. One degree equals 1.1111111 gradians, and one gradian equals 0.9 degrees. The table shows common values.

Degrees to Gradians formula

Gradians = degrees × 1.1111111

Worked example: convert 12.5 degrees to gradians. Multiply 12.5 × 1.1111111 = 13.888889 grad. To go the other way, multiply gradians by 0.9 (or divide by 1.1111111).

About angle units

Angles can be measured in degrees (360 per full turn), radians (2π per full turn) or gradians (400 per full turn).
