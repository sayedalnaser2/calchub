How it works

To convert newton meters to kilogram-force meters, multiply the number of newton meters by 0.10197162. To convert kilogram-force meters to newton meters, multiply by 9.80665 (or divide by 0.10197162). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Newton Meters to Kilogram-Force Meters formula

Kilogram-force meters = newton meters × 0.10197162

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
