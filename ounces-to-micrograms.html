How it works

To convert ounces to micrograms, multiply the number of ounces by 28,349,523. To convert micrograms to ounces, multiply by 3.5274e-08 (or divide by 28,349,523). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Ounces to Micrograms formula

Micrograms = ounces × 28,349,523

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounces to micrograms. Multiply 12.5 × 28,349,523 = 354,369,040 µg. To go the other way, multiply micrograms by 3.5274e-08 (or divide by 28,349,523).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
