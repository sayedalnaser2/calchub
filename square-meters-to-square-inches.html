How it works

To convert square meters to square inches, multiply the number of square meters by 1550.0031. One square meter equals 1550.0031 square inches, and one square inch equals 0.00064516 square meters. The table shows common values.

Square Meters to Square Inches formula

Square inches = square meters × 1550.0031

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 1550.0031 = 19375.039 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 0.00064516 (or divide by 1550.0031).
