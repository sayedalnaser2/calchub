Arcseconds to Radians Converter

Convert arcseconds to radians instantly. 1 arcsec = 4.84814e-06 rad.

How it works

To convert arcseconds to radians, multiply the number of arcseconds by 4.84814e-06. One arcsecond equals 4.84814e-06 radians, and one radian equals 206,264.81 arcseconds. The table shows common values.

Arcseconds to Radians formula

Radians = arcseconds × 4.84814e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcseconds to radians. Multiply 12.5 × 4.84814e-06 = 6.06017e-05 rad. To go the other way, multiply radians by 206,264.81 (or divide by 4.84814e-06).
