Kilowatts to Foot-Pounds per Second Converter

Convert kilowatts to foot-pounds per second instantly. 1 kW = 737.56215 ft-lb/s.

How it works

To convert kilowatts to foot-pounds per second, multiply the number of kilowatts by 737.56215. One kilowatt equals 737.56215 foot-pounds per second, and one foot-pound per second equals 0.0013558179 kilowatts. The table shows common values.

Kilowatts to Foot-Pounds per Second formula

Foot-pounds per second = kilowatts × 737.56215

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatts to foot-pounds per second. Multiply 12.5 × 737.56215 = 9,219.5269 ft-lb/s. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds per second by 0.0013558179 (or divide by 737.56215).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
