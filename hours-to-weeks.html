Hours to Weeks Converter

Convert hours to weeks instantly. 1 h = 0.005952381 weeks.

How it works

To convert hours to weeks, multiply the number of hours by 0.005952381. One hour equals 0.005952381 weeks, and one week equals 168 hours. The table shows common values.

Hours to Weeks formula

Weeks = hours × 0.005952381

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to weeks. Multiply 12.5 × 0.005952381 = 0.074404762 weeks. To go the other way, multiply weeks by 168 (or divide by 0.005952381).
