Millimeters to Meters Converter

Convert millimeters to meters instantly. 1 mm = 0.001 m.

How it works

To convert millimeters to meters, multiply the number of millimeters by 0.001. One millimeter equals 0.001 meters, and one meter equals 1,000 millimeters. The table shows common values.

Millimeters to Meters formula

Meters = millimeters × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 millimeters to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
