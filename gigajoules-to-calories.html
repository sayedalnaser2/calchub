Gigajoules to Calories Converter

Convert gigajoules to calories instantly. 1 GJ = 239,005,740 cal.

How it works

To convert gigajoules to calories, multiply the number of gigajoules by 239,005,740. One gigajoule equals 239,005,740 calories, and one calorie equals 4.184e-09 gigajoules. The table shows common values.

Gigajoules to Calories formula

Calories = gigajoules × 239,005,740

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigajoules to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 239,005,740 = 2,987,571,700 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 4.184e-09 (or divide by 239,005,740).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
