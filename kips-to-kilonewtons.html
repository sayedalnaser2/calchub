How it works

To convert kips to kilonewtons, multiply the number of kips by 4.4482216. To convert kilonewtons to kips, multiply by 0.22480894 (or divide by 4.4482216). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kips to Kilonewtons formula

Kilonewtons = kips × 4.4482216
