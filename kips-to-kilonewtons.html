Kips to Kilonewtons Converter

Convert kips to kilonewtons instantly. 1 kip = 4.4482216 kN.

How it works

To convert kips to kilonewtons, multiply the number of kips by 4.4482216. One kip equals 4.4482216 kilonewtons, and one kilonewton equals 0.22480894 kips. The table shows common values.

Kips to Kilonewtons formula

Kilonewtons = kips × 4.4482216

Worked example: convert 12.5 kips to kilonewtons. Multiply 12.5 × 4.4482216 = 55.60277 kN. To go the other way, multiply kilonewtons by 0.22480894 (or divide by 4.4482216).
