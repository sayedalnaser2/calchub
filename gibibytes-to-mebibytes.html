Gibibytes to Mebibytes Converter

Convert gibibytes to mebibytes instantly. 1 GiB = 1,024 MiB.

How it works

To convert gibibytes to mebibytes, multiply the number of gibibytes by 1,024. One gibibyte equals 1,024 mebibytes, and one mebibyte equals 0.0009765625 gibibytes. The table shows common values.

Gibibytes to Mebibytes formula

Mebibytes = gibibytes × 1,024

Worked example: convert 12.5 gibibytes to mebibytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,024 = 12,800 MiB. To go the other way, multiply mebibytes by 0.0009765625 (or divide by 1,024).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
