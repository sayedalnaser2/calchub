10K Pace for a 96:00 Finish Time

To run a 10K in 96:00 you need 15:27 per mile (9:36 per km). See split times.

How it works

Pace = finish time / distance. A 10K is 10 km (6.21 miles), so 96:00 works out to 15:27 per mile or 9:36 per kilometer.
