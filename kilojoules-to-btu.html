Kilojoules to BTU Converter

Convert kilojoules to BTU instantly. 1 kJ = 0.94781712 BTU.

How it works

To convert kilojoules to BTU, multiply the number of kilojoules by 0.94781712. One kilojoule equals 0.94781712 BTU, and one BTU equals 1.0550559 kilojoules. The table shows common values.

Kilojoules to BTU formula

Btu = kilojoules × 0.94781712

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilojoules to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 0.94781712 = 11.847714 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 1.0550559 (or divide by 0.94781712).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
