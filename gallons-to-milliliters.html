Convert gallons to milliliters instantly. 1 gal = 3785.4118 ml.

How it works

To convert gallons to milliliters, multiply the number of gallons by 3785.4118. One gallon equals 3785.4118 milliliters, and one milliliter equals 0.00026417205 gallons. The table shows common values.

Gallons to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = gallons × 3785.4118

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 3785.4118 = 47317.647 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.00026417205 (or divide by 3785.4118).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
