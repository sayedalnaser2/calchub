How it works

To convert gallons to milliliters, multiply the number of gallons by 3,785.4118. To convert milliliters to gallons, multiply by 0.00026417205 (or divide by 3,785.4118). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Gallons to Milliliters formula

Milliliters = gallons × 3,785.4118

Worked example: convert 12.5 gallons to milliliters. Multiply 12.5 × 3,785.4118 = 47,317.647 ml. To go the other way, multiply milliliters by 0.00026417205 (or divide by 3,785.4118).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
