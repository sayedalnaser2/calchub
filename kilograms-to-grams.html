Kilograms to Grams Converter

Convert kilograms to grams instantly. 1 kg = 1,000 g.

How it works

To convert kilograms to grams, multiply the number of kilograms by 1,000. One kilogram equals 1,000 grams, and one gram equals 0.001 kilograms. The table shows common values.

Kilograms to Grams formula

Grams = kilograms × 1,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilograms to grams. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000 = 12,500 g. To go the other way, multiply grams by 0.001 (or divide by 1,000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
