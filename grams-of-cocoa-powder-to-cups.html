Grams of Cocoa Powder to Cups

Convert grams of cocoa powder to cups. 1 cup of cocoa powder weighs about 84 g.

How it works

Cocoa Powder is measured by weight or by volume depending on the recipe. Using about 84 g per US cup, convert grams to cups by multiplying by 0.0119.

1 US cup of cocoa powder weighs about 84 g (3.0 oz), 1 tablespoon about 5.2 g and 1 teaspoon about 1.8 g. Weights vary with brand, moisture and how the ingredient is scooped or packed, so use a kitchen scale for baking. Values use the US cup of 236.6 mL.
