About fuel economy units

Fuel economy is reported as distance per volume of fuel (mpg, km/L) in the US, UK and much of Asia, and as volume per distance (L/100 km) in Europe, Canada and Australia. Higher is better for distance-per-volume units, lower is better for L/100 km. US and UK gallons differ (3.785 L vs 4.546 L), so mpg values are not interchangeable.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30 km/L in mpg UK?

30 km/L is about 84.74 mpg UK.

Which fuel economy unit is best?

None is better; they measure the same thing. Use whichever your region's vehicles and fuel prices are quoted in.
