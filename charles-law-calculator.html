Charles's Law Calculator

Calculate how gas volume changes with temperature at constant pressure.

How it works

Charles's law: V1 / T1 = V2 / T2 at constant pressure, with temperature in kelvin. Add 273.15 to Celsius to get kelvin.

Frequently asked questions

Why use kelvin?

Volume is proportional to absolute temperature, not Celsius.

What happens at 0 K?

The ideal gas model predicts zero volume, but real gases liquefy first.
