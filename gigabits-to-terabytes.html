Gigabits to Terabytes Converter

Convert gigabits to terabytes instantly. 1 Gb = 0.000125 TB.

How it works

To convert gigabits to terabytes, multiply the number of gigabits by 0.000125. One gigabit equals 0.000125 terabytes, and one terabyte equals 8,000 gigabits. The table shows common values.

Gigabits to Terabytes formula

Terabytes = gigabits × 0.000125

Worked example: convert 12.5 gigabits to terabytes. Multiply 12.5 × 0.000125 = 0.0015625 TB. To go the other way, multiply terabytes by 8,000 (or divide by 0.000125).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
