Meters to Mils Converter

Convert meters to mils instantly. 1 m = 39,370.079 mil.

How it works

To convert meters to mils, multiply the number of meters by 39,370.079. One meter equals 39,370.079 mils, and one mil equals 2.54e-05 meters. The table shows common values.

Meters to Mils formula

Mils = meters × 39,370.079

Worked example: convert 12.5 meters to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 39,370.079 = 492,125.98 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 2.54e-05 (or divide by 39,370.079).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
