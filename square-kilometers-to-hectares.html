Square Kilometers to Hectares Converter

Convert square kilometers to hectares instantly. 1 km² = 100 ha.

How it works

To convert square kilometers to hectares, multiply the number of square kilometers by 100. One square kilometer equals 100 hectares, and one hectare equals 0.01 square kilometers. The table shows common values.

Square Kilometers to Hectares formula

Hectares = square kilometers × 100

Worked example: convert 12.5 square kilometers to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 100 = 1,250 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 0.01 (or divide by 100).

About area units

Area measures a flat surface. The square meter is the SI unit; square feet and acres are widely used for property, flooring and land.
