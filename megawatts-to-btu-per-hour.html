Megawatts to BTU per Hour Converter

Convert megawatts to BTU per hour instantly. 1 MW = 3,412,141.6 BTU/h.

How it works

To convert megawatts to BTU per hour, multiply the number of megawatts by 3,412,141.6. One megawatt equals 3,412,141.6 BTU per hour, and one BTU per hour equals 2.93071e-07 megawatts. The table shows common values.

Megawatts to BTU per Hour formula

Btu per hour = megawatts × 3,412,141.6

Worked example: convert 12.5 megawatts to BTU per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 3,412,141.6 = 42,651,770 BTU/h. To go the other way, multiply BTU per hour by 2.93071e-07 (or divide by 3,412,141.6).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
