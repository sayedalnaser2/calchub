How it works

To convert milligrams to pounds, multiply the number of milligrams by 2.20462e-06. One milligram equals 2.20462e-06 pounds, and one pound equals 453592.37 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Pounds formula

Pounds = milligrams × 2.20462e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to pounds. Multiply 12.5 × 2.20462e-06 = 2.75578e-05 lb. To go the other way, multiply pounds by 453592.37 (or divide by 2.20462e-06).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
