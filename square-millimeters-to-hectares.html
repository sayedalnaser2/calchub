Square Millimeters to Hectares Converter

Convert square millimeters to hectares instantly. 1 mm² = 1e-10 ha.

How it works

To convert square millimeters to hectares, multiply the number of square millimeters by 1e-10. One square millimeter equals 1e-10 hectares, and one hectare equals 10,000,000,000 square millimeters. The table shows common values.

Square Millimeters to Hectares formula

Hectares = square millimeters × 1e-10

Worked example: convert 12.5 square millimeters to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-10 = 1.25e-09 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 10,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-10).
