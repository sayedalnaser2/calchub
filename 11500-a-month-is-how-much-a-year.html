$11,500 a Month Is How Much a Year?

$11,500 a month is $138,000 a year, or $66.35 an hour at 40 hours a week.

How it works

Annual pay is monthly pay times 12. $11,500 x 12 = $138,000. Dividing by 2,080 working hours gives the hourly equivalent.
