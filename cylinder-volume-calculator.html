Cylinder Volume and Surface Area Calculator

Calculate volume, lateral and total surface area of a cylinder.

How it works

Volume = pi x r^2 x h. Lateral area = 2 x pi x r x h. Total surface area adds two circular bases: 2 x pi x r x (r + h).

Frequently asked questions

How many gallons does a cylinder hold?

Compute the volume in cubic inches and divide by 231.

What if I know the diameter?

Halve it to get the radius.
