Convert megajoules to therms instantly. 1 MJ = 0.0094804343 therms.

How it works

To convert megajoules to therms, multiply the number of megajoules by 0.0094804343. One megajoule equals 0.0094804343 therms, and one therm equals 105.4804 megajoules. The table shows common values.

Megajoules to Therms formula

Therms = megajoules × 0.0094804343

Worked example: convert 12.5 megajoules to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0094804343 = 0.11850543 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 105.4804 (or divide by 0.0094804343).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
