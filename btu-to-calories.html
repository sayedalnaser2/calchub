How it works

To convert BTU to calories, multiply the number of BTU by 252.1644. To convert calories to BTU, multiply by 0.0039656668 (or divide by 252.1644). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

BTU to Calories formula

Calories = BTU × 252.1644

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU to calories. Multiply 12.5 × 252.1644 = 3,152.055 cal. To go the other way, multiply calories by 0.0039656668 (or divide by 252.1644).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
