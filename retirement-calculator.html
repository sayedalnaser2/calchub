Retirement Savings Calculator

Project your retirement savings and the income they could support.

How it works

Your current savings and each monthly contribution compound until retirement. The 4% rule suggests withdrawing 4% of the balance in the first year of retirement as a sustainable starting income.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 4% rule reliable?

It is a rule of thumb based on historical US data for a 30-year retirement. Your own timeline, taxes and market conditions may differ.

Is the result adjusted for inflation?

No. Use a return rate after inflation (for example 4-5% instead of 7%) to see results in today's dollars.
