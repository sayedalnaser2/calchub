Ounce-Inches to Pound-Inches Converter

Convert ounce-inches to pound-inches instantly. 1 oz-in = 0.0625 lb-in.

How it works

To convert ounce-inches to pound-inches, multiply the number of ounce-inches by 0.0625. One ounce-inch equals 0.0625 pound-inches, and one pound-inch equals 16 ounce-inches. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Inches to Pound-Inches formula

Pound-inches = ounce-inches × 0.0625

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounce-inches to pound-inches. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0625 = 0.78124999 lb-in. To go the other way, multiply pound-inches by 16 (or divide by 0.0625).

About torque units

Torque is rotational force. The newton meter is the SI unit; pound-feet are common for engines and wheel nuts, and pound-inches or ounce-inches for small fasteners and motors.
