How it works

To convert kilowatts to metric horsepower, multiply the number of kilowatts by 1.3596216. To convert metric horsepower to kilowatts, multiply by 0.73549875 (or divide by 1.3596216). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilowatts to Metric Horsepower formula

Metric horsepower = kilowatts × 1.3596216

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatts to metric horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 1.3596216 = 16.99527 PS. To go the other way, multiply metric horsepower by 0.73549875 (or divide by 1.3596216).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
