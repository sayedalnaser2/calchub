How loan payments work

Each payment covers interest on the remaining balance plus part of the principal. Early payments are mostly interest; later ones are mostly principal. A shorter term or lower rate cuts the total interest you pay.

Frequently asked questions

How can I lower my loan cost?

Improve your credit score before applying, compare lenders, choose a shorter term if you can afford the payment, and make extra payments toward principal.

What is APR?

The annual percentage rate is the yearly cost of borrowing, including interest and some fees.
