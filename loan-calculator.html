Loan Calculator

Calculate the monthly payment and total interest on any fixed-rate loan.

How it works

Each payment covers interest on the remaining balance plus part of the principal. Early payments are mostly interest and later ones mostly principal. A shorter term or lower rate reduces the total interest.

Frequently asked questions

How can I lower my loan cost?

Improve your credit score before applying, compare lenders, choose a shorter term if you can afford it, and make extra principal payments.

What is APR?

The annual percentage rate is the yearly cost of borrowing, including interest and some fees.
