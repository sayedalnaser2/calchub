Tax Refund Calculator

Estimate whether you will get a federal refund or owe tax.

How it works

Total tax is computed from income, the standard deduction and brackets, less credits. Comparing it to withholding gives a refund or balance due. Figures use 2025 US federal rules and are estimates only.

Frequently asked questions

Should I aim for a big refund?

A refund is an interest-free loan to the government. Adjust your W-4 if you prefer larger paychecks.
