IRR Calculator

Find the internal rate of return of a series of cash flows.

How it works

IRR is the discount rate that makes NPV equal zero. It is the annualized return implied by the cash flows.

Frequently asked questions

Is a higher IRR always better?

Not necessarily. Compare with project size, risk and your required return.
