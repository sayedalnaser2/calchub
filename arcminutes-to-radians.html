Arcminutes to Radians Converter

Convert arcminutes to radians instantly. 1 arcmin = 0.00029088821 rad.

How it works

To convert arcminutes to radians, multiply the number of arcminutes by 0.00029088821. One arcminute equals 0.00029088821 radians, and one radian equals 3,437.7468 arcminutes. The table shows common values.

Arcminutes to Radians formula

Radians = arcminutes × 0.00029088821

Worked example: convert 12.5 arcminutes to radians. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00029088821 = 0.0036361026 rad. To go the other way, multiply radians by 3,437.7468 (or divide by 0.00029088821).
