Square Miles to Square Feet Converter

Convert square miles to square feet instantly. 1 mi² = 27,878,400 ft².

How it works

To convert square miles to square feet, multiply the number of square miles by 27,878,400. One square mile equals 27,878,400 square feet, and one square foot equals 3.58701e-08 square miles. The table shows common values.

Square Miles to Square Feet formula

Square feet = square miles × 27,878,400

Worked example: convert 12.5 square miles to square feet. Multiply 12.5 × 27,878,400 = 348,480,000 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 3.58701e-08 (or divide by 27,878,400).
