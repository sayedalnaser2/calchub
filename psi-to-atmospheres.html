PSI to Atmospheres Converter

Convert psi to atmospheres instantly. 1 psi = 0.068045964 atm.

How it works

To convert psi to atmospheres, multiply the number of psi by 0.068045964. One psi equals 0.068045964 atmospheres, and one atmosphere equals 14.695949 psi. The table shows common values.

PSI to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = psi × 0.068045964

Worked example: convert 12.5 psi to atmospheres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.068045964 = 0.85057455 atm. To go the other way, multiply atmospheres by 14.695949 (or divide by 0.068045964).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
