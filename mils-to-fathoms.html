Mils to Fathoms Converter

Convert mils to fathoms instantly. 1 mil = 1.38889e-05 fathoms.

How it works

To convert mils to fathoms, multiply the number of mils by 1.38889e-05. One mil equals 1.38889e-05 fathoms, and one fathom equals 72,000 mils. The table shows common values.

Mils to Fathoms formula

Fathoms = mils × 1.38889e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to fathoms. Multiply 12.5 × 1.38889e-05 = 0.00017361111 fathoms. To go the other way, multiply fathoms by 72,000 (or divide by 1.38889e-05).
