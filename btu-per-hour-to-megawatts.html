BTU per Hour to Megawatts Converter

Convert BTU per hour to megawatts instantly. 1 BTU/h = 2.93071e-07 MW.

How it works

To convert BTU per hour to megawatts, multiply the number of BTU per hour by 2.93071e-07. One BTU per hour equals 2.93071e-07 megawatts, and one megawatt equals 3,412,141.6 BTU per hour. The table shows common values.

BTU per Hour to Megawatts formula

Megawatts = BTU per hour × 2.93071e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 BTU per hour to megawatts. Multiply 12.5 × 2.93071e-07 = 3.66339e-06 MW. To go the other way, multiply megawatts by 3,412,141.6 (or divide by 2.93071e-07).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
