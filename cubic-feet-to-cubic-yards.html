How it works

To convert cubic feet to cubic yards, multiply the number of cubic feet by 0.037037037. One cubic foot equals 0.037037037 cubic yards, and one cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Cubic Yards formula

Cubic yards = cubic feet × 0.037037037

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to cubic yards. Multiply 12.5 × 0.037037037 = 0.46296296 yd³. To go the other way, multiply cubic yards by 27 (or divide by 0.037037037).
