How it works

To convert grains to kilograms, multiply the number of grains by 6.47989e-05. To convert kilograms to grains, multiply by 15,432.358 (or divide by 6.47989e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Grains to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = grains × 6.47989e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 6.47989e-05 = 0.00080998637 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 15,432.358 (or divide by 6.47989e-05).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
