Liters to Cubic Meters Converter

Convert liters to cubic meters instantly. 1 L = 0.001 m³.

How it works

To convert liters to cubic meters, multiply the number of liters by 0.001. One liter equals 0.001 cubic meters, and one cubic meter equals 1,000 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Cubic Meters formula

Cubic meters = liters × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to cubic meters. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 m³. To go the other way, multiply cubic meters by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
