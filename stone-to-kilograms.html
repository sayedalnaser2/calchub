How it works

To convert stone to kilograms, multiply the number of stone by 6.3502932. One stone equals 6.3502932 kilograms, and one kilogram equals 0.15747304 stone. The table shows common values.

Stone to Kilograms formula

Kilograms = stone × 6.3502932

Worked example: convert 12.5 stone to kilograms. Multiply 12.5 × 6.3502932 = 79.378665 kg. To go the other way, multiply kilograms by 0.15747304 (or divide by 6.3502932).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
