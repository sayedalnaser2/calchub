How it works

To convert kilohertz to megahertz, multiply the number of kilohertz by 0.001. To convert megahertz to kilohertz, multiply by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilohertz to Megahertz formula

Megahertz = kilohertz × 0.001

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilohertz to megahertz. Multiply 12.5 × 0.001 = 0.0125 MHz. To go the other way, multiply megahertz by 1,000 (or divide by 0.001).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
