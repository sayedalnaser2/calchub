Convert metric horsepower to BTU per hour instantly. 1 PS = 2,509.6259 BTU/h.

How it works

To convert metric horsepower to BTU per hour, multiply the number of metric horsepower by 2,509.6259. One metric horsepower equals 2,509.6259 BTU per hour, and one BTU per hour equals 0.00039846576 metric horsepower. The table shows common values.

Metric Horsepower to BTU per Hour formula

Btu per hour = metric horsepower × 2,509.6259

Worked example: convert 12.5 metric horsepower to BTU per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 2,509.6259 = 31,370.324 BTU/h. To go the other way, multiply BTU per hour by 0.00039846576 (or divide by 2,509.6259).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
