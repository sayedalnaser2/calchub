Terabytes to Kilobytes Converter

Convert terabytes to kilobytes instantly. 1 TB = 1,000,000,000 KB.

How it works

To convert terabytes to kilobytes, multiply the number of terabytes by 1,000,000,000. One terabyte equals 1,000,000,000 kilobytes, and one kilobyte equals 1e-09 terabytes. The table shows common values.

Terabytes to Kilobytes formula

Kilobytes = terabytes × 1,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 terabytes to kilobytes. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 12,500,000,000 KB. To go the other way, multiply kilobytes by 1e-09 (or divide by 1,000,000,000).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
