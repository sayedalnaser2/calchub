How it works

To convert kilonewtons to kips, multiply the number of kilonewtons by 0.22480894. One kilonewton equals 0.22480894 kips, and one kip equals 4.4482216 kilonewtons. The table shows common values.

Kilonewtons to Kips formula

Kips = kilonewtons × 0.22480894

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilonewtons to kips. Multiply 12.5 × 0.22480894 = 2.8101118 kip. To go the other way, multiply kips by 4.4482216 (or divide by 0.22480894).
