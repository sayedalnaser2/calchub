Cups to Fluid Ounces Converter

Convert cups to fluid ounces instantly. 1 cup = 8 fl oz.

How it works

To convert cups to fluid ounces, multiply the number of cups by 8. One cup equals 8 fluid ounces, and one fluid ounce equals 0.125 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Fluid Ounces formula

Fluid ounces = cups × 8

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to fluid ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 8 = 100 fl oz. To go the other way, multiply fluid ounces by 0.125 (or divide by 8).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
