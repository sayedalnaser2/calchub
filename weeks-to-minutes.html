Weeks to Minutes Converter

Convert weeks to minutes instantly. 1 weeks = 10080 min.

How it works

To convert weeks to minutes, multiply the number of weeks by 10080. One week equals 10080 minutes, and one minute equals 9.92063e-05 weeks. The table shows common values.

Weeks to Minutes formula

Minutes = weeks × 10080

Worked example: convert 12.5 weeks to minutes. Multiply 12.5 × 10080 = 126000 min. To go the other way, multiply minutes by 9.92063e-05 (or divide by 10080).
