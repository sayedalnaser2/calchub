How it works

To convert kilometers per hour to mach, multiply the number of kilometers per hour by 0.00081629721. One kilometer per hour equals 0.00081629721 mach, and one Mach equals 1,225.044 kilometers per hour. The table shows common values.

Kilometers per Hour to Mach formula

Mach = kilometers per hour × 0.00081629721

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilometers per hour to mach. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00081629721 = 0.010203715 Mach. To go the other way, multiply mach by 1,225.044 (or divide by 0.00081629721).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
