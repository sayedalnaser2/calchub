Cubic Feet to Liters Converter

Convert cubic feet to liters instantly. 1 ft³ = 28.316847 L.

How it works

To convert cubic feet to liters, multiply the number of cubic feet by 28.316847. One cubic foot equals 28.316847 liters, and one liter equals 0.035314667 cubic feet. The table shows common values.

Cubic Feet to Liters formula

Liters = cubic feet × 28.316847

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic feet to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 28.316847 = 353.96058 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.035314667 (or divide by 28.316847).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
