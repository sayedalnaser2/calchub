Micrometers to Kilometers Converter

Convert micrometers to kilometers instantly. 1 µm = 1e-09 km.

How it works

To convert micrometers to kilometers, multiply the number of micrometers by 1e-09. One micrometer equals 1e-09 kilometers, and one kilometer equals 1,000,000,000 micrometers. The table shows common values.

Micrometers to Kilometers formula

Kilometers = micrometers × 1e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 micrometers to kilometers. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-09 = 1.25e-08 km. To go the other way, multiply kilometers by 1,000,000,000 (or divide by 1e-09).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
