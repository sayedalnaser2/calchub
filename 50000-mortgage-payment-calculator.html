$50,000 Mortgage Payment Calculator

Monthly payment on a $50,000 mortgage at any rate and term, including taxes and insurance.

How it works

Monthly principal and interest comes from the standard amortization formula P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), with r the monthly rate and n the number of payments. A $50,000 loan at 6.5% for 30 years costs $316.03 a month before taxes and insurance.
