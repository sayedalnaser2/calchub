How it works

To convert yards to miles, multiply the number of yards by 0.00056818182. To convert miles to yards, multiply by 1,760 (or divide by 0.00056818182). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Yards to Miles formula

Miles = yards × 0.00056818182

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00056818182 = 0.0071022727 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1,760 (or divide by 0.00056818182).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
