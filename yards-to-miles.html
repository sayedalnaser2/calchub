Yards to Miles Converter

Convert yards to miles instantly. 1 yd = 0.00056818182 mi.

How it works

To convert yards to miles, multiply the number of yards by 0.00056818182. One yard equals 0.00056818182 miles, and one mile equals 1760 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Miles formula

Miles = yards × 0.00056818182

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00056818182 = 0.0071022727 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 1760 (or divide by 0.00056818182).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
