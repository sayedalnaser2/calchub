Milliwatts to Foot-Pounds per Second Converter

Convert milliwatts to foot-pounds per second instantly. 1 mW = 0.00073756215 ft-lb/s.

How it works

To convert milliwatts to foot-pounds per second, multiply the number of milliwatts by 0.00073756215. One milliwatt equals 0.00073756215 foot-pounds per second, and one foot-pound per second equals 1,355.8179 milliwatts. The table shows common values.

Milliwatts to Foot-Pounds per Second formula

Foot-pounds per second = milliwatts × 0.00073756215

Worked example: convert 12.5 milliwatts to foot-pounds per second. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00073756215 = 0.0092195269 ft-lb/s. To go the other way, multiply foot-pounds per second by 1,355.8179 (or divide by 0.00073756215).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
