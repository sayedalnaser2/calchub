How it works

To convert kilowatts to milliwatts, multiply the number of kilowatts by 1,000,000. To convert milliwatts to kilowatts, multiply by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilowatts to Milliwatts formula

Milliwatts = kilowatts × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilowatts to milliwatts. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 mW. To go the other way, multiply milliwatts by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
