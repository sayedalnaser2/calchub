Screen Size and PPI Calculator

Calculate pixel density and physical dimensions from resolution and diagonal size.

How it works

Pixel density (PPI) is the diagonal in pixels divided by the diagonal in inches. Physical width and height follow from the diagonal and the aspect ratio of the resolution.

Frequently asked questions

What PPI looks sharp?

Around 100-110 PPI is fine for desktop monitors at arm's length, and phones usually exceed 400 PPI.

Does a bigger screen lower PPI?

Yes, at the same resolution a larger diagonal spreads the pixels out.
