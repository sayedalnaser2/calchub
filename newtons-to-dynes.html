Newtons to Dynes Converter

Convert newtons to dynes instantly. 1 N = 100,000 dyn.

How it works

To convert newtons to dynes, multiply the number of newtons by 100,000. One newton equals 100,000 dynes, and one dyne equals 1e-05 newtons. The table shows common values.

Newtons to Dynes formula

Dynes = newtons × 100,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 newtons to dynes. Multiply 12.5 × 100,000 = 1,250,000 dyn. To go the other way, multiply dynes by 1e-05 (or divide by 100,000).

About force units

Force is measured in newtons in the SI system. Pound-force and kilogram-force are common in engineering and everyday weight contexts.
