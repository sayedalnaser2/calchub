How it works

To convert mils to meters, multiply the number of mils by 2.54e-05. To convert meters to mils, multiply by 39,370.079 (or divide by 2.54e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Mils to Meters formula

Meters = mils × 2.54e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 mils to meters. Multiply 12.5 × 2.54e-05 = 0.0003175 m. To go the other way, multiply meters by 39,370.079 (or divide by 2.54e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
