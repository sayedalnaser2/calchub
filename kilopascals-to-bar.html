Kilopascals to Bar Converter

Convert kilopascals to bar instantly. 1 kPa = 0.01 bar.

How it works

To convert kilopascals to bar, multiply the number of kilopascals by 0.01. One kilopascal equals 0.01 bar, and one bar equals 100 kilopascals. The table shows common values.

Kilopascals to Bar formula

Bar = kilopascals × 0.01

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilopascals to bar. Multiply 12.5 × 0.01 = 0.125 bar. To go the other way, multiply bar by 100 (or divide by 0.01).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
