Days to Hours Converter

Convert days to hours instantly. 1 day = 24 h.

How it works

To convert days to hours, multiply the number of days by 24. One day equals 24 hours, and one hour equals 0.041666667 days. The table shows common values.

Days to Hours formula

Hours = days × 24

Worked example: convert 12.5 days to hours. Multiply 12.5 × 24 = 300 h. To go the other way, multiply hours by 0.041666667 (or divide by 24).

About time units

Time is measured in seconds in the SI system. Months and years here use average lengths (a year is 365.2425 days), so results for them are averages.
