Cubic Inches to Tablespoons Converter

Convert cubic inches to tablespoons instantly. 1 in³ = 1.1082251 tbsp.

How it works

To convert cubic inches to tablespoons, multiply the number of cubic inches by 1.1082251. One cubic inch equals 1.1082251 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.90234375 cubic inches. The table shows common values.

Cubic Inches to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = cubic inches × 1.1082251

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic inches to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 1.1082251 = 13.852814 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.90234375 (or divide by 1.1082251).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
