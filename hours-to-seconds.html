Hours to Seconds Converter

Convert hours to seconds instantly. 1 h = 3600 s.

How it works

To convert hours to seconds, multiply the number of hours by 3600. One hour equals 3600 seconds, and one second equals 0.00027777778 hours. The table shows common values.

Hours to Seconds formula

Seconds = hours × 3600

Worked example: convert 12.5 hours to seconds. Multiply 12.5 × 3600 = 45000 s. To go the other way, multiply seconds by 0.00027777778 (or divide by 3600).
