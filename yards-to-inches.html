Yards to Inches Converter

Convert yards to inches instantly. 1 yd = 36 in.

How it works

To convert yards to inches, multiply the number of yards by 36. One yard equals 36 inches, and one inch equals 0.027777778 yards. The table shows common values.

Yards to Inches formula

Inches = yards × 36

Worked example: convert 12.5 yards to inches. Multiply 12.5 × 36 = 450 in. To go the other way, multiply inches by 0.027777778 (or divide by 36).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
