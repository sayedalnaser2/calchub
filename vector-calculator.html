Vector Calculator (3D)

Compute dot product, cross product, magnitudes and angle between two 3D vectors.

How it works

Dot product = Σ aᵢbᵢ. The cross product gives a vector perpendicular to both. The angle comes from cos θ = A·B / (|A||B|).

Frequently asked questions

What does a zero dot product mean?

The vectors are perpendicular.
