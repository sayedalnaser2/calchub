Knots to Kilometers per Hour Converter

Convert knots to kilometers per hour instantly. 1 kn = 1.852 km/h.

How it works

To convert knots to kilometers per hour, multiply the number of knots by 1.852. One knot equals 1.852 kilometers per hour, and one kilometer per hour equals 0.5399568 knots. The table shows common values.

Knots to Kilometers per Hour formula

Kilometers per hour = knots × 1.852

Worked example: convert 12.5 knots to kilometers per hour. Multiply 12.5 × 1.852 = 23.15 km/h. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per hour by 0.5399568 (or divide by 1.852).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
