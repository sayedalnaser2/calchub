Megahertz to Hertz Converter

Convert megahertz to hertz instantly. 1 MHz = 1,000,000 Hz.

How it works

To convert megahertz to hertz, multiply the number of megahertz by 1,000,000. One megahertz equals 1,000,000 hertz, and one hertz equals 1e-06 megahertz. The table shows common values.

Megahertz to Hertz formula

Hertz = megahertz × 1,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 megahertz to hertz. Multiply 12.5 × 1,000,000 = 12,500,000 Hz. To go the other way, multiply hertz by 1e-06 (or divide by 1,000,000).

About frequency units

Frequency counts cycles per second. The hertz is the SI unit; rpm counts rotations per minute and is used for engines, motors and fans.
