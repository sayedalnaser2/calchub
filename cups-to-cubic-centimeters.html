How it works

To convert cups to cubic centimeters, multiply the number of cups by 236.58824. One cup equals 236.58824 cubic centimeters, and one cubic centimeter equals 0.0042267528 cups. The table shows common values.

Cups to Cubic Centimeters formula

Cubic centimeters = cups × 236.58824

Worked example: convert 12.5 cups to cubic centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 236.58824 = 2,957.353 cm³. To go the other way, multiply cubic centimeters by 0.0042267528 (or divide by 236.58824).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
