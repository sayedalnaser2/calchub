Convert tablespoons to imperial quarts instantly. 1 tbsp = 0.013010534 imp qt.

How it works

To convert tablespoons to imperial quarts, multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.013010534. One tablespoon equals 0.013010534 imperial quarts, and one imperial quart equals 76.860795 tablespoons. The table shows common values.

Tablespoons to Imperial Quarts formula

Imperial quarts = tablespoons × 0.013010534

Worked example: convert 12.5 tablespoons to imperial quarts. Multiply 12.5 × 0.013010534 = 0.16263168 imp qt. To go the other way, multiply imperial quarts by 76.860795 (or divide by 0.013010534).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
