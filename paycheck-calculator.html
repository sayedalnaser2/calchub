Paycheck Calculator

Estimate your take-home pay after federal tax, Social Security, Medicare and state tax.

How it works

Taxable income is gross pay minus pre-tax retirement contributions and the standard deduction. Federal tax uses 2025 brackets; Social Security is 6.2% up to the wage base and Medicare 1.45%. Figures use 2025 US federal rules and are estimates only.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my real paycheck different?

Health insurance, local taxes, other deductions and withholding choices all change it.

Does this include credits?

No. Tax credits such as the child tax credit are not included.
