Ounce-Force to Kilonewtons Converter

Convert ounce-force to kilonewtons instantly. 1 ozf = 0.00027801385 kN.

How it works

To convert ounce-force to kilonewtons, multiply the number of ounce-force by 0.00027801385. One ounce-force equals 0.00027801385 kilonewtons, and one kilonewton equals 3,596.9431 ounce-force. The table shows common values.

Ounce-Force to Kilonewtons formula

Kilonewtons = ounce-force × 0.00027801385

Worked example: convert 12.5 ounce-force to kilonewtons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00027801385 = 0.0034751731 kN. To go the other way, multiply kilonewtons by 3,596.9431 (or divide by 0.00027801385).
