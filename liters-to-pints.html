Liters to Pints Converter

Convert liters to pints instantly. 1 L = 2.1133764 pt.

How it works

To convert liters to pints, multiply the number of liters by 2.1133764. One liter equals 2.1133764 pints, and one pint equals 0.47317647 liters. The table shows common values.

Liters to Pints formula

Pints = liters × 2.1133764

Worked example: convert 12.5 liters to pints. Multiply 12.5 × 2.1133764 = 26.417205 pt. To go the other way, multiply pints by 0.47317647 (or divide by 2.1133764).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
