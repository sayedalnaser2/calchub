Inches to Miles Converter

Convert inches to miles instantly. 1 in = 1.57828e-05 mi.

How it works

To convert inches to miles, multiply the number of inches by 1.57828e-05. One inch equals 1.57828e-05 miles, and one mile equals 63,360 inches. The table shows common values.

Inches to Miles formula

Miles = inches × 1.57828e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 inches to miles. Multiply 12.5 × 1.57828e-05 = 0.00019728535 mi. To go the other way, multiply miles by 63,360 (or divide by 1.57828e-05).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
