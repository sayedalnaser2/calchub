Square Meters to Square Miles Converter

Convert square meters to square miles instantly. 1 m² = 3.86102e-07 mi².

How it works

To convert square meters to square miles, multiply the number of square meters by 3.86102e-07. One square meter equals 3.86102e-07 square miles, and one square mile equals 2,589,988.1 square meters. The table shows common values.

Square Meters to Square Miles formula

Square miles = square meters × 3.86102e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 3.86102e-07 = 4.82628e-06 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 2,589,988.1 (or divide by 3.86102e-07).
