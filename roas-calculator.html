ROAS Calculator (Return on Ad Spend)

Calculate return on ad spend, ad ROI and the break-even ROAS for your margin.

How it works

ROAS = revenue from ads / ad spend. Because ads must be paid for out of gross profit, the break-even ROAS is 1 / gross margin.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROAS?

It depends on margins; 4x is a common target for e-commerce but break-even is 1/margin.

ROAS vs ROI?

ROAS ignores costs of goods; ROI accounts for profit.
