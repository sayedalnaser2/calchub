How it works

To convert quarts to cubic inches, multiply the number of quarts by 57.75. To convert cubic inches to quarts, multiply by 0.017316017 (or divide by 57.75). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Quarts to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = quarts × 57.75

Worked example: convert 12.5 quarts to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 57.75 = 721.875 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.017316017 (or divide by 57.75).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
