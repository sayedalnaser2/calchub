Joules to Therms Converter

Convert joules to therms instantly. 1 J = 9.48043e-09 therms.

How it works

To convert joules to therms, multiply the number of joules by 9.48043e-09. One joule equals 9.48043e-09 therms, and one therm equals 105,480,400 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to Therms formula

Therms = joules × 9.48043e-09

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to therms. Multiply 12.5 × 9.48043e-09 = 1.18505e-07 therms. To go the other way, multiply therms by 105,480,400 (or divide by 9.48043e-09).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
