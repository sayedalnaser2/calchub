Grains to Stone Converter

Convert grains to stone instantly. 1 gr = 1.02041e-05 st.

How it works

To convert grains to stone, multiply the number of grains by 1.02041e-05. One grain equals 1.02041e-05 stone, and one stone equals 98,000 grains. The table shows common values.

Grains to Stone formula

Stone = grains × 1.02041e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 grains to stone. Multiply 12.5 × 1.02041e-05 = 0.00012755102 st. To go the other way, multiply stone by 98,000 (or divide by 1.02041e-05).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
