Cubic Centimeters to Tablespoons Converter

Convert cubic centimeters to tablespoons instantly. 1 cm³ = 0.067628045 tbsp.

How it works

To convert cubic centimeters to tablespoons, multiply the number of cubic centimeters by 0.067628045. One cubic centimeter equals 0.067628045 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 14.786765 cubic centimeters. The table shows common values.

Cubic Centimeters to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = cubic centimeters × 0.067628045

Worked example: convert 12.5 cubic centimeters to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.067628045 = 0.84535057 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 14.786765 (or divide by 0.067628045).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
