Convert acres to hectares instantly. 1 acre = 0.40468564 ha.

How it works

To convert acres to hectares, multiply the number of acres by 0.40468564. One acre equals 0.40468564 hectares, and one hectare equals 2.4710538 acres. The table shows common values.

Acres to Hectares formula

Hectares = acres × 0.40468564

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 0.40468564 = 5.0585705 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 2.4710538 (or divide by 0.40468564).
