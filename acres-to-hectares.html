How it works

To convert acres to hectares, multiply the number of acres by 0.40468564. To convert hectares to acres, multiply by 2.4710538 (or divide by 0.40468564). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Acres to Hectares formula

Hectares = acres × 0.40468564

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to hectares. Multiply 12.5 × 0.40468564 = 5.0585705 ha. To go the other way, multiply hectares by 2.4710538 (or divide by 0.40468564).
