Megapascals to Atmospheres Converter

Convert megapascals to atmospheres instantly. 1 MPa = 9.8692327 atm.

How it works

To convert megapascals to atmospheres, multiply the number of megapascals by 9.8692327. One megapascal equals 9.8692327 atmospheres, and one atmosphere equals 0.101325 megapascals. The table shows common values.

Megapascals to Atmospheres formula

Atmospheres = megapascals × 9.8692327

Worked example: convert 12.5 megapascals to atmospheres. Multiply 12.5 × 9.8692327 = 123.36541 atm. To go the other way, multiply atmospheres by 0.101325 (or divide by 9.8692327).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
