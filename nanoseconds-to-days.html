Nanoseconds to Days Converter

Convert nanoseconds to days instantly. 1 ns = 1.15741e-14 days.

How it works

To convert nanoseconds to days, multiply the number of nanoseconds by 1.15741e-14. One nanosecond equals 1.15741e-14 days, and one day equals 86,400,000,000,000 nanoseconds. The table shows common values.

Nanoseconds to Days formula

Days = nanoseconds × 1.15741e-14

Worked example: convert 12.5 nanoseconds to days. Multiply 12.5 × 1.15741e-14 = 1.44676e-13 days. To go the other way, multiply days by 86,400,000,000,000 (or divide by 1.15741e-14).
