Joules to BTU Converter

Convert joules to BTU instantly. 1 J = 0.00094781712 BTU.

How it works

To convert joules to BTU, multiply the number of joules by 0.00094781712. One joule equals 0.00094781712 BTU, and one BTU equals 1055.0559 joules. The table shows common values.

Joules to BTU formula

Btu = joules × 0.00094781712

Worked example: convert 12.5 joules to BTU. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00094781712 = 0.011847714 BTU. To go the other way, multiply BTU by 1055.0559 (or divide by 0.00094781712).

About energy units

Energy is measured in joules in the SI system. Calories describe food energy, kilowatt hours appear on electricity bills, and BTU is used for heating and cooling.
