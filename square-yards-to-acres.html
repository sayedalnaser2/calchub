How it works

To convert square yards to acres, multiply the number of square yards by 0.00020661157. To convert acres to square yards, multiply by 4,840 (or divide by 0.00020661157). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Yards to Acres formula

Acres = square yards × 0.00020661157

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to acres. Multiply 12.5 × 0.00020661157 = 0.0025826446 acres. To go the other way, multiply acres by 4,840 (or divide by 0.00020661157).
