Convert furlongs to mils instantly. 1 furlongs = 7,920,000 mil.

How it works

To convert furlongs to mils, multiply the number of furlongs by 7,920,000. One furlong equals 7,920,000 mils, and one mil equals 1.26263e-07 furlongs. The table shows common values.

Furlongs to Mils formula

Mils = furlongs × 7,920,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to mils. Multiply 12.5 × 7,920,000 = 99,000,000 mil. To go the other way, multiply mils by 1.26263e-07 (or divide by 7,920,000).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
