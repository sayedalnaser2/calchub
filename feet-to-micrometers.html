How it works

To convert feet to micrometers, multiply the number of feet by 304,800. To convert micrometers to feet, multiply by 3.28084e-06 (or divide by 304,800). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Feet to Micrometers formula

Micrometers = feet × 304,800

Worked example: convert 12.5 feet to micrometers. Multiply 12.5 × 304,800 = 3,810,000 µm. To go the other way, multiply micrometers by 3.28084e-06 (or divide by 304,800).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
