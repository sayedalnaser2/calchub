How it works

To convert kilonewtons to dynes, multiply the number of kilonewtons by 100,000,000. To convert dynes to kilonewtons, multiply by 1e-08 (or divide by 100,000,000). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Kilonewtons to Dynes formula

Dynes = kilonewtons × 100,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilonewtons to dynes. Multiply 12.5 × 100,000,000 = 1,250,000,000 dyn. To go the other way, multiply dynes by 1e-08 (or divide by 100,000,000).

About force units

Force is measured in newtons in the SI system. Pound-force and kilogram-force are common in engineering and everyday weight contexts.
