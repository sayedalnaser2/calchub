Kilonewtons to Dynes Converter

Convert kilonewtons to dynes instantly. 1 kN = 100,000,000 dyn.

How it works

To convert kilonewtons to dynes, multiply the number of kilonewtons by 100,000,000. One kilonewton equals 100,000,000 dynes, and one dyne equals 1e-08 kilonewtons. The table shows common values.

Kilonewtons to Dynes formula

Dynes = kilonewtons × 100,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 kilonewtons to dynes. Multiply 12.5 × 100,000,000 = 1,250,000,000 dyn. To go the other way, multiply dynes by 1e-08 (or divide by 100,000,000).
