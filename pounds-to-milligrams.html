Pounds to Milligrams Converter

Convert pounds to milligrams instantly. 1 lb = 453,592.37 mg.

How it works

To convert pounds to milligrams, multiply the number of pounds by 453,592.37. One pound equals 453,592.37 milligrams, and one milligram equals 2.20462e-06 pounds. The table shows common values.

Pounds to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = pounds × 453,592.37

Worked example: convert 12.5 pounds to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 453,592.37 = 5,669,904.6 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 2.20462e-06 (or divide by 453,592.37).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
