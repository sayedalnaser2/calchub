How it works

To convert pints to gallons, multiply the number of pints by 0.125. To convert gallons to pints, multiply by 8 (or divide by 0.125). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Pints to Gallons formula

Gallons = pints × 0.125

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.125 = 1.5625 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 8 (or divide by 0.125).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
