Convert square meters to square yards instantly. 1 m² = 1.19599 yd².

How it works

To convert square meters to square yards, multiply the number of square meters by 1.19599. One square meter equals 1.19599 square yards, and one square yard equals 0.83612736 square meters. The table shows common values.

Square Meters to Square Yards formula

Square yards = square meters × 1.19599

Worked example: convert 12.5 square meters to square yards. Multiply 12.5 × 1.19599 = 14.949876 yd². To go the other way, multiply square yards by 0.83612736 (or divide by 1.19599).
