Imperial Quarts to Liters Converter

Convert imperial quarts to liters instantly. 1 imp qt = 1.1365225 L.

How it works

To convert imperial quarts to liters, multiply the number of imperial quarts by 1.1365225. One imperial quart equals 1.1365225 liters, and one liter equals 0.87987699 imperial quarts. The table shows common values.

Imperial Quarts to Liters formula

Liters = imperial quarts × 1.1365225

Worked example: convert 12.5 imperial quarts to liters. Multiply 12.5 × 1.1365225 = 14.206531 L. To go the other way, multiply liters by 0.87987699 (or divide by 1.1365225).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
