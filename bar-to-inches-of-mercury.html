Bar to Inches of Mercury Converter

Convert bar to inches of mercury instantly. 1 bar = 29.52998 inHg.

How it works

To convert bar to inches of mercury, multiply the number of bar by 29.52998. One bar equals 29.52998 inches of mercury, and one inch of mercury equals 0.03386389 bar. The table shows common values.

Bar to Inches of Mercury formula

Inches of mercury = bar × 29.52998

Worked example: convert 12.5 bar to inches of mercury. Multiply 12.5 × 29.52998 = 369.12475 inHg. To go the other way, multiply inches of mercury by 0.03386389 (or divide by 29.52998).

About pressure units

Pressure is force per unit area. The pascal is the SI unit; psi is common for tires and plumbing, bar and atm in weather and engineering, and mmHg in medicine.
