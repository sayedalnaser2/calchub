Teaspoons to Gallons Converter

Convert teaspoons to gallons instantly. 1 tsp = 0.0013020833 gal.

How it works

To convert teaspoons to gallons, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.0013020833. One teaspoon equals 0.0013020833 gallons, and one gallon equals 768 teaspoons. The table shows common values.

Teaspoons to Gallons formula

Gallons = teaspoons × 0.0013020833

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013020833 = 0.016276042 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 768 (or divide by 0.0013020833).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
