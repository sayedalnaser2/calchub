How it works

To convert teaspoons to gallons, multiply the number of teaspoons by 0.0013020833. To convert gallons to teaspoons, multiply by 768 (or divide by 0.0013020833). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Teaspoons to Gallons formula

Gallons = teaspoons × 0.0013020833

Worked example: convert 12.5 teaspoons to gallons. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0013020833 = 0.016276042 gal. To go the other way, multiply gallons by 768 (or divide by 0.0013020833).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
