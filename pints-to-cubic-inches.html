Pints to Cubic Inches Converter

Convert pints to cubic inches instantly. 1 pt = 28.875 in³.

How it works

To convert pints to cubic inches, multiply the number of pints by 28.875. One pint equals 28.875 cubic inches, and one cubic inch equals 0.034632035 pints. The table shows common values.

Pints to Cubic Inches formula

Cubic inches = pints × 28.875

Worked example: convert 12.5 pints to cubic inches. Multiply 12.5 × 28.875 = 360.9375 in³. To go the other way, multiply cubic inches by 0.034632035 (or divide by 28.875).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
