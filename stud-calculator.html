Wall Stud Calculator

Estimate the number of studs for a framed wall.

How it works

Studs = wall length in inches / spacing + 1, plus extra for corners and each opening's king, jack and header studs.

Frequently asked questions

16 or 24 inch spacing?

16 in is standard for load-bearing walls; 24 in can work with advanced framing.
