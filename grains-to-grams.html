How it works

To convert grains to grams, multiply the number of grains by 0.06479891. To convert grams to grains, multiply by 15.432358 (or divide by 0.06479891). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Grains to Grams formula

Grams = grains × 0.06479891

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
