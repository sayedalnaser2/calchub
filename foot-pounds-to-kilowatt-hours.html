Foot-Pounds to Kilowatt Hours Converter

Convert foot-pounds to kilowatt hours instantly. 1 ft-lb = 3.76616e-07 kWh.

How it works

To convert foot-pounds to kilowatt hours, multiply the number of foot-pounds by 3.76616e-07. One foot-pound equals 3.76616e-07 kilowatt hours, and one kilowatt hour equals 2,655,223.7 foot-pounds. The table shows common values.

Foot-Pounds to Kilowatt Hours formula

Kilowatt hours = foot-pounds × 3.76616e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 foot-pounds to kilowatt hours. Multiply 12.5 × 3.76616e-07 = 4.7077e-06 kWh. To go the other way, multiply kilowatt hours by 2,655,223.7 (or divide by 3.76616e-07).
