How it works

To convert acres to square miles, multiply the number of acres by 0.0015625. To convert square miles to acres, multiply by 640 (or divide by 0.0015625). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Acres to Square Miles formula

Square miles = acres × 0.0015625

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0015625 = 0.01953125 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 640 (or divide by 0.0015625).
