Acres to Square Miles Converter

Convert acres to square miles instantly. 1 acres = 0.0015625 mi².

How it works

To convert acres to square miles, multiply the number of acres by 0.0015625. One acre equals 0.0015625 square miles, and one square mile equals 640 acres. The table shows common values.

Acres to Square Miles formula

Square miles = acres × 0.0015625

Worked example: convert 12.5 acres to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 0.0015625 = 0.01953125 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 640 (or divide by 0.0015625).
