Square Yards to Square Inches Converter

Convert square yards to square inches instantly. 1 yd² = 1,296 in².

How it works

To convert square yards to square inches, multiply the number of square yards by 1,296. One square yard equals 1,296 square inches, and one square inch equals 0.00077160494 square yards. The table shows common values.

Square Yards to Square Inches formula

Square inches = square yards × 1,296

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to square inches. Multiply 12.5 × 1,296 = 16,200 in². To go the other way, multiply square inches by 0.00077160494 (or divide by 1,296).
