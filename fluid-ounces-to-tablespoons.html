Fluid Ounces to Tablespoons Converter

Convert fluid ounces to tablespoons instantly. 1 fl oz = 2 tbsp.

How it works

To convert fluid ounces to tablespoons, multiply the number of fluid ounces by 2. One fluid ounce equals 2 tablespoons, and one tablespoon equals 0.5 fluid ounces. The table shows common values.

Fluid Ounces to Tablespoons formula

Tablespoons = fluid ounces × 2

Worked example: convert 12.5 fluid ounces to tablespoons. Multiply 12.5 × 2 = 25 tbsp. To go the other way, multiply tablespoons by 0.5 (or divide by 2).

About volume units

Volume measures how much space a liquid or container holds. The liter is the common metric unit; gallons, quarts, pints, cups and spoons here are US customary units, which differ from imperial ones.
