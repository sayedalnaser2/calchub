How it works

To convert furlongs to centimeters, multiply the number of furlongs by 20,116.8. To convert centimeters to furlongs, multiply by 4.97097e-05 (or divide by 20,116.8). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Furlongs to Centimeters formula

Centimeters = furlongs × 20,116.8

Worked example: convert 12.5 furlongs to centimeters. Multiply 12.5 × 20,116.8 = 251,460 cm. To go the other way, multiply centimeters by 4.97097e-05 (or divide by 20,116.8).

About length units

Length measures the distance between two points. The meter is the SI base unit; inches, feet, yards and miles are US customary and imperial units, all defined exactly in terms of the meter (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly).
