Arithmetic and Geometric Series Calculator

Find the sum of arithmetic and geometric series.

How it works

Arithmetic: S = n(a₁ + aₙ)/2. Geometric: S = a₁(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1), and a₁/(1 - r) if |r| < 1 for infinite terms.

Frequently asked questions

When does an infinite geometric series converge?

Only when the absolute value of the ratio is below 1.
