Car Cost per Mile Calculator

Calculate the true cost per mile of owning and driving a car.

How it works

Total yearly cost is fuel (miles / mpg x price) plus insurance, maintenance, depreciation and other costs. Dividing by miles driven gives the cost per mile.

Frequently asked questions

How does this compare to mileage reimbursement?

The IRS standard mileage rate is a useful benchmark for business driving.

Which costs dominate?

For many cars depreciation and insurance outweigh fuel.
