Milligrams to Ounces Converter

Convert milligrams to ounces instantly. 1 mg = 3.5274e-05 oz.

How it works

To convert milligrams to ounces, multiply the number of milligrams by 3.5274e-05. One milligram equals 3.5274e-05 ounces, and one ounce equals 28,349.523 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Ounces formula

Ounces = milligrams × 3.5274e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to ounces. Multiply 12.5 × 3.5274e-05 = 0.00044092452 oz. To go the other way, multiply ounces by 28,349.523 (or divide by 3.5274e-05).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
