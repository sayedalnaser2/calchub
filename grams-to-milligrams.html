Grams to Milligrams Converter

Convert grams to milligrams instantly. 1 g = 1000 mg.

How it works

To convert grams to milligrams, multiply the number of grams by 1000. One gram equals 1000 milligrams, and one milligram equals 0.001 grams. The table shows common values.

Grams to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = grams × 1000

Worked example: convert 12.5 grams to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 1000 = 12500 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 0.001 (or divide by 1000).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
