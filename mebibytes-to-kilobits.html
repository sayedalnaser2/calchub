Mebibytes to Kilobits Converter

Convert mebibytes to kilobits instantly. 1 MiB = 8,388.608 Kb.

How it works

To convert mebibytes to kilobits, multiply the number of mebibytes by 8,388.608. One mebibyte equals 8,388.608 kilobits, and one kilobit equals 0.00011920929 mebibytes. The table shows common values.

Mebibytes to Kilobits formula

Kilobits = mebibytes × 8,388.608

Worked example: convert 12.5 mebibytes to kilobits. Multiply 12.5 × 8,388.608 = 104,857.6 Kb. To go the other way, multiply kilobits by 0.00011920929 (or divide by 8,388.608).

About digital storage units

These conversions use decimal (SI) prefixes where 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Some operating systems use binary multiples of 1,024 instead, so reported sizes can differ slightly.
