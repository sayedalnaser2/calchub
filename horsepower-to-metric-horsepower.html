How it works

To convert horsepower to metric horsepower, multiply the number of horsepower by 1.0138697. One horsepower equals 1.0138697 metric horsepower, and one metric horsepower equals 0.98632007 horsepower. The table shows common values.

Horsepower to Metric Horsepower formula

Metric horsepower = horsepower × 1.0138697

Worked example: convert 12.5 horsepower to metric horsepower. Multiply 12.5 × 1.0138697 = 12.673371 PS. To go the other way, multiply metric horsepower by 0.98632007 (or divide by 1.0138697).

About power units

Power is the rate of energy use. The watt is the SI unit; horsepower is common for engines and BTU per hour for heating and cooling equipment.
