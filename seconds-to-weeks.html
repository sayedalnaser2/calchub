How it works

To convert seconds to weeks, multiply the number of seconds by 1.65344e-06. To convert weeks to seconds, multiply by 604,800 (or divide by 1.65344e-06). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Seconds to Weeks formula

Weeks = seconds × 1.65344e-06

Worked example: convert 12.5 seconds to weeks. Multiply 12.5 × 1.65344e-06 = 2.0668e-05 weeks. To go the other way, multiply weeks by 604,800 (or divide by 1.65344e-06).
