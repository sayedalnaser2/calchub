Overtime Pay Calculator

Calculate overtime earnings at time-and-a-half or double time.

How it works

Overtime pay equals overtime hours x hourly rate x the multiplier. In the US, non-exempt workers get 1.5x for hours over 40 per week.

Frequently asked questions

Who gets overtime?

Most hourly (non-exempt) US employees. Some salaried roles are exempt, depending on duties and salary level.
