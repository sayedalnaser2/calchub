How it works

To convert centimeters per second to kilometers per second, multiply the number of centimeters per second by 1e-05. To convert kilometers per second to centimeters per second, multiply by 100,000 (or divide by 1e-05). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Centimeters per Second to Kilometers per Second formula

Kilometers per second = centimeters per second × 1e-05

Worked example: convert 12.5 centimeters per second to kilometers per second. Multiply 12.5 × 1e-05 = 0.000125 km/s. To go the other way, multiply kilometers per second by 100,000 (or divide by 1e-05).

About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. Meters per second is the SI unit; miles per hour and kilometers per hour are used on roads, and knots at sea and in aviation.
