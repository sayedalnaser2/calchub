How it works

To convert short tons to milligrams, multiply the number of short tons by 907,184,740. One short ton equals 907,184,740 milligrams, and one milligram equals 1.10231e-09 short tons. The table shows common values.

Short Tons to Milligrams formula

Milligrams = short tons × 907,184,740

Worked example: convert 12.5 short tons to milligrams. Multiply 12.5 × 907,184,740 = 11,339,809,000 mg. To go the other way, multiply milligrams by 1.10231e-09 (or divide by 907,184,740).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
