Minutes to Nanoseconds Converter

Convert minutes to nanoseconds instantly. 1 min = 60,000,000,000 ns.

How it works

To convert minutes to nanoseconds, multiply the number of minutes by 60,000,000,000. One minute equals 60,000,000,000 nanoseconds, and one nanosecond equals 1.66667e-11 minutes. The table shows common values.

Minutes to Nanoseconds formula

Nanoseconds = minutes × 60,000,000,000

Worked example: convert 12.5 minutes to nanoseconds. Multiply 12.5 × 60,000,000,000 = 750,000,000,000 ns. To go the other way, multiply nanoseconds by 1.66667e-11 (or divide by 60,000,000,000).
