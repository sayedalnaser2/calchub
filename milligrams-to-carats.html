Milligrams to Carats Converter

Convert milligrams to carats instantly. 1 mg = 0.005 ct.

How it works

To convert milligrams to carats, multiply the number of milligrams by 0.005. One milligram equals 0.005 carats, and one carat equals 200 milligrams. The table shows common values.

Milligrams to Carats formula

Carats = milligrams × 0.005

Worked example: convert 12.5 milligrams to carats. Multiply 12.5 × 0.005 = 0.0625 ct. To go the other way, multiply carats by 200 (or divide by 0.005).

About weight and mass units

Mass measures the amount of matter in an object. The kilogram is the SI base unit; ounces, pounds and stone are customary units defined exactly in terms of the kilogram (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).
