Square Yards to Square Miles Converter

Convert square yards to square miles instantly. 1 yd² = 3.22831e-07 mi².

How it works

To convert square yards to square miles, multiply the number of square yards by 3.22831e-07. One square yard equals 3.22831e-07 square miles, and one square mile equals 3,097,600 square yards. The table shows common values.

Square Yards to Square Miles formula

Square miles = square yards × 3.22831e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 3.22831e-07 = 4.03538e-06 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 3,097,600 (or divide by 3.22831e-07).
