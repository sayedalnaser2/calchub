How it works

To convert square yards to square miles, multiply the number of square yards by 3.22831e-07. To convert square miles to square yards, multiply by 3,097,600 (or divide by 3.22831e-07). Type a value in either box and the other updates, so this one page works in both directions.

Square Yards to Square Miles formula

Square miles = square yards × 3.22831e-07

Worked example: convert 12.5 square yards to square miles. Multiply 12.5 × 3.22831e-07 = 4.03538e-06 mi². To go the other way, multiply square miles by 3,097,600 (or divide by 3.22831e-07).
